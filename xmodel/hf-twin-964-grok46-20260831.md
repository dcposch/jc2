### 3.2 A polynomial member (charged)

The associated curve is not a census representative. A proper polynomial parametrisation of degrees `(9,6)` with `deg(y^3-x^2)=4` realises the reduced sequence on a rational one-place curve. In the even/odd family


```text

y(t) = t^6 + 2 t^4 + (5/2) t^2 + 3/4
```

and


```text

x' = 9 t^8 + 21 t^6 + (105/4) t^4 + (105/8) t^2 + 63/32
```
