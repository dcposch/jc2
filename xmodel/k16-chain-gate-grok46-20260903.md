## 1. Lemma CHAIN

Work after a geometric point `A_t -> K` with `K` algebraically closed.  Residual
ring `K[v_1,...,v_n]`, `n=t`, `I=I_{t,+}` generated by the positive-weight
rows `T_{t,1},...,T_{t,2t-1}`.  (Weights: `wt(b4)=1`, `wt(q_{j,0})=j`,
`wt(b3)=t+1`.)  Homogeneity of positive weight forces `I subset (v_1,...,v_n)`
and `0 in V(I)`.

**Forward.**  If `v_i in sqrt(J)` then `sqrt(J)=sqrt(J+(v_i))`.  Taking
`J=I+(v_1,...,v_{i-1})` and iterating gives
`sqrt(I)=sqrt(I+(v_1,...,v_n))=(v_1,...,v_n)`, so `V(I)={0}` and `dim I=0`.

**Converse.**  If `dim I=0` then, the cone being `{0}`, Nullstellensatz gives
`sqrt(I)=(v_1,...,v_n)`, so every `v_i` already lies in `sqrt(I)`, hence in
`sqrt(I+(v_1,...,v_{i-1}))`.  Every ordering works.

**Split indices.**  `A_t=Q[y]/(H_t)` is a product `Q x Q` precisely at
`t=3s^2-1` (integer `s`; in small range, `t=2`).  Then
`A_t[v]/I \cong product of the two fibres` and Krull dim is the max of the
fibre dims, so `dim_{A_t}=0` iff both fibres have dim 0 iff the chain holds
on both iff it holds in `A_t[v]`.  The trap is operational, not the
equivalence: a step that inverts a zerodivisor of `A_t` (e.g. `alpha_2` on
`y=1/5`) is a fibrewise statement, not an `A_t`-statement.  Cone-gate
`t=2` is the witness.  All Q-PLANE unit tests below use `N != 0`, i.e.
nonzerodivisors.

**A sub-chart is a tail step.**  Step `i` is exactly `I` restricted to the
coordinate subspace `{v_1=...=v_{i-1}=0}`.  Proposition SUBCHART (cone gate:
CONFIRMED) collapses eliminated variables only when the surviving semigroup is
sparse, i.e. when few residual weights remain — large `i`.  The method
supplies the tail of a chain.

**The head `b4` is not a sub-chart statement.**  Three facts, not a slogan.
(i) `(C0)` is `b4 in sqrt(I)` with *no* coordinate set to zero.  By weighted
homogeneity this is equivalent to `I|_{b4=1}=(1)`, an *open* (dehomogenized)
chart.  Sub-chart restriction as used here is always a closed coordinate
subspace.  (ii) The `b4`-axis `{q_{*,0}=b3=0}` is a sub-chart, but it proves
only `b4 in sqrt(I+(q's,b3))` — a tail in a different order, not `(C0)`.
(iii) `wt(b4)=1`, so `Sem({b4})=N_{>=0}` and SUBCHART kills no eliminated
variable by weight.  On `{b4=0}` with `q_{2,0},...,q_{t-1,0}` free, the
weights `2,3,...,t-1` already generate every integer `>=2` for `t>=5`, so
SUBCHART is empty there as well.  Hence no reordering of sub-charts reaches
`(C0)`.

## 3. Lemma UNIQUE-POWER

On `S_r'={b4=q_{2,0}=...=q_{t-r-1,0}=0}` write
`prod_{i=1}^r q_{t-i,0}^{e_i} b3^f`.  Matching `t` and the constant in
`sum e_i(t-i)+f(t+1)=m(t-r)` as an identity in `t` gives `E+f=m` and
`E+M=m(1+r)` with `E=sum e_i`, `M=sum i e_i`.  Then `M<=rE` forces `E>=m`,
while `f>=0` forces `E<=m`, so `E=m`, `f=0`, `M=mr`, hence `e_r=m`.

Non-uniform extras: `E+f=m+k`, `k!=0`, yields `t=(M-f-mr)/k`.  For `k>0`,
`t<=r`; for `k<0`, `t<=m(1+r)-1`.  So uniqueness for all `t>=m(1+r)`.
`unique_power.py`: the only identity-in-`t` monomial is `q_{t-r,0}^m`; both
bounds are attained (`t=3r+2` for `m=3`, `t=4r+3` for `m=4`); no extras
above the bound for `r=1..6`, `t<80`.

Corollary: for `t>=3r+3`, band `k=t+3r+1` is terminal (`t+3r+1<=2t-1`) and
equals `A2^{(r)} q_{t-r,0}^3` on the *chain* chart `S_r'`, because the
coefficient of that monomial in the full polynomial is unchanged by setting
intermediate `q`'s to zero, and uniqueness kills every other weight-`3(t-r)`
monomial.  That is `(C_{t-r})` once `A2^{(r)}` is a unit.
