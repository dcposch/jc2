## 3. New avenue / mechanism

**Heegaard-Floer / semigroup-distribution attainment obstructions on the survivor
delta-sequences** (new engine; not in any of the 46 rows or overlays, which record
only splice admissibility, colouring, and log-BMY for this class).

Setting, from the promoted structure: a charged survivor is a reduced irreducible
plane curve `B`, one place at infinity, `A1` normalization (rational), sole affine
singularity unibranch with semigroup `S_a` of conductor `2*Delta_aff` (plane-branch
semigroups are symmetric, so conductor `= 2*delta`). Its projective closure is a
**rational cuspidal curve with at most two cusps** — the affine cusp and possibly
the place at infinity — of some degree `d`, with
`delta_infty = (d-1)(d-2)/2 - Delta_aff >= 0`, and with `S_infty` constrained by the
Abhyankar–Moh semigroup theorem (delta-sequence divisibility conditions) because the
place at infinity is one place.

Rational cuspidal plane curves are exactly the class where the strongest modern
nonexistence technology lives and has never been aimed at this campaign: the FLMN
semigroup distribution property (Fernández de Bobadilla–Luengo–Melle-Hernández–
Némethi, Proc. LMS 2006), proved for cuspidal curves via Heegaard-Floer d-invariants
(Borodzik–Livingston, JEMS 2014) and lattice cohomology (Bodnár–Némethi), with
multi-cusp counting versions via semigroup infimum-convolution (Bodnár–Celoria–
Golla); plus Varchenko spectrum semicontinuity and Orevkov's sharp BMY-type
multiplicity inequality; plus, in the unicuspidal one-Puiseux-pair regime, a
*complete classification* (the FLMN Fibonacci-type list). Exact statements and the
multi-cusp counting form must be literature-pinned at execution; I flag that now
rather than consume it.

Mechanism: for a fixed survivor `S_a`, the pairs `(d, S_infty)` compatible with the
genus split, AM divisibility, and Bézout are **finite and small**. Evaluate the
counting/distribution conditions on each pair. If every pair fails, no plane curve
realizes `S_a` in the charged class at all — an unconditional row kill strictly
stronger than colouring (it is colouring-independent). If some pair passes, that
`(d, S_infty)` is a concrete construction target for the falsification side (§5).
Two typing cautions, recorded per FALLACY discipline: (i) the counting conditions
are projective and blind to which line is at infinity — they transfer as necessary
conditions, which is the direction needed; (ii) the charged-class interface (sole
affine singularity, unibranch) must be carried verbatim from the promoted census
scope, not re-derived.
