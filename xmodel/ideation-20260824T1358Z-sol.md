## Detailed Card 1 — exact `(6,9)` Pfaffian vector field

**Status:** new exact calculation from this blind turn; unreviewed.

Start from the producer's constant-W normal form

```text
f = z^6 + a4*z^4 + a3*z^3 + a2*z^2 + a1*z + a0,
g = g0(a) + kappa*(z^3 + a4*z/2 + a3/2).
```

Form the `5 x 5` matrix `M(a,kappa)` whose rows are the coefficients of
`z^4,z^3,z^2,z,z^0` in

```text
J_xz = f_x*g_z - f_z*g_x
```

and whose columns multiply `(a0',a1',a2',a3',a4')`. Exact expansion gives a
generically nonzero determinant of total degree 20 and `kappa`-degree 5, so
the unresolved lower system is generically a rational algebraic vector
field, rather than merely a loose Pfaffian relation.

On the DS/Birch component

```text
(a0,a1,a2,a3,a4) = (6*lambda^3, 0, 10*lambda^2, 0, 4*lambda),
```

the full factorization is

\[
\det M=\frac{81}{32}\lambda^5
 (4\kappa+63\lambda^3)
 (8\kappa^2-72\kappa\lambda^3+729\lambda^6)^2.
\]

Set

\[
D_1=4\kappa+63\lambda^3,\qquad
D_2=8\kappa^2-72\kappa\lambda^3+729\lambda^6.
\]

Away from `lambda*D1*D2=0`, exact solution of `M v=e5` gives

\[
a_0'=\frac{2}{\lambda D_1},\qquad a_1'=a_3'=0,
\]

\[
a_2'=\frac{4(4\kappa^2-9\kappa\lambda^3+405\lambda^6)}
 {\lambda^2D_1D_2},
\qquad
a_4'=\frac{4(2\kappa^2+81\lambda^6)}
 {\lambda^3D_1D_2}.
\]

The two independent tangency defects to the DS parameterization factor as

\[
a_2'-5\lambda a_4'
 =\frac{-12\kappa(2\kappa+3\lambda^3)}
 {\lambda^2D_1D_2},
\]

\[
a_0'-\frac92\lambda^2a_4'
 =\frac{-4\kappa(5\kappa+36\lambda^3)}
 {\lambda D_1D_2}.
\]

Consequently, on the nonsingular open set, simultaneous tangency holds iff
`kappa=0`. At `kappa=0`,

\[
a_4'=\frac4{567\lambda^6},\qquad
\lambda'=\frac1{567\lambda^6}.
\]

Scaling the terminal RHS from `1` to `j/s` recovers exactly

\[
567s\lambda^6\lambda'=j,
\]

the producer's pure-DS source ODE.

## New speculative connections and falsifiers

- **Pfaffian field to differential algebra.** The determinant factors are
  candidate invariant/pole divisors. Weighted projective compactification,
  Darboux-polynomial searches, and local Kovalevskaya/Painleve balances can
  seek a theorem excluding polynomial-boundary trajectories. A failed
  balance is only a search result.
- **AS109 branch radius to cap growth.** If `A` were polynomial, the canonical
  gauge `(A,Q D(A))` would be polynomial, conflicting with the producer's
  polar noncancellation theorem; hence a conditional lift forces a
  nonpolynomial algebraic Tate branch. A generic critical value
  `P=g(alpha)`, `D(alpha)=0` may bound its radius and force linear coefficient
  or truncation-cap growth. The missing identity-component and upper-bound
  steps are load-bearing.
- **AS109 depth-three refinement.** Unreviewed algebra suggests the producer's
  cap-`p` contradiction extends to every cap `D<2p-1`: the degree cap in
  `g(X)` kills the relevant `[x^p]` correction, divergence kills the
  `[x^(p-1)y]` correction, and the top `[x^(2p-2)y]` coefficient remains `1`.
  The identity construction attains `2p-1`, so this would make the depth-three
  minimum exact after review. Nothing here proves the conjectured
  `1+(n-1)(p-1)` bound for `n>=4` or excludes an unbounded lift.
- **Arithmetic transfer to `(6,9)`.** An integral AS109 initial form of the
  five-coefficient-plus-`kappa` system could test whether the characteristic-
  zero frontier survives mod `p`; this is meaningful only with a theorem
  preserving the special fibre and all boundary data.
- **Polar divisor to infinity.** The gcd/no-cancellation mechanism may become
  coordinate-free through Rees valuations or an `A_infinity` divisor. If it
  does not survive changes of symplectic chart, it supplies no global route.
- **Orbit-degree failure to topology.** The type-failure of reducing at one
  cubic root clarifies why one-coordinate passports are insufficient; any
  topological successor must couple both coordinates and the selected orbit.
