### Card A — Fourier--Verdier nonproperness defect

**Role:** strongest proof attack; genuinely new zero-base object.

**Exact target claim.** For a hypothetical Keller map

> the perverse/irregular boundary constituents of `M_F`, equivalently the
> nonproperness defect detected by `K_F`, have zero conormal multiplicity;
> hence `K_F=0` and `F` is proper.

The endgame is exact conditional deduction: proper plus etale makes `F`
finite etale; `A^2_C` has no nontrivial connected finite etale cover, so the
generic degree is one and the Keller map is an automorphism.

An equivalent attack surface is Fourier--Laplace. For a nonzero target
covector `lambda=(a,b)`, the Fourier fiber of `M_F` is governed by the twisted
de Rham complex of `exp(aP+bQ)`. Since `d(aP+bQ)` never vanishes in affine
space, every nonzero class is a vanishing cycle at infinity. The proposed
surface lemma is that the pure-boundary determinant identity and positivity
of perverse characteristic cycles leave no such infinity class in dimension
two. If the singular support is only the zero section, regularity and the
source de Rham cohomology then force generic rank one.

monodromy and all branches. Unlike TRACE-REG, it does not ask local data to
reconstruct coordinate multiplication.
