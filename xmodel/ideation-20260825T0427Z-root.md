## 2. Bottleneck reranking

Proof side:

1. **Coverage/landing, now in a concrete local form.**  TD6 has emptied a
   genuine three-parameter center atlas, but q-boundary, dead stretch, F1,
   pole, and other source moduli are outside it.  We need a theorem carrying
   the unit obstruction across those directions, not hundreds of slices.
2. **Absolute or cofinal complexity.**  Even a whole TD6 theorem does not put
   every Keller map in TD6 or bound the sheet/topological-degree ladder.
3. **Q8 component algebraization and good reduction.**  This is narrower
   than the first two but currently closest to an end-to-end exclusion of one
   difficult selected maximum-twelve lane.

Counterexample side:

1. **Exact canonical state and time-homogeneity.**  Unreduced affine charts
   are insufficient; canonical division carries must be part of a finite,
   representative-independent transition state.
2. **A bounded-support recurrent orbit preserving collision.**  Finite-depth
   survival is nearly worthless unless it yields a cycle or another all-depth
   certificate at one fixed polynomial cap.
3. **Characteristic-zero polynomial descent.**  A synchronously periodic
   finite carry cycle would solve this by making every stabilized coefficient
   rational; without such recurrence, an unrestricted 3-adic analytic limit
   is not a counterexample.

The global proof wall remains the conjunction of coverage and a ceiling.  No
bounded Q8 or TD6 result, however clean, should be described as approaching a
full proof unless it changes one of those two quantifiers.

## 3. New mechanism and new connections

### New mechanism: periodic canonical carry cycles as rational algebraizers

Represent each exact `/3` operation by a finite carry transducer whose state
stores the bounded coefficient support, canonical residue digits, quotient
digits needed by the next source row, and collision witnesses.  If a state
repeats under a time-homogeneous successor, all subsequent base-three digits
are eventually periodic.  Every coefficient is then in `Q intersect Z_3`,
not merely in `Z_3`; with fixed finite support the limiting maps are
polynomials over `Q`.  Polynomial identities holding in `Q_3[x,y]` then hold
over `Q`, and a periodically lifted collision with distinct rational limits
would be an actual JC2 counterexample.  Conversely, an acyclic exact state
graph proves nonlifting within the registered cap.

This connects avenues 19 and 21 to finite automata/model checking and removes
the usual unsupported leap from all-Witt survival to characteristic zero.
It is useful even if no cycle exists because it supplies a fail-closed stop
condition.

### Connection: TD6 adjoints as a logarithmic obstruction-line connection

The repeated one-dimensional P12 cokernel class `-k/50` across generic and
raw divisors suggests a line bundle over source-moduli space, not a sequence
of coincidental row certificates.  Differentiate the transported complex and
its adjoint projector along q-boundary/dead-stretch directions.  If the
obstruction section `r` satisfies `nabla r = omega*r`, with logarithmic
`omega` whose residues are controlled on the already closed divisors, then
nonvanishing propagates across connected parameter strata.  This joins the
TD6 sheet atlas (2), strip/residue ODE technology (3), log-boundary methods
(28), and universal Fitting/adjoint certificates (32).

### Connection: Q8 component blocks as a finite-field ZMT/monodromy client

Moving-v Hensel/Padé can produce an actual graph over the candidate plane
curve; full Jacobians make it a multiplicity-one component; the DVR lemma
then controls specialization; primitive Galois contact blocks plus infinity
exclude the all-eight component.  This is an exact composition of formal
germs (4), component monodromy (25--26), and integrality/Zariski Main (31).
Each link has a direct falsifier, unlike a generic appeal to algebraization.
