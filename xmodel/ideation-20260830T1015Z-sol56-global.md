## 5. Exact q=6 attack and why q=8 does not copy verbatim

The first task is to freeze the ordinary-blowup reconstruction. In the
standard nine-blowup `F_2` marking, let `I` be the six points carried by the
strict boundary class, let `ell` be the distinguished complementary point,
and let `o_1,o_2` be the other two points. The candidate reconstruction to
check chart-by-chart is

```text
T = S + 3F - sum_(i in I) P_i,
M_6 = 2F - 2P_ell - P_o1 - P_o2,
C_tot = R-M_6
      = 4S + 9F - 2 sum_(i in I)P_i - P_o1 - P_o2.
```

Thus the total multiplicity vector is, up to marked permutation,
`c=(2,2,2,2,2,2,1,1,0)`, and crucially

```text

```

whereas both contacts vanish in q=8. This formula is a preregistered
reconstruction target, not a promoted claim; a mismatch stops every
descendant and replaces the input before enumeration.

Write a strict prime as

```text
C_j = a_j S + b_j F - sum_i x_ji P_i,
s_j = C_j.S = b_j-2a_j,
t_j = C_j.T = a_j+b_j-sum_(i in I)x_ji.
```

The exact nonnegative decomposition constraints are

```text
sum_j a_j=4,  sum_j x_ji=c_i,
sum_j s_j=1,  sum_j t_j=1,
a_j>0, x_ji>=0, s_j>=0, t_j>=0,
2p_a(C_j)-2=C_j.(C_j+K)>=-2,
K=-2S-4F+sum_i P_i.
```

For distinct primes the Gram entry is

```text
C_j.C_k = 2a_j*a_k + a_j*s_k + a_k*s_j - sum_i x_ji*x_ki.
```

Let `Q=sum_(i,j)x_ji^2`, and let `a_s` be the `a`-degree of the unique
component with `s_j=1`. Then

```text

   = 2 sum_(j<k)a_j*a_k - 9 - a_s + Q/2.
```

This gives a useful exact baseline check. For generic `B3/A2`, three positive
degrees force `(a_j)=(2,1,1)` up to order and convexity gives `Q>=14`, hence
`E>=6`. For `U3/A3`, two positive degrees summing to four give `E>=1` over
`(1,3),(2,2),(3,1)`. These inequalities are desk proofs; they do **not** yet
eliminate q=6.
