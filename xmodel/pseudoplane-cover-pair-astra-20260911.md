## 1. The cover and the deleted line, verified

Write S=Spec C[A,U,Z]/(U²-A-A²Z) and T=Spec C[x,t,Z]/(t²-1-x²Z). Both are smooth: simultaneous vanishing of the defining partial derivatives would force A=U=0, respectively x=t=0, and then contradict the remaining derivative or equation. Put q(x,t,Z)=(x²,xt,Z), with sigma(x,t,Z)=(-x,-t,Z).

The invariant ring is precisely C[x²,xt,Z]: every even-parity monomial in x,t is generated by x²,xt,t², and t²=1+x²Z. It has the displayed S relation. The extension is finite since x,t are integral. More explicitly, on A!=0 it is obtained by adjoining x with x²=A and t=U/x; on 1+AZ!=0 it is obtained by adjoining t with t²=1+AZ and x=U/t. These two opens cover S, and both quadratic presentations have invertible derivative. Thus q is finite locally free etale of rank2. Sigma has no fixed point, since x=t=0 contradicts T's equation; it interchanges D_+ and D_-.

On T minus D_-, the formulas y=(t-1)/x² where x!=0 and y=Z/(t+1) where t+1!=0 agree and cover the open. Their inverse is t=1+x²y, Z=2y+x²y². Thus this open is exactly A2, and q restricts to the prescribed Phi. No full function-field Galois conclusion about a hypothetical Keller pair follows from this intermediate double cover.

## 2. Exact full-pair ramification constraint

Assume hypothetically H,G in O(S) with {H,G}=c!=0. Work over an algebraic closure of C(h), so the generic H-fibre is a disjoint union of smooth connected affine curves C_i. Work on one component C; do not assume geometric connectedness. The independent differentials dH,dG imply that dG restricted to C is nowhere zero. In particular G is nonconstant on C.

Let bar C be its smooth projective completion, D=bar C minus C, g its genus, and n the degree of G:bar C->P1. The projective map is finite; no finiteness of G:C->A1 is assumed. Split D into poles D_infinity, cardinal p, and finite-valued deleted points D_f. At a point P write e_P for the ramification index of this projective map. At finite values ord_P(dG)=e_P-1; at poles it is -e_P-1. Every point of C has e_P=1.

Riemann--Hurwitz, or the divisor degree2g-2 of dG, therefore gives the exact equation

    R_f := sum_(P in D_f)(e_P-1) = 2g-2+n+p.             (R)

This uses the FULL-PAIR noncriticality, not only an H-fibre Euler characteristic. Finite-valued deleted points cannot be discarded: they supply all finite ramification. Their indices need not be1. No bound on their number or their indices is provided by smoothness of H.

Let L={A=U=0} and p_H(Z)=H|L. For a generic h, either p_H is constant and C misses L, or the intersections C with L are transverse and finite, since h avoids the finitely many critical values of p_H. Let m_C count them on this component. Locally at L, 1+AZ is a unit and A=U²/(1+AZ). Transversality makes U a parameter on C, so A|C has a zero of order2 at each intersection and no other affine zero. This also shows directly why adjoining sqrt(A) is unramified there, despite A vanishing.

The inverse image tilde C in T is an etale double cover of C. Its normalized projective extension branches only at D. Equivalently, branch points are exactly the points of D where ord_P(A|C) is odd; units in the completed local ring have square roots in characteristic zero over our algebraically closed field. The cover is either connected or two copies of C. Removing D_- deletes precisely ONE of the two lifts of each C-intersection with L. Every such extra deleted point is finite-valued and unramified for G pullback, since it was an ordinary point of the smooth etale cover. Its contribution to R_f upstairs is zero. The other lift on D_+ remains.

## 3. The involution adds no ramification inequality here

In the connected case, let b_f and b_infinity count branch points of the completed double cover at finite G-values and poles respectively; b=b_f+b_infinity. Its fixed points are exactly these branch points, never the paired D_+/D_- intersection points. The exact cover formulas are

    2*g_tilde-2 = 2*(2g-2)+b,
    n_tilde=2n,             p_tilde=2p-b_infinity.

In particular b is even. At an unbranched finite-valued puncture the two contributions sum to2(e_P-1). At a branched one the single index for G pullback is2e_P, so its contribution is2(e_P-1)+1. Consequently, even after the one-sided D_- deletion,

    R_f_tilde = 2*R_f+b_f.

Applying (R) upstairs gives exactly the same expression:

    2*g_tilde-2+n_tilde+p_tilde
       =2*(2g-2+n+p)+b_f.

Thus upstairs noncriticality/Riemann--Hurwitz does not strengthen downstairs (R) plus double-cover Riemann--Hurwitz. The m_C new finite-valued punctures contribute zero, not m_C units of ramification. For a disconnected cover use the two copies of C separately; b=0 and the summed relation is simply doubled. It would be wrong to substitute a fictitious single genus into the connected formula. Summing over all geometric fibre components is then legitimate, without a connectedness or local-triviality assumption.

There is also a local parity formulation: at a branched point Q over P, ord_Q(dG_pull)=2*ord_P(dG)+1. At finite values this is a positive odd zero order; at poles it is a negative odd order at most-3. These are allowed because the points are deleted. Parity alone supplies no forbidden zero in the affine fibre.

## 4. Explicit hyperbolic curve control, in every mate degree

This is a consistency control for the curve constraints, NOT a realization as a fibre on S or of a polynomial Keller map. Take

    C=P1_z minus {0,1,infinity},       G=z^n,       n>=1,
    tilde C=P1_w minus {0,1,-1,infinity},
    z=w²,                  sigma(w)=-w.

The cover is connected etale on these affine curves and branches only at0,infinity on their completions. Both affine curves are hyperbolic (three and four deleted points). G and its pullback have nowhere-zero differential on the respective affine curves. Here g=0,p=1,R_f=n-1; upstairs g_tilde=0,p_tilde=1,R_f_tilde=2n-1. These satisfy (R) and R_f_tilde=2R_f+1 exactly, for EVERY n, not a degree-four case.

Choose any m>=0 distinct a_j outside {0,1}. Put P(z)=product_j(z-a_j) and A_C=z*P(z)². Its affine zeros at the marked a_j all have order2. Its NORMALIZED Kummer cover is exactly z=w², with sqrt(A_C)=w*P(w²); the odd orders are at0 and infinity. The raw equation X²=A_C is not claimed etale at these zeros. Each marked point of the normalized cover has two interchanged lifts. Delete one lift per pair; each new puncture has finite G-value a_j^n and ramification index1, while its mate remains. The derivative of w^(2n) is nonzero at every such lift. All the required marked-divisor, parity, involution and finite/pole ramification constraints remain satisfied for arbitrary m.
