## 2. Source theorem and notation

There is a dangerous order reversal in the printed paper.  Moh writes
((f,g)=(G,F)), while this campaign writes (F) first.  I therefore define

\[
  \widehat T_i(F,G):=T_i^\psi(G,F),\qquad
  B_i:=\left({\partial T_i^\psi\over\partial f}\right)(G,F)
      ={\partial\widehat T_i\over\partial G}.
\]

With (J(A,B)=A_xB_y-A_yB_x), the chain rule is consequently

\[
             J(F,\widehat T_i)=B_iJ(F,G).                 \tag{2.1}
\]

A prime on a symbol below is never silently read as differentiation; the
derivative is always written explicitly.

The printed sources give the following exact chain.

* Proposition 2.2, printed p.152 with proof on pp.153–154, says that when
  (M_i\le e),
  (\deg_y\widehat T_i=-\mu_i=D_i).  When (M_i<e), its leader is a unit;
  p.154 explicitly explains that “monic” means unit leading coefficient.  In
  (k[x]) this is a nonzero scalar.  The terminal (M_i=e) case has a
  different (c f_e(x)) leader and is not substituted for the effective case.
* Proposition 3.1, printed p.157 (proof pp.157–159, specialization remark
  p.159), gives the unique standard-monomial expansion
  (T_{r+1}=T_r^{n_r}+\sum c_{j,\alpha}g^jT^\alpha), the strict weight bound,
  and the unique equality-weight term, whose exponent of (T_r) is zero.
* Theorem A of the charged characteristic-degree report, lines 30–40,
  packages Proposition 2.2 for every effective index of a realized monic
  Keller datum: the specialized constant-target polynomial attains (D_i)
  and has a nonzero scalar leader.  It licenses all upper rows, the complete
  coefficient-minus-leading-target row, and a leader inverse.  It does not
  put a Keller hypothesis on an arbitrary chart point.
* The total-degree use below has an additional license, not hidden in
  Proposition 2.2: the charged report, lines 70–79, derives
  (\deg\widehat T_2\le D_2) and (\deg\widehat T_3\le D_3) from the printed
  Proposition 4.5 minimum-root argument.
* For the second derivative step, Proposition 3.2(3), printed p.159 with proof
  on pp.160–161, gives
  (\deg_y B_{r+1}=D_{r+1}+M_{r+1}).  Proposition 3.1 alone supplies the
  recurrence; Proposition 3.2 supplies this derivative equality.

This separation matters: a lower degree bound is not attainment, and a
polynomial merely having the Proposition-3.1 shape is not by itself the

## 7. Acceptance boundary and FALLACY-v2 check

verified unit would soundly prove
`UNIT — BRANCH DEAD ON THE NECESSARY T2+T3 CHART — PROVED-HERE`.

canonical resultant recursion.  The loud requested PROPER type is reserved
for a complete nonnegative-dimensional exact-Q result plus the triangular
canonical map and source lift.  Neither promotion condition occurred.

  count or price is asserted.

  attainment; (T_2,T_3) attainment has Proposition 2.2/Theorem A, while the
  full (T_3) face has Proposition 4.6.

* No pole/interior claim is made.

  exact round trip.  Matching names or incidence never substitutes for a map.
* No opaque saturation, raw remainder heuristic, algebraic root selection, or
  vanished-leader division is used.
* Fixed characteristic indices are kept distinct from source arrival tags.
* The family-C zero-weight statement remains conditional on its missing child
  license.

No new exit-price assertion is made, so `charge_basis=` would be inapplicable
and is intentionally absent.
