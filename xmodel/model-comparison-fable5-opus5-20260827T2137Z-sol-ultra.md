## 0. Bottom line

**Opus5 adds material capability beyond Fable5.** The increment is not a
generic quality or style advantage. It is campaign-specific and
nonduplicate: Opus supplied the round's strongest blind theorem, the
geometric `mu_4`-torsor packaging of the four upper gate receivers, and then
derived the exact all-row Lagrange coefficient formula while reviewing
Fable. Those are global geometric/closed-form moves absent from Fable's
sealed report.

Fable remains independently necessary. Her clean sealed yield was the
uniform lower-endpoint local theorem, and her late review of Opus found the
licensed exact-`p`-power linear-vacuity correction at rows `m >= 28`. That
correction survived independent exact hostile replay and repaired an error
in the existing promoted record, including Fable's own earlier review.
Fable was also the stronger operational/custody designer, although most of
her sealed implementation proposals needed material repair.

The right roster conclusion is therefore **complementarity, not
replacement**: Opus deserves equal-standing automatic inclusion, but its
allocation-facing claims still need a Fable/Sol-style hostile audit. This
agrees with both reciprocal reviews, both independent hostile reviews, and
the synthesis; the score below is my independent reconstruction from their
claim-level evidence.

## 2. Evidence: valid unique yield

### 2.1 Fable5

**Blind submission — one clean theorem.** For

```text
4K g' - 3K' g = 4K,        K=xi (xi-rho)^gamma,
```

Fable proved that each distinct root forces one evaluation condition,
`g(0)=g(rho)=0`, that
`h(rho)=4/(4-3 gamma)` after writing `g=(xi-rho)h`, and that the apparent
degree-one branch cannot have the two required roots. Polynomial solvability
therefore requires `gamma == 3 (mod 4)`. Opus independently verified every
step and generalized the displayed `-4/17` value; the synthesis accepted the
uniform theorem. This is a real improvement over
the earlier finite-range check, but it is a local necessity theorem, not an
LF40 verdict or a family exclusion.

**Review phase — one stronger exact correction.** While reviewing Opus,
Fable proved that, in the licensed exact-`p`-power presentation, the
`F_n`-linear term of every `k_m=1` row with `m>=28` is class-exact. Her
frozen-window ranks

```text
P rows 23..36: 3,4,3,4,3,0,3,0,3,0,3,0,1,0
Q rows 23..36: 4,5,4,4,4,0,4,1,4,0,4,1,2,0
```

and totals `27/37` survived a different-model reconstruction. This is the
best evidence for Fable's research value in the round: she did not merely
criticize Opus's rate claim; she found the exact algebraic source of the
failure and filed an erratum against her own prior work.

**Not counted as unique valid yield.** The gauge identity
`T_m(HY)=H T_(m+4)(Y)` is exact, but equivalent periodicity was already in
the reviewed baseline and Opus independently sealed the same gauge. The
fixed-receiver `Gamma_a` direction is promising ideation, not a constructed
coherent-family theorem or an effective `N0`. The prefix ladder and partial
forest validator are engineering designs, not mathematical theorems.

### 2.2 Opus5

**Blind submission — the round's strongest promoted theorem.** Opus
recognized the four periodic upper receivers as the character summands of
the geometric Kummer torsor

```text
V_H = {p^4=H} -> U=A1-Z(H).
```

After the necessary geometric-base and disconnected-component qualifier,
both Fable and Sol independently confirmed

```text
H1_dR(V_H) = direct_sum_(j=0)^3 H1_dR(U,nabla_j),
b1(V_H)    = 4(r-1)+c,
c           = gcd(4,e_1,...,e_r) = sum_j k_j.
```

This explains the exact frozen totals `42/51/12/87` as three four-row
periods and gives a reusable target-space/height ceiling. The theorem is
global conceptual compression rather than another fixture calculation.

**Review phase — exact all-row formula.** While reviewing Fable, Opus used
Lagrange inversion to derive, for the pre-reversion root
`phi(t)=F(t)^(1/8)`,

```text
q_n = (2/(n+2)) [t^n] phi(t)^(n+2)
    = (2/(n+2)) [t^n] F(t)^((n+2)/8).
```

The independent post-seal exact review confirmed the formula with the
notation guard that `P` in Opus's display must mean the pre-reversion
`phi(t)`, not `P(s)`. It yields the character and triangular leading term at
all orders and resolves Fable's mistaken “one functional per sector” reading.

**Directly tied source-review capability.** Opus's same-round G2 hostile
review proved the VGG 7.3/7.4 transpose-subsumption theorem: the alleged
physical-other-chart packet is a weaker presentation of native 7.1/7.2 on
the same `upper_dir=(-3,1)` edge. It also caught two reconstructed external
source hashes, split `C74-PLACE` into L1--L5, and confirmed the finite-end
identity. Because this work was already live at the packet seal and Opus
consulted its own artifact, I count it as review capability and strategic
accuracy, not as blind-submission novelty.

## 3. Evidence: error severity and scope control

### 3.1 Fable5 — high-severity load-bearing errors

1. **The finite-determination proof failed.** Krull separation proves that a
   section whose every coefficient vanishes is zero; it does not prove that
   some uniform finite prefix detects zero. The coherent module was not
   constructed, rank jumps were not stratified, and algebraic residue
   pairings were asserted rational without proof. No effective pointwise or
   uniform `N0` followed.
2. **The lower transfer was mislicensed.** The correct transformed series is
   `P^-3`, not a copied upper `P^2` tower; raw label 20 is resonant; the
   direct operator has offset `m-8`; and finite raw support through row 40
   does not truncate the transformed series. The resulting localized
   nonresonant receiver count through 40 is `27`, not `29`, and neither
   count predicts unit versus proper.
3. **`29 versus 36` was a category error.** The receiver coordinates are
   F-side generator ceilings while the 36 count was G-side and already
   consumed/coupled by earlier rows. The literal compiler cokernel is a
   different polynomial/windowed object. The claimed PROPER direction had
   no mathematical support.
4. **Operational tools needed material guards.** Early prefix rungs omitted
   the affine `D22` targets and were guaranteed proper; the “two proper” stop
   was invalid; the held 466-generator object was full-ideal-equivalent, not
   merely a top prefix. The partial finite-end validator needed certified
   leaf-to-marked-end injectivity and independent right-hand invariants.
5. **Post-seal source truth erased the top proof allocation.** The
   `C74-PLACE -> EXIT-RPMC(C)` physical-chart corridor was refuted. This is
   not a blindness failure—Fable labelled the parent atoms provisional and
   supplied rollback semantics—but it substantially reduced realized yield.

### 3.2 Opus5 — correct core, severe extrapolation errors

1. **Target dimension was promoted into an operational schedule.** The
   torsor theorem bounds abstract receiver coordinates, not independence or
   realized codimension. `P57/Q51` crossed only the 121-slot subwindow; the
   safe full-124 not-before bounds are `P58/Q52`, and no row of collapse is
   proved.
2. **`GATE-MARCH` was not a global linear solver.** New blocks enter
   triangularly only while they exist, later rows are nonlinear in old
   coefficients, and Fable's exact review showed that the licensed linear
   part is already vacuous at the `k_m=1` rows from 28 onward. The proposed
   stall semantics would have fired for a theorem-driven reason and
   misdiagnosed tower failure.
3. **The endpoint no-go was false.** Although the isolated gate ideal is
   F-only, support only at the F-origin would force `D22=0`, contradicting
   the inhomogeneous endpoint target `D22=1`; free G coordinates do not block
   an F-only decision. Raw determinant rows also couple F and G.
4. **Allocation language overran the theorem.** “One third of a
   computation,” the held-quotient prediction, and the claim that no
   computation through row 34 could empty the face did not apply to the
   live rows-1--22 affine endpoint object.
5. **One review repair also overreached.** The all-row raw sequence `q_n` is
   algebraic/P-recursive, but Opus's cross-review treated de Rham gate
   coordinates as automatically P-recursive. The exact hostile review kept
   that projection/creative-telescoping step open.

The distinction behind the error-control score is important: Fable's errors
invalidated the load-bearing proof of her primary mechanism; Opus's errors
invalidated the allocation and algorithmic consequences while leaving the
torsor theorem itself intact.

## 4. Evidence: review discipline, breadth, and implementability

### Review discipline

Both reviewers passed the mandatory hash gate, read the charged report and
truth delta, supplied itemized verdicts and errata, named the narrowest valid
theorems, and separated peer value from peer error.

- Fable confirmed Opus's torsor theorem without dilution, explicitly answered
  that Opus added unique capability, recomputed every budget, recounted the
  frozen `F/G` slots, found the row-28 linear-vacuity theorem, and accepted an
  erratum against her own previous record. Her proposed symbolic `D28`
  discriminator nevertheless had the wrong affine target, became bilinear
  with symbolic `F_6`, lacked a compatible lower-target point, and tested
  descent/window rank rather than the class claim.
- Opus credited Fable's lower local theorem, derived the exact all-row
  Lagrange formula, detected the Krull converse error, distinguished
  localized from polynomial/windowed cokernels, corrected the LF40 budget
  semantics, and deleted the stale corridor. His proposed automatic passage
  from raw `q_n` recurrence to gate-coordinate recurrence remained unproved;
  his review header also names `ops/adapters/claude.sh` although the pinned
  Opus run used `ops/adapters/opus.sh`, a minor provenance typo.

Opus's blind report disclosed one filename-only `jc2-lean` boundary touch
from an early repository-wide search; no content was opened or used. Fable
reported no such touch. This is a small process defect, not mathematical
contamination.

### Avenue breadth

Both satisfied the complete 46-avenue contract rather than tunnel on the
gate tower. Fable supplied five explicit avenue raises, ten guarded
composition/separation checks, all eight lane calls, and three detailed
cards spanning upper/lower gates, coverage, finite ends, branch cycles, K00,
and AWS custody. Opus changed eight avenue calls and ranged across the same
portfolio with additional source geometry, topological degree, torsor/index,
Ritt primitivity, and explicit cover/base separations. Fable's breadth was
more operational; Opus's was more conceptual. Neither has a meaningful
breadth advantage.

### Implementability

Fable's best implementable sealed idea was the one-sided prefix-unit design,
but it required all affine targets from rung one, removal of the “two proper”
stop, literal-subset discipline, and replay against the authoritative full
manifest. Her validator becomes safe only after a marked place injection.
Her coherent compression and lower ledger were research programs, not ready
tools.

Opus's four small receiver projectors and fixed-point row evaluation are
cheap and useful. The advertised solution-locus march, random-survivor rate
semantics, and AWS allocation conclusions were not implementable as stated.
The all-row formula makes an algebraic/creative-telescoping successor much
more concrete, but the gate-coordinate recurrence and uniform singular-index
bound remain open.
