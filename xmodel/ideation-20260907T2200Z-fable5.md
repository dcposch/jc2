polynomial on the quotient line E/σ.** Setting from 14v/14y: R=p²V,

σ-even subfield K(E)^σ is K(u) for a coordinate u on that line. Odd
functions are p·K(u). The 14y 3j-level residue, with the centralizer
kernel f(R) retained, reads 9D²−C³−RW ∈ K[R]; reducing mod V leaves
9D²≡C³+f(0). Evaluating at O, where C(O)=0 and every odd D vanishes
(D(O)=D(σO)=−D(O)), kills f(0). Hence, whenever the 3j residue has that
shape with D a polynomial, C≢0 mod V forces C ≡ (3D/C)² in K(E), i.e.
C|_E is the square of an odd function, i.e. C/p² is a square in K(u).
Concretely: ord_Q(C/p²) is even at every non-fixed point Q of E and
divisible by 4 at O and the three infinity points. The 14y collision
