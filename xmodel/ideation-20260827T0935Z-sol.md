## New mechanism and cross-avenue connection

The N-power ladder and the K00 normal-degree ladder are the same computational
object in dual form: a persistence problem for inverse systems under a
distinguished filtration.  At weight `5N`, the ordered-`a1` question asks
whether evaluation at `a1^N` survives in the annihilator of the ideal slice;
at K00 cutoff `D`, the question asks whether the target jet survives after
successive normal-degree extensions.  Both should use one incremental sparse
engine that maintains an affine solution/dual space, transports it to the
next filtration level, isolates only the new obstruction block, screens over
two primes, and reconstructs Q only at a birth/death event.  Full independent
product replay remains the endpoint gate.

This is a campaign-new mechanism, not a new commutative-algebra theorem.  It
connects avenues 2, 31, and 32 and should reduce repeated matrix construction,
rank work, and exact solve cost.

The strongest proof attack is to determine the minimal special-fibre
`a1`-nilpotence exponent by inverse-system persistence while the direct
total-rho eliminant runs, then solve only that exact total weight.  In
parallel, decide K00 local membership by a global syzygy/colon criterion and
feed the answer directly into the mixed `Lambda^19` system.

The strongest actual-counterexample attack remains AS109: turn observed
unbounded polar support into a gauge-invariant obstruction, with residual
degree six as the first non-history-closed case.  A failure of that invariant
should produce a concrete bounded-gauge direction rather than another
unstructured search.

## Three executable idea cards

### Card 1 — `K00-COLON-CONSTANT`

- **Dependencies:** reviewed normalized rows `r1,...,r7` at `C6=1`; exact
  source hashes; no assumption from D8 compatibility.
- **Mechanism:** compute global syzygies of `(r1,...,r6,r7)` and project their
  seventh coordinates.  For `m=(d0,...,d5)`,
  `r7 in (r1,...,r6)_m` iff there is
  `sum u_i r_i + h r7=0` with `h(0)!=0`, equivalently
  `(I:r7)` is not contained in `m`.  This bypasses the currently broken Mora
  transformation and does not require guessing the denominator.
- **Cheapest discriminator:** two-prime module-syzygy/constant-projection
  screen, then one exact-Q syzygy with coefficientwise replay if positive.
- **Positive outcome:** local membership is proved by an explicit denominator
  unit; stop pure D9+ and move to mixed reachability/Taylor realization.
- **Negative outcome:** a certified projection obstruction proves local
  nonmembership, identifying a finite local obstruction without relying on a
  global `dp` remainder degree.
- **Stop:** two term orders with identical projection rank; if both exceed the
  registered resource cap, return to the incremental filtered engine, not
  more local-order wrappers.
- **Information gain:** very high; it directly decides the fork that D2--D8
  only approximate.
