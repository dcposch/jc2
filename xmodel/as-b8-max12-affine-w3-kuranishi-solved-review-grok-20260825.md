## 2. Complete `W2` fibre

RREF of the constant `276 x 172` matrix `A3` has rank 104 and 68 free columns. Dual-AWS `result.json` records `w2_rank=104`, `w2_kernel_dimension=68`, and `T0==parent_sol2`. The particular vector has support only in the `R`-block at monomials `x^2 y` and `x y^5` with coefficients `1` and `2`, which is the already-confirmed digit `R2=x^2 y + 2 x y^5`, `S2=0`.

The integer combination `T(t)=T0+sum t_i K_i` is not reduced modulo 3. Kernel vectors satisfy `L(K_i) ≡ 0 (mod 3)` coefficientwise as integer polynomials, so `L(K_i)/3` is an integer vector. Coefficientwise division by three is therefore a polynomial operation on these parameters. Reducing `T` to canonical `{0,1,2}`-digits first would drop a multiple of 9 into the next Witt digit, which is the illicit carry the unreduced lift is designed to avoid. As `F_3`-vectors, `t |-> T(t) mod 3` is bijective onto the affine solution space, so every first digit is represented exactly once.

## 3. Quadratic formula

Bilinearity of `jac` gives the integer identity

```text
det(F0+3T+9W)-1
  = det(F0)-1 + 3 L(T) + 9 J(T) + 9 L(W) + 27 cross(T,W) + 81 J(W),
```

with `L(T)=jac(P0,S)+jac(R,Q0)`, `J(T)=jac(R,S)`, and `cross(T,W)=jac(R,B)+jac(A,S)`. On the `W2` fibre, `L(T) ≡ u^2 (mod 3)` and `det(F0)-1=-3u^2`, so `det(F0+3T)-1` is divisible by 9 and the `W3` obstruction is `L(W) ≡ -(det(F0+3T)-1)/9 (mod 3)`. Solvability is the vanishing of the left-cokernel projection `pi` of that residual. The left kernel of `A3` has dimension `276-104=172`, matching `ambient_cokernel_dimension`.

The analytic ANF is the polarization of that expansion along the unreduced kernel:

```text
linear_i   = pi( L(K_i)/3 + cross(T0,K_i) ),
diagonal_i = pi( J(K_i) ),
cross_{ij} = pi( cross(K_i,K_j) ).
```

The interpolation ANF uses full determinant evaluations, not those bilinear pieces. Over `F_3` a quadratic satisfies

```text
f(0)=c,
f(e_i)=c+L_i+D_i,
f(2e_i)=c+2L_i+D_i,          because 2^2=1,
f(e_i+e_j)=c+L_i+L_j+D_i+D_j+C_{ij},
```

so `L_i=f(2e_i)-f(e_i)`, `D_i=f(e_i)-c-L_i`, and `C_{ij}=f(e_i+e_j)-f(e_i)-f(e_j)+c`. Those are exactly the compiler identities. The functions `1,t,t^2` on `F_3` are linearly independent (`t^2` takes values `0,1,1` while `t` takes `0,1,2`), so squares are not absorbed into linear terms. Agreement of the two ANFs is a genuine comparison of polarization against interpolation, not two names for one coefficient path. They do share `jac` and the same left kernel; that is one compiler, as the charge requires.

Constants of the 81-equation basis all vanish, matching `t=0` being the already-confirmed frozen point.

## Routing remark (not part of the theorem)

If a characteristic-zero lift of actual total degrees `(8,12)` existed with `det J=1`, the degree-18 part of the Jacobian would have to vanish, so the leading forms `P_8` and `Q_12` would be functionally dependent. Over a field of characteristic zero, two binary homogeneous forms with vanishing Jacobian are powers of a single homogeneous form `K`. Degree reasons force `deg K` to divide `gcd(8,12)=4`. The primitive such pair is `P_8=a K^2`, `Q_12=b K^3` for a homogeneous quartic `K` (the linear and quadratic cases `K=L^4` or `K=Q^2` are absorbed by renaming). The B8 residue has leading forms `(y^8,y^{12})=((y^4)^2,(y^4)^3)`, so any such `K` would have to be associated to `y^4` modulo 3.

That is an all-depth conditional sieve on actual-total-degree `(8,12)` lifts. It is not a hypothesis, lemma, or corollary of the finite-depth `A^95` theorem, and it does not constrain the next 3-adic digit of the present family.
