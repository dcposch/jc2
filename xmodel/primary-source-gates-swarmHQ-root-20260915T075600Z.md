## 1. A new regularity criterion meets an already recorded wrong-ring gap

[Pat Lank, arXiv:2609.15691v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.15691v1), submitted
September 14. Theorem 1.1 characterizes regularity by perfectness of the
derived structure-sheaf pushforward for every blowup. Lemma 5.3 says that a
finite birational map of integral Noetherian affine schemes is an isomorphism
if that pushforward is perfect **over the target of the birational map**.

For a canonical graph ring B and its finite normalization S, this asks for
finite projective dimension of S over B. Perfectness over a polynomial
ambient ring, or of a cotangent complex after passing to the source, is
not this premise. No such implication from the actual Keller hypotheses
was found. This connects directly to the
[September 14 conductor attack](../notes.md#2026-09-14-0432-utc--bounded-conductor-attack-closed-request-status-verified),
which already retained finite projective dimension OVER B as missing.

Read scope: introduction, Lemma 5.3 and its proof, Proposition 5.4, plus
selected construction passages. The splitting references invoked inside
Lemma 5.3 were not independently audited. No regularity theorem is promoted
and no derived-module/control-family retry is selected.

## 2. Conductor lifting is not normalization

## 3. Integrability and nilpotent classifications keep their extra hypotheses

[León–Scárdua, arXiv:2609.14807v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14807v1), dated
September 13, announced September 15: Theorem 1.1 concerns a foliation on
projective n-space, n >= 3, with invariant hyperplane and irreducible reduced
singular hypersurface of maximal, prime-power degree. Theorem 1.2 substitutes
smooth invariant/singular divisors and a maximal-degree condition. These
conditions are not obtained from a general plane Keller map. The arithmetic
condition here concerns a degree; it is not an automatic reduction-mod-p
integrability argument. Read introduction and Theorems 1.1–1.2/Corollary 1.3;
no proof audit or first-integral theorem is imported.

partial-z-degree restrictions. No reduction of arbitrary JC2 sources into
