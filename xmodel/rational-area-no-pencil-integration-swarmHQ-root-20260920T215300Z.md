## Proof interfaces checked by both producer and reviewer

1. Write D(x,y)=(x^2,y/(2x)), H(u,v)=(v,v^4-u), and F=H composed D.
   Both Jacobians equal 1, and H^{-1}(s,t)=(s^4-t,s). With p=x^2,
   q=y/(2x), the extension C(x,y)/C(p,q) is generated by x satisfying
   x^2=p, with y=2qx. The p-adic valuation shows p is nonsquare in the
   purely transcendental field C(p,q); hence the degree is exactly 2.
   Postcomposition by H does not change the output field. It is NOT
   a conjugacy and need not preserve D's pencil.
2. T(x,y)=(x,y/(2x)), with inverse T^{-1}(u,v)=(u,2uv), gives the genuine
   birational conjugacy

       G = T F T^{-1} = (v, v^3/2-u^2/(2v)).

   Only dynamical degrees are transferred by conjugacy. The area statement
   is in the original coordinates: T^{-1*}(dx wedge dy)=2u du wedge dv,
   consistently with det DG=u/v. T is not a polynomial symplectic change.
3. Weighted degree delta(u)=1, delta(v)=3 extends to rational functions
   by subtraction of numerator and denominator weights. For G^n=(u_n,v_n),
   induction gives delta(u_n)=3^n and delta(v_n)=3^(n+1). In the second
   coordinate of G^{n+1}, the competing weights are 3^(n+2) and -3^n,
   which cannot cancel. This is an all-iterate proof, not a sampled fit.
4. A homogeneous representative of G^n of projective degree d_n supplies
   v_n=P_n/Q_n with both ordinary degrees at most d_n. Positive weights
   give delta(v_n)<=delta(P_n)<=3d_n, so d_n>=3^n. Consequently
   lambda1(G)>=3, and birational invariance gives lambda1(F)>=3.
   Common-factor cancellation does not change delta(P_n)-delta(Q_n).
5. The Dinh--Nguyen product formula for a surface semiconjugate to P1
   gives lambda1=max(a,b), lambda2=ab, with a,b>=1 and degree-zero
   relative dynamical degree 1. Hence lambda1<=lambda2. For an invariant
   pencil of F^m, this contradicts lambda1(F^m)>=3^m>2^m=lambda2(F^m).
   P2 and P1 satisfy the projective hypotheses; connected generic fibers
   of the original pencil are not required. Dominance makes the base
   map nonconstant. No generic-fiber interpretation or integrality of b
   is needed.
6. F is regular and etale on {x!=0}, but this open is not forward invariant:
   F(x,0)=(0,-x^2). Its iterates are rational maps on dense domains, not
   iterated self-morphisms of that open. Removing a pole line is not
   replacing the source by the whole affine plane.
