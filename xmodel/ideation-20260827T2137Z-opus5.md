
```text

least M with the same sum      >= 124 :          P: M = 58    Q: M = 52
```

## 6. Correctness-preserving software / AWS acceleration

**`GATE-MARCH`: precomputed cokernel projectors + triangular forward march.**

- **Exact inputs.**  The survivor `H` (branch P: `A = X^4-1`, `V = 1` fixture);
  the frozen polygon windows `F_n`: X-degrees `max(0, ceil((n-8)/3)) .. 16-n`;
  the promoted `q_n` recursion from `P^8 = F(X,sP)`.  All exact `Q`.
- **Precomputation (four small solves, once).**  For each `j` in `{0,1,2,3}`,
  build the exact-`Q` projector `pi_j: Omega^1(U) -> H^1_dR(U, nabla_j)`,
  `dim = r-1+k_j`, by solving `4Hc' + jH'c = 4H w` on a bounded-degree window.
  This is the identical computation I ran this session at desk scale in
  seconds.  Freeze each `pi_j` with a SHA-256.
- **March.**  Row `m = n+22` reduces to one matrix-vector product
  `pi_{(n+2) mod 4}(r_n) = 0`, linear in `F_n` given `F_1..F_{n-1}`.  One small
  linear solve per row.
- **Parallel decomposition.**  The four residue classes are independent given
  the `q_n` stream; the `q_n` stream is a single forward pass (`P` is algebraic
  in `s`, hence its coefficient sequence is generated by a fixed recursion).
  Rows split trivially across cores.  There is nothing to distribute across
  primes or monomial orders because there is no Gröbner basis.
- **Certificate / replay format.**  Per row emit
  `(n, j, sha256(pi_j), r_n as exact rationals, residual vector, primitive c_m)`.
  A PASS is **self-certifying**: verify `4Hc_m' + j H' c_m = 4H q_n` by exact
  differentiation — one line, no solver trust.  A FAIL emits the nonzero
  residual in the frozen `pi_j` basis.  Replay = recompute `pi_j` from `H` and
  re-apply; byte-comparable.
- **Resource estimate.**  One core, minutes to low hours through row 60,
  under 1 GiB.  Compare with the five live lanes at 6.2–59.0 GiB RSS and
  100–280 GiB caps.  It can run on the local desk under the packet's
  desk-scale allowance, or on one spare core.

  conditions only**.  It is *not* a substitute for the literal raw exact-`Q`
  endpoint lanes, it does not see the 276 `G` slots, and its output must never
  be fed back into the raw ideal or used to delete an endpoint equation.
  Label `INTERNAL-UNREVIEWED` until different-model review.  It sits beside,
  not inside, the authoritative lanes.
- **Accounting for live jobs and the held quotient.**  It launches nothing on
  AWS, touches none of the five Box03 lanes, r6a, r6b, or Box02, and consumes
  no host headroom, so the 150-GiB precondition on the held 466-generator
  quotient is untouched.  Its value to that quotient is a *prediction*: if the

## 9. One insight a differently-trained researcher is likely to miss

**The cutting rate of the gate tower is pinned by a topological Euler
characteristic and cannot be improved by any choice of rows, twist
normalization, or algebraic cleverness.**

A strong researcher trained on Gröbner/elimination practice will treat
"row `m` imposes `r-1+k_m` conditions" as a measured quantity — as the campaign
did, computing it row by row and flagging rows 31–34 as outside the "stable
range", needing dense solving.  The index-theoretic reading makes all of it
free and, more importantly, makes it **unimprovable**:

```text
chi_dR(U, nabla_j) = rank . chi(U) = 1 - r      (regular singular, so no
                                                 irregularity term)
h^2 = 0   (affine curve)
h^0 = k_j (the horizontal section H^{-j/4} is rational iff 4 | j e_i for all i)
=>  h^1 = r - 1 + k_j,  for every j, with no window and no computation.
```

Two consequences that are easy to miss and hard to un-see:

1. `chi(U) = 1-r` does not depend on `j` at all.  So no reindexing, no twist
   shift, no smarter choice of which determinant rows to impose can raise the
   rate above `b_1(V_H)/4` per row.  The only lever is **more rows**, and §3.3
   prices them: `>= 34` more on P beyond row 23, `>= 28` on Q.
2. The arithmetic that decides which rows are "cheap" (3 conditions) and which
   are "expensive" (4) — the entire `k_m` schedule that the campaign derived by
   operator analysis and cross-checked against the R5 rational-mode schedule —
   is nothing but `h^0`, i.e. **the number of connected components of an étale
   `mu_4`-torsor**.  `k_m` is a component count, and `4 | e_i m` is triviality
   of a monodromy character.  That is why `k` is 4-periodic, why `4 | e_i(12-m)`
   iff `4 | e_i m`, and why the R5 `H^0` schedule and the `q` `H^1` obstruction
   had to coincide: they are `h^0` and `h^1` of one object.

Exact check, so this is not a slogan: on the `r=8` squarefree control,
`k_m = 1` exactly for `m in {24,28,32}` in `23..34`, giving
`9 x 7 + 3 x 8 = 87`; on branch P (`e_i = 2`), `k_m = 1` iff `m` is even,
giving `6 x 3 + 6 x 4 = 42`; on branch Q, `k_m = 1` iff `4 | m`, giving
`9 x 4 + 3 x 5 = 51`; on the `r=1` control (`e_1 in {4,8}`), `k_m = 1` always,
giving `12`.  All four are the campaign's own repaired numbers, and all four
are `3 x b_1(V_H)`.
