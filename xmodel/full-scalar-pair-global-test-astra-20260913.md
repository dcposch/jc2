## 1. What an actual complete pair gives

Suppose {H,G}=1. Set D=X_H and E=-X_G. These are globally regular,
commuting derivations with D(G)=1, E(H)=1, D(H)=E(G)=0; together they form
a tangent frame. Both must be non-locally-finite by the charged theorem.
Neither H nor G can belong to C[A]. These facts survive every polynomial
symplectic target change of coordinates; they do not merely constrain a
selected sparse presentation.

The ruling derivation delta=X_A is locally nilpotent, with

    delta(A)=0, delta(U)=2A^2, delta(Z)=4U.

Relative to the complete-pair frame it satisfies the exact identity

    delta = delta(G) D + delta(H) E.

Its coefficient ideal is precisely (A,U): since dH,dG form a coframe,
the ideal of delta's values is the same as (2A^2,4U), and
A=U^2-A^2 Z identifies this with (A,U). This is the fixed reduced curve
P={A=U=0}, not a globally principal divisor; P^2=(A) as invertible ideals.
The identity is compatible with a full pair and gives no descent of either
coefficient to C[H,G]. A rank or coefficient-ideal calculation alone would
therefore not move the global target.

## 3. Why the full-pair formal action still does not supply preservation

The complete pair does give more than an arbitrary formal vector field.
Let L=Frac(R) and K=C(H,G). Their extension is finite because H,G are
algebraically independent and L is a finitely generated field of the same
transcendence degree. For a generator r of R, a polynomial relation for r
over K gives, under exp(tD), a relation with G replaced by G+t. Thus the
formal image r_t is algebraic over K(t), while its coefficients are regular
on S. This is an algebraic translation CORRESPONDENCE, not a single-valued
regular automorphism of S for every t.

The unknown step is precisely uniform global regularity and single-valued
continuation of those algebraic series. Finite field degree does not prove
that the series terminate, that no moving denominator or branch locus
appears, or that their inverse specializes globally. The cofinite image of
the pair does not fix this: a nonproper étale map can lose sheets along
curves which remain in its image. The charged LF review already warns
against treating such a map as a covering.

Conjugating delta by the formal translation produces a compatible LND on
every nilpotent t-quotient. This establishes neither ordinary local
nilpotence on R[[t]] nor a derivation of the polynomial base change R[t].
Nilpotence on each coefficient allows nilpotence orders increasing without
bound. The classification of actual additive rulings therefore cannot be
applied to this completed conjugate. The same obstruction persists for the
two commuting formal translations; having a coframe does not bound their
degree growth. This is the task's existing formal-to-algebraic gap, not a
new theorem or a proposed extra control experiment.
