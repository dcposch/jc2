## 3. New mechanisms and cross-avenue connections

### New mechanism: Smith-radius multivariate Hensel

Let `f` be the vector of all nonzero determinant-coefficient equations in a
fixed support, evaluated at an approximate integral point `a`.  If an exact
local presentation has `m<=n`, an `m x m` Jacobian minor has 3-adic valuation
`e`, and `f(a)` vanishes to precision `N>2e`, the multivariate Hensel/Newton
criterion can produce an exact zero near `a`.  In Smith coordinates the
relevant conditioning is the largest invariant-factor valuation, not merely
the mod-three rank.  For an overdetermined or rank-deficient presentation,
one must first certify that the selected rows generate the completed local
ideal, or retain the residual cokernel module; selecting independent rows and
forgetting the others is forbidden.

This connects avenues 4, 19, 21, 36, and 46.  It is especially attractive
now because B9 has depth eleven and a stable-looking late rank.  The known
leading-core defect of the displayed representative at valuation ten is a
valuable negative control: a claimed Hensel radius that freezes that digit
while asserting an exact Keller root is necessarily wrong.

### New connection: integral common-core incidence as the AS-to-Q8 bridge

The equal-degree top Jacobian row gives a unique integral target shear on the
broad B9 family because the `y^12` coefficient is a unit.  After that shear,
introducing `H` or `K` as an **incidence variable** avoids the ramified root
extraction that invalidates a naive reduction of the characteristic-zero Q8
normalization modulo three.  Saturating the integral incidence by its unit
leading coefficients and separating horizontal from vertical components

43.  It replaces both root extraction and numerical leading-form tests by an
