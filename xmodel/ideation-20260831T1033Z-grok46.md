## 3. New avenue or mechanism

**Residual unramified sheet of the unique rank-four branch.**

This is not “apply Abhyankar–Moh,” not “run more colourings,” and not “generalize `m=1` by copying `2m <= N-1`.”

**Setup, from promoted facts only.** An actual rank-four proper block has irreducible reduced charged branch `B`, `normalization(B)=A1`, and generic branch partition `(2,1,1)` or `(3,1)` with one ramification point (`H_inertia` at `5d7df7ce…:84–104`, consumed by the `m=1` integration). So a generic point of `B subset A_F` has fibre cycle type either one 2-cycle plus two 1-cycles, or one 3-cycle plus one 1-cycle. Write `B^sm` for the smooth locus of `B`. The unique ramified preimage along generic `B` is the ramified sheet. The complementary preimages form an unramified covering `Sigma -> B^sm` of degree 2 in the `(2,1,1)` case and degree 1 in the `(3,1)` case. These are *sheets of the finite map*, not dicritical divisors and not flags of a compactification. Flag/place/series: this covering is not identified with an Orevkov dicritical, nor with a physical place of `B`, nor with the cover series of a splice diagram.

**Mechanism.** Because `B` is rational with `A1` normalization, `B^sm` is isomorphic to `A1` minus a finite set of punctures (the preimages of the affine singularities). The residual map `Sigma -> B^sm` is unramified of degree 1 or 2; it need not be connected. Connectedness is typed `OPEN` until a lemma says otherwise.

- `(3,1)`: degree 1, so a section of `F` over `B^sm`. A holomorphic section of a nonproper map along its nonproper-value curve cannot extend to a proper section over all of `B` (that would split `F` over `B` and contradict that `B` consists of nonproper values: Chau 2003, `A_F` is the union of images of dicritical series). The obstruction is therefore *extension across the affine singularities and across infinity*. If the section extends through every affine singularity, `B` would carry a polynomial right-inverse on a Zariski-open of itself; that is a coordinate-recognition statement on the unique `A_F` component, i.e. a correctly typed Abhyankar–Moh client (row 6), not the old multi-place fibre abuse. If it cannot extend, the obstruction is supported at the singularities and is therefore a function of the δ-sequence / conductor of `B`.
- `(2,1,1)`: degree 2, either two sections or a connected étale double cover of `B^sm`. Two sections reduce to the `(3,1)` extension problem with multiplicity two. A connected double cover of `A1 minus s` punctures is classified by the subset of punctures of odd ramification (plus possibly infinity). The numerical semigroup of a one-place curve determines those punctures (Assi–García-Sánchez, arXiv:1407.0490: a δ-sequence determines the semigroup, the conductor, and the affine singularities). A colourable survivor whose semigroup cannot carry either two sections or such a double cover is combinatorially unrealizable as `A_F`, even though it is colourable as a braid. Which of the two topologies occurs is `OPEN` until typed; do not fill by cap.

**Why this is new.** Colouring sees meridians of the *link at infinity* acting as transpositions in `S_4`. The residual sheet sees the *restriction of `F` along `B` itself*. Those representations are conjugate only after one uses C1 (meridians of the knot and of the irreducible curve are conjugate) *and* a path from a fibre meridian to a meridian of `B`; that path is exactly the conjugacy sentence the one-node packet made mandatory, and it is not automatic for cabled/unibranch conductors. The avenue therefore attacks the survivors that the one-node theorem does not reach, with a covering of `B^sm` rather than a graph-knot of the smoothing.

**Primary literature.** Chau, *Two remarks on non-zero constant Jacobian polynomial maps of `C^2`*, Ann. Polon. Math. 82.1 (2003), Lemma 5: `A_F` equals the union of images of dicritical series. Orevkov 1987, (4) and Cor. 4.3, used only as already typed at plane rank four (no export). Assi–García-Sánchez, *Algorithms for curves with one place at infinity*, J. Symbolic Comput. / arXiv:1407.0490, for the semigroup-to-punctures dictionary. Galindo–Monserrat–Moreno-Ávila–Moyano-Fernández, *On δ-sequences and surfaces at infinity*, arXiv:2408.15931, for the dual graph of the resolution at infinity from a δ-sequence. Neumann–Rudolph unfolding and Neumann's minimal-Seifert theorem as already consumed by the promoted one-node packet (C1 mandatory).

**What it is not.** It is not a claim that `m=1` holds at every degree. It is not an identification of the residual cover with a dicritical. If the extension obstruction cannot be typed, the output is `OPEN`, not a cap.

## 4. New connection between existing avenues

**Orevkov remainder on the unique branch equals the numerical excess that the one-cusp table reads as `Theta_h`.**

Rows involved: **#2** (dicritical / Eggers–Wall / Orevkov budget), **#27** (splice / δ-diagram of the unique `B`), **#6/#7** (one-place `A(F)`), and the one-cusp valuation table (not itself a numbered row; it is the live client of **#29** after LND died).

**The identification that is licensed.** At rank four, `m=1` plus (D2) (dicriticals surject onto `Irr(A_F)`) implies every Orevkov dicritical maps to the same unique curve `B`. Orevkov 1987 formula (4) gives

`sum_l (mu_l + sum_{x in pi(l), x != infinity} (mu_x - mu_l)) = N-1 = 3`

with each inner summand nonnegative (Cor. 4.3). Theorem 10.1 plus `H_inertia` charges at least one owner dicritical with `mu >= 2`. The *remainder* `3 - mu_owner` is therefore in `{0,1}` and is a numerical invariant of the unique branch. Domrina–Orevkov (one dicritical component) and Domrina, Izv. Math. 64 (2000) (two dicritical components) already classified the number of *dicritical compactification components* for `N=4`; that number is 1 or 2 and is *not* `m`. Flag/place/series: dicritical component, `A_F` component, and cover-cycle type stay distinct. The remainder is a count of multiplicities on compactification divisors mapping onto `B`, not a count of places of `B`.

**The identification that is new.** The one-cusp partition table's discriminator is `Theta_h = d_h - r_h - 2`, with `gamma_h = Theta_h / 2` required to be an integer for the row to exist. Here `d_h` is the mapping degree of a horizontal (dicritical) boundary component and `r_h` is its residual intersection count with the rest of the boundary. That is the same numerical species as `(mu_l, corrections)` in Orevkov's inner sum: `mu_l` is a mapping degree on a dicritical, and the correction terms are excess intersection/ramification along `pi(l)`. For the unique rank-four `B`, the remainder `{0,1}` is therefore a *parity and size constraint on `Theta_h` of every dicritical mapping to `B`*, and conversely a one-cusp SNC computation of `(d_h, r_h)` is a computation of the Orevkov remainder in the special case where the compactification is the one-cusp ring.

**Consequences that are not restatements.**

- Attainment of a colourable δ-sequence can be tested without building a Keller pair: the dual graph of the resolution at infinity is a combinatorial function of the δ-sequence (Galindo et al., arXiv:2408.15931). That graph either does or does not admit a horizontal vertex of degree `mu in {2,3}` with remainder `3-mu in {0,1}`. Failure is a realizability kill; success is only a floor.
- The cheapest one-cusp computation and the cheapest rank-four attainment computation share a pipeline: typed dual graph in, `(d, r, Theta, gamma)` out, fail-closed `OPEN` if the horizontal vertex is untyped. That is the software card in §6–§7.
- Row 25's colouring census and row 2's dicritical budget, which the 2026-08-21 surveys treated as one “sheet-number ladder,” are now *separated*: colouring is saturated as a genus-killer; the budget remainder is not.

**What this connection refuses.** It does not say the one-cusp horn *is* the rank-four irreducible horn. One-cusp is a special compactification shape (function-pair reduction `2531a89d…`: `mu=d1`, charged branch already irreducible, companion with immersive `A1` normalization). Rank-four irreducible one-place is the general actual block. They share a numerical excess; they do not share a configuration. Mixing them would identify a cover series with a place.

### Card I — Residual unramified sheet of the unique rank-four branch

**Dependencies.** Promoted `m=1`; promoted `H_inertia` (generic partition `(2,1,1)` or `(3,1)`, one ramification point, `normalization(B)=A1`); conjugacy sentence C1 of the one-node packet (mandatory in citations, harvest §4); Chau 2003 Lemma 5 (`A_F` = images of dicritical series); Assi–García-Sánchez δ-sequence dictionary. Does *not* depend on (2.3), Prop. 4.3, C3, inverse-Kummer, or any reducible-row lemma.

**Cheapest discriminator.** Partition the finite list of colourable surviving δ-sequences into `(3,1)`-capable and `(2,1,1)`-capable using only the already-computed colouring (cycle types of meridians). For one cheapest `(3,1)` sequence: decide, from the semigroup alone, whether a degree-1 section of a finite map over `B^sm` can extend across every affine singularity. For one cheapest `(2,1,1)` sequence: list the punctures of `B^sm` from the δ-sequence and decide whether the residual degree-2 map can exist either as two sections or as a connected double cover (a homomorphism `pi1(B^sm) -> Z/2` whose ramification support is compatible with a generic `(2,1,1)` fibre). Do not assume connectedness. Desk-scale; no CAS; no compactification of a Keller pair.

**Outcomes.**

- `(3,1)` section extends through all affine singularities: this is a correctly typed AM/coordinate-recognition client on unique `A_F`. Either `B` is a coordinate value (contradicting nonproperness of an actual block) or the extension produces an explicit polynomial right-inverse along `B` that can be checked against `J=1`. Either way the `(3,1)` horn dies or yields a map. Information: high.
- `(3,1)` section cannot extend: the obstruction is supported at named singularities; it becomes a conductor constraint on `(3,1)` survivors. Keep the card, apply the constraint to the rest of the `(3,1)` list.
- `(2,1,1)` residual exists as neither two sections nor a connected double cover: that δ-sequence is combinatorially unrealizable as `A_F`. Kill the row. This is attainment, not colouring.
- `(2,1,1)` residual exists in at least one topology: floor only. Do not claim realization. Move to Card II on that sequence. Do not pick a topology by analogy.
- Discriminator untyped (cannot read punctures from the δ-sequence without a missing lemma): `OPEN`. Do not fill by cap.

**Stop condition.** Stop the card if every surviving colourable sequence has been given one of {killed, reduced to a conductor congruence, reduced to Card II, `OPEN`}. Stop immediately if C1 is found not to apply to cabled meridians of `B` itself — then the residual cover is not conjugate to the colouring representation and the card needs a separate conjugacy lemma, not a silent identification.

**Expected information gain.** Splits the post-ladder survivor list by a covering of `B^sm` that colouring does not see. Even a total `OPEN` is information: it isolates C1-for-cables as the missing lemma, which is also the gap in “unibranch beyond one-node.”

### Card II — δ-sequence dual graph against Orevkov remainder 3

### Card III — Inertia-weighted Orevkov bound at general degree, retyped or `OPEN`

**Dependencies.** Promoted all-degree nontrivial-`pi1` branch-component theorem (integration `9579d3a1…`: *at least one* reduced branch component has nontrivial `pi1`; not that every component does, and not that the branch is connected).
