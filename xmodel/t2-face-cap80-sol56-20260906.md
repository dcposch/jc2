## 3. The cap still fails after deleting zero rows

The exact raw-list totals already trigger the requested stop, but a zero-row objection does not change the result. The fixed highest forms give a small symbolic witness to more than 80 distinct nonzero coefficient polynomials.

For both 99 charts,

\[
F_{\rm top}=y^{27}(y-x)^{72},\qquad
G_{\rm top}=y^{18}(y-x)^{48}.
\]

Therefore the `bFG` member of Q contributes

\[
b,y^{45}(y-x)^{120}.
\]

For each `q=56,…,165`, the scalar row at `p=165-q` contains a nonzero rational multiple of the independent variable `b`. No other family term contains `b`, so this monomial cannot cancel identically as a polynomial in the declared coefficient ring. These are **110 distinct nonzero upper-y generators**, already above 80 before adding any face equation or inverse.

For D108 the analogous product is

\[
b,y^{40}(y-x)^{140},
\]

which gives **117** distinct nonzero upper-y rows for `q=64,…,180`. This argument uses only the fixed top forms and retention of all five target scalars. It is independent of deeper coordinates and applies before any Gröbner or row-pivot operation.

No full scalar expansion is needed or retained: the 110/117 symbolic witnesses already certify the cap failure under the stricter convention that identically zero rows are deleted.

## 4. Why the apparent small UNIT is rejected

An exploratory prototype tried to compress the scalar rows through the formal identity obtained from

\[
F=h^3+Ah+B,\qquad G=h^2+Ch+D.
\]

The first formal coefficients are

\[
L_5=3C+b,qquad
L_4=-2A+3C^2+3D+bC+a.
\]

Separately imposing the coefficients of `L5`, then a reduced `L4`, together with the face rows produces presentations of 61, 61, and 53 rows. Those stronger ideals quickly become unit ideals after the attained leader inverse is added. That observation is **quarantined and is not a result of this lane**.

The required reverse ideal inclusion was never proved. The formal `L5*h^5` and `L4*h^4` contributions overlap in ordinary y-degree; monicity by itself does not authorize setting each formal block to zero separately from the scalar coefficients of their sum. The checks showed only that the compressed block equations make every required scalar row vanish, i.e. the raw attained-row ideal is contained in the stronger compressed ideal. A unit in the stronger ideal does not imply a unit in the raw ideal. The charged report itself requires rational pivots while retaining every unselected row ([line 59](/tmp/jc2-lane.x7VhB7/inputs/char-degree-instrument-astra-r2-20260905.md:59)).

This is the decisive FALLACY-v2 point: replacing the literal row family by an unproved stronger presentation would manufacture a branch kill. The final driver therefore contains no formal-block substitution. No Gröbner calculation was run on any required cap-failing ideal. Earlier tiny Singular probes applied only to the rejected stronger presentations, produced no retained CAS output, and confer no status on the requested rings.
