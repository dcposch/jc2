## New mechanism and connection

The new mechanism is a **graded Spencer--Macaulay dual complex** for the
ordered landing system.  At each grade, separate first-occurrence jets from
old variables.  The left kernel of the new-jet symbol gives exact compatibility
rows (Spencer side); the dual of each fixed-weight Macaulay span records which
localizer powers survive (inverse-system side).  Multiplication by `a1` and
`k` connects consecutive dual spaces.

This connects avenues 4, 16, 31, and 38 in a way not present in the prior
portfolio.  V36's `Tg19_7` is the prototype compatibility class on one
component.  V37's sparse exact functionals are not merely negative
certificates: if they extend compatibly under multiplication, they witness a
nonnilpotent localized quotient or an associated component; if they fail to
extend, the failure itself yields the next localizer-power membership
certificate.  The same construction gives a principled stop rule for deeper
row exports.

## Strongest attacks

The strongest proof attack is to compute the generic first-occurrence symbol
on `rho=0,D(a1*k)`, derive every low-degree compatibility operator, and reduce
those operators in the saturated ordered chart.  A localizer power closes the
chart; an involutive symbol proves that deeper raw compatibility rows cannot
do so and forces an early pivot to honest routing/globalization.

The strongest disproof/falsification attack remains the source-gauged AS109
arithmetic-Newton corner at partial `y`-degree twelve, coupled to the canonical
exterior polar divisor.  Its purpose is two-sided: either return a finite
source-complete polynomial lift candidate, or certify exactly why every
bounded gauge/support grammar fails.  No all-Witt-to-polynomial inference is
licensed.

The highest-value software acceleration is one incremental sparse exact
engine that maintains (i) weighted row spans, (ii) first-occurrence symbol
ranks and left kernels, (iii) compatible dual functionals and multiplication
maps, and (iv) fail-closed certificate replay.  Modular arithmetic may select
pivots only; Q replay decides.  Shard weights/components across idle AWS cores
and stop rebuilding full Groebner bases for questions that are linear at a
fixed weight.
