## 3. New mechanism: the closed-form exit charge `Λ` — label `NEW`

### 3.1 The observation

The td8 kill and the td12 pin are usually reported as two facts. They are one
computation run twice. Write, at a charged extra-direction vertex,

```text
delta  :=  tau_0^min  -  kbar          (the area/level defect)
q_j    :=  kappa_H(F_j) / kappa_F(F_j) (ramification ratio of flag j)
lambda_j = q_j ( tau_0^(j) - kbar )    (actual weight of flag j)  in  Z_{>0}
```

Both reviewed arguments are exactly: *minimise `sum_j lambda_j` subject to
integrality of each `lambda_j`, the descent bound `tau_0^(j) >= tau_0^min`,
and the arity constraint that the flags exhaust the multiplicity group.*

- **td8 trunk:** `delta = 17/2 - 7 = 3/2`. `q=1` gives the nonintegral `3/2`,
  so `N=1` forces `q` even and `lambda >= 2*(3/2) = 3`; `N>=2` forces
  `>= 2+2 = 4`. Minimum **3**.
- **td12 B:** `delta = 25 - 17 = 8`. `N=1, q=1` gives `lambda = 8`; `q>=2`
  gives `>= 16`; two flags give `>= 16`. Minimum **8**.
- **td8 A-copy:** charge exactly **2**, consistent with `delta_A = 2`.

### 3.2 The conjectured closed form

> **`ARITY-LAW` (CONJECTURE, unproved).** At a charged extra-direction
> vertex with defect `delta = tau_0^min - kbar` written in lowest terms,
>
> ```text
>       Lambda(v)  =  min(  num(delta) ,  2 * ceil(delta)  ).
> ```
>
> The first term is the single-flag branch: a lone flag must have `q`
> divisible by `den(delta)` to make `q*delta` an integer, so it costs at
> least `den(delta)*delta = num(delta)`. The second term is the multi-flag
> branch: at least two flags, each of weight at least `ceil(delta)`.

Reproduced exactly on all three reviewed values, with no free parameters:

```text

delta = 8    ->  min( 8, 2*8 ) = 8    matches td12 B     (reviewed once)
delta = 2    ->  min( 2, 2*2 ) = 2    matches td8 A-copy (reviewed once)
```

and it reproduces both quoted branch inequalities verbatim — `q>=2` at the
td8 trunk gives `2*(17/2-7)=3`, and `q>=2` at td12 B gives `2*(25-17)=16`.
