### B. Universal Cartier obstruction morphism — **NEW extension, exact target**

For the direct F-only depth-`n` coefficient scheme `X_n(D)` in
characteristic `p`, the next determinant digit is a polynomial residual
`R_n`.  Modulo next-digit changes it lives in the finite divergence cokernel

```text
C_D = k[x,y]_{<=bound} / (partial_x k[x,y] + partial_y k[x,y]),
```

whose Cartier-visible coordinates include monomials
`x^(pa-1)y^(pb-1)`.  Therefore `R_n` defines a polynomial morphism
`K_n:X_n(D)->C_D`, not merely a pointwise diagnostic.  The zero scheme of
all Cartier coordinates is a necessary next-lift locus.  Eliminate on that
smaller locus before adding ordinary support rows.

This connects avenue 19 (Witt lifting), avenue 20 (Cartier/de Rham), and
avenue 36 (structured SAT/search).  The checksum found the frozen pointwise
`x^2y^2` obstruction but no scheme-wide obstruction morphism or certificate
recursion.  It makes no all-depth claim: nonzero Cartier kills a residue;
zero Cartier only restores solvability of the divergence part.
