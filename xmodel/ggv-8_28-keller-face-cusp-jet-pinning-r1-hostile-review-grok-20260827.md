## 4. Local/raw `U_{14}` distinction — CONFIRMED

A *direct* local term `U_{14}=X` as a coefficient of `t^{14} X` in `F`
would be an `F_{14}` summand with `i=1`. The slice relation then forces

```text
j = 8 + 3·1 - 14 = -3,
```

so the source monomial is `x y^{-3}`. That point is not in `2S` (`j<0`),
and `y^{-3}` is not a polynomial source monomial. Independently: the
substitution `x=t^3 X`, `y=t^{-1}` sends `x y^{-3}` to `t^6 X`, and
`F=t^8 f` places it at `t^{14} X`, which is exactly `F_{14}=X`.

Thus `U_{14}=X` is a local/rootwise interpolant, valid modulo `H` and at
every simple `H`-root, and is **not** a raw `2S` row of type `F_{14}=X`.
Raw `F_{14}` can only be of type `X^2`.

The R1 caveat is also correct, and is necessary to keep the type repair
from overclaiming. Formal Morse/Weierstrass cleanup shifts the local
coordinate `u` by a series in `t` and re-completes the square. Those
shifts mix lower raw jets into the local coefficient `U_{14}`. Direct-slice
failure of `F_{14}=X` therefore proves only that this monomial is not a
raw source row; it does **not** prove global nonexistence of a local
`U_{14}=X` produced by a combination of lower raw terms. A compiler that
recorded only the raw `n=14` row would miss that combination.

### 5.1 `C` is necessarily constant

Pair slot `8` with slot `0`. The `(3/2)HP` piece cancels by the same
weighted Euler identity that forces `G_n=(3/2)H F_n` off resonance. The
remainder `R` in `G_8=(3/2)HP+R` contributes

```text
E_8 ⊃ 8 H (H' R - H R').
```

Vanishing of the Wronskian `H'R-HR'` in characteristic zero means `R` and
`H` are linearly dependent over the coefficient field. Since `H` is
nonconstant, `R=C H` with `C` constant. Equivalently, `R/H` is constant in
the rational function field, so `C` remains constant even if one allows
rational `C`. A nonconstant polynomial `C` fails: already `C=X` produces
a nonzero `E_8` of degree `16`.

Off resonance, slot `14` has no such remainder. Pairing `G_{14}=(3/2)HA+R`
with slot `0` yields

```text
(12-14) H' R - 4 H R' = 0,
```

i.e. `-2H'R-4HR'=0`. A nonzero polynomial `R` of degree `d` has leading
coefficient `(8+2d)\mathrm{lc}(R)\ne 0` on the left-hand side; a nonzero
constant `R` leaves `-2H'R\ne 0`. Thus `R=0` and `G_{14}=(3/2)HA`
exactly.

### 5.2 Complete `n=22` cross contribution

Inside this two-slot ansatz the raw slices `F_{22}` and `G_{22}` are empty,
so the only pairs that can contribute to `E_{22}` are `(8,14)` and
`(14,8)`:

```text
E_22 = (12-14) F_8' G_14 + (8-8) F_8 G_14'
     + (12-8)  F_14' G_8  + (14-8) F_14 G_8'
     = -2 P' · ((3/2) H A)
     + 4 A' · ((3/2) H P + C H)
     + 6 A · ((3/2)(H' P + H P') + C H'),
```

using that `C` is constant, so `(CH)'=C H'`. Expanding,

```text
E_22 = -3 H A P' + 6 H P A' + 4 C H A'
     + 9 H' A P + 9 H A P' + 6 C H' A
     = 6 H (P A)' + 9 H' P A + 4 C H A' + 6 C H' A.
```

On the other side, set `Y=A((3/2)P+C)=(3/2)AP+C A`. Then

```text
Y' = (3/2)(PA)' + C A',
M(Y) := 4 H Y' + 6 H' Y
```

The two polynomials are identical:


This is an identity in `P,A` and constant `C`, not a sample. The verifier's

The fixture `P=0`, `C=1/48`, `A=X` gives `Y=X/48` and recovers the R0

## 7. Operator degree obstruction — CONFIRMED

Let `H=X^8-1`, so `H'=8X^7` and

```text
M(Y) = 4 H Y' + 6 H' Y = 4(X^8-1) Y' + 48 X^7 Y
```

over a characteristic-zero coefficient field. Treat `Y=0` separately:
`M(0)=0\ne 1`.

Now let `Y` be nonzero of degree `d\ge 0`, leading coefficient
`\lambda\ne 0`. Then `Y'=` (if `d=0`) `0`, or (if `d\ge 1`)
`d\lambda X^{d-1}+\cdots`.

- `4 H Y'` has degree `d+7` if `d\ge 1`, leading coefficient `4 d \lambda`;
  and is `0` if `d=0`.
- `6 H' Y` has degree `d+7` always, leading coefficient `6·8·\lambda=48\lambda`.

The two degree-`d+7` terms therefore have combined leading coefficient

```text
(4d + 48) \lambda = 4(d+12) \lambda.
```

In characteristic zero this is never zero. There is no cancellation, so

```text
deg M(Y) = d+7,     lc M(Y) = (4d+48) lc(Y)
```

for every nonzero polynomial `Y`. In particular `deg M(Y)\ge 7>0=\deg(1)`,
so `M(Y)=1` has no polynomial solution.

The finite mechanical loop `Y=X^d` for `d=0..64` in the verifier is not
the proof. The leading-coefficient identity is algebraic and holds for
every `d`. An independent check on `d=0..79` and on `d=100,128,255,1024`
found no exception; that check is diagnostic only.

Both named ansatzes land in the image of `M` on polynomials: the first
with `Y=A((3/2)P+C)`, the second with `Y=3A^2/4`. Therefore neither
ansatz can produce global `E_{22}=1` with polynomial `(P,A,C)` or
polynomial `A`. This excludes `M(Y)=1` *inside those two displayed
normalized ansatzes*. It does not exclude `E_{22}=1` for omitted slots,
Laurent data, or non-polynomial cleanup.

## 8. Exact `13H/12` remainder — CONFIRMED

For `Y=X/48` one has `Y'=1/48`, hence

```text
M(X/48) = 4 H · (1/48) + 6 H' · (X/48)
        = H/12 + (1/8) · 8 X^7 · X
        = H/12 + X^8
        = (X^8-1)/12 + X^8
        = (13/12) X^8 - 1/12
        = 1 + (13/12)(X^8-1)
        = 1 + (13/12) H.
```

The two polynomials are identical in `\mathbb Q[X]`, coefficients
`(-1/12, 0,0,0,0,0,0,0, 13/12)`. Reducing modulo `H` recovers the
rootwise unit `1`. The extra `(13/12)H` is a genuine global remainder of
the fixture `P=0`, `C=1/48`, `A=X`, not a local-chart artifact.

## 9. Local carrier discussion, and the unproved typed-normal-form gap — CONFIRMED

The charged local scalar equations are used only to see why the two named
patterns are the plausible first unit carriers, not as a completeness
theorem.

From the R0 generating function, independently rewritten,

```text
E = F_X (12 G - t G_t) + G_X (t F_t - 8 F).
```

In the local cusp quotient `u=H(X)` (étale at simple roots, so
`u_X=H'`), with `t`-only coefficients `U,V,W`,

```text

+ (24 W - 2 t W_t) u
+ V(t) (t U_t - 8 U).
```
