## Hidden assumptions to attack

### Square/full-source proposal

Substituting `K=L^2` and `N=LM` globally is not licensed merely because the
first-normal support is square.  A genuine source arc may leave that reduced
support through transverse variables at the next valuation.  Conversely, a
positive-dimensional solution of the square-restricted recurrence may be
only a higher-contact zero section.  The receiver must therefore retain
`R,S,k10` (and their first permitted rescalings), use the frozen tail
convention, and impose the terminal `[6,2]` and Taylor rows at their actual
valuation.  Neither a recurrence solution nor a coefficient-curve component
proves that the source map lands there.  The special intersection `s=0`,
where `L=z^2` and `beta=0` permits `M=alpha*z`, must be split rather than
silently included in the generic chart.

### Normalized-Rees proposal

Normalizing the blow-up of a **reduced component ideal** need not enumerate
arcs of the full, possibly nonreduced source ideal.  The order-two
discriminant gate already exhibits scheme thickness, so its Rees valuations
can differ from those of the radical support.  Required proof obligations are:

1. the chosen ideal is the source ideal (or a proved arc-equivalent ideal),
   with the degeneration parameter and load variables typed correctly;
2. every relevant source arc lifts to one enumerated normalized chart;
3. chart localizations and overlaps preserve boundary points and nilpotents;
4. the pulled-back source rows, not just the exceptional fan, are checked;
5. terminal/Taylor receivers are transformed in every chart;
6. ordinary blow-up, normalized blow-up, and integral closure are not
   interchanged without proof.

The D1 V8--V11 closure is only relative to its frozen ordinary cone and
constant loads; it is not evidence that the omitted correction-aware fan is
empty.  Likewise, a Cech cover of `(a^3,h)` is a software control, not global
D1 coverage.
