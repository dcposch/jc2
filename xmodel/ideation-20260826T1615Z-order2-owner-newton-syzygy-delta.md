## 1. Full multigraded support of `K` is the next high-value order-two client

This is worth computing, but a raw Newton hull is insufficient.  The client
should substitute independent

```text
a,E,M,U,V,R1,R0,S1,S0,lambda,k10,k6,k2,mu2,mu4
```

into the frozen complete rows `P1,...,P5`, form `K` before any one-parameter
specialization, and emit a canonical sparse coefficient/support table.  The
coefficients live over the unit base ring in `E,M`; they must be factored and
stratified rather than evaluated at one point.  `mu6` and `J` cannot occur in
this five-row functional, but their exact source timings remain omission
sentinels.

For every support monomial `m`, compare its valuation form with the intrinsic
form `3*v(lambda)`.  The open cones on which

```text
v(m)>3*v(lambda)  for every m != E*lambda^3*M^3
```

give immediate direct-unit regions.  Equality hyperplanes are not results:
reduce their initial coefficients by the complete predecessor-row initial
ideal, then split by every new coefficient factor.  This produces a finite
relative Newton fan with exact route labels rather than an integer box.

Mandatory controls and stop conditions are:

- recover the confirmed delayed cone and its coefficient `-1/16` exactly;
- recover the `q<6`, `q=6`, and cubic/load tie walls without hard-coding
  them;
- retain all load and target support, moving center, complements, and

- reject the fan if a known q6 omitted-center counterexample is absent;
- stop a cone at the first outside denominator, unproved predecessor

The computation should run dual AWS, but support enumeration itself is
likely small: the input rows are finite and the three loads are linear.  The
expensive part is equality-face reduction, which should be sharded only
after the open cones are known.

## 2. Reusable exact row-syzygy miner

Build one source-pinned compiler with four layers:

1. ingest complete row polynomials, target masks, character/parity labels,
   and a valuation schedule;
2. solve over exact Q for a support-minimal ansatz
   `sum c_i(base)*P_i` whose coefficients have bounded invariant degree;
3. verify the resulting identity against the literal source and emit its
   complete residual support, coefficient factors, and second-prime control;
4. derive direct-unit cones and typed equality faces, with every source
   omission and localization printed.

Use the affine `E*H3+H5` functional and the D1 odd-row recurrence as positive
controls.  A perturbed row and the old q6 center truncation are mandatory
negative controls.  Deck-character blocks from the generic parity theorem
should restrict the ansatz before solving; this turns symmetry into a search
compression, not an assumed vanishing theorem.

First production targets after the controls should be the remaining
order-two `p=0`/all-zero receiver and the horizontal `AC/RC` equality faces.
Only then try TD6, where the analogous object is a fraction-free functional
of the 38 FIRST rows plus P12.  A TD6 candidate must survive the full rank
atlas and allowed-denominator audit before it receives nonlinear meaning.

## 3. Cross-avenue consequences

- **Order two:** one multigraded `K` support can replace many fixed load
  slopes.  It does not replace the total-Rees atlas, the `m=0`/`p=0`
  complements, Pell/Chebyshev receiver, terminal `[6,2]`, or Taylor maps.
- **D1:** the same miner should certify a finite tail recurrence only after
  an automatic source-jet ceiling proves no high jet was omitted.  If that
  tail composes with the confirmed finite contacts, stop the serial ladder.
- **TD6:** reuse the identity format and exact residual-division checks, but
  projectivize the augmentation boundary and quotient triangular target
  shears before launching dozens of unit charts.
- **Symmetry/monodromy:** invariant/anti-invariant row blocks can generate
  candidate functionals; exceptional coefficient factors become the finite
  branches to send to terminal/passport and Ritt/Pell analysis.
- **Global proof/disproof:** no change.  Landing/coverage and a cofinal
  degree ceiling remain the decisive global walls; protected
  counterexample/finite-support work should continue independently.
