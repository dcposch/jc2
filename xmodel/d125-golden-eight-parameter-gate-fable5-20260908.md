## 2. Free identity and T rewrite — CONFIRMED

    [A,B] = [R,F]·(chi phi' − psi' − chi' F) + mu phi'(R)[R,L] + mu[F,L].

    [A,B]=[R,T]+[F,mu L],   T=−delta F−(5/3)R F²+mu(3R²+alpha)L.

## 3. Degree-12 rows — CONFIRMED

## 4. K2 rewrite, w row, constant row — CONFIRMED

Modulo (U,V): u=2tau t/lambda, v=2tau/lambda, so R−H=(2tau/lambda)p²M+wp and (5lambda²/3)R(R−H)L=(10lambda tau/3)R·p²ML+(5lambda²/3)wRpL. Since p²ML=pD the first term cancels −(10lambda tau/3)RDp exactly, leaving

    T=(5/3)R K2+alpha mu L−delta F,   K2=lambda²w pL−tau²p².

Constant row: T₁=alpha mu L−delta tau p in all cases (the other summands of T have degree ≥3), R₁=wp, F₁=tau p, so J₀=[wp,alpha mu L−delta tau p]+mu[tau p,L]=−mu(alpha w+tau), using [p,g]=[p,L]=−1. This row has no U,V contamination at all; modulo w it is −mu tau, so tau ∈ I and then u,v ∈ I. Only unit multipliers appear; no reducedness or squarefreeness is used anywhere, as the chain is pure ideal membership.

## 5. Remaining rows and the unit — CONFIRMED

Modulo (U,V,w,tau): R=H, F=lambda D, K2=0, T=alpha mu L−delta lambda D, and exactly
