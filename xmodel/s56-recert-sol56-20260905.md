## 2. The repaired chart, with its group elements

Pairs below are `(b,a)=(x-power,y-power)`. For deficit `i`, the emitter now
takes exactly

\[
D_i=\{(b,a):0\le a<K,\ 0\le b\le
\lfloor\delta_1a-iB\rfloor\},
\]

\[
G_i=\{(b,a):0\le a<K,\ 0\le b\le
\lfloor d(iK-a)\rfloor\},\qquad d=-\delta_s,
\]

and `S_i = D_i union G_i`. Before the terminal constant gauges, it uses `S_1`
for `h`, `S_i` for every `alpha_i`, and `S_i` for `beta_i`, `i>=2`. This is
the source-complete over-approximation proved in the frozen support gate, not
the refuted total-degree/x-width inventory.

The source normalization is explicit. Here `N=qK=6` and

\[
\eta(x)=-[y^5]Q/6,
\qquad \phi(x,y)=(x,y+\eta(x)),
\qquad \phi^{-1}(x,y)=(x,y-\eta(x)).
\]

This is a polynomial automorphism in characteristic zero, its determinant is
one, and it fixes `x`; hence it preserves the declared monomial Jacobian target.
There is no parameter denominator or discarded branch. The outer-disc bound
then gives `deg_x [y^a]F <= floor(d(L-a))`, while monic approximate-root
recursion and monic division give `G_1` and the later `G_i`. The exact source
argument is frozen at `moh-hsupport-gate-astra-20260905.md:89-136`.

`beta_1=0` is structural: `h` is the unique quadratic approximate root of
`Q`. It is not a coefficient gauge. The only coefficient gauges used are the
constant translations

```text
(P,Q) -> (P-lambda,Q),   inverse lambda -> -lambda
(P,Q) -> (P,Q-kappa),    inverse kappa -> -kappa
```

which remove the constants of `alpha_3` and `beta_2` and preserve the
Jacobian. No scalar shear is used. Nonvanishing of the target coefficient is
encoded by the actual Rabinowitsch generator `T*c-1` (or its mapped image), not
by silently entering a fraction field.

The corrected erratum is retained throughout:

Every native builder contains the literal `H0 = H0 - c*x^8` target operation,

## 4. What “partition” means after completion

```text

```

But `S_1` now contains every nonmonic monomial of each face with its own free
