## Generator mathematics and permitted divisions

The normalized leading coefficients are F=v/w, H=a=1/w, b=1/f5. The generator
uses the fixed leading recurrence and checks the entire leading identity.
It computes L,w,f5 and their inverse witnesses in Q[v]/p using rational
polynomial extended Euclid, then explicitly checks each product equals1.
It does not factor p, select a field or divide an arbitrary residue class.

For normalized lower coefficients U,E,D0,V0,V1,K1,K2,K3 it constructs

    Ahat=S*tau^3+(S*Dcal-U)*tau^2
                    +(z-U*Dcal+S*Vcal)*tau+Kcal,
    Pihat=z*tau-U*tau^2+S*tau^3.

The entire descending Euler recurrence uses target

    -E*tau*Pihat-tau*Pihat^2.

It does not insert s^7 or U*s^5*tau into upper equations, because those are
precisely the two unsolved low targets. All nonresonant inversions are fixed
rational eigenvalues j-3i; nonzero resonant forcing is a hard failure. Both
accepted mate gauges remain zero, not new gauge choices.

First and second completed matrices are defined by full-source band coefficient
projections on their three basis vectors, with the accepted rho read-back row.
The two determinants are the accepted proved units; their adjugate inverses
and both matrix identities are checked before use. The first map solves to
(0,0,x), the second to the ACTUAL inhomogeneous forcing and y. The z=0 calls
used solely to extract fixed coefficient matrices do not specialize the final
source: these matrices lie in B, and final construction retains z polynomial.

The third map uses the accepted finite C^-2/antiderivative left inverse and
checks lambda3*h3=1. Its affine forcing comes from the whole Euler mate and
full low bracket, not the erroneous shifted D_(i+1) formula. Thus the full
target -2z*S*tau^5 and all previous mixed terms enter. The checker independently
binds c3 to the original source rows, with the corrected scope of the third
gate. The V4/V3 distinction is respected by the full recurrence and gauges.

The fourth map uses a smaller column-ideal Bezout calculation in Q[v], rather
than evaluating the accepted seven-by-seven rational Gram recipe. It computes
the ideal(Acol,Bcol,p), checks its gcd is1 and verifies the literal identity
lambda'_A*Acol+lambda'_B*Bcol=1 in B. Neither column entry is inverted.

representatives. If lambda is the accepted Gram inverse and d=lambda'-lambda,
then d=t*(Bcol,-Acol), with t=d_A*lambda_B-d_B*lambda_A. Hence

    E'-E=-t*Phi4.

## Independent read-back and whole-source coverage

The checker does not import generator.py, invert product-algebra elements, or
repeat its Euler recurrence. It uses the frozen original exact.json coefficient
and row wires, binding their complete input SHA. For any stage map it substitutes
the original parameters using the respective powers of s:

    u=U/s, ell=E/s^3, d0=D0/s, d1=F/s,
    v0=V0/s^2, v1=V1/s^2, v2=H/s^2,
    k_i=K_i/s^3 (i=1,2,3), k4=a/s^3,
    omega=w*f5*s^8, z=s^2.

This is an injective comparison of B[x,y,z] polynomials with their images in
B[x,y,s,s^-1], not a same-source normalization s=1. It works over the complete
finite coefficient algebra and does not assume B is a field. Emitted wires
are required to have no s powers, so an expression proportional to z-s^2 cannot
hide inside an emitted polynomial and evade the check.

The checker binds all completed matrix columns to the original highest rows
and the original mate's rho coefficients. It checks inverse and determinant
identities, the triangular back-maps, complete second/third/fourth forcing and
both affine column identities. It then compares EVERY original A/B coefficient
and every S coefficient slot, including zero slots, under the scale map.

It independently differentiates the full normalized A/B, computes Jhat with
both complete nonlinear target terms, checks all upper bands, and compares
all original residual slots. The two low comparisons retain the exact
additions U*s^5 and s^7. The top two rows and guard are literal zero identities;
the first two bands vanish; the third/fourth row pairs are exactly the displayed
left-inverse multiples of Phi3/Phi4. It also checks the five whole forcing

All three accepted gauges, actual A/B leading coefficients and the full guard
