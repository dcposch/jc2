## 3. Idea card A — logarithmic coframe on the canonical finite normalization

**Kind:** new proof mechanism; strongest proof attack. Connections: avenues
2, 7, 27–29, 31, 33, and 39.

**Target.** For a hypothetical nonproper Keller map
`F=(P,Q): A² -> A²`, let `X -> A²` be the finite normalization in
`C(x,y)/C(P,Q)` supplied by Zariski Main, with `A²` embedded as an open subset
of `X`. Compactify/resolve `(X, X minus A²)` functorially enough to retain the
map.
The exact coframe `dP,dQ` trivializes the cotangent bundle on the open A² and
extends meromorphically/logarithmically across the boundary. Its determinant is
the fixed volume form `dx∧dy`. Compute the localized elementary divisors and
second-Chern/different contribution of this rank-two frame.

The proposed invariant is not scalar BMY. It records where each of the two
sections acquires poles/zeros and how their directions coincide. The working
hypothesis is that constant Jacobian forces a nonnegative local boundary index
whose global sum is fixed by `c₂(Ω¹(log D)) = e(A²)=1`; a nonproper finite
normalization may force excess index or an impossible different/conductor
divisor. If true uniformly, it supplies a cap-free obstruction and a pure
source theorem in one architecture.

**Novelty.** The repo has pole-mass, scalar Euler/BMY, action residues, local
different/contact, commuting frames, and Rees ideas separately. It has not
placed `dP,dQ` as a rank-two meromorphic log frame on the canonical ZMT finite
normalization and localized its Chern/different defect.

**Dependencies/tier.** Standard Zariski Main and finiteness of normalization
over the excellent base are theorem-grade. The existence, positivity, and
exact formula for the localized index are CONJECTURAL. It must not assume GGV,
Sigray completeness, or a bounded td menu.

**Cheapest discriminator (one focused theory/computation lane, 1–2 days).**

1. Derive the divisor matrix of a two-section logarithmic frame under one
   boundary blowup and express its localized `c₂`/Fitting contribution.
2. Replay it on a triangular automorphism and a tame product (required
   negative controls).

   retaining both section directions. If incomplete tails enter, report the
   missing fields as a source-schema result rather than filling them.

**Outcome meanings.** A strictly positive excess forced on every allowed local
vertex gives a new global inequality and an immediate scoped kill. Universal

stops the lane. Dependence on unrecorded boundary directions gives a precise
typed endpoint for the coverage compiler, still valuable but not a theorem.

**Stop condition.** Stop after the automorphism controls and two nontrivial
