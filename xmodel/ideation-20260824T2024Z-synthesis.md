## 3. Exact new mathematics and typed conjectures

### 3.1 Differential-subfield ramification lemma

The coordinator's new lemma is exact and elementary:

> If nonconstant `t in C(x)` satisfies `t'=R(t)` for some `R in C(t)`, then
> `[C(x):C(t)]=1`; hence `t` is a Mobius transformation of `x`.

At a finite source point of ramification index `e>1`, the order of `R(t)` is
divisible by `e`.  If the target value is finite, `ord(t')=e-1`; if it is
infinite, `ord(t')=-e-1`.  Neither is divisible by `e`.  Thus all finite
source points are unramified.  A degree-`d>1` rational map cannot put the
Riemann--Hurwitz total `2d-2` at source infinity alone, whose contribution is
at most `d-1`.  Therefore `d=1`.

This gives a complete terminal filter once a normalized rational component
and a descended autonomous derivation have actually been proved.  It does
not apply while a Kummer scale, moving load, or second coordinate remains.
