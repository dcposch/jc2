## New mechanism and cross-avenue connections

### A. Equality at degree 16 is a finite Belyi passport — **NEW exact mechanism**

Let `p,q` be coprime univariate polynomials of degrees `12,9`, and put

```text
R = p^3-q^4,        deg R = 16.
```

The degree equality itself forces the Pinchuk/DZ differential condition.  An
exact identity is

```text
q R' - 4 q' R = p^2 (3 p' q - 4 p q').
```

The left side has degree at most 24 and `p^2` has degree 24, so
`3p'q-4pq'` is constant.  It is nonzero because `p,q` are coprime and
nonconstant.  Consequently `p` and `q` are squarefree and disjoint.

Normalize the leading coefficients so `p^3/q^4` tends to one at infinity.
Then

```text
beta = p^3/q^4,        beta' = c p^2/q^5,
```

and `beta-1=R/q^4` has a zero of order `36-16=20` at infinity.  Every finite
zero of `R` is simple.  Thus `beta:P^1->P^1` is a degree-36 Belyi map with
passport

```text
over 0:        3^12
over infinity: 4^9
over 1:        20,1^16.
```

The ramification defects are `24+27+19=70=2*36-2`, so the genus-zero ledger
closes exactly.  Conversely a normalized rational map with this passport
recovers a DZ pair of the required degrees.  This converts the abc equality
boundary into finite transitive permutation triples, exact Belyi
reconstruction, and then only affine/scaling trajectories over the
coefficient field.  It connects avenues 3, 25, 26, 27, and 43.

The packet's deformed spectral polynomial also has a useful exact norm form:

```text
W = Res_U(U^3-f^2, g-U^2-kU)
  = g^3-f^4-3 k f^2 g-k^3 f^2.
```

At `W=0`, its cubic discriminant in `g` is

```text
-27 f^4 (f^2-k^3)^2.
```

Hence the `k!=0` spectral cover is a cyclic/Kummer cubic away from the
explicit branch strata `f=0` and `f^2=k^3`.  This does not itself exclude a
fibre, but it gives the exact stratum ordering and a cross-check on the
approximate-cubic compiler.
