## 4. New mechanisms and cross-avenue connections

### Card 1 — certificate-directed transport instead of full `G2-PSC`

The unit certificate may use a tiny subset of low-grade literal rows.  To
exclude a source chart it is enough to transport those rows and their
multipliers into the honest source ideal; one need not first prove fidelity
of the entire target presentation.  This is a weaker, checkable transport
obligation:

```text
source pullback(sum H_i Tg_i) = pullback(a1^N U(t)).
```

Dependencies: exact minimal support, frozen source maps, typed localizers.
Cheapest discriminator: generate the pullbacks of only the nonzero
multipliers through V46R1's naturality interface and compare them with one
already-reviewed endpoint manifest.  PASS reduces `G2-PSC` for this chart to
a certificate-sized commuting square; FAIL identifies the exact missing
row/localizer and does not affect target closure.  Stop after the first
honest endpoint mismatch unless the mismatch is a mechanical alias error.
Expected information gain: very high because global transport, not local
emptiness, is now the wall.

### Card 2 — joint two-fibre circuit and PID annihilator

Independent generic and special certificates give the crude exponent
`D+Ms`.  Search instead for a *common* small row circuit and compute the
annihilator of the target class in each fixed sigma-weight quotient viewed as

find the primitive annihilator `U(t)` directly, minimizing `t`-pole, row
support, and final exponent together.  Seed with the generic support union
the 17 special-cascade rows.

Dependencies: the two exact identities; no review wait is needed under a
provisional label.  Cheapest discriminator: exhaustive/monotone deletion on
the 11 generic rows plus `t^s` membership for `s=0,...,6`, followed by the
same test on the small generic/special union.  A smaller `s` or common unit
circuit sharply compresses the promotion object; no improvement still leaves
the reviewed converter intact.  Stop after the first reviewed total
certificate unless compression materially reduces review cost.  Expected
information gain: high and computationally cheap relative to monolithic

### Card 3 — circuit-deletion survivor atlas (disproof side)

Dependencies: exact circuit minimality and a source/client map for any
