### 2.3 `LIPSHITZ-CODOMAIN` (Opus Card B) — two exact missing premises

1. **Coverage-as-entailment is outside the decidable fragment.** Lipshitz
   1978 gives decidability of the *positive existential* theory of
   `(N,+,|)`; the `forall-exists` fragment is undecidable. Containment of
   `exists`-definable record families — "no cell escapes" — is
   `not exists x (phi_cell ∧ not phi_records)` with `not phi_records`
   universal over the records' internal witnesses: a `Sigma_2` question.
   The card's headline benefit ("coverage becomes entailment... an
   unsatisfiability check") is therefore **not licensed**. What *is*
   licensed: satisfiability (nonemptiness of a cell/record), witness-driven
   refutation of a claimed coverage, and negations of *atoms* — note
   `not (t|nu)` is `exists r (0<r<t ∧ t | nu−r)`, so quantifier-free record
   bodies complement fine; existentially-quantified ones do not.
2. **Cofactor binding.** `nu = tK` with `K` *reused elsewhere* is the graph
   of multiplication, which is not `exists`-definable in `(Z,+,<,|)` — if it
   were, Lipshitz decidability would solve Hilbert's 10th. `t|nu` asserts the
   cofactor exists; it cannot bind it to a variable. The promoted U2 pure
   `nu`-set survives (`exists t (t ≡ 5 mod 6 ∧ t|nu ∧ nu >= K_0 t)` is in
   the fragment, since `K_0` is a constant), but any record coupling `K`
   into `dq` or another coordinate leaves the fragment. This is precisely
   what the card's hand-encoding discriminator will hit; run it with this
   checklist, count cofactor couplings, and expect `R2.2` (partition
   quantifier) to escape.

Both premises are repair-able and the discriminator survives, but the card
must be rewritten before any packet, and the external theorem (plus the
`Sigma_2` undecidability rider, to prevent future scope creep) pinned at the
next websweep. Until then: defer.
