16, 28, 31, 32, 33, and 39.

- **Target claim (PROPOSED, not known).** Let `A=C[P,Q]`, `K=Frac(A)`,

## 5. Shared hidden assumptions and hostile audit

1. **Formal source assumption.** D schemes are being treated as truncations of
   one stationary formal problem. D1 proves one typed projection only; changing
   coordinate registries, late corrections, or new state can destroy the
   shift-module premise.
2. **Actual-map assumption.** Neither a D tail nor a residue-A boundary record
   is an actual polynomial Keller map. `G2-PSC`, landing/coverage, and
   polynomial provenance cannot be supplied retrospectively.
3. **Local-to-global assumption.** The constant-Jacobian two-form is locally
   Darboux-flexible. The exact construction in Card A strongly attacks any
   claim that local residues, local different data, or one boundary component
   force properness.
4. **Modular-transfer assumption.** Agreement at two primes says nothing by
   itself about a common integral component, flatness, or characteristic zero.
5. **Finite-normalization assumption.** The normalization is canonical and
   finite, but computing it or proving a useful signed degree formula may be
   exactly as hard as properness; naming it is not progress unless CAN-DIFF's
   discriminator survives. A vanishing divisorial discriminant also does not
   silently prove flatness or remove a codimension-two defect; that gate must
   be explicit.
6. **Independent-evidence assumption.** Different terminal presentations and
   repositories can share the same reduction, field, authorship neighborhood,
   or model lineage. Correctness edges are not independent-vote edges.
7. **Positive-control assumption.** No nonautomorphic Keller map exists as a
   positive control. Automorphism covariance and non-Keller separation can
   falsify an invariant, but cannot establish its completeness.

**Strongest attack on my leading proof proposal.** The local Darboux
completion already shows that `J=1`, tame ramification, and an arbitrary
quadratic trace residue coexist formally. A canonical normalization
discriminant is certainly effective, but there is no present reason its global
degree should be zero; a hypothetical counterexample is expected to put its
entire branch/different divisor at the missing boundary. Unless one writes a
ten-line signed equality whose right side vanishes for a specifically Keller
reason and survives automorphism covariance, CAN-DIFF is only Zariski Main in
new notation and must stop.

**Strongest attack on my leading disproof proposal.** Even a finite free
shift module would yield, at best, a formal modular inverse system in one
residue-A chart. It would not supply characteristic zero, convergence,
algebraization, a polynomial map, `G2-PSC`, or complete source coverage. Its
scientific value is to decide whether the D tower is a coherent formal
mechanism, not to claim a counterexample.
