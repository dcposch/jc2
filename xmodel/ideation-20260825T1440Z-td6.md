## 3. Genuinely new mechanism and cross-connections

### Obstruction-section trichotomy

For a source-typed staged system

```text
A(z) x = b(z)
```

over a parameter ring `R`, retain the original-row module
`C=coker(A)` and the obstruction section `[b] in C`, rather than only ranks
or fraction-field pivots.  The three live fronts exhibit three distinct
outcomes:

- TD6 V64: after the P12/N13 source composition, `[b]` is the frozen unit
  `-k/50` on a named open, so that open is empty;
- Q8: the selected-localizer ideal is a unit on the frozen generic-`c`
  slice, but the missing source cofactor denominator records precisely the
  exceptional base where emptiness has not been proved;
- AS: the provisional corrected projection says the candidate section
  `omega` is zero on the current partial Q9 image, so it may be a boundary in
  the deformation complex rather than a global obstruction.

The new rule is to compute the **annihilator/Fitting support of the section
and an original-row cofactor**, then either kill its nonzero open or quotient
out a proved-zero section and compute the next obstruction.  Do not keep
sampling a section after it is source-proved zero.  The negative control is
the familiar `A=[x], b=y`: generic rank or `gcd=1` does not imply global
membership, and multivariate `gcd=1` is not comaximality.

### Cech/Rees glue as shared software

TD6's factor set `{U,V,P3,QH}` and Q8's unknown `D(c)` are the same software
problem: convert local source identities into a finite principal-open cover,
retain every exceptional divisor, and require explicit localized Bezout/Cech
identities before union.  A normalized Rees/valuation fan can enumerate the
Q8 mixed-order charts; the same content-addressed source-DAG engine can prove
the TD6 factor strata.  AS uses the dual operation: quotient by a source-zero
obstruction and form the next Fitting section.  This is a cross-lane algebra
compiler, not a new global theorem.

### A precise H-divisor topology check

On `D(U)`, put `T=V^2/U^3`.  Then

```text
P3/U^6 = T^2 - 32T + 128,
QH/U^6 = T^2 + 8T - 64,
((5T-136)QHnorm - (5T+64)P3norm)/512 = 1.
```

Thus the `P3` and `QH` strata are disjoint on `D(U)`, but neither is
eliminated.  This exact identity makes the final H-cover proof DAG small:
generic `D(UVP3QH)`, `V=0,D(U)`, each of the two disjoint divisor strata,
then direct `U=0`.  It is a cover plan, not a theorem until every source
certificate is independently frozen.
