## 1. Canonical boundary source: preserve the modest theorem, attack the added invariant

The strongest common idea has two logically separate layers that the reports
frequently merge.

### 1.1 What is worth keeping

For `A=C[P,Q] ⊂ B=C[x,y]` and `L=Frac(B)`, the normalization `X` of
`Spec(A)` in `L` is finite over the target. Because a Keller
map is quasi-finite, Zariski Main supplies an open immersion of the source into
this finite object. This is an honest, GGV-free starting point. It can be used
to state precisely:

- which components of `X \ A²` are present;
- how a resolved boundary and dicritical images produce `A(F)`;
- which valuation, contact, and branch fields a downstream tree/compiler must
  retain; and
- whether a minimal resolution gives a choice-independent source record.

That program bypasses `G2-PSC`; it does not prove it. It also does not make the
boundary finite-state in the sense needed by a full book. The affine
normalization is canonical; the chosen compactification, blowups, logarithmic
extension, and Sigray decoration are extra data whose independence must be
proved.

**Cheapest falsifier of the source claim.** Construct the record for two
different legitimate SNC resolutions of the same tame automorphism and for a
minimal resolution after contracting redundant boundary curves. If the fields
consumed by a proposed book kill change under an allowed blowup/down, then the
record is not yet canonical. If they agree only after retaining essentially
the entire resolution, record that honestly: it is a source schema, not a
finite passport.

**Call:** continue one precise theorem/specification lane from ZMT open
immersion to a typed boundary record. Do not attach a capacity or complete-book
conclusion to it until those are separately proved.

### 1.2 Why the Fitting/log-coframe consensus is fragile

The identity

\[
 F_XG_Y-F_YG_X=jZ^{d+e-2}
\]

and `Fitt_0(Q)=(Z^(d+e-2))` are exact and dual-confirmed. But the determinant
support records total boundary torsion, not its distribution. The stronger
claim—that the full module `Q`, its Smith filtration, or the logarithmic
coframe has a nonnegative blow-up capacity—is wholly open.

Three hostile controls attack it immediately:

1. **Presentation covariance.** The identity map and
   `T_n=(x,y+x^n)` are polynomially equivalent. For the identity the
   boundary exponent is zero; for `T_n` it is `n-1`. Homogenization,
   degree padding, `Q`, and its boundary filtration change with `n`.
   Hénon compositions amplify the same problem. Any claimed invariant must
   either be computed only after a proved, choice-independent minimization or
   explicitly describe its covariance. “Starts from the map” is not enough.
2. **Fixed global Chern total.** On any smooth SNC compactification of the
   source, the logarithmic Euler/Chern total is fixed by
   `e(A²)=1`, while tame/Hénon presentations can create arbitrarily
   complicated boundary histories. Therefore a strictly positive charge per
   visible jump cannot be both presentation-independent and globally bounded
   without signed cancellation or a minimality theorem. Positivity is the
   missing result, not a consequence of the coframe.
3. **No positive counterexample-side control.** A “residue-A leading pair
   without tails” is not a polynomial Keller pair and generally does not
   satisfy pure Fitting support. The omitted tails are precisely what would
   have to cancel the residual Jacobian terms. Computing `Q` on leading
   forms cannot yield an “algebraization obstruction at depth zero”; that
   would assume away the unknown completion. Current pilots can compare
   automorphisms with non-Keller decoys, but neither is a hypothetical
   nonautomorphic Keller object.

**Cheapest falsifier.** Before any residue-A calculation, compute the full
localized module—not merely its determinant—for the identity,
`T_n` at two values of `n`, `T_n⁻¹∘T_n`, and the first two Hénon
controls, both before and after orbit minimization. Track exactly which Smith,
Fitting, primitive-polar, and localized-Chern quantities survive equivalence.
Then ask whether the survivor is stronger than the tautology “total exponent
= `d+e-2`.” This is an hours-to-one-day kill test.

**Outcome discipline.** Coordinate dependence kills the advertised invariant,
not the exact pure-boundary identity. Invariance with a budget growing like
`d+e` gives at most a type/degree-relative checksum. A quantity that recovers
entry data but has no positive uniform charge may help the source compiler but
not KJN or cofinal control. Only a quantity that passes equivalent-map
controls, uses full polynomial data, and separates at least one exact decoy is
eligible for a theorem lane.

**Call:** run the kill test once. Stop the primitives/log-`c2`/torsion-capacity
cluster if it collapses to determinant order or changes arbitrarily under
equivalence. Redesign any survivor as a source checksum first, not a proof
invariant.

## 2. TRACE-REG: a correct equivalence disguised as a smaller target

Nash's trace proposal is the cleanest direct properness formulation, but its
logical cost is understated. For a degree-`d` extension, regularity of

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(x^m),\operatorname{Tr}(y^m)\in A,
 \qquad 1\le m\le d,
\]

implies integrality by Newton identities. Conversely, if `B` is finite over
`A`, these traces are integral over the normal ring `A` and lie in its
fraction field, hence lie in `A`. Thus, at the stated scope, trace regularity
is essentially an equivalent reformulation of the missing finiteness theorem,
not a weaker lemma. “Only finitely many traces” also hides the unknown,
unbounded field degree `d`.

The proposed local trace engine has a second circularity. A field trace sums
all conjugate inverse branches with their global pairing. Current books omit
branches and downstream contexts, and formal residue-A data do not define the
field extension of an actual polynomial map. If the engine fills those gaps,
it has assumed the global object whose absence motivates TRACE-REG.

**Cheapest falsifier.** Before implementing Puiseux summation, prove that the
filed passport/tree data determine the first nontrivial trace principal part.
Explicitly search for two branch pairings or completions with identical
retained local decorations but different Newton-sum residues. One collision
shows that the current local inputs cannot prove TRACE-REG. As a paper sanity
check, derive the `m=1,2` residue formula on a nontrivial tame automorphism
and identify the exact step where constant Jacobian, rather than already-known
finiteness, forces cancellation.

**Call:** redesign as a one-day residue-formula/underdetermination audit. Do not
fund a general trace engine until a separating identity is written that does
not already use complete inverse-branch pairing or integrality. A collision
does not refute TRACE-REG; it refutes the proposed local proof surface.

## 3. D43: the consensus next step is still one gate too late

All five lanes raise D-series/Hensel work, but there is a material split:
three favor immediate common-integral D43 lifting, while root and Nash first
ask whether the depth projections carry information. The hostile ordering is
to settle the projection question first.

A standard-smooth characteristic-zero point of one underdetermined 218-row
finite system could be geometrically generic. It would cross an honest
evidence boundary, but it would not show:

- that this point projects to and extends a compatible source-level tower;
- that the inverse system is Mittag-Leffler or finite type;
- that stable tangent symbols integrate to actual points;
- that a unique infinite formal solution is algebraic; or
- that an algebraic local curve comes from two global polynomials and is
  nonautomorphic.

The finite-stage smoothness result may therefore have much lower disproof
information than the consensus assigns it.

## 4. `G2-PSC` fingerprint and Belyi portability: useful route-killers, weak proof candidates

The fingerprint proposal correctly asks whether the **recorded** GGV packet is
information-complete. But failure is nearly the default: a Newton polygon and
corner chain do not normally determine coefficients, Puiseux contacts, or a
decorated Eggers--Wall tree. A collision would kill packet-only transport; it
would not refute a theorem allowed to use the full original Keller pair and
its polynomial normalization. Conversely, a unique compatible tree for the
empty special `(72,108)` packet may be an artifact of severe arithmetic
constraints and says nothing about all globally minimal pairs.

**Cheapest falsifier.** On the exact retained pre-Laurent packet, vary the
first coefficient invisible to its combinatorial fields and compute whether
the Puiseux/key-polynomial fingerprint changes. Prefer a variation satisfying
the full available bracket equations; a generic non-Keller collision proves
only combinatorial insufficiency. Stop at the first two nonisomorphic trees
with the same recorded packet. If extracting even this invariant requires the
whole resolution, the pure-Sigray source architecture has already won the
design decision.

**Call:** permit one one-to-three-day packet-injectivity falsifier, not a bridge
proof. Stop the hybrid critical path on a collision or whole-resolution
augmentation. Continue hybrid work only on a unique, covariance-checked match
that uses no Laurent object as a Keller pair.

The proposed degree-21/degree-4 Belyi “portable weapon” is weaker. Sharing the
word Belyi, or even a passport-to-number-field workflow, is not a cross-avenue
theorem. Ordinary residue-A passports already admitted all 169 transitive
tuples. Unless an explicit pullback, factorization, or second-coordinate
Jacobian coupling is written, comparing the degree-4 A4 map with the
degree-21 quintic is priority archaeology, not a proof attack.

**Call:** stop Belyi portability after the cheap polynomial/factor comparison

## 5. Local bounds and the sublinear-type proposal fail an immediate arithmetic control

The observation that a sublinear type theorem would turn type-relative KJN
into an absolute bound is a correct conditional deduction. It is not presently
a plausible consequence of the sheet entry arithmetic. For any
\(\beta=\alpha+1\), consider two necessary entry records with

\[
 a_i=b_i=1,\qquad \nu_i=\alpha,\qquad
 \Lambda_i=\frac{\alpha\beta}{\alpha}=\beta.
\]

The divisibility menu permits this because `ν_i | α` and
`ν_i | (β-1)`. Their total degree is `td=2β`, whereas
`αβ ~ td²/4`. This is only a formal necessary-entry family; it
may fail later tree or polynomial-origin constraints. It is nevertheless a
decisive falsifier of deriving sublinear type growth from T7, pole mass, or a
fixed-`td` entry census alone.

The DIR pole-mass and A-SCALE carrier families already make the analogous
point for the two local bounds. Algebraizing either suitable nonautomorphic
family would be a disproof; failure of those named families would prove
nothing universal.

**Cheapest falsifier for any renewed cofinal proposal.** Require it to reject
the two-entry family above, the exact Hénon automorphism tower, the non-Keller
rooftop family, and the formal DIR/A-SCALE towers for explicitly different
reasons. If the proposed invariant merely restates mass, determinant order,
conductor, or orbit complexity, one of these controls kills it.

**Call:** stop `SUBTYPE` and local DIR/A-SCALE as independent proof lanes.
Retain the families as a common adversarial test suite. Resume only when a
global polynomial-origin invariant first survives the boundary kill test; then
sublinear growth can be a downstream target rather than a card of its own.

## 6. External certificate IR: unanimous, useful, and easy to overread

The external-artifact crosswalk is the one consensus item that should launch
immediately, because it is bounded and protects public correctness. Its output
must be scoped more narrowly than several reports suggest.

- Replaying `1=\sum h_if_i` proves emptiness of that exact terminal ideal.
  It does not prove that the terminal system is equivalent to a campaign
  stratum, that saturation/localization branches are covered, or that the
  upstream GGV reduction is exhaustive.
- Equal quintics, dessins, supports, or normalized certificate hashes prove
  mathematical overlap. They do not prove social dependence or lack of
  independent derivation. Conversely, different hashes do not prove
  mathematical independence.
- A coordinate crosswalk must include localizations, excluded factors,
  number-field embeddings, branch unions, and maps in both directions. A
  sparse variable isomorphism alone cannot import an external cofactor into
  cCa2/cCa6.
- The newly found repositories are unverified inputs. Their prose, manifests,
  and solver headers are not evidence until exact identities replay in a
  pinned, isolated environment.

**Cheapest falsifier.** Freeze one advertised commit, run only its short
verifier in a sandbox, and independently substitute one terminal certificate.
A false identity or unresolved manifest stops that actor branch immediately.
Only after this pass should the larger lineage diff run.

**Call:** continue now in an evidence/software lane. Cap it at the two exact
suites and a bidirectional provenance map. Do not consume the primary proof
slot, do not revise an independent-line count before the crosswalk, and do not
infer any global JC2 progress from a valid bound-125 artifact.

Internal cCa rational recovery should remain selective. If Helali/Suzuki and a
new artifact supply sound, independently checked terminal routes, cCa2/cCa6
cofactors are hygiene rather than critical path. Reopen exact recovery only
for public wording or downstream R1/Conjecture-E claims that actually depend
on the demoted trace.

## 8. Shared-assumption audit

1. **Consensus equals independent evidence.** It does not here; model family,
   packet, and exact-new-identity anchoring are strongly shared.
2. **Canonical normalization yields a canonical finite passport.** The first
   noun does not imply the second; resolution choices and complete boundary
   context remain.
3. **A matrix with pure determinant has bounded useful complexity.** Hénon and
   target-coordinate changes are exact warnings against this.
4. **Polynomial origin is local.** Every formal/book proposal risks omitting
   global branch pairing, integrability of both columns, and nonproperness.
5. **A positive finite-depth result has monotone counterexample value.** D23,
   D25, and D43 show it may instead measure underdetermined truncations.
6. **Smooth finite jets approximate algebraic maps.** Smoothness gives lifts
   under exact hypotheses; it does not give algebraicity, finite support,
   global gluing, or nonautomorphism.
7. **Tangent compatibility equals point compatibility.** Symbols can be
   unobstructed while higher nonlinear lifts fail; actual projection points
   are required.
8. **Recorded GGV fields determine the Sigray tree.** This has not passed even
   an information-injectivity test.
9. **Type will become bounded after local KJN.** Necessary entry arithmetic
   already permits quadratic growth; provenance is not a technical appendix.
10. **External exactness transfers across coordinate systems.** Only a
    bidirectional, localization-aware map permits that inference.
11. **Infinite tail support means a nonpolynomial map.** Local expansions of
    polynomially defined algebraic curves are often infinite; the relevant
    algebraicity condition must be proved for the exact D variables.
12. **Residue-A is representative.** Incomplete off-axis/post-jump coverage
    makes it a selection-biased survivor until a total landing theorem exists.
