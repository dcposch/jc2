## 4. New mechanism and new connection

### 4.1 `NEW`: normalize the `k!=0` spectral curve before decomposing coefficients

Let

```text
S_k(F,G) = G^3-F^4-3*k*F^2*G-k^3*F^2.
```

Two exact identities, obtained directly from the packeted polynomial, expose
a much smaller geometry:

```text
S_k(u^3, u^4+k*u^2) = 0,
Disc_G(S_k) = -27*F^4*(F^2-k^3)^2.
```

The first follows by expanding `u^6*(u^2+k)^3`; the second is the cubic
discriminant with coefficients `-3*k*F^2` and
`-F^2*(F^2+k^3)`.  Over the characteristic-zero coefficient field the
spectral zero curve is rational, with generic inverse

```text
u = F*(k^2+G)/(F^2+k*G).
```

(Euclidean elimination combines `u^3-F` and
`k*u^2+F*u-G`.)  Thus `u` is the normalization parameter; all
non-etale/singular behaviour is forced into `F=0` and `F^2=k^3` (plus the
chosen treatment of `k=0`).

Instead of primary-decomposing the full approximate-cubic coefficient fibre,
compute the normalization module and conductor of this plane curve over
`k[k]`, pull the Kuranishi/invariant loads to `u`, and attach the original
Taylor boundary values there.  The square discriminant also predicts a
cyclic-three normalization symmetry after adjoining the appropriate roots of
unity, matching—but not replacing—the source Kummer typing.

This could fail because `W=0` describes only the common spectral component,
while a genuine lower-fibre path has nonzero `W` or nonzero invariant loads.
That is useful: the first term outside the conductor becomes a canonical
transverse coordinate rather than another ambient coefficient.

### 4.2 `NEW CROSS-AVENUE CLIENT`: extremal DZ gives one explicit degree-36 dessin

On the `k=mu=nu=0` extremal slice, assume the source-honest coprime/squarefree
conditions needed for `deg(g^3-f^4)=16`, with
`deg(f),deg(g)=(9,12)`.  Then

```text
beta(z)=g(z)^3/f(z)^4
```

has degree 36 and the exact three-value passport

```text
over 0:        (3^12),
over infinity: (4^9),
over 1:        (20,1^16).
```

Indeed `beta-1=(g^3-f^4)/f^4` has sixteen finite simple zeros and a zero of
order `36-16=20` at `z=infinity`.  The ramification sum is
`12*(3-1)+9*(4-1)+(20-1)=70=2*36-2`, so no fourth branch value remains.

This is a precise bridge among Avenue 3's maximum-12 fibre, Avenue 25's
dessins, and Pinchuk Theorem 4.1.  It does **not** revive ordinary passports
as a global tail bound.  The bounded task is to enumerate/classify this exact
passport (or consume a primary classification such as the relevant
Pakovich--Zvonkin result after audit), reconstruct normalized `(f,g)`, and
impose the original `r3,r6,r8` and Taylor-boundary rows.  Multiple dessins are
expected; their finiteness is the accelerator, not a contradiction.
