## 4. Correct chart rank, including fraction-free Bezout identities

Let `P_g` be the exact-Q polynomial ring in every coordinate occurring
strictly before grade `g`, let `I_{<g}` be the ideal generated by all frozen


The hash-pinned identity `Tg11_1=(3/8)A*u` gives `u=0` in every `R_g`.
This is an ideal identity, not merely a radical or field-point statement.
Consequently row 3 of the displayed symbol is identically zero in `R_g`.

Row 1 remains split: its `EC` coefficient is `(3/8)A`, a unit.  Row 2 is
also split everywhere on `D(A)`, not just generically.  After `u=0`, put

```text

s := s2 = (5/1024)r^3,
```

Then the exact fraction-free identity


At grades 18 and 19 there is an even shorter certificate.  With
`h=h2|_{u=0}=(3/128)r^2`,

```text

```

Therefore the symbol map


## 5. What this says about V36, and what it does not say

V36's obstructing `Tg19_7` is exactly one component of the intrinsic
compatibility class.  Its lack of grade-19 newcomers is therefore structural
and shared by rows 3--7.  Adding `ell8, cs7, rs7, k10_6, ac8, az8, ez8,
k6_1`, or `ec9` cannot cancel `Tg19_7`, nor can it cancel any of the other
four compatibility rows.

That conclusion is grade-local.  Older coordinates set to zero on the V34
support may be activated at earlier grades, and they can change the
compatibility values.  Thus this does **not** close ordered `T-a1`; it says
where every repair must enter.

There is a sharper exact support observation.  Let

```text
S = {a1, ell2, cs1, rs2, aa0, ee1, ec3}
```

be the seven coordinate names retained before V34 normalizes `a1`.  Of the
552 monomials in the exact-Q `Tg19_7`, 28 are supported in `S`, and zero
monomials have outside-variable-name support of cardinality one.  Every
term involving a coordinate outside `S` uses at least two distinct outside
coordinate names.  Therefore, for every single name `x` outside `S`, the
literal coordinate restrictions satisfy

```text
Tg19_7 restricted to S union {x} = Tg19_7 restricted to S.
```

At the rational V34 representative this is also visible infinitesimally:
the only nonzero partial derivatives of `Tg19_7` are in the seven `S`
directions.  All outside-coordinate partials vanish, while
`Tg19_7(P)=-7077888`.

The quantifier here is deliberately narrow.  It holds with the coordinate
restriction fixed.  It does **not** say that one may add `x`, re-solve the
grades 11--18 equations, and retain the V34 basis relations on the `S`
coordinates.  Earlier rows may move the base coordinates as functions of
`x`.  Consequently no enlarged-support unit-ideal claim follows without a
new exact solve.  What is proved is that a direct one-coordinate term in
the grade-19 compatibility cannot be the repair; any direct new term needs
at least a pair of previously omitted coordinates.

   maximum twelve, source/landing coverage, `G2-PSC`, any bounded-degree
