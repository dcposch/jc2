## 1. What the all-degree program should be

The programme should be stated without euphemism:

> Given a Moh (1)--(13) skeleton and a formally integral bottom packet with
> `N >= 6`, prove that no single pair `f,g in C[x,y]` realises its complete
> branch tower and the identity `f_x g_y-f_y g_x=1`; or construct such a pair.

There are three layers, and every future lane should say which layer it tests.

1. **Numerical admissibility.**  Moh's divisibility chain, radii, Galois choices,
   bottom packet capacity and exact pinned `N`.  This is fast and useful, but it
   is only a superset of actual maps.  The rebased census has 14,016 groups
   through `D=200` and empties no degree above 100
   (`xmodel/census-rebase-opus5-20260902.md:894-924`).
2. **Branch-packet realisability.**  Approximate-root/semigroup data must assemble
   the flags, conjugate factors and bottom dessins into the branch packet of one
   polynomial `g`.  This is where Moh's unexplained 658-to-6 reduction and the
   orbit interpretation of (10)/(11) belong.  A root orbit, a geometric disc,
   and a cover series are three different objects until an injection or quotient
   map is proved.
3. **Global Keller mate.**  On a generic fibre `g=c`, the equation is
   `D_g f := g_y f_x-g_x f_y=1`.  It must have a solution with the prescribed
   bidegree/total-degree support, simultaneously on all branches and at infinity.
   Local no-log solutions, a representative bottom star, and vanishing of one
   residue are necessary, not attainment.

The computational core for layers 2--3 should be exact linear algebra over a
function field.  Put

```text
K = Q(x,c),                    h(y) = g(x,y)-c,
B = K[y]/(h),                  rank_K B = n,
nabla = partial_x - (g_x/g_y) partial_y,
W_m = span_K(1,y,...,y^(m-1)), C : B -> B/W_m.
```

Here `g_y` is inverted only on the squarefree generic fibre; exceptional
discriminant components must be split off explicitly.  A Keller mate is a vector
`F in W_m` satisfying `nabla F=1/g_y`, together with the polynomial-in-`x`
and degree constraints that distinguish `C[x,y]` from `K[y]`.  Differentiate
`C(F)=0`, substitute the connection equation, and prolong.  This produces an
inhomogeneous observability/Krylov matrix.  Its left kernel gives compatibility
functionals on `g`; its rank defect gives the only places where a candidate can
survive.  For the `D=105` shape `(n,m)=(105,70)`, the first projection already
has `105-70=35` output coordinates: at each genuine exponent there can be up to
35 compatibility equations before new coefficients of `g` and gauge freedom
are counted.

This formulation unifies several banked ideas that had been pursued separately:
Lagrange interpolation on the 105 roots, Hermite/Bezoutian trace identities,
the Hamiltonian derivation `D_g`, the Brieskorn/Gauss--Manin viewpoint, and
linear-systems observability.  It also gives a machine filter with a proof
certificate: a nonzero exact minor or a left-kernel functional whose numerator
cannot vanish under the skeleton's leading data.  Numerical rank is only a
candidate detector; every promoted obstruction must be recovered over `Q` (or
by enough primes plus rational reconstruction) and checked on the correct ideal
component.

The resulting campaign is not “search degrees until the list becomes empty.”
It is: derive a **uniform rank/semigroup obstruction** on a symbolic family, use
the `D=105` trio as the smallest honest unit tests, and use surviving symbolic
rays as adversarial inputs.  If the obstruction is uniform, it proves the
theorem.  If it repeatedly lifts a ray, it is the shortest route toward an
actual counterexample attempt.

## 3. Reranked bottlenecks and attacks

### 3.1 Proof bottlenecks

1. **Uniform global filtered cokernel.**  Prove that the differential moment

2. **Whole-tower coefficient and sibling gluing.**  Recover an exact condition

3. **Actual orbit packet.**  Determine which formal `k` choices are geometric:

   nor mixed knapsack is attainment.
4. **Template extraction.**  Convert finite quotient-algebra computations into
   a theorem on symbolic divisor-chain/semigroup templates.  A table through
   `D=10^6` would still not be an all-degree proof.
5. **Algebraisation at infinity.**  If the formal global system survives, prove
   that its series terminate with the required polynomial degree bounds.  This
   is where a counterexample construction must ultimately succeed and where a
   proof may obtain its last obstruction.

The old ordering put “find a boundary ceiling” ahead of these.  That item is
closed negatively, not merely stalled.

### 3.3 Strongest proof attack

Exploit the determinant structure of the global moment code.  Before
differentiation, its parity-check matrix is generalized Vandermonde; its minors
are Vandermonde products times powers of `g_y(tau_i)`.  The Moh tree supplies
valuations of root differences.  Therefore the leading rank problem is a
tropical determinant/minimum-weight matching problem on the branch tree, and
the exact next coefficient decides ties.  This is far more structured than a
generic 105-by-unknown matrix.

The candidate uniform theorem is a **valuated-MDS defect**: for a counterexample
packet, the prescribed valuations of the primitive vector force a nonzero
parity syndrome before the allowed coefficient filtration can absorb it.  On
the fixed family, the number of parity coordinates grows as `n-m=7L`, while
the formal `N=6`, `V_2=1`, cubic bottom packet can be held at 12 discs (36
proper `g` roots).  A proof must explicitly retain the remaining non-proper
coordinates; if they always repair the parity syndromes, their Schur complement
is the invariant to study.  This connects the proper/non-proper decomposition
to coding-theoretic minimum distance without pretending that the zero-`N`
block is absent (`AUDIT.md:15441-15470`).

The attack is: compute the associated-graded moment matrix symbolically for
`L=5,13,21,29`; identify its minimum-weight bases; conjecture the stable minor
or left-kernel functional; prove its valuation and leading coefficient for
general `L=8a+5`; then lift the associated-graded nonvanishing through the
connection filtration.  A single certified cofinal obstruction beats any
finite degree census.  Conversely, a stable full-rank Schur complement tells
the counterexample lane exactly which non-proper variables supply the missing
freedom.

Start with group A, `D=105,k=12`, because it is the first point of the unbounded
family, has the smallest packet reaching `N=6`, and uses the explicit cubic

If all three primes give the same dimension and an exact rational lift, repeat
at `L=13`; search the two solutions for a recurrence in `L`.  Each desk run is
