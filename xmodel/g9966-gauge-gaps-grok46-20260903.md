## 2. N3: `w_0-a_0` is not a modulus

### 2.1 Transitivity

On the plane `8w_0+3a_0+h_{10}=0` the two translations act faithfully
(`b=c=0` is the only kernel point). The unique solution of
`(w_0+c-b, a_0-b)=(0,0)` is `b=a_0`, `c=a_0-w_0`. Substituting into Vieta
gives `h_{10}^{new}=0` automatically.

Exact model, the intercept-product of the same multiplicities as `h3`:

```text
h3 = (y-a_0)^3 (y-x-w_0)^8.
```

Its degree-11 homogeneous part is exactly `P` for every `(w_0,a_0)`;
`[y^{10}](h3-P)=-8w_0-3a_0`; and

```text
h3(x+(a_0-w_0), y+a_0) = y^3(y-x)^8 = P
```

identically. So both centres, and consequently `h_{10}`, are killed by a
source translation, with the chart shape preserved.

The same transitivity holds in Moh's intermediate frame (major horizontal
`y=kappa`, minor `y=m x+e`, `m!=0`): `y`-translation moves both intercepts,
`x`-translation moves only the minor intercept, and
`(beta,c)=(kappa, (kappa-e)/m)` kills both. The charged probe that
"`a_0 != w_0` is not a translate" is correct for the *one-parameter*
`y`-group and does not constrain the full translation group.

### 2.2 Approximate roots and the tower

The `d`-th approximate root is unique monic of `y`-degree `N/d` with
`deg_y(Q-h^d)<N-N/d`. If `Q'(x,y)=Q(x+c,y+b)` then `h'(x,y)=h(x+c,y+b)`
satisfies the same bound and is monic, so the tower translates. Tschirnhausen
omissions are identities of the approximate root in char 0, not gauges, and
are preserved.

### 2.3 Why the desk-scale probe does not obstruct

### 2.4 Pivot support, in case one insisted on enlarging the chart

The Jacobian mechanism of dossier §5, below the `A2/A3/B2` onset, is
