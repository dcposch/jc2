scheme; not licensed by the current map-only or growing-support towers.**

```text

P(a,b)=P(c,d),
Q(a,b)=Q(c,d),

```

makes them a prefix-closed finitely branching tree.  Koenig gives a compatible

proper; a maximal ideal and Zariski's lemma then give a `Qbar` point, hence a
complex polynomial Keller pair.  The inverse equation preserves a genuine
off-diagonal collision.  Periodic/rational digits are unnecessary.

The hidden requirements are load-bearing:

- support and the number of raw variables are fixed at every depth;
- every equation is one raw integral polynomial equation, not merely a
  divided-carry, chosen-representative, or phase predicate;
- the collision and inverse equations occur at every depth;
- level sets are literal reductions of the same scheme, so reduction maps
  solutions into the previous level.

Current AS evidence does not meet these hypotheses: its known unrestricted
all-Witt control grows support, and its present accepted states are map-only
until collision variables are added.
