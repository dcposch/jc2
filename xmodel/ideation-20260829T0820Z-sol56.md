## 6. Strongest attacks and a decomposition bypass

**Strongest current proof attack — total landing plus type/cofinal control.**
Produce, from an arbitrary hypothetical Keller pair, one complete finite
resolution record with source provenance, physical exit partitions, every
boundary component and pullback, and then prove that such records occupy a
typed cofinal universe. This is the only attack currently positioned to turn
local exclusions into a theorem; the td8 repair is a concrete warning that an

**Strongest new post-landing global consumer — `PIC-DISC`.** Resolve a
hypothetical counterexample, by modifications that are isomorphisms over the
source affine plane and with a target compactification retaining its affine
plane, to a generically finite morphism `phi:X->Y` of smooth projective
rational surfaces, of degree `d=td`. On Neron-Severi lattices, with pullback
matrix `A`,

```text
A^T Q_X A = d Q_Y.
```

If `M=A(NS(Y))`, `Mbar` is its saturation, `h=[Mbar:M]`, and
`K=Mbar^perp`, unimodularity gives the exact necessary identity

```text
|disc K| h^2 = d^(rho(Y)).
```

The canonical divisor formula adds
`K_X-phi^*K_Y=R_phi>=0`, with `R_phi` supported at infinity under those
affine-isomorphism hypotheses because the affine Jacobian is constant
nonzero. An Neron-Severi class gives only a necessary effectivity test; exact
ramification coefficients require divisor-level pullback/valuation data. This
mechanism can kill a complete boundary record without enumerating its local
M2 coefficient descendants. It therefore bypasses the current decomposition
after landing, although it does not bypass the need for a complete source and
boundary morphism.

**Strongest counterexample attack — K00.** Protect the already exact
characteristic-zero seed and demand the correctly sharpened conjunction:
nonzero/projective, source-open, rank-exact, full-`P6` compatible, then one
prolongation and collision/nonproper witness. Do not substitute a rational
point on the base (the origin is trivial), a rank-closed point, or one selected
minor chart. No new heavy launch is justified by this blind round.

**New connection — `COVER-MOD` (Avenues 7 + 31 + 34).** Reverse the desired
asymptotic cover through Zariski Main and affine modifications. The first
toric one-modification class is already an exact negative control. For integer
exponents, `k>=0`, and `h in C[x]`, put

```text
s=x,  t=(y-h(x))/x^k,
u=s^a t^b,  v=s^c t^d.
```

Polynomiality requires `b,d>=0`, `a-kb>=0`, `c-kd>=0`, while

```text
J(u,v)=det([[a,b],[c,d]])
       * x^(a+c-1-k(b+d)) * (y-h)^(b+d-1).
```

A nonzero constant Jacobian forces `b+d=1` and `a+c=k+1`; the polynomiality
inequalities then force `|det|<=1`. Hence exactly this displayed one-step
toric monomial ansatz contains no degree-at-least-two cover; the dominant
survivors are triangular-coordinate cases. This says nothing about arbitrary
sums, nonmonomial units, multiple charts, or two modifications. It is useful
negative evidence, not permission for an unbounded successor search.
