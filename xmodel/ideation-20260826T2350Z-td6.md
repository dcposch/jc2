## 4. New mechanisms and cross-avenue connections

### New mechanism: a half-open weighted valuation selector

For literal weighted blowup generators `g_i` of weights `w_i`, route a DVR
arc after finite ramification to the lexicographically first index minimizing

```text
v(g_i)/w_i.
```

Then every integral cross-power ratio
`g_j^(w_i)/g_i^(w_j)` has nonnegative valuation.  Ties are assigned to one
chart by the fixed lexicographic rule.  If every `g_i` vanishes identically,
route to the separately typed closed receiver.  A finite ramified extension
is harmless for a nonexistence argument: an arc before extension induces one
after extension.

On chart `i`, it is enough to have a regular, denominator-licensed identity

```text
L^N = sum_j a_j R_j + pi*b,
```

where `L` is a registered chart localizer, `R_j` are literal total-source
rows, and `pi` has positive valuation.  At a row solution with `L` a unit,
the left side is a unit and the right side lies in the maximal ideal.  This
is an arc-exclusion certificate.  It does **not** prove equality of Rees
schemes, reducedness, or a scheme-theoretic glued unit ideal.

This mechanism may eliminate most overlap/Čech work from the *valuative
nonexistence* proof.  Overlaps remain necessary only if the campaign wants a
global scheme identity or if the chart coordinates are not the literal
cross-power ratios.  This is speculative until the six frozen chart maps and
weights are checked exactly.

### New connection: avenue 1 plus avenue 3

The promoted prime-ray family has the same edge direction `(3,-1)` for every
`p` and every final `gamma`.  Its terminal corners are
`Corner(gamma+3,3,gamma)`; `p` primarily controls the available interval,
although the one-edge ancestry may retain `p`.  Regrade the emitted rows in
edge-normal coordinates and test whether the strip/Ore operator and its
residue functional depend only polynomially on `gamma`, with `p` confined to
boundary data.  If so, `3P-E31` becomes a one-parameter recurrence plus a
finite set of boundary checks.  If not, the first mixed `p*gamma` coefficient
