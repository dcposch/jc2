
```text

           y+(x+y^3)^4),                  deg_y=(9,12).
```


The function-field extension is nevertheless exactly degree three:


because each `Bi` is an automorphism.  Thus these are not new characteristic-
three residue isomorphism classes; they are exact tame right-compositions of
the old Artin--Schreier seed.
