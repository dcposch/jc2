## 3. New mechanism and new connections

### New mechanism: a shift-Noetherian strip module

This is **speculation with an exact arithmetic input**.  For the promoted
prime-ray family, every viable one-edge MN system has

```text
d1=gamma-3,  d2=gamma,  d2-d1=3,
4<=gamma<=(3p-1)/4,  3 does not divide gamma.
```

The literal number of systems grows, but their degree gap does not.  Instead
of regarding the systems as unrelated polynomial rings, first test whether a
legal monomial/row regrading turns their boundary operators into a fixed-
band family `M_gamma` over `Q[gamma]`.  The desired certificate is a finite
difference/Ore relation, for example a bounded-order covector recurrence

```text
lambda_(gamma+1) S_gamma - lambda_gamma = eta_gamma M_gamma,
```

or more generally `sum_i a_i(gamma) S^i`, whose terminal residual is a
nonzero polynomial in `(p,gamma)` on the admissible lattice.  Such a
certificate would prove emptiness of the conservative covering systems, and
hence `3P-E31`, without enumerating their linearly growing members.  A
nonempty emitted system would refute only that emptiness certificate, not
the Keller-pair exclusion.

This mechanism is not present in the charged history as a theorem or run.  It
is stronger and more falsifiable than the generic instruction “make the
family parametric”: it predicts bounded regraded bandwidth and bounded Ore
order.
