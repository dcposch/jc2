## 1. Polynomial lift and chart calculation

Introduce an auxiliary variable z and set u=1+xy. Define polynomials

    P=z u³+y²u(3u+1),
    Q=y+3xz u²+3xy²(3u+1),
    R=2x−3x²y−x³z.

Their restrictions to z=1 are exactly the three given polynomials.
On x≠0 use t=1/x, s=y+t and ρ=R. Then

    x=1/t, y=s−t, u=s/t,
    z=5t²−3st−ρt³.

Substitution gives, without a target-identity assumption,

    P=s²+st−ρs³,       Q=4s+2t−3ρs²,       R=ρ.

For example (s−t)²(3s+t)=3s³−5s²t+st²+t³; its terms with negative
t-powers cancel those from z in both P and Q. Thus the displayed formulas
are actual chart identities, not just a determinant ansatz.

The determinant in (s,t,ρ) coordinates is

    2(2s+t−3ρs²)−s(4−6ρs)=2t.

Also s_x=t_x=−t², s_y=1, t_y=s_z=t_z=0 and ρ_z=−t⁻³, whence
det ∂(s,t,ρ)/∂(x,y,z)=−1/t. Multiplication proves

    dP∧dQ∧dR=−2 dx∧dy∧dz.

Both sides have polynomial coefficients. The equality on x≠0 therefore
extends across x=0; no denominator remains in the identity used below.
