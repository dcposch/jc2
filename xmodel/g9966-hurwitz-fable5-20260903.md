## 1. The identity, with proof (PROVED-HERE, page-free)

**Setting.** `(f,g)` a Keller pair over `K` algebraically closed of characteristic `0`, `J := f_x g_y -
f_y g_x in K*`, both monic in `y` after a generic linear change, `n = deg_y g`, `m = deg_y f`.  Fix
generic `c`; `C_c := {g = c} ⊂ A²` is smooth (`dg != 0` everywhere since `J != 0`) and irreducible (`g`
is not a composite `h(phi)`, `deg h >= 2`, since `J(f, h(phi)) = h'(phi) J(f, phi)` would be non-constant),
so its smooth projective model `Cbar_c` has a genus `g_c`.

**(i) `f|_{C_c}` is unramified.** On `C_c` the Gelfand–Leray form `omega = dx/g_y = -dy/g_x` is regular and
nowhere zero (one of `g_x, g_y` is non-zero at every point), and `df|_{C_c} = (f_x dx + f_y dy)|_{C_c}`
with `dy = -(g_x/g_y)dx` on `C_c` gives `df|_{C_c} = ((f_x g_y - f_y g_x)/g_y) dx = J omega`.  Hence `df`
vanishes nowhere on `C_c`: `f|_{C_c}: C_c -> A¹` is étale.  Its degree is `N := deg(f|_{Cbar_c}) =
#{f = a, g = c}` for generic `a`, which is `deg Phi = [K(x,y):K(f,g)]` (§3.1).

**(ii) Places at infinity.** `g - c` is monic in `y`, so every point of `Cbar_c \ C_c` lies over
`x = infinity` and corresponds to a Galois orbit `{alpha, ..., alpha^{(e)}}` of Puiseux roots `alpha in
K<<t>>` of `g(t^-1, y) = c`, `x = t^-1`, with `e = e_x(P)` the ramification of `x` at `P`; the local
parameter at `P` is `tau = t^{1/e}`.  `f|_P = f(t^-1, alpha(t)) in K((tau))`.  `P` is **proper** if
`ord_t f(alpha) < 0` (`f -> infinity`, `P` lies over `infinity in P¹`) and **non-proper** if `ord_t f(alpha)
>= 0` (`f -> a_0(P) := f(alpha)|_{t=0}`, the escape value; `P` lies over the finite point `a_0(P)`).
Over `infinity`, `e_P = -e * ord_t f(alpha)`; over `a_0(P)`, `e_P = e * ord_t(f(alpha) - a_0(P))`.

**(iii) Lemma E (`e_P` from the tree).** Let `S_g(alpha) := -ord_t g_y(alpha) = -sum_{beta != alpha,
g(beta) = c} ord_t(alpha - beta)` (the `sum` form is the factorisation `g_y(alpha) = prod_{beta != alpha}
(alpha - beta)`, `g - c` monic).  Xu's Lemma 4.1 (p.4; a chain-rule identity, no hypothesis on `J`),

```text
g_y(alpha) d f(alpha)/dt - f_y(alpha) d g(alpha)/dt = -J(t^-1, alpha) t^-2,
```

with `g(alpha) = c` constant gives `d f(alpha)/dt = -J t^-2 / g_y(alpha)`, a Puiseux series of **exact**
order `S_g(alpha) - 2`.  Integrating term by term: `S_g(alpha) = 1` is impossible (a `t^-1` term has no
Puiseux primitive), and otherwise `f(alpha) = a_0 + (const != 0) t^{S_g(alpha) - 1} + ...`.  Hence

```text
P proper      <=>  S_g(alpha) < 1,   e_P = e (1 - S_g(alpha));
P non-proper  <=>  S_g(alpha) > 1,   e_P = e (S_g(alpha) - 1).
```

This is the `f <-> g` mirror of the gate's `ord g(alpha) = S(alpha) - 1` (xu-screen-gate §2).  The
escape value `a_0(P)` needs Puiseux constants; the order `e_P` does not.

**(iv) Riemann–Hurwitz** for `f: Cbar_c -> P¹`, degree `N`, unramified on `C_c` by (i): `2g_c - 2 =
-2N + sum_{P at infinity}(e_P - 1)`.  Split the sum into proper and non-proper places and use
`sum_{proper} e_P = N` (the poles of `f` count its degree):

```text
2 g_c - 2 + N + r_prop = sum_{P non-proper} (e_P - 1).        (RH-f)
```

QED.  The relation to the `pi`-root data: for a non-proper packet that becomes final (the `g_c`-roots
separate) at order `delta`, the same argument as Xu p.6 / PFR-DIST (3.3) gives `S_g(alpha) = delta` (all
other roots in the packet at contact exactly `delta`, `g_sigma(pi) - c` squarefree for generic `c`), so
`e_P = e (delta_final - 1)`; for a major packet `S_g(alpha) = 1 + ord_t f(alpha)` and the pole order is
`e |ord_t f(alpha)|`.  Both are read off the contact tree.

## 2. Theorem (PROVED-HERE): with `e_P` from Lemma E, (RH-f) is the plane-curve genus formula

Riemann–Hurwitz for `x: Cbar_c -> P¹` (degree `n`): finite ramification is `{g_y = 0} ∩ C_c` counted with
multiplicity, `= I(g - c, g_y) = deg_x Res_y(g - c, g_y)` (Xu's `I`, monic case), and at infinity
`sum_P (e_x(P) - 1) = n - r`, `r = r_prop + r_np`.  So

```text
2 g_c - 2 + r = I(g - c, g_y) - n.                             (RH-x)
```

Now `I(g - c, g_y) = deg_x prod_alpha g_y(alpha) = sum_alpha (-ord_t g_y(alpha)) = sum_alpha S_g(alpha)`
(resultant identity, `g - c` monic; no Jacobian input).  On the other side, by Lemma E,

```text
sum_{np}(e_P - 1) - N - r_prop = sum_{alpha minor}(S_g - 1) - r_np - sum_{alpha major}(1 - S_g) - r_prop
                               = sum_alpha S_g(alpha) - n - r,
```

so (RH-f) `<=>` (RH-x).  Since (RH-x) holds for every smooth affine plane curve, (RH-f) holds for every
contact tree, and reads as a *formula*:

```text
g_c = 1 + ( sum_alpha S_g(alpha) - n - r ) / 2 .                (G)
```

The same number is the adjunction value `(n-1)(n-2)/2 - sum_{points at infinity} delta_pt`, with `delta_pt =
(1/2) sum_{alpha != beta at pt}(1 + contact) - (n_pt - r_pt)/2` (branch delta-invariants + pairwise
intersections); the driver computes all three and they agree on every tree (§4).  This is the campaign's
`Delta_inf - K_inf = 2 - 2g_C - s` (AUDIT:11147) in tree coordinates.  **Consequences.** (a) The only
arithmetic constraints are `sum_alpha S_g(alpha) in Z` and `sum_alpha S_g(alpha) - n - r` even; (b) with delta
17(x), `g_c >= 1`; (c) `sum_np (e_P - 1) >= N + r_prop` (my 1200Z §5.2) is just `g_c >= 1` again.
Everything finer (distribution of the non-proper places over escape values, monodromy) needs the Puiseux
constants `a_0(P)` — the lift problem itself.
