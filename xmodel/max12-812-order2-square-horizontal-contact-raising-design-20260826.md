# `(8,12)` order-two square branch: horizontal contact-raising design

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: **HAND NEWTON-FAN DESIGN; NO SQUARE-BRANCH OR ORDER-TWO VERDICT.**

## 1. Setup and firewall

On the generic square chart `D(p*k)` after the reviewed first-normal and
half-weight gates, write

```text
K=L^2+sigma^2 R,       D=L A+C,       L=z^2+p/2,
a=ord_sigma(A),        r=ord_sigma(R), c=ord_sigma(C).
```

The already-launched vertical clients normalize `a=0`.  If they force the
leading `A` section to vanish, that is a contact raise `a>=1`, not an empty
square branch.  In particular one must not saturate by the leading `A`
coordinates.

## 2. Complete lower Newton hull

For positive finite `a,c,r`, the only possible lowest negative-tail orders
are the following eight.  The displayed divisor is the leading rational
term over the squarefree quadratic `L`.

| label | absolute order | leading rational term (nonzero scalar omitted) | unique-minimum consequence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `AC` | `10+a+c` | `A*C/L` | opposite-root allocation is possible |
| `C2` | `10+2c` | `C^2/L^2` | impossible for nonzero linear `C` |
| `RA2` | `12+r+2a` | `R*A^2/L^2` | impossible by degree/root multiplicity |
| `A3` | `15+3a` | `A^3/L^3` | impossible by degree/root multiplicity |
| `R3` | `10+3r` | `k*R^3/L` | impossible for nonzero linear `R` |
| `RC` | `11+r+c` | `k*R*C/L` | opposite-root allocation is possible |
| `R2A` | `13+2r+a` | `k*R^2*A/L^2` | impossible by degree/root multiplicity |
| `A2` | `14+2a` | `k*A^2/L` | impossible for nonzero linear `A` |

This list is complete.  Further powers of `R` in `D^2/K` or `K^(5/2)`
increase the order by a positive multiple of `2+r`; higher powers of `D`
are strictly above `AC,C2,A3`; and the omitted loaded `D` and `D^2` terms
are strictly above one of `RC,R2A,A2`.  Thus the contact fan is finite even
though its cones contain infinitely many integer triples.

When `a>=1,r>=2`, `RA2>=A2` (equality only at `r=2`) and `A3>A2`.
Moreover `R2A` can never lie below both `R3` and `A2`.  The essential lower
envelope is therefore generated by

```text
AC, C2, R3, RC, A2,
```

with `RA2` and `R2A` inserted on their tie hyperplanes.  Unique `AC` and
`RC` cells are not empty by divisibility alone: over the algebraic closure,
`L=u*v` and the two linear numerators may occupy opposite roots.

## 3. First horizontal deductions

Subject to confirmation of the low-contact clients, `c=1,2` are excluded
without assuming `a=0`, so every raised-contact arc has `c>=3`.  Then for
`a>=1`, `r=1` makes `R3` the unique order-13 term and is impossible; hence
`r>=2`.

Already at `r>=2,c>=3`, new cones appear which are not reparameterizations
of the vertical `c=1,2,c>=3` clients:

```text
(a,c)=(1,3),(1,4),(2,3):  AC is uniquely lowest, but permits root allocation;
r=2:                       R3 ties or precedes the load A2 gate;
c=a+4:                     AC ties A2;
r=a+3:                     R2A ties R3;
2r=a+1:                    R2A ties A2;
r+1<c<2r-1:                RC can be uniquely lowest and root-allocated.
```

Thus dividing by the first nonzero `A` coefficient does not reproduce the
old three clients.  Absolute Faber/load offsets matter, and `R` or `C` can
lead before the next `A` separator.

## 4. Exact successor

1. Enumerate the lower faces of the eight affine order functions over
   `a>=1,c>=3,r>=2` using exact rational polyhedral arithmetic on AWS.
   Record unbounded cells parametrically; do not enumerate a large box of
   integer triples as a substitute.
2. Discard unique-minimum cells killed by the divisor column above.
3. For each remaining face, split the two orientations of `L=u*v` whenever
   `AC` or `RC` is present.  Retain moving `p,k`, every conormal correction
   through one grade beyond the initial face, and the raw nonreduced ideal.
4. Shard one face/orientation per capped AWS core.  The target is a
   contact-raising containment, not a unit saturation: show that the
   nominated leading coefficient vanishes or moves to a strictly higher
   face.
5. Keep `A=infinity` as a separate zero-section receiver.  If all finite
   `A` contacts are raised away, pass that section to the terminal
   `r7'!=0` and both Taylor/divisibility screens.  Never delete it by
   saturating the leading `A` pair.

The cheapest exact separator is the `r=1` grade-13 source identity followed
by the three smallest `AC` root-allocation charts `(1,3),(1,4),(2,3)`.
They test the only new geometry before a broader fan compiler is justified.
