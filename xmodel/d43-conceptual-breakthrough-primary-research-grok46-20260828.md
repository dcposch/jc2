
and the Nullstellensatz over an algebraic closure of `K_i` says `V(F)` is
geometrically nonempty on that factor.

This is the algorithm memo’s G6, restated as a theorem rather than as

## Q1. Kummer, Galois, norms, traces, valuations, invariants

**Answer:** none of these forces inconsistency of the honest 22-cell, and none
constructs a characteristic-zero point before elimination. After emission,
C1 forbids inconsistency on the framed components.

### A lemma that is new as algebra, old as a solve

Write `ε = 2 + r3`, the fundamental unit of `Q(√3)`, so `ε^{-1} = 2 - r3` and
`N(ε) = -1`. Then

```text
ε^3 = 26 + 15 r3,     1 + ε = 3 + r3 = A1^3,     1 + ε^{-1} = 3 - r3 = A2^3.
```

On `W2 != 0` the displayed relation `E` is a unit multiple of

```text
ε^3 A1 W1^4 + A2 W2^4 = 0,
```

equivalently

```text
(W1/W2)^4 = - A2 / (A1 ε^3).
```

The coefficient identity uses only `r3^2 = 3` and `(9+5 r3)(9-5 r3) = 6`, so
it is a lemma about `E`, not a D43 computation. Cubing to kill `A1,A2` gives
the weaker invariant

```text
(W1/W2)^{12} = - ε^{-10}
```

over `Q(√3)`, which forgets the cube-root branch of `A2/A1`. Both forms
classify the Kummer type of the E-cover: a degree-`≤4` Kummer extension of
each `K_i`, with 4th-power class `[-A2/(A1 ε^3)]` in `K_i^×/(K_i^×)^4`. That
is Rail E’s univariate, rewritten. It does not pin scale, does not constrain
tails, and does not say whether the class is trivial.

### Attacks that die on the identities already in hand

**Galois averaging of the seven leftover labels.** The candidate complement


is not a union of `Gal(Q(zeta42)/Q)`-orbits on `eta`. The cyclotomic action
`eta ↦ u · eta (mod 42)` for `u in (Z/42Z)*` has:

- `eta = 0` fixed (the unique `+42` slot);

- `3` in `{3,9,15,27,33,39}`;
- `18` in `{6,12,18,24,30,36}`;
- `26` in the full set of even residues not divisible by 3.

The leftover set mixes fragments of several orbits with pivot labels in `P`.
That is expected: `P` versus `Q` is a linear-algebra chart, not a Galois
section. Averaging the seven local classes cannot be typed as a descent of

**Valuation at the ramified prime `(√3)`.** One has `v_𝔭(3+√3) = v_𝔭(3-√3) = 1`

**Norm/trace of `E` as a point factory.** The norm of `E` to `Q(√3)[W1,W2]` is

**Fourth-power residue symbol after `K0-SPLIT`.** For each field factor `K_i`,
whether `-A2/(A1 ε^3)` is a 4th power is a finite Kummer computation. If it
is, the ρ-cover splits; if not, one works in a degree-2 or degree-4
extension. Neither case empties the J-scheme or writes tails. Duplicate of

**Invariant theory of binary sextics / C7-invariants of through-blocks.** The
through-block is a product of `n7` linear factors, so it is a cyclic

**Constructing a point from `ε`, `A1`, `A2`.** A monomial guess such as
`W1 = A2`, `W2 = A1 ε^{3/4}` satisfies a model of `E` on one branch and has

## Q2. Moment map, determinantal/quiver, cocycle, representation theory

**Answer:** the 29-row block is already a named bilinear concomitant, and no
imported dimension/nonemptiness theorem applies to the constrained map.

### What the equations actually are

The pristine evaluator is the truncated Euler identity

```text
Euler = θ_φ · γ_η − φ_η · θ_γ + 42 · 1_{(0,20)},
```

with `θ_φ(h,slot) = (slot-12) φ`, `θ_γ(h,slot) = (slot-18) γ`, eta-derivatives
dropping the eta exponent, `φ` the product of two C7-orbits times `B^6`, and
`γ` the product of four C7-orbits times `B^9`. The 184 selected coefficients
are the campaign’s support of that series. On the 22-tail cell the forecast
is that 155 coefficients vanish identically and 29 quadrics remain.

So the 29-row scheme is

```text
Ψ^{-1}( Wronskian^{-1}(42 · 1_{(0,20)})  ∩  22-support ),
```

where `Ψ` is the C7-orbit product map from `(W, tails)` to the pair of jets
`(φ,γ)`. That sentence is a reading of `source_rows`, not a new model.

### Why no theorem comes with it

**Wronski map / Mukhin–Tarasov–Varchenko / Shapiro.** Those theorems are
about the Wronski map on a Grassmannian of linear series in one variable.
Here `(φ,γ)` are not a free 2-plane of univariate polynomials. They lie in
the image of `Ψ`. Nonemptiness of a free Wronskian level set does not
descend along `Ψ`. Category error. Adjacent to Avenue 10’s Wronskian
language, not a D43 theorem.

**Moment map of a Hamiltonian `SL(2)` or `G_m`.** The free Wronskian is
`SL(2)`-invariant, and is homogeneous under `(φ,γ) ↦ (λφ, λ^{-1}γ)`. The
C7-product locus is not an `SL(2)`-orbit closure in any identified way, and
the D43 scheme is affine algebraic, not a compact symplectic manifold.
Atiyah–Guillemin–Sternberg convexity does not apply. Identifying Euler with
“a moment map” without a group that preserves `Ψ` is Avenue 33 costume
(untwisted action residues already automatic). The only surviving `G_m` is
the local scale of `(W, tails)`, broken by `+42`; that is already the
inhomogeneous scale-pin, not a convexity theorem.

**Nakajima quiver / multiplicative preprojective / cyclic necklace.** The
through-block is a product of cyclic linear factors; the other-block is an
elementary-symmetric C6-projection of a generating function; the B-block is
the literal `(((1+η t^{20})^7 - 3/2)^{6 or 9})`. That is a specific
polynomial, not a general quiver moment map with a cited dimension formula.
Rename of the C7-orbit construction.

**Determinantal variety of 2×2 minors.** Euler is one 2×2 determinant in jet
space. The 29 scalar rows are coefficients of that determinant after `Ψ`.
The Fitting ideals of the band-20 matrix `A(W)` are determinantal in the
ordinary linear-algebra sense; that is the algorithm memo’s D3/D4, not a
Giambelli/Thom–Porteous nonemptiness theorem. Expected dimension of 29
quadrics in 24 variables is negative; the modular zeros show the 29 are
dependent or the emptiness is char-`p`. That dependence is the thing to be
proved, not a corollary of a named determinantal class.

**Cocycle / deformation complex.** See Q3. The pair `(T_p = 53, obstructions
in 55 dimensions)` is the cotangent complex of `F` at one point. It is not
an identification of the 29-row block with a global deformation complex of
Keller maps. Virtual dimension `182-184 = -2` at the ambient level, versus
tangent 53, only restates that the 184 coefficient functions are dependent
at the point.

**Representation-theoretic rank bound for `A(W)` (unproved, not ranked).** A
plausible guess, not a chain: band 20 is affine in the ten D21 tails, so
`A(W)` is the Jacobian in those tails and is independent of them. The ten
etas at slot 20 are the arithmetic progression `0,3,...,27`. The ten columns
split as two sides `{1,2}` times `{φ-type, γ-type}`, with `tg0` lacking a
level-37 `W` and possibly spanning the same γ-type space. If that
factorization holds as polynomial matrices, generic rank of `A` is `≤ 4`,
all 5×5 minors vanish, and the modular rank-4 witnesses lie on the generic
stratum. The implication is honest, the hypothesis is not proved, and the
discriminator is already D3. A single nonzero 5×5 minor kills the guess and
forces the modular fibres onto a Fitting drop. Do not treat the guess as a
substitute for that minor.

Q2 therefore adds no ranked existence/emptiness theorem. The correct
structural sentence is: 29 support-truncated Euler coefficients, not seven
global generators, not a quiver variety with a formula for `dim`.

## Q3. Rank 129, tangent 53, cokernel 55, and smoothness

**Answer:** these numbers do not imply formal smoothness, local generation, or
an obstruction to all branches. C2 is the only upgrade that does not compute
higher digits, and it does not reach smoothness.

Facts that are already settled and must not be re-promoted:

- Essential ambient 182, rows 184, rank 129, `182-129=53`, `184-129=55`.
- Inverse-chart `uW` explains the old `131` versus `129` split; dummy
  level-71/73 tails are invisible.
- One kernel-zero `p^2` correction with vanishing 55-syndrome exists. That is
  a first-order lift of one section of the 53-space, not a 53-dimensional
  family, not a `Z_p` point, not formal smoothness.
- On the 22-cell, tail Jacobian rank 22 at two points is étale-over-`W` on
  `D(Δ_*)` after the leftover classes vanish. It is a different linearization
  from the ambient 184×182 matrix. Mixing them is how the “seven conditions”
  slogan was born.

**Why rank 129 cannot be upgraded by slogan.** For a map `F` to `A^{184}`,
rank `dF(p)=129` says the conormal space at `p` is 129-dimensional. Among 184
gradients, 55 linear dependences exist at `p`. Those dependences are
syzygies of the *gradients*, not syzygies of the *polynomials*. Constant
left-kernel vectors over `K0` would be global linear relations among the 184
Euler coefficients and would give local generation by 129 rows; the 55
cokernel basis presently lives in `F_p^{184}` and is not known to lift to
`K0`. The Euler series being “one equation” in free jets does not give 55
global syzygies on the 182-parameter family, because `Ψ` has already
constrained `(φ,γ)`.

Krull dimension `≤ 53` always. Formal smoothness needs Krull dimension `=53`
(regularity) plus, for a mixed-characteristic statement, `p`-flatness. A
53-parameter family of points, a regular sequence of length 129, or an
isomorphism to a known smooth scheme would suffice. None is available.
Setting the 53 free Newton digits to zero tests one formal branch, which is
the high-Hensel lane already firewalled as not branch evidence.

**Quadratic cone versus higher digits.** C2 computes `κ` at `p`. That is the
content of Q3. Infinite Hensel on the kernel-zero section, even if it
converged in `Z_p`, would still be one branch; Artin approximation would
then give an algebraic germ, not a polynomial map (Avenue 4). That is not an
upgrade of the rank data, it is a different program.

**22-cell versus ambient.** Even a smoothness theorem for the 184-row scheme
at the banked point would not be a smoothness theorem for the leftover
seven-in-`W` residual, and conversely. The 155 rows that vanish on the cell
may still cut the complement to first order; their contribution to rank 129
is normal to the cell. Do not transport C2’s cone dimension onto the
seven-class chart.
