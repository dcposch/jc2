### A2. "collision-ideals" — Lean 4 planar obstruction/sheet formalization (methodological neighbor)
https://github.com/what-social-construct/collision-ideals (created 2026-07-25, updated 2026-08-14,
4 stars; paper + Lean 4). Formalizes collision ideals I_R(F) (differences F_i(x)−F_i(y)), an
obstruction module Obs(F) vanishing iff F is an automorphism, off-diagonal sheets of the fiber
product, secant-determinant constructions, valuation-theoretic ramification, and explicit sheet
decomposition via double cosets D_E\G/H. Targets BOTH the planar char-0 case (conditional theorem:
under `PlanarBoundaryCoherence` + `PlanarRamificationRigidity` — both left unproven — the planar
obstruction vanishes) and a separable-cubic S₃ contrast in dim 3. Anonymous author.
**Relevance: ACTIONABLE.** Closest public methodological neighbor to our sheet-number/covering
program (their unproven "planar ramification rigidity" is cousin to our rigidity half of conj:R).
Worth one focused read to check for usable lemmas or collision-of-notions with SHEET6 terminology.

### A3. Char-2 dimension-2 separable JC counterexample (plane-adjacent, cautionary for mod-p logic)
Romy Mondello, "A Dimension-Two Counterexample to the Separable Jacobian Conjecture in Characteristic
Two", arXiv:2608.02634 (submitted ~Jul 29): étale, Jacobian 1, non-injective in **dimension 2**, char 2.
Predecessor: Irit Huq-Kuruvilla, arXiv:2607.20968 (Jul 23), explicit char-2 separable counterexample

or lift arguments quietly use p=2 or a separability-only hypothesis.

### A5. Canonical framing sources for Paper 1's "why n=2 is different" paragraph
(a) Shuhong Gao, "Counterexamples to the Jacobian conjecture in dimensions greater than two",
arXiv:2608.00222 (Jul 31) — the systematic write-up: tangent-sweep from plane curves → direction
fields on hypersurfaces; explicit maps dims 3–5, degrees 4–12; counterexamples of arbitrarily large
geometric degree in every dim > 2; characterizes them as non-proper étale covers. (b) Will Sawin's
argument (in comments of Speyer's post) that the mechanism needs dim ≥ 3: P¹×P^(d−1) → P^d with
class-group obstructions blocking an affine version for d < 3. (c) Zbigniew Jelonek, "On mappings
with Jacobian one", arXiv:2607.20597 (Jul 22) and Frederico Xavier, arXiv:2606.22041 (Jun 20,
prescient): Keller maps of bounded degree form components consisting either of automorphisms or
generically of counterexamples — a structural dichotomy directly relevant to degree-bound campaigns.
**Relevance: ACTIONABLE (writing).** These are the references reviewers will expect our plane-case
paper to situate itself against.
