## 1. Row index and derivative functional

Let `C=Σ_{i+j≤D} c_{ij}γ^iπ^j` and `φ: γ↦v^{−1}, π↦v⁴u−λ2v²−λ3v−v^{−1}`. Negative `e` forces `i+j>5t`, so `t≤⌊(D−1)/5⌋` and at fixed `t` the exponent runs over `5t−D≤e≤−1`. Writing `s=5t−e` gives `5t+1≤s≤D`, i.e. `0≤t≤⌊(s−1)/5⌋`; the map `(s,t)↦(t,5t−s)` is a bijection onto the negative support of the consumed gate (C2: 30 and 75 pairs, 105 total).

Collecting the row `[u^t v^{5t−s}]` over all monomials: a summand lands there iff `i+j=s+3b+2d`. With `b=d=0` we have `i+j=s`, `z=j−t`, coefficient `(−1)^{j−t}C(j,t)`, and `Σ_{i+j=s}c_{ij}(−1)^{j−t}C(j,t)=f_s^{(t)}(−1)/t!` for `f_s(z)=Σ_{i+j=s}c_{ij}z^j`. ## 4. Unimodularity

`M_{t,i}=(−1)^{s−i−t}C(s−i,t)`, `0≤t,i<r`. First proof (audit of the producer's): `(−1)^{s−i−t}=(−1)^{s−i}(−1)^t`; extracting these gives sign `(−1)^{rs−r(r−1)/2+r(r−1)/2}=(−1)^{rs}` and leaves `N_{t,i}=C(x_i,t)`, `x_i=s−i`. Since `C(x,t)=x^t/t!+`lower, `det N=Π_{i<j}(x_j−x_i)/Πt!`; with descending nodes `x_j−x_i=−(j−i)`, and `Π_{i<j}(j−i)=Π_{t<r}t!`, so `det N=(−1)^{r(r−1)/2}`. Orientation is therefore correct as printed.

Second, genuinely distinct proof (C4): column `i` is the Taylor expansion at `−1` of `z^{s−i}`, i.e. the coefficients of `(w−1)^{s−i}` in `Z[w]/(w^r)` (Horner shift). Hence `M=U·T`, where `U` is multiplication by the unit `z^{s−r+1}` in `Z[w]/(w^r)` (lower-triangular Toeplitz, diagonal `(−1)^{s−r+1}`) and `T` is the matrix of `z^{r−1−i}`, anti-triangular with unit anti-diagonal. So `det M=(−1)^{(s−r+1)r}(−1)^{r(r−1)/2}=(−1)^{rs+r(r−1)/2}` because `r(r−1)` is even. C4 verifies the factorisation, both determinants by integer Bareiss (not Fraction elimination), and an adjugate inverse with integer entries satisfying `M·M^{−1}=I`, for every `s=1..24` (`r=1..5`); the unsigned mutation fails at `s=1`. CONFIRMED.

## 5. Exact graph ideal

Order coefficients by descending total degree. At level `s<D` the `r_s` rows equal `M·p_s + L_s(q_s) + F_s(c_{>s},λ)` with `p_s` the pivots, `q_s` the same-level non-pivots (unknowns or face constants), and `L_s,F_s` `Z[λ]`-linear. Since `M∈GL_r(Z)`, left-multiplying by `M^{−1}` is an invertible change of generators over `Z`, so the level-`s` rows generate the same ideal as `p_s − G_s(q_s,c_{>s},λ)` with `G_s` affine-linear over `Z[λ]`. Higher pivots inside `G_s` are then replaced by their own graph expressions; each replacement is a rewrite modulo generators already in the ideal, so the ideal is unchanged and the recursion terminates because dependence is strictly on higher degree. Thus `⟨R_{s,t}⟩=⟨p_k−G'_k(retained,λ)⟩` and `K[λ][all c]/⟨rows⟩≅K[λ][retained c]` as `K[λ2,λ3]`-algebras, with no denominator in λ, no field change and no rank assumption; the argument uses only `Z`-unimodularity, so it holds over any base ring, in particular `Q`, `Q[ρ]/(ρ²−3ρ+1)`, and any nonreduced quotient. For any further ideal `J` (Jacobian rows, `z·c−1`, face relations) the induced map `K[λ][c]/(rows+J)≅K[λ][retained]/(J|_{graph})` is an isomorphism provided every generator of `J` is substituted; it is not a statement about geometric points only.

C6 realises this on a degree-12 fixture with the fixed top `z⁹(z+1)³` and pseudo-random retained integers: solving the 1×1, 2×2 and 3×3 blocks descending with my integer inverses makes all 21 negative rows vanish identically in `K[λ2,λ3]`; the 18 graph values have integer coefficients (no denominators), are genuinely λ-dependent, and are affine-linear in the retained data (checked by `G(N1+N2)−G(N1)−G(N2)+G(0)=0`). Replacing `M^{−1}` by its unsigned version leaves rows nonzero; perturbing a retained coefficient of degree 8 breaks rows at levels `≤8` only, and perturbing the degree-1 coefficient breaks exactly one row, whose deletion is caught by the drop-row control. CONFIRMED.
