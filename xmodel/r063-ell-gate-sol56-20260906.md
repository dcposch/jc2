### 2.2 Equations (4.3) and (4.4)

For a root \(\alpha\) of \(P'_\xi\), the derivative of \(P'(\alpha)\) is
zero. If \(\alpha\) is major, differentiation lowers the nonzero order of
\(Q'(\alpha)\) by one, and Lemma 4.1 gives

\[
 \operatorname{ord}(P'_Y(\alpha)Q'(\alpha))=-1-\ell.
\]

If it is minor, Proposition 3.3 gives the unchanged value \(-\delta_\sigma\).
Summing all \(m\) roots gives the exact shift

\[
 (4.3)^\ell:\quad
 I(P'_\xi,P'_YQ')=(1+\ell)m+
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal P_m}|D^{P'_\xi}_\sigma|
       (\delta_\sigma-1-\ell).
\]

The side bound used in (4.4) does **not** shift. The frozen producer reached
the right bound but asserted along the way that
\(\operatorname{ord}Q'(\beta)=0\); that equality need not hold if the
off-\(P'_\sigma\) root of \((P'_\sigma)'\) is also a root of
\(Q'_\sigma\). The following valuation argument repairs the proof without
that assertion.

Choose related roots \(\alpha\) of \(P'_\xi\) and \(\beta\) of \(P'_Y\) in
a final minor disc of radius \(\delta\). Proposition 3.3 makes
\(\operatorname{ord}(\beta-\alpha)=\delta\). For every root \(\gamma_j\) of
\(Q'\), put \(q_j=\operatorname{ord}(\alpha-\gamma_j)\le\delta\), and let
\(S=\{j:q_j=\delta\}\), which is nonempty. For \(j\notin S\), ultrametricity
gives \(\operatorname{ord}(\beta-\gamma_j)=q_j\); for \(j\in S\), write that
order as \(r_j\ge\delta\). Since \(\alpha\) is minor,
\(\sum_jq_j=\operatorname{ord}Q'(\alpha)=0\). Every product summand in
\(Q'_Y(\beta)\) then has order at least \(-\delta\): removing an index in
\(S\) leaves

\[
 \sum_{j\notin S}q_j+\sum_{j\in S\setminus\{i\}}r_j
 =-\delta+\sum_{j\in S\setminus\{i\}}(r_j-\delta)\ge-\delta,
\]

and removing an index outside \(S\) is even larger. Hence
\(\operatorname{ord}Q'_Y(\beta)\ge-\delta\), with no use of \(J\).

Lemma 4.1 at a root \(\beta\) of \(P'_Y\) now gives an exact cost
\(1+\ell\) when \(\operatorname{ord}P'_\xi(\beta)<0\), and a cost at most
\(\delta\) in the zero-order minor case. Since Proposition 3.3 gives
\(|D^{P'_Y}_\sigma|=|D^{P'_\xi}_\sigma|-1\),

\[
 (4.4)^\ell:\quad
 I(P'_Y,P'_\xi Q'_Y)\le(1+\ell)(m-1)+
 \sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal P_m}
 (|D^{P'_\xi}_\sigma|-1)(\delta_\sigma-1-\ell).
\]

Thus both displayed packet formulas in the frozen report have the correct
shift, while the load-bearing side condition remains \(\ell\)-free.

### 2.3 The derivative resultant and Theorem 4.7

\[
