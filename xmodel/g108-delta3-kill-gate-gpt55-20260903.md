## Source Audit: Common `h3`

Moh Definition 5.1 defines one tower of major discs, with one general point in
each disc, carrying simultaneous root counts for `g` and the approximate-root
family; see the extracted source at
`box/g108gate-20260903/moh_p179_196.txt:65` and `:89`.  The tower is common,
not one tower per final child.

quasi-approximate roots `T_i` for the pair at
`box/g108gate-20260903/xu_p10_13.txt:21`, states the principal-minor
multiplicities at `:25`, and writes the leading coefficients in terms of a
single polynomial `p(pi)` at `:47` with `deg p=u_s` at `:51`.  In the split
case, Corollary 7.5 explicitly writes one product
`p(pi)=(pi-c1)...(pi-c_us)` with distinct roots at `:112`-`:119`, and the same
single `p` is used in the leading forms of `f`, `g`, and `(T_s)_f` at
`:115`-`:127`.

Therefore the two 12-packets at `delta=3` are roots of one quadratic `p`; they
share the same pre-extension `h3` leading polynomial.  At the child step
`delta'=4`, each packet may have its own final local continuation, but that is
after restricting to one root of the same `p`.  Replacing this by two unrelated
`h3` polynomials is not a sourced weakening; it changes the Moh/Xu tower.
