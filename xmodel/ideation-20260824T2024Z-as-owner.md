### 4.1 `NEW-CANDIDATE`: the unordered critical-value norm polynomial

On the `(9,12)` order-three `nu!=0` landing, let `B(z)` be the licensed
quadratic ramification polynomial and put

```text

C(T)=Res_z(B(z),R_T(z)).
```

Because `deg_z B=2` and `R_T` is linear in `T`, `C(T)` has degree at most two
in `T`.  On the open set `disc(B)*Res(B,f)!=0`, if `alpha_1,alpha_2` are the
two roots of `B`, then after dividing the explicit leading/content factor,

```text
C_norm(T)=(T-beta(alpha_1))*(T-beta(alpha_2)),
beta=g^3/f^4.                                      (1)
```

Thus three invariant equations give the exact first collision split:

```text
C_norm(1)=C_norm'(1)=0     both B-roots absorbed by W=g^3-f^4;
C_norm(1)=0, disc_T(C)!=0 exactly one B-root absorbed;
disc_T(C)=0, C_norm(1)!=0 equal non-1 critical values;
C_norm(1)!=0, disc_T(C)!=0 no absorption and unequal values.       (2)
```

The omitted loci `disc(B)=0` and `Res(B,f)=0` are named separate strata, not
silently divided away.  Equations (1)--(2) are invariant under swapping the
two roots and live over the coefficient field.

### 4.2 Exact AS matrix-factorization residue compiler

The vertical D8 coefficient map has the form

```text
M(X,Y)=(-A*X+B*Y,-H*X-A*Y),
H=h^3, A=P+Q*z, B=(s+w*z)*(R+T*z),
deg X<=2, deg Y<=1.                                (3)
```

With

```text
Delta=A^2+H*B,
Msharp=[[-A,-B],[H,-A]],
```

one has the exact polynomial identity

```text
Msharp*M=M*Msharp=Delta*I.                         (4)
```

For an inhomogeneous pair `(u,v)`, every solution therefore satisfies

```text
Delta | (-A*u-B*v),
Delta | ( H*u-A*v).                                (5)
```

On `Delta!=0`, the quotients in (5), together with their degree caps, recover
`X,Y`; on `Delta=0`, (5) is not sufficient and the rank-zero/one-factor
strata must be rebuilt directly.  This turns a seven-by-five coefficient
matrix into two univariate remainder calculations plus explicit boundary
rows.  The exact determinant was already known; the proposed advance is to
make (5) the accepted-digit recursion and to carry its degenerate strata
