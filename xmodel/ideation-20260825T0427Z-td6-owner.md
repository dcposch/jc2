## New mechanism and new connections

### New mechanism: normalization-idempotent component certificate

Let `A` be the finite source quotient over the good `w`-open in
`F_127[w]`, after replaying every localization used by the source equations.
Rather than first reconstructing all seven missing coordinates as rational
functions of `(w,v)`, seek an exact element/projector `e in A` such that

```text
e^2=e,       trace_A(e)=190,
H(w,v)e=0,   and every original source row annihilates the e-summand.
```

The exact formulation may instead use a kernel projector or a coprime
factor/cofactor Bezout identity; the invariant content is a rank-190 direct
summand of the finite algebra whose characteristic polynomial in `v` is the
candidate `H`.  The 123 shape bases and three exceptional fibres supply
interpolation/degree data, while `H(0)=Q8bar*C` with multiplicity one supplies
a sharp specialization check.  Idempotents lift uniquely through a complete
local ring once the integral source model and denominator open are exact, so
this gives a focused good-reduction/no-merger bridge.  It does **not** by
itself prove geometric irreducibility, attach all contacts, or lift to
characteristic zero; each remains an explicit gate.

This mechanism connects avenues **25/26** (passport and primitive contact
grouping), **28** (normalization/contact specialization), and **31**
(finite algebra, integrality, and idempotent lifting).  The useful point is
that group theory supplies the binary geometric question, while finite
algebra supplies a checkable certificate for which side the source quotient
actually realizes.

A second connection links avenues **19/21/36**: canonical AS carries should
be represented by Smith/Hermite normal data for the divided integral map,
together with a support/conductor envelope.  Hensel propagation then acts on
proof-carrying signatures rather than on arbitrary RREF representatives.
This makes bounded-support failure observable instead of silently merging it
with affine compatibility.
