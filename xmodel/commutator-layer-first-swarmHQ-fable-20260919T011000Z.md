## Six independent mathematical checks

Use A=C<X,Y>, c=XY-YX, I=(c), R=A/I=C[x,y], and e=c mod I^2.
The ring identification is explicitly

    R tensor_C R -> B=C[x1,y1,x2,y2],
    p tensor q -> p(x1,y1) q(x2,y2).

Subscript1 is LEFT multiplication and subscript2 RIGHT multiplication on
I/I^2. P1,P2 are evaluation labels; P_x,P_y mean partial derivatives.
Put dx=x1-x2, dy=y1-y2, D=(dx,dy), Delta_P=P1-P2, and similarly Q.

1. **Relation module — CONFIRMED.** The two R actions factor through
   I/I^2 and commute, giving a surjection theta:B->I/I^2, f->f e.
   In the triangular representation

       rho(X)=[[x1,1],[0,x2]],  rho(Y)=[[y1,0],[0,y2]],

   rho(c)=-dy E12 and rho(u c v)=-u_ab(x1,y1)v_ab(x2,y2)dy E12.
   Also rho(I^2)=0. Thus theta(f)=0 implies dy*f=0 in the domain B,
   hence f=0. This proves freeness, not merely cyclicity. In particular
   [Y,c] maps to -dy^2 E12, so c is not central modulo I^2.

2. **Ordered lifts — CONFIRMED.** With X before Y, the off-diagonal
   entry for P~ is the polynomial divided difference

       a_P=(P(x1,y2)-P(x2,y2))/dx,
       b_P=(P(x1,y1)-P(x1,y2))/dy.

   Both divisions are literal polynomial divisibility, not localization.
   Delta_P=dx*a_P+dy*b_P. For H=a_P*b_Q-b_P*a_Q, direct multiplication
   gives rho([P~,Q~])_12=Delta_P*a_Q-Delta_Q*a_P=-dy*H.
   Freeness therefore identifies [P~,Q~]=H e in I/I^2.

3. **All corrections — CONFIRMED.** Every f in B is a finite sum of
   p_i tensor q_i and is realized by sum p_i~ c q_i~ in I. Corrections
   F,G of classes f e,g e give

   The signs follow from left value minus right value; correction products
   vanish modulo I^2. Existence is exactly H-1 in J=(Delta_P,Delta_Q).
   If H-1=alpha*Delta_P+beta*Delta_Q, take f=beta,g=-alpha.
   This step does not need the Keller hypothesis.
