## Three detailed cards

### Card 1 — Perfect-square full-source reduction, not another tail jet

**Fingerprint:** order-two / exact square component / full source and terminal
rows / factor substitution.

On the square component write

```text
L=z^2+s,   K=L^2,   N=L*(alpha*z+beta).
```

Then both terms of the reviewed first-normal functional are polynomials:

```text
N^2/K=(alpha*z+beta)^2,
K^(5/2)=L^5.
```

This explains, structurally, why all seven first tails vanish and why `k10`
is invisible there.  The new test is to substitute this factorization into
the **full** reviewed one-`Lambda` source, terminal `[6,2]`, and both Taylor
systems before doing further ambient elimination.  Retain transverse/higher-
contact variables only when the exact source creates them.

- Dependencies: reviewed one-`Lambda` reduction, reviewed Padé/support
  theorem, exact source rows and terminal/Taylor compilers.
- Cheapest discriminator: exact substitution and row-factor audit over `Q`,
  followed by a fraction-free linear/small-ideal solve on the declared opens;
  use the two-prime next-jet tables only as controls.
- If unit: the square component is killed at a named full-source row.
- If positive-dimensional: freeze the resulting source-honest recurrence and
  prolong two steps, searching for a rational/algebraic parametrization.
- If the factorization ceases to be source-closed: route the first offending
  row into the transverse Rees chart; do not call it a survivor.
- Stop rule: stop ambient square-component Gröbner work once either a unit
  certificate or a lower-dimensional exact source recurrence is obtained.
- Expected information gain: **very high**; it attacks the only current
  order-two branch that visibly survives the next modular gate and serves
  both proof and disproof.
