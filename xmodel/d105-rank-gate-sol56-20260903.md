## 2. What the sealed framework actually requires

Put `t=x^-1` and choose a common field `C((z))` with `x=z^-R`.  For a requested
last exponent `Q`, the report's decorated skeleton is

```text
D_Q = (bare Moh tuple,
       rooted cluster tree on n labelled leaves,
       cyclic Galois action and orbit/stabilizer data,
       truncated Puiseux template tau_i(z) for every leaf,
       declarations identifying shared tame coefficients,
       Q and a sufficient valuation guard).
```

This contract is substantive, not serialization overhead.  If
`D_i=product_(j!=i)(tau_i-tau_j)`, then every entry of `D_i^-1`, every time
value, and every moment sees contacts with all other 104 roots.  A missing
outer leaf can change a denominator leader, the first retained exponent, a
residue, a moment coefficient, and the rank.

For a complete decoration the intrinsic unknown count is

```text
U(Q) = h + u_0 + |Z_Q|,                                    (4.13)
```

where `h` is the number of branch orbits, `u_0` is the number of
unspecialized leading/star parameters after gauges, and `Z_Q` is the set of
independent tame symbols that can reach the equations through the complete
inverse/integration guard.  None of `h`, `u_0`, or `Z_Q` follows from `k`.
In particular, “one orbit of bottom discs” does not by itself give the number
of orbits on the 105 leaves, and two local kernel coordinates per displayed
disc are not formula (4.13).

Likewise the intrinsic equation count is

```text
C(Q) = C_res + C_deg(Q) + C_poly(Q),                        (4.11)
C_res = h.
```

Its `C_deg` summands require the initial exponents of the 35 moment identities;
its `C_poly` summands require the initial exponents of the surviving
coefficients.  Those exponents are absent here.  The normalized shortcut
`C=h+35+105 K_ord` applies only after one has proved the stationary invariant
support `t^0,...,t^K_ord`; `K_ord` is an order cutoff, not the skeleton value
`K=n-m=35`, and it cannot be used to invent that support.

## 5. Exact system schema and order reached

Once a complete separable 105-root packet of one polynomial `g-c_2` is
supplied, its Galois covariance is verified, and the inverse guard is closed,
the exact moment-form system is:

```text
D_i = product_(j != i) (tau_i-tau_j),       W_i=D_i^-1,
[z^R] W_i = 0,                              NO-RESIDUE,
q H_i[q] = -c R W_i[q+R]  (q != 0),         H_i[0]=a_orbit,
M_r = sum_i H_i tau_i^r W_i.
```

At each retained exponent, the monic degree block is the 35-row family

```text
[z^q]M_r=0                 for r=0,...,33,
[z^q]M_34 = 1 if q=0, and 0 otherwise.
```

At a forbidden positive invariant exponent, polynomiality adds the 70 rows

```text
[z^q]M_r=0                 for r=35,...,104,
```

which, after DEG and using that the elementary symmetric coefficients of the
complete root packet lie in `C[x]`, is unit-triangularly equivalent to testing
the remaining Lagrange coefficients after the already-counted monic identity.
Thus “35 equations
per order” is only the DEG block.  A positive invariant global order normally
has 105 DEG-plus-POLY slots before dependencies; NO-RESIDUE contributes `h`
once when its guard is reached.

The first nonvacuous global exponent is

```text
Q_* = first exponent in the invariant support at which one of
      M_0,...,M_34-target or a forbidden tail is nonzero as a polynomial.
```

It cannot be read from `delta_1-delta_2`.  The junction number is provenance
for activation of outer factors, while `Q_*` is an actual exponent of a
global orbit trace.  In the minimal uniformizers the junction indices
`7,7,11` are not multiples of `R=36,26,34`; a nontrivial character can cancel
at the trace before influencing a later invariant order.  The complete
templates decide which products do so.


## 8. Reading the result

### 8.1 Does the first global order overdetermine?

For the **blind restricted count**, A is overdetermined at `k=12,14,16` and
not at `k=18,20,22,24`; B is overdetermined by 9; C is overdetermined by 1.
That faithfully reproduces the charged GPT-5.5 table.

For the **exact global system**, the answer is `UNDECIDED` for A, B, and C.
There is no known `Q_*`, no intrinsic `U(Q_*)`, and no instantiated
`C(Q_*)`.  At a positive invariant order POLY may add 70 rows, while outer and
shared variables may add columns.  Galois descent may remove repetitions and
identify parameters.  One cannot select only whichever side helps a kill.

C's `34`-versus-`35` razor is therefore a well-motivated experiment design,
not a computed cokernel.  In the restricted 35-by-34 fiction, full column
rank would leave a row cokernel of one, exposing a left-cokernel compatibility
condition.  It is inconsistent only if the affine forcing has a nonzero
component in that cokernel (or the saturated ideal is empty); rank 34 alone is
not a kill.  The actual global matrix may have more or fewer intrinsic columns
and dependent rows.

### 8.2 What a rank dependency would mean

A dependency means that a nominal coefficient condition does not cut a new
tangent direction on the component being tested.  Possible sources include a
universal root identity, a Galois repetition, a common-additive gauge,
character cancellation, an ancestor-sharing identity, or a relation special
to the star/outer coefficients.  For C, one exact dependency in a genuine
35-by-34 descended matrix would remove the count razor and identify a
structural compatibility to propagate to later orders.  It would not by
itself prove existence or all-order survival.

Conversely, a sampled ambient rank at arbitrary values of algebraically
related star and root-of-unity parameters is not a rank on a solution
component.  It cannot be promoted to a kill without the ring map and component
check.

### 8.3 What `SATURATED-EMPTY` would buy

If, after enumerating every actual decoration/component of A, B, and C, each
ideal saturated at the full nondegenerate product were empty, all three true
`D=105` groups would be killed.  Since the rebased census identifies 105 as
the smallest surviving degree above 100 and the next surviving degree is 108,
the conclusion would be only

```text
D_min >= 108.
```

It would not prove a uniform degree theorem or the Jacobian conjecture.  This
lane has no such emptiness result, so even that limited conclusion is not
available here.
