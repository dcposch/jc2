### Card A — the derivation-conductor test

**Label:** **strongest proof attack / genuinely new mechanism / proposed exact
experiment**.

**Target claim.**  Let

```text

Cbar = integral closure of A in L,
delta_P = (Q_y d/dx - Q_x d/dy)/J,
delta_Q = (-P_y d/dx + P_x d/dy)/J.
```

polynomial on `B=C[x,y]`, then no height-one prime of `Cbar` above an affine
prime of `A` can be a pole of `x` or `y`.  If true, valuation-theoretic
integrality gives `x,y in Cbar`, hence `B` finite over `A`, finite étale, and an
automorphism.  Only the implication after the proposed height-one lemma is an
**exact conditional deduction**; the lemma is a conjectural target.

normalization lattice and the polynomial inverse-Jacobian frame.  Define the


`B`.  The campaign has studied raw boundary cokernels and traces, but not this
