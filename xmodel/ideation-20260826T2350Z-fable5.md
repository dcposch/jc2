## 3. New mechanism and new connections

### New mechanism (primary): deck-parity descent of membership certificates

Target: the named open **deck-equivariant square bridge** and part of the
closed-receiver debt.  Status: navigation now; a small exact lemma after a
desk check.

Observation: the campaign's landing certificates are ideal memberships
`k*U = sum_i c_i r_i` on a chart.  Let `tau` be the deck involution
(`rho -> -rho` on the charged family; the V17 coefficient is recorded
rho-even, and the T-rs unit `U = 1 + 32*rho^2*qcs^2*(8*rho^2*qcs^2+3)` is
rho-even).  In characteristic zero, if `tau` stabilizes the chart ring and
the rows `r_i`, the localizer `k`, and `U` are all `tau`-invariant, then
splitting each cofactor into even and odd parts and comparing parities
forces `k*U = sum_i c_i^{even} r_i` **already** — the odd parts contribute
an odd element equal to the odd part of an even element, i.e. zero.  The
certificate therefore lives in the invariant subring at no computational
cost.  If the invariant subring presents the square-receiver chart
(`rho^2 -> R`, others fixed — the generic shape for a sign action), the
square-receiver membership follows from the *existing* certificate with
zero new saturation.  If instead `tau` swaps two charts (e.g. exchanges the
`rs`/`cs` specializations), the same argument applied to the pair of
certificates on the common localization gives a symmetrized certificate on
the quotient — which is exactly a glued/receiver statement for that pair.

This is not in the charged files: the compiler checks row-level deck
invariance, and the overlays list the deck/square bridge as open, but
nobody has proposed descending the **cofactor identities** by parity.  The
whole cost is a parity table plus one exact re-verification.  Idea card 1.
