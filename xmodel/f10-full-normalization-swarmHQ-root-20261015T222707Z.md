## Exact question and hypotheses

Work over C. Let r>=1, m=3r+1, n=5r+2, and scalars u,ell. Set

    Pi=t-u*t^2+S*t^3,
    Delta=1+u*t-ell*t*Pi-t*Pi^2,
    A=S*t^3+(S*d-u)*t^2+(1-u*d+S*v)*t+k,
    B=sum_(j=0)^5 B_j(S)*t^j, B_5=S^2.

Assume deg d<=r, deg v<=2r, deg k=m, a=[S^m]k!=0;
deg B_j<=n-r*j, b=[S^n]B_0!=0. Retain the WHOLE equation
[A,B]_(S,t)=Delta and polynomiality of BOTH Abar and Bbar under

    S=p*z^3-z^2+u*z, t=1/z.

No leading-only relaxation is licensed. The candidate claim is that the known
two-chart affine surface Y is the FULL normalization of C[A,B] in C(S,t).
Moreover its complement of the reconstructed plane is exactly one smooth A1.
This would remove a carrier-identification hypothesis, not furnish the missing
Keller-pair exclusion on that carrier. The focused research question after this
lemma would still require a genuinely new implication, not a repeat of old

## 1. The whole affine surface, without confusing its two bases

In C(p,z)=C(S,t), put x=S*t=p*z^2-z+u and y=S*t^2-u*t=p*z-1.
Let T0=C[p,S,x,y]. The identities

    x^2=u*x+S*y, x*y=S*p-x, y^2=p*x-u*p-y

make T0 a finite C[p,S]-module generated by 1,x,y. On D(y), z=(x-u)/y;
on D(y+1), t=p/(y+1). Consequently

    T0[y^-1]=C[p,z][y^-1],
    T0[(y+1)^-1]=C[S,t][(y+1)^-1].

The two principal opens cover Spec T0. They give the gluing of C[p,z] and
C[S,t] along z*t=1 and S=p*z^3-z^2+u*z: in the first chart, a point with
y=0 has p*z=1 and is in the second chart; in the second, y+1=0 implies
t*(S*t-u)=-1 and hence it is in the first. Thus Y=Spec T0 is smooth,
normal and affine, with both entire planes as charts. Its global ring is
T=C[p,z] intersect C[S,t]=T0. The intersection equality also follows from
the two localizations and comaximality of powers of y and y+1.

Finiteness over C[p,S] is NOT finiteness over C[A,B]. The latter is the new
conditional step below. The old rank three over C[p,S] is NOT the map degree.

## 2. Every target-finite valuation lands in one of these charts

Use weights (S,t)=(1,r). The leading forms are S^m*C(theta), S^n*D(theta),
theta=t/S^r, where C is monic cubic, D monic quintic, C(0)=a and D(0)=b.
The full Jacobian equation gives

    m*C*D' - n*C'*D = -theta^7.

In particular every root of C is nonzero and simple, and C,D have no common
root: at a common nonzero root, the displayed equation is impossible.

Let V be ANY discrete valuation ring of L=C(S,t) containing C[A,B], with
valuation nu. Constants are units. Suppose nu(S)<0. Divide A by S^m and
substitute theta=t/S^r. This is a monic cubic in theta with coefficients
in V; all their lower-in-S terms have positive valuation, and A/S^m has
positive valuation. Thus theta is integral over V, so theta belongs to V.
Reduction gives C(theta_bar)=0. Likewise B/S^n reduces to D(theta_bar),
which is nonzero. Hence nu(B)=n*nu(S)<0, a contradiction. Therefore S is in V.

If t is in V, the C[S,t] chart already contains T. If nu(t)<0, put z=1/t.
Use the EXACT identity, not merely the leading form,

    Abar=p*(1+z*d(S)+z^2*v(S))+k(S)-d(S)-z*v(S)+u*v(S).

Since S is in V and z has positive valuation, the coefficient of p is a unit
and every other displayed term is in V. Thus p is in V. The C[p,z] chart
then contains T. In all cases T is contained in V. This includes the missing
z=0 chart and arbitrary finite target specializations; it is not only a count
of poles on the generic A-fibre.

## 3. Identification with the full normalization

Delta is nonzero, so A,B are algebraically independent and L/C(A,B) is finite.
Let N be the integral closure of C[A,B] in L. Use the standard finite-normalization
theorem for finitely generated C-algebras: N is a finite normal noetherian domain.
For every height-one prime q of N, N_q is a DVR containing C[A,B]. Section 2
gives T subset N_q, hence T subset intersection_(ht q=1) N_q=N. Conversely N
is contained in T because T is normal, has fraction field L, and contains A,B.
Therefore T=N. In particular Y -> A2_(A,B) is finite, on ALL target fibres.

This proof does not assume properness of Y over the target, uniqueness of a
dicritical, finiteness of the reconstructed plane map, or a genus ceiling.

## 4. Exact complement of the reconstructed plane

In the p,z chart write g=p^2+ell*p-u-z. Then g=-z*Delta on the overlap.
The divisor D0={g=0} is the entire graph z=p^2+ell*p-u, hence a smooth A1.
In the S,t chart it is {Delta=0}; this locus never meets t=0, so no further
component or isolated omitted point is hidden there.

On Y minus D0 set

    V=1/g, U=g^3*p+g^2                         (p,z chart),
    W=1-u*t+S*t^2,
    K=u^2-S-u*S*t+ell*W^2+t*W^3,
    V=-t/Delta, U=Delta^2*K                    (S,t chart).

These agree because 1-Delta*W=t^2*K and Pi=t*W. All expressions are regular
on the stated complement. Conversely, from arbitrary (U,V) in A2 put

    p=U*V^3-V, H=p^2+ell*p-u, J=1-V*H.

On V!=0 use z=H-1/V and the p,z chart. On J!=0 use t=-V/J and

    S=-U+p*H^3+(2-3*U*V^2)*H^2+3*U*V*H+u*H
      +(1-U*V^2)*(p+ell).

These two opens cover A2 because J=1-V*H. The S formula is a polynomial,
obtained by expanding p*(H-1/V)^3-(H-1/V)^2+u*(H-1/V).
On J!=0, Delta=1/J; on V!=0, g=1/V. Thus both charts land in Y minus D0.
The maps are inverses on their common dense open and hence everywhere on
their domains. At V=0 the inverse gives t=0 and S=u^2+ell-U: the entire
source line is present. Therefore Y minus D0 is exactly A2_(U,V).

ROOT's initial scratch expansion omitted -U*V^2*(p+ell) in S. The displayed
formula above includes it; no erroneous scratch formula was banked as a result.
The literal reconstruction p=U*V^3-V, g=1/V is the same plane-source interface
as the complete compact source. No additional source coverage is asserted.

Finally dAbar wedge dBbar=g*dp wedge dz, since det d(S,t)/d(p,z)=-z.
Thus the reduced ramification support of this finite map is precisely D0,
with generic ramification index two in characteristic zero. No tangential
degree-one assertion is needed or independently proved here. Neither D0's
image singularities nor its local escaping degrees are thereby controlled.
