# Independent probability estimates — 2026-08-21

These are rounded epistemic probabilities based on the repository snapshot and my own judgment. They are not frequencies, confidence levels, or averages of other forecasts. I take **JC2** to mean the two-dimensional Jacobian conjecture over characteristic zero. Unless stated otherwise, “false” means mathematically false, not merely unproved or incompletely documented.

## 1. FC3 or another load-bearing dependency is false

**Estimate: 65%.** I interpret FC3 as the intended universal closure claim behind the td-11 refile: every genuine Q+E5/E5F realization is represented by a finite, completeness-preserving typed refile and is eliminated by a named instrument. “Another load-bearing dependency” means a mathematical claim actually consumed by the current promoted record—such as FC1-R, the corrected Sigray trust subset, GGV22 Proposition 4.3, or exact certificate provenance—not an openly conjectural avenue such as TD-BOUND/G5, a known retired claim, or the mere absence of a proof. I put FC3 alone at about 45%. It remains explicitly UNKNOWN and “the wall”; its cap-free compiler still needs several completeness and surjectivity obligations. The prior refile/census breakages, the td-11 expansion from 159 to 411 rows after an omitted realized chart, the refutation of the proposed beyond-core bound, and the stalled FC1 exploration make a substantial miss probability rational. The wider union risk is raised by the Sigray audit’s nine new errata and seven gaps among 72 audited items, incomplete auditing of later consumed sections, and the pinned unrefereed GGV22 dependency. Against this, the narrow promoted claims are carefully scoped and often have exact replay, independent cross-checks, and hostile review, so I would not put the union near certainty. See `TOWER-TD11.md` §18, `xmodel/sol-td11-13-scope.md`, `SIGRAY-AUDIT.md`, and `REDUCTION.md`.

**Main sensitivity.** The largest downward mover would be an independently implemented, typed, cap-free FC3 compiler together with a proof that every admissible raw route maps to its canonical record, followed by a line-by-line validation of the exact Sigray subset actually used; a clean result would lower this estimate to roughly 25–35%. One coefficient-realized E5F route outside the quotient, or one false claim in the consumed trust set, would make the union event essentially certain. A capped green census without a completeness proof would move it little.

## 2. JC2 is true

**Estimate: 70%.** This is an unconditional probability of mathematical truth, not a probability that this campaign proves JC2. The plane case has survived a long search, exhibits rigidity absent in higher dimensions, and the repository contains strong exact exclusions such as the independently replayed `(72,108)` computation. Those facts deserve a real but modest update toward truth. They do not justify proof-like confidence: the repository also records the failure of the Jacobian conjecture in every dimension above two, while its own end-to-end plane reduction lacks GGV-to-sheet landing, omits sectors, and has no absolute topological-degree bound. Residue A surviving D23/D25 is weak evidence against JC2, but only weak: D25 is a finite-window modular, chart-local nonemptiness result and supplies neither an inverse-limit point nor a characteristic-zero formal germ, algebraic branch, global Keller map, or polynomial counterexample. My plausible range is roughly 55–82%, with 70% reflecting the special status of dimension two without treating longevity or failed searches as decisive evidence. See `CROSSCHECK.md`, `RECON.md`, `REDUCTION.md`, and `SHEET6-DIRECTIONB.md`.

**Main sensitivity.** The strongest credible downward evidence short of an explicit counterexample would be an independently reproduced, characteristic-zero-compatible all-depth residue-A germ together with a plausible algebraization and Keller-pair bridge. The strongest upward evidence would be a valid global counterexample-to-sheet landing plus exhaustive all-sector closure, or a different general plane-rigidity theorem. Additional finite-depth mod-p survival by itself would move me only a few points.

## 3. The campaign’s current avenues lead to a proof or counterexample

**Estimate: 5% by 2031-08-21 (a five-year horizon).** I count success only as an expert-checkable characteristic-zero proof of JC2 or an explicit characteristic-zero polynomial Keller counterexample. “Current avenues” means the named programs in this snapshot and their direct technical descendants—sheet-book/FC closure, the D43/D75/H29 residue-A experiments, PCC/WTC/log-ramification, Euler-characteristic/nonproperness, DC2, and the off-axis/formal-construction work—pursued at roughly comparable intensity; I exclude a conceptually unrelated future breakthrough merely adopted by the campaign. The positive case is genuine: repeated computational cliffs have yielded small exact certificates, D25 gives 576 disjoint affine 14-cells at each tested prime, and the radical-fiber symmetry is proved. But D25 survived rather than died, no germ was certified in the sampled cells, D23 and D25 used the same depth window, and D43 is only the first new operator measurement while still consuming conjectural x-side and registry inputs. The proof routes lack a complete global landing or absolute degree bound; the counterexample route must still cross modular-to-formal, characteristic-zero, algebraization, globalization, and polynomiality gaps. Calibrationally, I assign about 6% campaign success conditional on JC2 being true and about 1% conditional on it being false; using the 70/30 truth split gives 4.5%, rounded to 5%.

**Main sensitivity.** A single global bridge would dominate the forecast: either theorem-level exhaustive landing with an absolute type/degree bound, or a B-unfrozen characteristic-zero all-depth germ/kill result with a credible algebraization/globalization path. An independently reviewed bridge of that strength would move the five-year estimate into roughly the 15–30% range. More finite-depth modular survival or timeouts without an all-depth theorem would push it below 2% rather than upward.

## 4. A JC2 solution exists that does not require the residue-A cell tower

**Estimate: 90%.** I interpret “solution exists” in a horizon-free, proof-object sense: there is a finite, independently checkable resolution in ordinary characteristic-zero mathematics—a proof if JC2 is true or an explicit polynomial counterexample if it is false—whose indispensable dependency graph does **not** include exhaustive construction, termination, or a verdict for the particular residue-A, B-frozen, no-log, PIN42, `W1W2 != 0` cell tower at unbounded depth. The solution may reuse general lemmas discovered while studying that tower; its verification may not require the tower verdict. This is not the probability that the campaign finds such a solution soon. Residue A is a narrow modular chart, and no theorem in the repository makes it the universal endpoint of every counterexample or forces global approaches through it. The repository itself contains logically different candidates—global ramification/concentration, Euler-characteristic and nonproperness arguments, and DC2. Conditional on JC2 being true, I put about 86% on some finite non-tower proof existing; conditional on JC2 being false, I put about 99% on a directly verifiable counterexample not requiring the discovery tower. With the 70/30 truth split, that is approximately 90% unconditionally.

**Main sensitivity.** The main downward mover would be a universal reduction proving that every counterexample canonically lands in residue A, together with evidence that all viable global proofs also factor through the same all-depth obstruction; that could lower the estimate sharply. A clean global-invariant or DC2 proof, or any explicit counterexample outside the scoped chart, would raise it toward 99%.

The 5% in §3 and the 90% in §4 are not in tension: §3 forecasts discovery by this campaign within five years, whereas §4 concerns the horizon-free existence of an alternative proof object.
