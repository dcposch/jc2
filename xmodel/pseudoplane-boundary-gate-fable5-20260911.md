## A. The U≠0 chart, its inverse, and the boundary criterion

On Spec R the relation reads U²=A(1+AZ). If U≠0 then A≠0 and 1+AZ≠0. Put t=AZ, u=U. In R[U⁻¹] both A and 1+t are units because A·(1+t)·U⁻²=1, so A=u²/(1+t) and Z=t/A=t(1+t)/u². Conversely, for any (u,t) with u≠0, t≠−1 the triple A=u²/(1+t), U=u, Z=t(1+t)/u² satisfies AZ=t and U²−A−A²Z=u²−u²/(1+t)−u²t/(1+t)=0. So the map C[u,u⁻¹,t,(1+t)⁻¹]→R[U⁻¹], u↦U, t↦AZ, is surjective; it is injective because Frac(R)=C(U,AZ) has transcendence degree 2, so U and AZ are algebraically independent. Hence the open set U≠0 IS Spec C[u,u⁻¹,t,(1+t)⁻¹]: t=0 is allowed (points (a,±√a,0), a≠0), t=−1 is excluded exactly, and t is not inverted. The surface is smooth everywhere (the gradient (−1−2AZ,2U,−A²) vanishes only if U=A=0, where its first entry is −1), so on this open set dH=H_u du+H_t dt and "critical" means H_u=H_t=0.

Boundary: at (0,0,z) the relation has differential −dA, so the surface cotangent space is C dU⊕C dZ, and for H=P+UQ, dH=Q(0,z)dU+P_Z(0,z)dZ; also H|L=P(0,Z). So H is submersive along L iff Q(0,z),P_Z(0,z) have no common zero, and H|L is affine iff P_Z(0,z) is constant. At o the accepted weights 1,−2 of U,Z mean a homogeneous H of weight w has dH(o)=0 unless w∈{1,−2}; a weight-0 component q(AZ) has d(AZ)=Z dA+A dZ≡0 at o. All uses in B–E are consistent with this. Attack tried: does the chart miss critical points at U=0? Irrelevant, since every critical point constructed has u≠0 and lies in the chart; conversely the positive control E treats U=0 separately.

## B. Two separated weights: H=U f(t)+Z^d g(t), d≥1, f,g≠0

Chart form: Z^d=t^d(1+t)^d u^(−2d), so H=u f(t)+u^(−2d)h(t), h=t^d(1+t)^d g(t). Exact derivatives: H_u=f−2d u^(−2d−1)h and H_t=u f'+u^(−2d)h'. Where f(t)h(t)≠0: H_u=0 ⟺ u^(2d+1)=2dh/f; then u^(2d)H_t=u^(2d+1)f'+h'=(2dhf'+fh')/f=K/f with K=2dhf'+fh'. Conversely those two equations give H_u=f−f=0 and H_t=u^(−2d)K/f=0. So the critical equations are exactly u^(2d+1)=2dh/f, K=0, as displayed.

M=hf^(2d)=t^d(1+t)^d g f^(2d) is nonzero, has distinct roots 0 and −1 (d≥1), degree N≥2d≥2 and r≥2 distinct roots. M'=h'f^(2d)+2dhf^(2d−1)f'=f^(2d−1)K, an identity. Multiplicity count: at a root of M of multiplicity e, M' has multiplicity exactly e−1 (characteristic zero), so M' has N−r zeros on M=0 with multiplicity while deg M'=N−1; the remaining r−1≥1 zeros of M' are off M=0. Take one, t0. Then f(t0)h(t0)≠0, so t0∉{0,−1} and t0 is in the chart; K(t0)=M'(t0)/f(t0)^(2d−1)=0; 2dh(t0)/f(t0)≠0 has a nonzero (2d+1)st root u. The point (u,t0) satisfies H_u=H_t=0: an actual critical point of H on the surface. Critical value: u^(−2d)h=u·h/u^(2d+1)=uf/(2d), so H(u,t0)=(2d+1)u f(t0)/(2d)≠0; it is nonzero but plays no role. ALL such roots t0 lift (any of the 2d+1 choices of u). No squarefreeness, simple-root or f(0)g(0)≠0 hypothesis is used. Negative control on the mechanism: at d=0 the same steps break exactly where they should, since the right side 2dh/f becomes 0 and forces u=0 outside the chart; d=0, f=1 is the submersive family of E. So d≥1 is load-bearing, not decorative.

## C. Corollary: constant plus at most two nonconstant homogeneous components

Weight spaces from the normal form: A^iU^εZ^j has weight 2(i−j)+ε. Hence R_(2m)=A^m C[t] (m≥0) or Z^(−m)C[t] (m<0), and R_(2m+1)=U·A^m C[t] or U·Z^(−m)C[t]. Every even-weight space is U-free; every odd one is a U-multiple. Therefore H|L=P(0,Z)=Σ_j g_j(0)Z^j, where Z^j g_j(t) is the weight −2j component; boundary degree d≥2 forces a component Z^d g(t) with g(0)≠0 of weight −2d≤−4∉{1,−2}. Since o∈Spec R and H is submersive at o, the other nonconstant component must have weight 1 or −2 (a weight-0 or any other component gives dH(o)=0; the constant has no differential). Weight 1: H=c+Uf(t)+Z^d g(t) with f,g≠0, critical by B. Weight −2: H=c+Zf(t)+Z^d g(t) is U-free, so Q≡0 and dH|_(0,0,z)=P_Z(0,z)dZ with P_Z(0,Z)=f(0)+dg(0)Z^(d−1) of degree d−1≥1, which has a complex root z0; H is critical at (0,0,z0)∈L. Both cases contradict submersion, so d≤1: H|L is affine, degree ≤1 including constant. One nonconstant component is the same argument (it must be Uf or Zf, with boundary 0 or f(0)Z). No finite-degree cap enters: d, deg f, deg g are arbitrary.

## D. Three weights: H=U+Z^d+q(AZ), every d≥1, every q

Chart form: H=u+u^(−2d)h(t)+q(t) with s=t(t+1), h=s^d, N=2d+1. Exact derivatives H_u=1−2d u^(−N)h, H_t=u^(−2d)h'+q'. Critical ⟺ u^N=2dh and h'+u^(2d)q'=0 (u≠0 in the chart). If q'≡0: t=−1/2 gives s=−1/4, h≠0, h'=d s^(d−1)(2t+1)=0; any u with u^N=2dh≠0 is critical. Hand check at d=1, q=0 in surface coordinates: A=2u², U=u, Z=−1/(4u²), u³=−1/2; relation 2u²−u²=u² holds; there 1+2AZ=0, so the cotangent relation is 2u dU=4u⁴dZ, i.e. dZ=dU/(2u³)=−dU, and dH=dU+dZ=0. If q'≠0: N is odd, so raising u^(2d)q'=−h' to the N-th power and substituting u^(2dN)=(u^N)^(2d)=(2dh)^(2d) gives the necessary equation E=(h')^N+(2dh)^(2d)(q')^N=0. Factorization recomputed: (h')^N=d^N s^((d−1)N)(2t+1)^N and (2dh)^(2d)=2^(2d)d^(2d)s^(2d²); since 2d²−(d−1)(2d+1)=d+1 and N−2d=1, E=d^(2d)·s^((d−1)N)·R exactly, R=d(2t+1)^N+2^(2d)s^(d+1)(q')^N. The second term has degree 2d+2+N·deg q'>N=2d+1 and leading coefficient 2^(2d)lc(q')^N≠0 (s monic), so R is nonconstant; R(0)=d and R(−1)=−d because N is odd. Hence R has a root t0∉{0,−1}: s(t0)≠0, h(t0)≠0, t0 in the chart, E(t0)=0, and no discarded s-factor supplies the root. Lifts: if q'(t0)=0 then d(2t0+1)^N=0, so h'(t0)=0 and u^N=2dh(t0)≠0 gives a critical point. If q'(t0)≠0 then (h')^N=−(2dh)^(2d)(q')^N≠0 so h'(t0)≠0; u=−2dhq'/h' is nonzero, u^(2d)=(2dh)^(2d)(q')^(2d)/(h')^(2d)=−(h')^(N−2d)/(q')^(N−2d)=−h'/q', and u^N=u^(2d)·u=2dh. Both equations hold; every root of R lifts, the exceptional ones included; every division is in a proved nonzero case. CONFIRMED.

## E. Positive control: H=U+q(AZ) is everywhere submersive

U≠0: H=u+q(t), H_u=1. U=0, A≠0 (there 1+AZ=0): {U,A}=−2A², {AZ,A}=A{Z,A}=−4AU, so {H,A}=−2A²−4AUq'(AZ)=−2A²≠0; the bracket is a biderivation, so dH_p=0 would force {H,A}(p)=0. The three sets partition the surface; no U=0 or A=0 point is omitted. H|L=q(0) is constant, affine in the degree≤1 sense; U itself (q=0) shows that "nonconstant degree 1" would be false wording. Deleting Z^d from D therefore genuinely flips a universally critical family into a universally submersive one; this is the meaningful negative control for the "critical" verdicts, alongside the d=0 breakdown recorded in B.
