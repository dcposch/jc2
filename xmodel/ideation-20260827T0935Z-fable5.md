**Claim (desk proof, to be independently reviewed).**  Let
`S' = T^{-1}S` with `T = {u(rho): u(0) != 0}`.  Then

```text
K+(rho)=(1)
  <=>  a1 in sqrt(J*S')
  <=>  [ a1 in sqrt(J*Q(rho)[X]) ]  AND  [ a1 in sqrt(J0) ],    J0=J|_(rho=0)
  <=>  J|_(a1=1) = (1) over Q(rho)   AND   J0|_(a1=1) = (1) over Q.
```

Proof sketch.  (i) `K+(rho)=(1) <=> exists N, u(rho), u(0)!=0` with
`u*a1^N in J` is the reviewed weight-zero-projection argument (the
multiplier's weight-0 part lies in `Q[rho]` because `rho` is the only
weight-zero variable); this is exactly `a1 in sqrt(J*S')`, since clearing
the `Q[rho]`-denominators of a radical membership over `S'` produces such a
`u`.  (ii) Primes of `S'` are the primes `P` of `S` with
`P cap Q[rho] in {(0),(rho)}`; the `(rho)` case gives the special-fibre
radical condition, the `(0)` case gives the generic-fibre condition (if
`u*a1 in P` with `u in Q[rho]\{0}` and `P cap Q[rho]=(0)` then `a1 in P`).
(iii) The dehomogenization equivalences use the weight `G_m`-action (weight
of `a1` is 5; over an algebraically closed field any point with `a1 != 0`
scales onto the `a1=1` slice) plus the Nullstellensatz over `Q(rho)` and
`Q`.  Toy check (hand-verified): `J=(x(x-rho*y))`, weights `x,y = 1`:
special fibre passes (`x in sqrt(x^2)`), generic fibre fails (survivor point
