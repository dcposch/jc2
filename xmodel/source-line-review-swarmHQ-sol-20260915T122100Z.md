hence has codimension two; it is not contained in the empty divisor of `1`.
The total base locus is empty because `1` is a section. These are the lemma's
actual hypotheses. Thus a general member, the inverse image of a general affine
line, is geometrically irreducible. Etaleness over that line makes it smooth and
therefore geometrically integral. No projective embedding or pencil Bertini is used.

`D(h)` over `kbar` lies inside the Bertini-good open. Expand the finitely many
coefficients of nonzero `h` in a `C`-linearly independent basis, obtaining
`h=sum beta_j h_j(A,B)`. Some `h_j` is nonzero, and on `D(h_j)(C)` linear
independence prevents `h` from vanishing. This produces a nonempty complex open

closed complement has no curve component, since a polynomial vanishing on such a
