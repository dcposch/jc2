and stop there. The sharpening: at a U1 merge the incoming chains determine

chart, and Theorem E / the Grok solve demand exactly

At td=8 (`r=2`): `(c_2/c_1)^{4+3t} = -1`. The incoming `(21,15)` chains are
fixed (they do not vary with `t`), so the chain side forces a ratio
`zeta = c_2/c_1` that is one algebraic number, and the whole infinite family
survives its first coefficient consumer iff `zeta` is a root of unity whose
order meets the progression `nu_G = 4+3t`. Three sharply different outcomes:
non-torsion ratio (kills all but finitely many `t` — the "infinite family"
collapses at the coefficient tier); matching torsion (families survive with
exact coefficient data — the strongest germ seeds); or independent per-chain
gauges (gate vacuous; the wall moves to deck/monodromy decorations and is
precisely named). The promoted td=6 control already shows the mechanism's
teeth: OBSTRUCTION O forces the non-torsion ratio `2±sqrt(3)` there.

and the *reduction to one torsion test plus one gauge-typing question* is
