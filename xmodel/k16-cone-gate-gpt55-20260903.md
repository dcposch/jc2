## Lemma CONE

Let

```text
A_t = Q[y]/(H_t),    H_t=12q^2y^2-12q(t+1)y+(t+1)(3t+2),    q=2t+1,
d=2qy-(t+1),         so A_t = Q[d]/(3d^2-(t+1)).
```

A geometric point of `Spec A_t` means a morphism `Spec K -> Spec A_t`, where
`K` is algebraically closed; equivalently, choose a root of `H_t` in `K`.  At
split indices `t=3s^2-1`, the algebra over `Q` is `Q x Q`, with factors
`d=+s` and `d=-s`, i.e.

```text
y=(t+1+s)/(2q),      y=(t+1-s)/(2q).
```

At `t=2`, these are `y=2/5` and `y=1/5`.  Statements over a split index must be
checked factorwise; inverting a nonzero zero divisor in the product algebra is
not allowed.

On any geometric fibre, write

```text
I_{t,+}=<T_{t,1},...,T_{t,2t-1}>,      T_{t,0}=c+tau_t,
```

where `c` is the image of the banked unit and `tau_t` is homogeneous of weight
`4t+1`.  Since the positive rows are homogeneous for weights

```text
wt(b4)=1, wt(q_{i,0})=i, wt(b3)=t+1,
```

`Z_+=V(I_{t,+})` is stable under
`lambda.(b4,q_i,b3)=(lambda b4,lambda^i q_i,lambda^(t+1)b3)`.  If `tau_t`
vanishes on `Z_+`, then `T_{t,0}=c` on `Z_+`, and `c != 0` on the fibre, so the
full terminal zero set is empty.  Conversely, if some `x in Z_+` has
`tau_t(x) != 0`, algebraic closedness gives `lambda^(4t+1)=-c/tau_t(x)`, hence
`lambda.x in Z_+` and `T_{t,0}(lambda.x)=0`.  Nullstellensatz gives the
fiberwise equivalence

```text
<I_{t,+},c+tau_t>=[1]  iff  tau_t in sqrt(I_{t,+}).
```

If `dim I_{t,+}=0` as a weighted-homogeneous ideal on the fibre, then the cone
`Z_+` is only the origin: any nonzero point would carry a positive-dimensional
`G_m` orbit.  Since `tau_t` has positive weight, `tau_t(0)=0`, so `dim=0`
suffices for (8.1).

Verdict: **CONFIRMED**, fibrewise for every integer `t>=2`, with the split-index
qualification above.

## Proposition SUBCHART

Let `R` be a subset of the residual variables
`{b4,q_{2,0},...,q_{t-1,0},b3}` and let `S` set the complement to zero.  In the
affine solve each eliminated variable `z_w` has the form

```text
z_w = -rho_w / p_w,
```

where `p_w` is a weight-zero unit of `A_t` and `rho_w` is homogeneous of weight
`w` in the residual variables, after earlier substitutions by homogeneous
solved values.  On `S`, every monomial involving a killed residual variable
vanishes.  What remains is the span of monomials in `R` whose weights sum to
`w`.  Therefore `z_w|_S=0` if `w` is outside the numerical semigroup generated
by the surviving weights; if there is a unique monomial of weight `w`, the
restriction is a single `A_t` scalar times that monomial.

This is a grading plus affine-elimination statement.  It does not by itself
assert radical membership in the original multivariable ideal.

Verdict: **CONFIRMED**, with scope limited to coordinate subcharts after the
weighted affine solve.
