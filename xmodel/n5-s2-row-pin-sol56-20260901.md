## 2. Promoted S2 cage and exact row calculus

The promoted necessary cage is an irreducible polynomial curve $D$ with
normalization $\mathbb A^1$, one place at infinity, immersive normalization,
and only two-smooth-branch affine singularities.  In degree-minimal target
coordinates it has

\[
 (p(t),q(t)),\qquad \deg p=d>n=\deg q\ge1,qquad g=\gcd(d,n)\ge2,
\]

and no target automorphism may lead to a coprime pair or the killed `(6,4)`
pair.  There is a surjection of the affine complement group onto $S_5$:
generic meridians are transpositions generating $S_5$, and the two branch
meridians at every affine double point are disjoint transpositions
(`K:104-121,539-559`; `R:389-418`).  Their edge graph needs at least four
edges, so $d\ge4$ (`K:279-287`).  The coprime cycle-order table is not reused
on cables.

For a contact-$k_z$ double point, $\delta_z=k_z$.  Put

\[
 r_1=\#\{k_z=1\},\qquad T=\sum_{k_z\ge2}k_z.
\]

The promoted identities and survivor gate are

\[
 \delta_{\rm aff}=r_1+T,qquad
 \delta_\infty+\delta_{\rm aff}=\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}2,qquad
 M_\infty=\operatorname{mult}_{P_\infty}\bar D+\bar\beta_h-1,
\]
\[
 M_\infty+2T\ge3d-2. \tag{2.1}
\]

Here $P_\infty$ is the physical point and $\bar\beta_h$ a maximal-contact
value of its unique branch; neither is a cover flag or a series label.
Sources are `K:63-80,239-251` and `R:89-93,455-460`.  Thus `(M-INF)` means
$M_\infty\ge3d-2$ for $T=0$, while `(M-INF-T)` is exactly (2.1), with
coefficient $2T$, not $4T$ (`R:410-412`).  Failure means
$M_\infty+2T\le3d-3$, hence cyclic complement by promoted N-A-RES.

**New degree-reduction lemma.**  Declare
$\phi:\mathbb C[X,Y]\to\mathbb C[t]$, $X\mapsto p$, $Y\mapsto q$, in
that generator order.  If $d=kn$, monicity makes
$R=p-q^k$ have degree $<d$.  This is induced by the target automorphism
$\alpha^*(X)=X-Y^k,\alpha^*(Y)=Y$, whose inverse sends $X$ to $X+Y^k$.
If $R$ is zero or constant, then
$\mathbb C(p,q)=\mathbb C(q)\ne\mathbb C(t)$ because $n>1$, contradicting
birationality (`K:224-226`).  If $R$ is nonconstant, the new maximum degree
is $\max(\deg R,n)<d$, contradicting degree-minimality.  Therefore

\[
                         n\nmid d.                                  \tag{2.2}
\]

This proves a minimal-row statement; it does not silently identify every raw
`(8,4)` presentation with the reviewed `(6,4)` family.

For the first residual pairs, $g$ is prime, so an Abhyankar--Moh sequence
must be exactly $\Delta=(d,n,a)$.  With $e=d/g$, Theorem 2.1 gives

\[
 \gcd(g,a)=1,qquad ga\in\langle d,n\rangle,qquad 0<a<en.           \tag{2.3}
\]

The pole semigroup is $\langle d,n,a\rangle$.  Its Apéry representatives
modulo $d$ are $in+ja$, $0\le i<e,\ 0\le j<g$; hence its number of gaps is

\[
 \delta_{\rm aff}=
 \frac{(e-1)n+(g-1)a-d+1}{2}.                                      \tag{2.4}
\]

Indeed the normalization quotient
$\mathbb C[t]/\mathbb C[p,q]$ has one echelon class for every pole-semigroup
gap, while its dimension is the sum of the affine delta invariants.  This does
not confuse that pole semigroup with the local infinity semigroup.

For `(8,6)` and `(9,6)`, $d-n=g\mid d$.  Proposition 2.1's dividing branch
gives $\bar\beta_h=d^2/g-a$.  Since the initial infinity multiplicity is
$d-n$, the promoted cluster identity and genus identity yield

\[
 M_\infty=d-n+\frac{d^2}{g}-a-1,qquad
 \delta_\infty=\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}2-\delta_{\rm aff}.                 \tag{2.5}
\]

Equations (2.3)--(2.5), rather than a floor or an attainment assumption, drive
the census below.

## 6. Audit, limitations, and typed OPEN items

**Promotion typing.**  The charged promotion is unchanged: coprime and `(6,4)`
are dead, while noncoprime S2 is necessary-only.  The new content offered for
review is narrower: (i) the explicit degree-minimal divisibility lemma (2.2),
and (ii) the primary-sourced AM numerical census through $d=9$, with the
cluster and scalar-gate arithmetic checked row by row.  Neither statement
asserts a representative or Keller carrier.  In particular, the raw `(8,4)`
presentations retained by `R:405-416` are not declared killed here; they are
absent only as *degree-minimal labels*, and their route to a terminal minimal
row is not supplied.

`OPEN[N5-S2-ROW-PIN-d>=10]` remains because no promoted degree cap exists.
At composite gcd, a delta sequence may have more than one later generator, so
the prime-gcd three-entry calculation used at $d=8,9$ cannot be extrapolated.
A finite all-degree residual ledger therefore does not follow.

`OPEN[N5-S2-LOWROW-REALIZATION]` remains for every scalar-live row in §5:
the AM conditions are necessary semigroup data, not witnesses that the chosen
embedding is immersive, has no triple fibre, has precisely the selected
node/tangency partition, or carries the required $S_5$ quotient.  Equality
in (2.1) is only saturation of a necessary inequality.

`OPEN[N5-S2-LOWROW-CABLE]` remains for each realized low row: recover the full
approximate-root remainders/multiplicity sequence, write the iterated cable
braid in a based geometric fibre, and decide fixed generating transposition
tuples.  A delta sequence fixes the equisingularity type at infinity, but this
report has not manufactured the projection-specific fold relation barred by
`K:337-373`.
