## Disposition

**Downgrade the raw `21 equations / 23 coefficient functions` proposal as a
duplicate starting point.**  The shared Faber theorem is stronger than its
raw linearization: over a characteristic-zero differential field it exactly
and nonlinearly integrates all eleven high rows, with unique Faber constants,
and reduces the determinant equation to

```text
r_1'=...=r_(m-2)'=0,       m r_(m-1)'=j/u.
```

It already allows unbounded coefficient-`x` degree.  Rebuilding the full
21-by-23 Ore matrix would discard this exact triangular quotient and is not a
new invariant.  The word *Cartier* also belongs to the characteristic-three
carry client, not automatically to the characteristic-zero FT2 proof lane.

Cube V2 supplies the correct live admission stratum.  For B9 one must first
derive `K=h(x)y^3` from a source-valid filtration and require `h` noncubic in
`k(x)` (equivalently `K-T` geometrically integral).  The outer monomial test
`gcd(deg h,3)=1` is only a sufficient control.  My earlier symbolic-total-
degree/core proposal is therefore not a second general landing theorem; it
is at most a monomial/infinity subchart until its filtration is identified
with the actual partial-`y` source.

## Genuinely new remainder

The missing object is a **Faber-reduced lower-Laurent/infinity state**, not a
raw high-row operator.  On the admitted B9 primitive-`h` stratum (`m=9`) it
must retain simultaneously:

1. the exact algebraic expressions for `r_1,...,r_8` after every legal target
   quotient, rather than only the frozen `r_1,r_2` controls;
2. the seven constant equations `r_1'=...=r_7'=0` and the terminal primitive
   equation `9 r_8'=j/u`, with `u^3=h` in the licensed root field;
3. principal parts at every zero/pole of `h` and at `x=infinity`;
4. both charged original Taylor-boundary families
   `u^ell f^(ell)(A/9)/ell!` and
   `u^ell g^(ell)(A/9)/ell!`, including their polynomiality/provenance; and
5. rank-drop/nonreduced strata of the lower map, so a Kuranishi cokernel is
   computed only after the exact Faber quotient.

Neither input supplies that complete state: the shared theorem stops after
two explicitly reconstructed Laurent controls and leaves both Taylor
families charged; cube V2 names the primitive-core admission and proposes
descending bands but does not yet furnish a finite infinity-state theorem.
That is the narrow new contribution worth registering.

## Cheapest source-honest discriminator

Freeze one primitive B9 source cell and compile `r_3` through `r_8` plus the
leading principal parts of both Taylor families directly from the Faber
coordinates.  At roots of `h` and at infinity, test whether the terminal
class `dx/u` and Taylor polynomiality either:

- give an explicit impossible fractional principal part; or
- close on a bounded transition state independent of `deg h`.

Use a nonprimitive `h=g^3` point as the mandatory negative control: the extra
centralizer generator `gy` must make the primitive-state compiler fail
closed.  Use an admitted primitive outer-monomial point only as a positive
orientation control, not as coverage of arbitrary primitive `h`.

**Stop rule.**  Stop the finite-state claim if the minimal principal-part or
Taylor state grows with `deg h`, if a chart transition needs discarded
coefficient history, or if direct original determinant/Taylor rows disagree.
Do not respond by recomputing the eleven solved high rows or by sampling more
degrees.
