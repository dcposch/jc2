## 2. Hostile audit of the consensus clusters

### 2.1 Canonical normalization, different, trace, and `A(F)`

There is one useful exact correction to the submissions. If

```text
A = C[P,Q]  subset  B = C[x,y]  subset  L = Frac(B)
Cbar = integral closure of A in L,
```

then `Cbar subset B`: an element integral over `A` is integral over `B`, and
`B` is integrally closed in `L`. Thus the hostile sentence in Bacon that `B`
and `Cbar` may be incomparable is wrong. For a Keller map, Zariski Main gives
the canonical geometry the cluster wants: `Spec B` is the relevant open part
of the finite normalization `Y=Spec Cbar`. The issue is not existence of a
canonical source. The issue is proving that the missing boundary
`Y \ Spec B` is empty.

That correction strengthens the receiver but weakens the claimed novelty of
several cards. The following distinctions remain load-bearing:

- The different/discriminant belongs to the finite extension `Cbar/A`; trace
  moments also depend on the rational coordinate functions `x,y` and hence on
  the open embedding `Spec B -> Y`. The different cannot encode those
  functions by itself.
- `delta_P,delta_Q` preserve `B`, while their failure to preserve `Cbar` is
  ordinary ramification/derivation-lattice data. A derivation conductor is
  interesting only if it yields a relation involving the open subring `B`
  that is strictly weaker than `B=Cbar`. Equality with a standard different,
  or a sign whose proof assumes `x,y` integral, is `TAUTOLOGY`.
- The height-one discriminant may be effective and supported over the
  asymptotic locus. There is no current reason its global degree is zero; a
  hypothetical counterexample is expected to place positive defect exactly
  on the missing boundary. `J=1` on the open part does not erase that boundary.
- The union of polar supports of sufficiently many coordinate traces is a
  plausible exact receiver for `A(F)`, but it must be rederived. An individual
  moment can cancel, and the required field degree `d` is map-dependent and
  unbounded. `m=2` is a packet discriminator, not a properness theorem.
- Fable's dicritical direct-image picture is compatible with the receiver on
  a resolved actual map. It does not show that the campaign's filed books
  contain every such divisor or determine its coordinate coefficients. That
  is still landing/coverage plus transport.

The decisive local attack is exact. Fix

```text
R = k(q)[[t]],   S = k(q)[[u]],   t = u^e.
```

For any Laurent series `x=z_b(u)` with `x_u != 0`, put

```text
y = q * e*u^(e-1) / (c*x_u).
```

Then `dP wedge dQ = c dx wedge dy` for `(P,Q)=(u^e,q)`. The
extension `S/R`, ramification and different `(e*u^(e-1))` are fixed while the
coordinate moment can vary with `b`. This is a formal local observation, not a
polynomial Keller map, but that is enough to refute any lemma whose hypotheses
are only the completed local algebra, its different, retained contact data,
and the local Jacobian two-form. It forces every surviving proof proposal to
name a genuinely global polynomial-origin input.

The `TRACE-HAM` proposal needs a sharper gate for the same reason. Since
`L/K` is finite, a first-order system closes over `K` after one admits a full
multiplication basis; that is generic finite-extension algebra, not a Keller
separator. Success requires an `A_p`-lattice, defined independently of the
desired integrality, on which the two extended derivations have controlled
singularities. If the proposed closure uses the full multiplication algebra
with unknown polar coefficients, or its regular lattice is `Cbar` plus the
assumption `x,y in Cbar`, stop: it is TRACE-REG in differential notation.

Therefore:

- **continue** the normalization as a canonical receiver and explicit-map
  instrument;
- **stop** local different/conductor positivity after the fixed-different,
  varying-moment control;
- **hold** theorem-scale signed intersection or trace-connection work until
  one explicit covariant global identity is written before computation; and
- **merge**, rather than create, Fable's proposed row 47 into avenues 7 and
  31. It is a useful input/output contract, not yet a new theorem mechanism.

### 2.2 D finite shift, involutivity, and gauge

`D-TRANSFER`, `SHIFT-ORE`, and the Spencer/involutivity card share one premise:
the D windows are truncations of a single stationary source system. D1 proves
one typed `X27 -> X25` transition only. Periodic exponent residues do not imply
bounded state: new coordinate types, nonlinear coefficients, base strata, or
inhomogeneous corrections may appear without bound.

The legal staged synthesis is:

1. **Source stationarity.** Derive, from the unreduced Euler product rule, a
   symbolic relabeling of consecutive six-band blocks and a commuting square
   with the registered projections. Banked rows may falsify this formula but
   may not be used to fit it.
2. **First compatibility class.** Only on a reviewed coherent D2 base, reduce
   the first source-defined inhomogeneity modulo the exact left-syzygy module
   after the registered localization/saturation. `UNIT-OBSTRUCTION` kills the
   cell; `ZERO-FIRST-CLASS` does not yet mean involutive.
3. **All-order theorem.** Only after stages 1--2, prove finite generation or
   Spencer acyclicity/constant-rank hypotheses. Then, and only then, compute an
   Ore/Fitting/annihilator object. A free summand remains a modular formal-tail
   signal, not characteristic zero, a germ, or a map.

Stop the shift card on the first source-row mismatch or unbounded new state.
Stop the involutivity card after the first nonzero obstruction; do not replace
failure with band 28 or another fixed depth. A zero first obstruction is review
debt, not permission to say `INVOLUTIVE`.

Grok's Aut/gauge card, as written, should not consume a root. The promoted
torsor and cyclic permutations are finite/discrete at the two good primes and
contribute no infinitesimal tangent directions. Residual scalings may be
continuous, but they first must preserve the normalization, the residue-A
cell, and the source recurrence. Elementary shears generally change degree,
support, or chart and cannot be projected onto the ten first-occurrence
coordinates by choice. Require an exact algebraic-group action and a commuting
source diagram before differentiating it. Failure is `NO-GAUGE-ACTION`, not an
`EXTRA-FIBER` discovery. If such an action is later proved, its tangent-image
intersection with the banked kernel is a cheap micro-test inside the D root.

The P4P1 issue is orthogonal correctness debt, not a mathematical descendant.
Before any D43 integral or source-reduced wording re-enters the queue, compare
the checkpoint row and assembled row with the correction isolated. Record
whether it is zero, origin-only, or load-bearing. Until then, say only
“assembled modular vanishing with a band-42 correction outside the audited
source-to-NF trace,” never “all 218 rows are source-reduced.” This audit does
not justify D43 work and need not run while D2 owns the D root.

4. Populate the canonical receiver from the native GGV packet. Emit a typed

5. Run the automorphism/nonproper controls only to test covariance and pole

## 6. Failure modes this portfolio is designed to prevent

1. **Normalization laundering.** `Cbar` is canonical and finite; the desired
   global sign and surjectivity are not. Never promote the noun as the theorem.
2. **Receiver/source laundering.** A target-local schema does not prove that a
   GGV packet maps to it. Missing fields remain type errors.
3. **Formal-to-actual contamination.** The Darboux pair and P2 completions are
   route-killing formal controls, never Keller counterexamples.
4. **Stationarity laundering.** Period-six labels and two successful blocks do
   not prove finite state. Positive samples require a source formula and
   structural induction.
5. **Involutivity inflation.** One zero obstruction is not formal
   integrability; one free modular module is not characteristic zero.
6. **Branch explosion.** The receiver stops on the first missing arrow or
   collision; D stops before new depth; Witt stops at one fixed cap.
7. **Anchoring/correlation.** P1/P2 and D1 are common inputs to several blind
   reports, not independent confirmations. W preserves one orthogonal bet.
8. **Review debt.** Every positive result replaces its producer root with a
   reviewer. No positive result has a descendant in this portfolio.
9. **Coverage amnesia.** No local receiver, D cell, primitive-group range, or
   Witt cap repairs complete landing, a cofinal type ceiling, characteristic
   zero, or algebraization unless that separate edge is explicitly proved.
