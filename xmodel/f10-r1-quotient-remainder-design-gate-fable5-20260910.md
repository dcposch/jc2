## A. Squarefree splitting tree, inverse test, CRT, idempotents, degree conservation

Own check. P monic squarefree of degree 7 (17m premise); every P_a divides P, so every leaf modulus is squarefree and A_a=Q[v]/(P_a) is reduced (char 0, squarefree, hence a finite product of number fields). At a non-ALL-ZERO node the T^d coefficient of the smallest-index degree-d generator is a nonzero element of A_a, so its representative c_a of v-degree < e_a is a nonzero polynomial; g=gcd(P_a,c_a) in Q[v] is therefore never P_a (deg c_a < e_a), so the "inconsistent-state" refusal is correct and never a discarded branch. Inverse test: c_a is a unit mod P_a iff gcd(c_a,P_a)=1 (Bezout u c_a + w P_a = 1 gives the inverse u; conversely u c_a = 1 + w P_a forces any common divisor to divide 1). If 0<deg g<e_a, h=P_a/g and gcd(g,h)=1 because a common prime would square-divide P_a. Binary CRT: from U g + W h = 1, W h is 1 mod g and 0 mod h, U g the converse; e_g=W h, e_h=U g satisfy e_g+e_h=1, e_g e_h = U W g h = 0 mod P_a, e_g^2 = e_g(1-e_h) = e_g. So x -> (x mod g, x mod h) has inverse (x_g,x_h) -> e_g x_g + e_h x_h, both sides exact rational polynomial maps. Final idempotents: with Q_a=P/P_a and J_a Q_a = 1 mod P_a, E_a=J_a Q_a mod P is 1 mod P_a and 0 mod every P_b (P_b | Q_a), hence sum_a E_a = 1 mod P (1 mod every coprime factor of P), E_a E_b = 0 mod P for a != b, and x = sum_a E_a lift(pi_a x) mod P (both sides agree mod every P_a). Each leaf embedding sends 1 to E_a, i.e. is non-unital exactly as the producer states; only the full product recombination is unital. Degree conservation: a genuine split replaces e_a by deg g + deg h = e_a with both parts >= 1, total 7 over all leaves at all times, hence <= 7 leaves, <= 6 splits, and a binary tree with L <= 7 leaves has 2L-1 <= 13 nodes, i.e. <= 13 visits. Each visit does one finite coefficient scan and at most one Euclidean gcd in Q[v] (terminates by strict remainder-degree descent, divisions only by nonzero rationals). Maximal-degree rule: on a REGULAR leaf c_a is a unit of A_a, so it is nonzero on every field factor and the selected generator has degree exactly d there, while all other reduced f_i have degree <= d on A_a hence on every factor; this is the one rule submitted and it is what section 5's bound 2d_a-1 needs. Own extra observation, not a defect: on the child h the same generator is automatically REGULAR, because gcd(c_a,h) | gcd(c_a,P_a)=g and gcd(g,h)=1; on the child g it vanishes and the fresh scan is genuinely required, which the producer already mandates. No field algebra is assumed, no factor of P is selected, no leaf is abandoned, no generic inverse is invented. Verdict: CONFIRMED.

## C. Monic remainder algebra C_a, small columns span the ideal, <=35 x 280

Own check. fhat_a = c_a^{-1} f_{i_a} is monic of degree d_a in A_a[T] (c_a a unit by A). Over any commutative ring, division by a monic polynomial exists (cancel the top term with coefficient 1, induct on degree) and the remainder of degree < d_a is unique: if fhat_a Q1 + r1 = fhat_a Q2 + r2 with Q1 != Q2 then fhat_a (Q1-Q2) has leading coefficient 1 * lc(Q1-Q2) != 0 and degree >= d_a, while r2-r1 has degree < d_a, contradiction. So C_a = A_a[T]/(fhat_a) is free over A_a on 1,T,...,T^{d_a-1} and free over Q on v^l T^j (l<e_a, j<d_a), dimension e_a d_a, with no reducedness of C_a used (T^2 gives a nonreduced C_a and the argument is unchanged). The kernel of R_a is (fhat_a) = (f_{i_a}) since c_a is a unit, and R_a(f_{i_a}) = 0. Span: R_a is a ring homomorphism, so for m in C_a with expansion m = sum r_{l,j} v^l T^j, m R_a(f_i) = sum r_{l,j} R_a(v^l T^j pi_a(f_i)); hence the Q-span of the 8 e_a d_a small columns is exactly the C_a-ideal generated by the eight remaining remainders, not a "constants times rows" subspace, and multiplication is taken in the possibly nonreduced C_a. Consequently b_a in I_a iff R_a(b_a) in W_a: forward is immediate, backward because b_a - sum m_i f_i lies in ker R_a = (f_{i_a}). Size: rows e_a d_a, columns 8 e_a d_a per leaf; summing over positive-degree leaves, r = sum e_a d_a <= 5 * sum e_a <= 35 and columns 8r <= 280, target r entries; target and bookkeeping are outside the bound, and no rank, density, height, native-phase or speed claim is made or implied, as the producer says. Verdict: CONFIRMED.
