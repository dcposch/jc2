## 2. Affine supports, 1,362-monomial cubic bound — CONFIRMED as a bound; measurements GAP

At λ₃=1 every lift row is Q-linear in the member's coefficients with constant integer multinomial scalars; the only k-dependent inputs are A(2,1)=k, B(8,5)=5k/3, B(1,0)=5k²/9 (consumed 14c gate, thirteen fixed nonzero slots). A Jacobian row is bilinear with rational differentiation scalars, so its support lies in the product set, which I enumerated (C5): 1,180 `a_i b_j` + 60 `{1,k,k²}a_i` + 118 `{1,k}b_j` + 4 `{1,k,k²,k³}` = **1,362**, maximal degree 3, and no `k²b_j` monomial. Denominators: A maps integral (integral inverses, constants 1, κ³=1, k); B maps carry lcm(3,9)=9 and the target −5k³/9 keeps 9; integral inverses preserve the (1/9)Z lattice.

Scope qualifier, as the producer states: the bound covers the affine Hermite maps and the retained bilinear Jacobian rows only. My mutation replacing one A-map generator by a product `a₀a₁` (a single nonlinear elimination step) raises the degree bound to 4 and is rejected at the degree gate; any use of the 92 nonlinear B-Jacobian pivots voids the bound. **GAP (typed, admitted by producer):** coefficient bit length, actual per-row term counts and construction runtime are unmeasured; 1,362 is a support ceiling, not a measurement.
