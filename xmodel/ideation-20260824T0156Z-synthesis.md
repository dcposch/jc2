## 3. Mathematical corrections from cross-pollination

These corrections apply to proposals, not to promoted JC2 claims.

1. If `A=C[P,Q]`, `B=C[x,y]`, `L=Frac(B)`, and `Cbar` is the integral closure
   of `A` in `L`, then `Cbar` is contained in `B`: every element integral over
   `A` is integral over `B`, and `B` is integrally closed. Thus
   `Spec B -> Spec Cbar` is the canonical open immersion supplied by Zariski
   Main. The missing theorem is that its boundary is empty, not that a
   canonical finite receiver exists.
2. A local different or derivation lattice does not determine coordinate
   moments. In the exact formal family `t=u^e`, `x=z_b(u)`,
   `y=q e u^(e-1)/(c x_u)`, the local algebra, ramification, different, and
   Jacobian two-form stay fixed while coordinate traces can vary with `b`.
   Any local different-only proof must survive this control by naming a truly
   global polynomial-origin input.
3. Finite trace closure over the fraction field is generic finite-extension
   algebra. `TRACE-HAM` is informative only if it constructs a non-circular
   regular `A_p`-lattice; assuming `x,y` integral or using the full unknown
   multiplication algebra merely restates TRACE-REG.
4. Period-six source labels do not prove a stationary finite-state tail.
   Source-derived shift equivariance and a commuting projection square must
   precede every Ore/Fitting or involutivity conclusion.
5. The promoted finite torsor and cyclic permutations have zero infinitesimal
   tangent. A D-kernel gauge quotient first needs an exact positive-dimensional
   source-preserving action; otherwise the correct verdict is
   `NO-GAUGE-ACTION`, not `EXTRA-FIBER`.
6. A moment-carrying receiver does not supply its own source functor. Native
   GGV fields must populate every target-divisor tag, completed factor,
   coefficient, and coordinate map. The first unavailable field is the useful
   verdict `NO-TYPED-FUNCTOR`.
7. Two evaluations cannot show that the D43 P4P1 correction vanishes
   identically. That hygiene gate requires symbolic subtraction and exact
   reduction, or a proved degree-bounded interpolation set.
8. Raw resultant denominators give at best a candidate polar-support upper
   bound until saturation, generic-degree control, normalization, local-factor
   handling, and the union-of-trace-poles criterion are proved.
