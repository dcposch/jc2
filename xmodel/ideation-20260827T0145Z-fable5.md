### 3.2 Lemma A (candidate exact micro-theorem): the fibre unit already excludes the off-E part

**Claim.** The Fact implies there exist `N,M ≥ 0` and a polynomial `c` with

```text
cs^N * k^M  -  c * rho  ∈  (rows∘subst, qrs)                    (A)
```

in the substituted presentation. Consequently `rho` is a unit in
`R_{cs·k}/(rows, qrs)`; since localizing at `cs` makes the `cs`-saturation
invisible (`(J : cs^∞)_{cs} = J_{cs}`), this localized ring *is* the honest
localized chart-stratum ring. Hence:

- the `rho=0` fibre of the ordered `T-cs` stratum is contained in
  `V(cs) ∪ V(k)`;
- every DVR arc with `ord(rho)>0` whose blowup lift has closed point in the
  `D(cs*k)` part of the stratum is already excluded — **without waiting for
  V19**.

*Proof sketch.* Substitute `u→1/cs, v→1/k` in the Rabinowitsch identity and
clear denominators to get (A). In `R_{cs·k}/(rows,qrs)` this reads
`1 = (c/(cs^N k^M))·rho`, so `rho` is a unit there; units pass to every
further quotient. ∎ (Elementary; needs different-model review. It does not
contradict the promotion firewall, which correctly refuses the *full-chart*
DVR inference; this is the off-E part only.)

### 3.3 Lemma B (typing criterion): where the multiplicative upgrade can fail, exactly

The staged calculus requires the multiplicative form
`cs^N k^M (1+rho W) ∈ (rows, qrs)` because only that form survives division
by the exceptional power in the saturated chart ring. The precise
relationship:

**Claim.** A typed direct total certificate exists **iff** an additive
certificate (A) exists whose `rho`-cofactor `c` is divisible by `cs^N`
modulo the ideal. (⇐: write `c=cs^N W'`; then `cs^N(k^M - rho W') ∈` ideal;
saturation strips `cs^N`; localize at `k` only. ⇒: expand — the cofactor
`cs^N k^M W` is visibly divisible.)

So the *entire* gap between the promoted fibre theorem and a whole-stratum
closure is `cs^N`-divisibility of the `rho`-cofactor, i.e., an obstruction
that lives **on the exceptional divisor `E = V(cs)` of the chart**. This is
load-bearing: the charged collision arcs meet the center, so their lifts'
closed points lie on `E`; the off-E exclusion of Lemma A is the cheap part,
and `E` is where the theorem is actually needed.
