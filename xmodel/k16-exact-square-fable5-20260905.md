## 4. Lemma DEGREE-FORCED (the fact the lever's premise overlooked)

Let `s_t := max{ w : (S_t/I_{t,+})_w ≠ 0 }` be the socle weight (top weight) of the
graded quotient, finite iff (V0) holds at t (the ideal is homogeneous for positive
weights, so dim 0 ⟺ finite length ⟺ `s_t < ∞`).

**Lemma.**  If (V0) holds at t and `f` is weighted-homogeneous of weight `> s_t`,
then `f ∈ I_{t,+}`.  In particular, if `8t+2 > s_t` then `tau_t^2 ∈ I_{t,+}`, and if
moreover `4t+1 ≤ s_t` the exponent `N_t = min{N : tau^N ∈ I_{t,+}}` can only be 1 or 2.

*Proof.*  `(S_t/I_{t,+})_w = 0` for every `w > s_t` by definition of `s_t`.  ∎

This is trivial, and that is the point: at every index where the exponent 2 has been
"banked" the inequality `8t+2 > s_t` holds (§3, §5), so the banked exponent is a
corollary of (V0) plus a socle bound and carries **no information about a certificate**.
Astra's audit notes already observed this for the weaker membership `b3·tau ∈ I_+`
("degree-forced there: the full quotient has top weights 14 and 21"); the same
remark applies verbatim to `tau^2` (weights 26, 34).  A "uniform certificate"
extracted from t ≤ 8 data would be a certificate that the Macaulay matrix of
`I_{t,+}` in weight 8t+2 has full rank — i.e. a proof of (V0) with a socle bound —
not something weaker than (V0).

## 6. What this does to the lever (17(llllll)) — three separate statements

**(a) "Cofactors supported on the top-tail rows" is FALSE from t=4 on (exact).**
The canonical form of that claim is `tau^2 ∈ J_t^tail`.  It holds at t=3 only because
`wt(tau^2) = 26` exceeds the tail's complete-intersection socle `(2t-1)(t+1) = 20`
(Lemma), and it fails at t=4 exactly (`depth4` job: `J_4^tail` has dim 0, length 572,
socle 35; `tau ∉`, `tau^2 ∉`, `tau^3 ∈` — the last again by degree, 51 > 35).  This
reproduces Astra's banked "tau squared nonzero modulo tail" at t=4 and explains it.
For every t ≥ 4, `8t+2 < (2t-1)(t+1)`, so tail membership of `tau^2` is never
degree-forced again; at t=4 the tail quotient in weight 34 is one-dimensional (the
CI series is palindromic, `[s^34]P_4 = [s^1]P_4 = 1`) and `tau^2` is nonzero in it.
Every lift of `tau^2` computed here (t=2 both fibres, 3, 4, 5, 6) uses ALL 2t−1 rows
(`LIFT_SUPPORT` lines), and the canonical drop-one profile says no single row is
necessary at t=3, 4 (each `<T_i : i ≠ k>` is still zero-dimensional with socle
≤ 16 resp. ≤ 24 < 8t+2).  Cofactor support is therefore not a meaningful invariant
here: it is "everything" by the Lemma whenever the ideal is a fat point below 8t+2.

**(b) The exact-square identity is degree-forced wherever it has been verified, and
the socle law predicts that it STOPS HOLDING at t=9.**  §5 records that the weighted
Hilbert function of `S_t/I_{t,+}` equals the Fröberg-type expected series for the
degree data (2t−1 forms of weights 4t..2t+2 in weights 1..t−1, t+1), truncated at
its first non-positive coefficient, at every index measured (exact t=2, 3, 4, 5;
mod p t=6, 7).  Under that law `s_t = 7, 14, 21, 30, 39, 49, 61, 74, 88, 102, ...`
while `8t+2 = 18, 26, 34, 42, 50, 58, 66, 74, 82, 90, ...`: the square is forced for
t ≤ 8 and, at t=9, `wt(tau^2) = 74 = s_9` with `H(74) = 2648`; for t ≥ 10,
`8t+2 < s_t`.  A weight-(8t+2) form lies in `I_{t,+}` at such t only if it is zero in
a nonzero graded piece, which nothing in the ray's structure suggests for `tau^2`
(every natural quantity tested here of weight ≤ s_t is NOT in `I_{t,+}`: `b_2^2`,
`b4·tau`, `b3 b_1` at weight 14 = s_3; `b_1 R` at weight 21 = s_4; `b_2 R^2`, `b_2^2 R`,
`b_1^2`, `b3·tau`, `R·tau`, `b_2·tau` at weights 37, 38, 38, 32, 37, 38 < s_6 = 39 —
all `IN_IPLUS 0`; and everything of weight > s_t is in).  The generic exponent under
the law is `N_t = ⌊s_t/(4t+1)⌋ + 1`, which is 2 for 3 ≤ t ≤ 8 and 3 for t = 9..14,
growing without bound with `s_t`.  So "tau_t^2 ∈ I_{t,+} for all t ≥ 3" is predicted
FALSE, not merely unproved; the first test index is t=9, weight 74, a 2648-dimensional
graded piece of a length-1,074,496 quotient — outside this lane's budget and outside
any std-based instrument (Astra's t=8 mod-p std timed out at 30 min).

**(c) The only uniform certificate is (B2), and it reduces the square to `b_2^2 R^2`.**
`tau^2 = g^2 b_2^2 R^2 + (g b_2 R − tau)·Σ_k b4^k T_{t,k}` is an exact identity with
closed-form cofactors (§2).  Everything else in the "exact square" is the single
membership `b_2^2 R^2 ∈ I_{t,+}`, weight 8t+2, and for that no identity beyond the
Lemma was found: the b3-multiples reduce by (B1) to `b_1`-expressions
(`b3 b_2^2 R^2 = −y b_1 b_2^2 R`, `b3^2 b_2^2 R^2 = y^2 b_1^2 b_2^2`), which are again
degree-forced or not according to the socle.  I did not find, and do not expect, a
closed form `c_k(t)` with `tau^2 = Σ c_k(t) T_{t,k}`; under (b) none exists for t ≥ 9.
