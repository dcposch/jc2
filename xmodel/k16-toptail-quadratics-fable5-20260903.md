## 5. Elimination: the resultants, the square system, and the top-weight trap

### 5.1 The resultants

For a quadratic `f=a1 b3^2+b1 b3+c1` and `g=a2 b3^2+b2 b3+c2`,
`Res_b3(f,g)=(a1c2-a2c1)^2-(a1b2-a2b1)(b1c2-b2c1)` (the `2x2` Sylvester
identity; checked on `x^2-1, x^2-4 -> 9` and a common-root pair `-> 0`).  With
`f=T_(t,2t-1)`, `lc_b3 f = alpha_t` a unit, so `Res=0` at a point iff `f,g`
share a `b3`-root there, whatever `g`'s leading coefficient does.  The
resultant is isobaric: with `wt b3=t+1`, `wt(a1)=0`, `wt(c1)=2t+2`,

```text
wt Res_b3(T_(t,2t-1), T_(t,k)) = deg_b3(T_k)*(2t+2-2(t+1)) + 2(4t+1-k) = 8t+2-2k,
```

independent of `deg_b3 T_k` (2 on the top tail, 3 on `k<=t-2`).  Stage A
computed all `2t-2` resultants at `t=3..7` (top tail by the formula, lower
rows by Singular's `resultant`) and printed `homog=1`, `wdeg=8t+2-2k` for
every one.  Sizes grow fast (`t=6`: 674..1465 terms for the top tail,
1745..3262 for the lower rows; `t=7`: 1904..5402 and 7756..14778).

### 5.3 The top-weight part certifies finiteness, not emptiness

Let `J=I_(t,+)|_(b4=1)`, `K=J cap A_t[q]`, or the resultant ideal, filtered by
the `(q,b3)`-weight.  For a weighted form `f`, `f|_(b4=1)` has top-weight part
`f|_(b4=0)`.  Hence the ideal generated by the initial forms of the generators
of `J` is `I_(t,+)|_(b4=0)` -- the `b4=0` cone ideal, whose dim-0 statement is
RESIDUAL-ZERO -- and for the resultants it is `<R_r|_(b4=0)>`.  Two facts:

* `in_top(f|_(b4=1)) = f|_(b4=0)` whenever the latter is nonzero, so the
  ideal of initial forms of the generators contains `I_(t,+)|_(b4=0)`, and
  RESIDUAL-ZERO forces `dim in_top(J)=0`.  Moreover
  `in_top(J) = (I_(t,+):b4^infty)|_(b4=0)` and `dim_K A[q,b3]/J = dim_K A[q,b3]/in_top(J)`
  (Groebner degeneration along a positive weight).  So `dim in_top(J)=0`
  (cone `={0}`) is equivalent to `V(J)` FINITE, i.e. to `V(I_(t,+))` being
  `{0}` union finitely many weighted lines with `b4!=0`.  Emptiness of `V(J)`
  is `in_top(J) ni 1`, i.e. `J=(1)` itself -- nothing is gained.
* The inference "top-weight part has dim 0 (or RESIDUAL-ZERO holds) hence the
  `b4=1` chart is unit" is FALSE.  Exact witness (`negctrl_topweight.sing`,
  weights `wp(1,2,4)` as at `t=3`): `I=<q2-b4^2, b3>`.  Its `b4=0` slice is
  `{0}` (`lead=b4,q2,b3`), the initial forms of `I|_(b4=1)=<q2-1,b3>` generate
  `<q2,b3>` of dim 0, yet `I|_(b4=1)` is not unit (`unit=0, dim=0`: the point
  `q2=1,b3=0`), `b4^N notin I` for `N<=12`, and the cone is the curve
  `q2=b4^2, b3=0` (dim 1).  This is the `b4`-axis report's counterexample (4.3)
  run as a Singular control.

Consequently the requested "compute the top-weight part and test dim=0" is
exactly the tail (RESIDUAL-ZERO / the `q`-chain), already proved on
sub-charts and exact at `t<=7`; it cannot deliver `(C0)`, because `b4!=0` is
open and every weight degeneration lands in `b4=0`.  `(C0)` is a statement
about the closure of `V(I) cap {b4!=0}`, i.e. about `I:b4^infty`, which no
initial-ideal computed from generators sees.
