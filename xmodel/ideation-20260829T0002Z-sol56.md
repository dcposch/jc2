### Card 1 — `M2-RANK-OR-LASSO`

**History:** `NEW` discriminator on a `KNOWN` target. It connects Avenue 2's
missing state theorem with Avenue 36 exact SAT/search and Avenue 38's

**Claim sought.** For the smallest omitted fixed-type, two-pole, post-jump

exact repeatable lasso/unbounded ray.

the corrected Proposition 4.2, 5.1, 5.4, 6.7/6.8, and actual-weight Sections
7–9 perimeter; preservation of unused branches, fibre/root/deck provenance,
divisibility cases, and every local inequality; proof that the chosen grammar
is complete at its registered scope; and exact replay from any abstraction to
the source equations.

**Cheapest exact discriminator.** On one fixed type with two poles, emit the
smallest explicit omitted two-jump SCC. Ask for either a Farkas certificate
for a linear/lexicographic ranking or one abstract lasso, then replay that
lasso exactly. This first template is desk-scale and needs no AWS launch.

**Meaning of every outcome.**

- A ranking certificate on every SCC proves termination only for that exact
  grammar. A finite full book follows only after finite branching and state
  bounds are separately proved.
- An exact replayed lasso or unbounded ray refutes bounded delay within that
  grammar. It becomes an Avenue 4/36 algebraization target, not a Keller map.
- A spurious lasso identifies a missing residue/root/deck or divisibility
  field; add exactly that field and rerun once.
- Neither ranking nor lasso means the abstraction is inconclusive, not that
  the grammar terminates.
- Failure to establish source-transition completeness makes any positive
  ranking result invalid; the card stops before computation.

**Stop condition.** At most two abstraction refinements. Stop immediately if
the registered transition relation is not complete or exact replay cannot be
made fail-closed. Do not replace failure by a larger enumeration cap.

**Expected information gain:** `9/10`. It can return a proof certificate or a
precise formal falsifier at the campaign's first genuinely unbounded state
wall.
