   forget the data needed to decide this global statement.

   tower is compatible, finite etale, determinant one, and colliding at every
   finite level, yet its limit lies in a Tate algebra.  A proof must detect
   horizontal divisors or prove fixed finite type; inverse limits alone are

   complete landing/coverage, and a provenanced bounded/cofinal type menu are

   normal surface over the target with an explicit `A^2` open, constant-volume
   restriction, and a marked collision.  This is more direct than asking a

   coupling.**  The triangular Artin–Schreier lift necessarily uses the
   nonpolynomial reciprocal `1/(1-px^(p-1))`.  A real counterexample seed must
