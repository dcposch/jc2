## 3. New avenue or mechanism

### Floer correction terms as an **attainment** sieve

For a surviving rank-four candidate, homogenize the proposed irreducible `A1`-normalized one-place curve to $\bar B\subset\mathbb{CP}^2$. When every singularity of $\bar B$ is unibranch, the candidate is a rational cuspidal plane curve; its projective degree and local value semigroups must satisfy the Heegaard–Floer correction-term inequalities of Borodzik–Livingston and their arbitrary-genus extension. These are effective inequalities on semigroup-counting functions, so the delta-sequence compiler can test them by integer arithmetic. The primary sources are Borodzik–Livingston, *Heegaard Floer homology and rational cuspidal curves*, Forum Math. Sigma 2 (2014), e28, [arXiv:1304.1062](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1062), and Borodzik–Hedden–Livingston, *Plane algebraic curves of arbitrary genus via Heegaard Floer homology*, Comment. Math. Helv. 92 (2017), 215–256, [doi:10.4171/CMH/411](https://doi.org/10.4171/CMH/411).

This is genuinely different from Avenue 27's splice colourings: a colourable knot can still fail the smooth four-manifold correction-term constraints required of an algebraic plane-curve realization. It directly targets the new live gap—attainment of the explicit survivors [A:12–22, 41–48]—and never upgrades a passing necessary-condition test to existence.

For noncuspidal controls, Borodzik–Liu–Zemke construct link-Floer/knotification obstructions and explicitly treat transverse double points in *Heegaard Floer homology, knotifications of links, and plane curves with noncuspidal singularities*, Algebr. Geom. Topol. 24 (2024), 4837–4889, [doi:10.2140/agt.2024.24.4837](https://doi.org/10.2140/agt.2024.24.4837). That paper licenses a node branch of the compiler and a regression against the promoted one-node kill; it does **not** by itself license arbitrary tangencies. A tangential or general multibranch record therefore returns typed `OPEN` until its exact link-cobordism hypotheses are proved.

## 4. New connection between existing avenues

### A Shapiro–twisted-Alexander receiver for monodromy

Connect Avenues 7, 25–27 and the source-incidence data as follows. Put `U=C^2\B` and, only after checking that this is the actual finite étale locus, `V=C^2\F^{-1}(B)`. The degree-`N` monodromy gives a permutation local system `C^N = 1 + V_std` on `U`. Shapiro's lemma identifies homology with coefficients in `C^N` with ordinary homology of `V`; hence the standard summand measures the difference between source-complement and target-complement homology. The number and incidence of irreducible components of `F^{-1}(B)` can therefore provide a *lower* constraint, while the singular links of `B` provide an *upper/divisibility* constraint.

The latter is not a slogan: for a unitary representation, Cogolludo Agustín–Florens prove that the twisted Alexander polynomial of a plane-curve complement divides the product of the local twisted polynomials, with the quotient controlled by a Blanchfield form; see *Twisted Alexander polynomials of plane algebraic curves*, J. Lond. Math. Soc. 76 (2007), 105–121, [doi:10.1112/jlms/jdm004](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/jdm004), [arXiv:math/0504356](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0504356). A finite permutation representation is unitarizable. Thus a candidate `S4` colouring can be tested against source-cover homology, not merely against a Hurwitz identity tuple. Before promotion one must separately prove the affine/projective-at-infinity convention, torsion hypothesis, and exact linking map; if any is missing the result is `OPEN`, not a vanishing claim.

A second connection is immediate but presently conditional: if the degree-four dicritical proof types at general `N`, then the distinct-per-component charges give `2m <= N-1`; pairing this with the promoted theorem that at least one branch component has nontrivial `pi1` turns the all-degree topology lane into a bounded-component classification rather than an unrestricted curve-complement problem [P:24–39; R4:19–44].
