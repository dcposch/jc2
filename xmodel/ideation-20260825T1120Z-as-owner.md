specialization and let `b=x5*(x3-2*x5)`.  Form a saturated projective closure,
discard purely vertical artifacts only with certificates, and normalize each


together with the pulled-back terminal and true-centre Taylor rows.  On a
projective escape branch replace differentials by the corresponding Rees
valuations.  A rational trajectory avoiding the Q8 open must have the whole
zero divisor of `w` supported on these boundary branches.  The terminal ODE
and Kummer/load relations prescribe the allowed orders of `r6,r8`; the
normalization supplies the actual orders.  Any mismatch kills the branch.

```text

x1=a, c=1/a, d2=d4=0, r6=-4*a^3/81, r8=0.
```

The new calculation is not another value substitution: linearize the six
rows at generic `a`, compute the tangent image of `dr8`, and ask whether the
source-licensed terminal right-hand side lies in it.  In parallel audit the
`x3-2*x5=0` branch using `dr6` and the nonzero-load relation.  This may turn
the complement into two one-row cokernel certificates before any global

deficit forces an open Q8 contact; an excess or illegal order kills the
