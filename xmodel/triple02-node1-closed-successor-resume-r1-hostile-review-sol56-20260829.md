## Custody attack audit

### Blocking defect C1 — unbound copied candidate and post-archive TOCTOU

`aws_job_worker.sh` derives `candidate` from the classifier at lines 303-317, writes a generic worker gate at 329-330, and copies the candidate at 331. The supervisor freezes and installs the archive at lines 393-449. This admits both pre-freeze copied-field substitution and post-freeze disagreement between archive and marker. It also leaves `FINALIZATION_LATCHES.txt`, the decision transcript, and the promoted marker outside the reviewed archive. Required repair: bank one content-addressed decision record binding job tag/nonce, source-archive hash, lease/worker identity, production and classifier summary/verdict hashes, complete artifact-manifest hash, and candidate; have the late authority verify all equalities against the freshly extracted archive; bind the public marker to the archive/decision digest; and eliminate the live post-archive candidate reread.

### Blocking defect C2 — stages are self-hashed, not job-bound or regenerated

`run_singular_stage.py` checks live PGID/SID and writes an identity JSON, but the result JSON does not hash that identity or bind the job tag, lease, worker/supervisor start times, expected PGID/SID, Singular binary digest, or source archive. `Recursor.run_stage` records only node, stage, return code, timeout, script hash, and stdout hash. `classify_resume.stage_lines` checks only self-consistency of script/stdout/stderr/result hashes; it neither requires the identity file nor regenerates dynamic reduce/rank/saturation/chart scripts from the frozen builders.

The accepted synthetic fixtures prove the gap: arbitrary one-line fixture scripts and no identity files pass the real dead/survivor classifier when paired with fabricated marker transcripts and matching self-hashes. Required repair: make the classifier regenerate and byte-compare every dynamic script and required artifact set, verify current-job identity JSON and its hash from each result, verify runner/child starttime/uid/PGID/SID/job tag/cgroup/cap/argv/Singular digest, and reject copied or missing identities. The bounded no-verdict chain must likewise be compared to the driver's exact remainder literals, hashes, bound, stage sequence, and reason.

### Blocking defect C3 — supervisor death is not fail-safe containment

TERM/INT/HUP/normal EXIT run cleanup, but `SIGKILL` or abrupt supervisor death cannot run traps. The transient systemd scope has `MemoryMax`/`TasksMax` but no independent runtime deadline or supervisor-death kill binding. The PGID fallback also has no `PR_SET_PDEATHSIG`, parent-starttime watchdog, or external lease monitor. A worker/CAS tree can therefore continue after supervisor death, with neither the 21,600-second whole-job enforcement nor a terminal. It should not produce a mathematical marker, but it violates bounded custody and cleanup.

Required repair: put the entire supervisor/worker tree in an externally owned cgroup/service with independent `RuntimeMaxSec`, `KillMode=control-group`, and kill-on-owner-loss semantics, or add an equivalent parent-death/watchdog mechanism. Disallow the PGID fallback for pilot success unless its supervisor-death behavior is implemented and live-tested.
