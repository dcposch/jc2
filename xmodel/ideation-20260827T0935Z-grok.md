## 7. Adversarial scope audit of the best proposal (Card A)

Attack the claim “specialize `J0`, skip exact duals at dead `N`, and
the first surviving exact `N` plus a DVR lift decides ordered `T-a1`.”

1. **Specialization can only raise a floor, never certify an exponent.**
   Membership specializes; nonmembership does not reverse. A specialized
   `a1^7 in J0(F_p)` is consistent with `a1^7 notin J0` over Q. Card A
   therefore *must not* treat a specialized kill as `N_{J0}=7`. The
   exact dual/primal at that `N` remains mandatory. The advertised
   saving is only the skip after specialized *nonmembership*.
2. **The 17-row unit may not be `J0`.** Selected rows generating 1 at
   `a1=1` give `a1^{N_17}` in a *subideal*. That upper-bounds `N_{J0}`
   only if those rows are in `J0`. If they are a different presentation
   (chart bilinears mixed in, a localization inverted, a rho-localized
   lift of the V24 type), the ceiling is costume. The cap-hit in Card A
   is the fail-closed test of this.
3. **Homogenization vs dehomogenization.** A Groebner basis `{1}` after
   `a1=1` yields some `a1^N` in the homogenized ideal only after the
   cofactors are rehomogenized. Inhomogeneous slices can lie. The
   specialization probe should be run on the homogeneous `J0`, not on
   the dehomogenized 17-row script, whenever both exist.
4. **Seeded duals can be false certificates.** Imposing
   `Lambda_N(a1·m)=Lambda_{N-1}(m)` restricts to a proper affine subspace
   of the dual space. A solution of the residual block that fails
   unrestricted replay is not a dual. V43's target-component-only matrix
   was already insufficient; the seed makes that failure mode more
   likely, not less. Unrestricted replay is load-bearing, not polish.
5. **`ez9` and later rows.** `ez9` is a spectator in `J0` but live in
   `J`. A special-fibre dual does not constrain total rows that die at
   `rho=0`. A DVR identity can use those rows. Card A’s `J0` exponent
   is necessary for `U(0)=1` but the lift uses `J`, not `J0`. Conflating
   them is the saturation/specialization gap the criterion exists to
   name.
6. **Weight 35 is not final.** Grade-`g>19` rows have weight `g` and
   *can* contribute to weight 35. For the *frozen* chart they are absent,
   so a frozen dual is a frozen-chart theorem. For the honest total
   source they are only a floor. Do not advertise a frozen `N=7` dual as
   all-depth.
7. **V43 is unreviewed as a result.** Compiler delta is reviewed;
   the 284,766-product replay is producer-tier pending Opus5. Card A
   descendants must remain `PROVISIONAL` on that pin. A REFUTED V43
   collapses the floor back to V38’s `N>=6` and relaunches `N=6`,
   which this packet would have wrongly skipped.
8. **A known `N_{J0}` does not close the chart.** The reviewed
   converse is only `K+(rho)=(1) ⇔` some `a1^N U(t) in J` with
   `U(0)=1`. Special-fibre membership is the `t=0` fibre of that
   identity. The live `lift`/`lp`/eliminant lanes are still required.
   Card A is a floor/ceiling instrument, not a replacement certificate.
9. **Compute boundary.** “Specialization” becomes a Groebner the moment
   the specialized ideal is not 17 univariate polynomials. If an
   implementer saturates a 66-variable specialized ring locally, that
   is a different, AWS-only, preregistered lane. The card as written is
   random evaluation of complementary products, the same shape as V43’s
   replay, over `F_p`.

Surviving core after these attacks: V43 plus V38 give a genuine floor;
specialized nonmembership is a legal floor-raiser; exact work should
occur at one `N` at a time with unrestricted replay; the DVR lift
remains a separate object; the 17-row ceiling is conditional on
custody. That core is worth running. The claim that this “decides
`T-a1`” is **NAVIGATION**, not a theorem, until a `U(t)` is exhibited
or the DVR module is proved torsion-free.
