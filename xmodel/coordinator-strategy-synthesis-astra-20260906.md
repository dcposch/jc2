## Judgment

Keep the source-calibrated Moh framework, but stop treating its finite
elimination campaign as the whole strategy. The highest-leverage change is
to distinguish three goals:

1. **Proof:** a genuinely uniform exclusion using information that the
   arithmetic/receiver reduction has not already exhausted.
2. **Counterexample:** a nonempty full polynomial-realization system.
   One such system suffices; no all-degree coverage theorem is needed.
3. **Finite progress:** harvest the corrected compressed systems, with their
   source implications checked, without mistaking that progress for (1).

The campaign's most expensive recurring error has been solving the wrong
necessary system, not failing to purchase enough computer time. Conversely,
we should not demand a reverse equivalence where a one-way implication
already suffices for an exclusion. Demand the exact arrow the conclusion uses.

The reviewed baseline remains 24,063 -> 90 -> 64 necessary configurations
at n <= 200: 44 u_s=1, 20 split-class rows. Coverage necessities are
all-degree; the enumeration and exclusions are not. (99,66), D=108, and
uniform K16 remain open.

For every actual m>=4 and every field factor of Q[d]/(3d^2-m),

The b=0 boundary remains separate. B!=0 forces primitive support under the

### Parent residues: a small pilot, with a stronger negative control

[Sol's September 6 proposal](ideation-20260906T0000Z-sol56.md), Q2.A,
uses the original generic fibre g=T:
omega_g=dx/g_y=-dy/g_x and df=j*omega_g. Residues of omega_g at actual
infinity places therefore vanish. After monomial-Jacobian descent only
gamma^ell*omega_Q is forced exact. This is a valid reason to look for a
coefficient-sensitive condition not captured by the arithmetic transport.

However, [KELLER-PENCIL-GENUS](keller-pencil-genus-opus5-20260902.md),
section 2, already considered Gelfand–Leray exactness: pole/zero **counts**
return Riemann–Hurwitz, not an additional inequality. The candidate here is
a residue coefficient of a fixed embedded source, not another count.
I located no completed dedicated R009/R050 parent-residue pilot in the
recent reports; this is a limited search result, not a claim of novelty.

There is an immediate stronger control, worked out here from the familiar
polynomial g=x+x^2*y. It has no affine critical point. Its generic fibre is
C* with y=(T-x)/x^2, and omega_g=dx/x^2=d(-1/x), so even **full generic-fibre
exactness**, not just vanishing residues, holds. Nevertheless it has no
polynomial Keller mate.

Indeed, if J(f,g)=j in C* with f polynomial, substitute y=(T-x)/x^2.
The resulting A(T,x) belongs to C[T,x,x^-1] and satisfies
partial_x A=j/x^2. Hence A=-j/x+h(T), with h in C[T]. In C(x,y) this says
f=-j/x+h(g); multiplying by x and setting x=0 gives 0=-j. The obstruction
is extension of the primitive over the whole affine plane.

This elementary control is a coordinator derivation, not a promoted
campaign theorem. It sharply limits the pilot: do not upgrade generic
exactness to a global polynomial mate. Compute a genuine residue on a
certified physical place, with the generic T retained, and ask whether it
adds a condition beyond current equations. Stop if it is redundant or the
place identification is unproved. Global polynomial extension across the
exceptional fibres is the stronger unresolved requirement. This pilot ranks
below the complete-ideal reverse gate.

## Independent construction and the literature

The last located broad sweep is
[September 3 10:10Z](websweep-20260903T1010Z-grok46.md).
It is overdue for renewal. I supplemented its findings with targeted live
primary-source checks; these are not a replacement broad sweep.

- The verified higher-dimensional counterexample is real and does not settle
  dimension two. The
  [Isabelle AFP entry](https://isa-afp.org/entries/Jacobian_Counterexample.html)
  certifies the three-variable map. There is no automatic two-dimensional
  counterexample obtained by dropping a variable or projecting a surface.
- [Van Dobben de Bruyn, arXiv:2608.27341v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27341v1)
  classifies affine-space complements of **relative hyperplane divisors in
  projective bundles over P1**. In dimension two its affine-plane case is
  extremely restrictive. This is a cheap recognition/rejection tool for
  constructions in that class, not a theorem about arbitrary JC boundaries.
- The [public (75,125) frontier](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wstrinz/plane-jacobian-75-125/main/POST_FREEZE_FRONTIER_2026_08_30.md)
  explicitly retains a missing source-to-reduced adapter and distinguishes
  finite truncation witnesses from source realization. Its periodic
  source recurrence is a possible reusable mechanism; it supplies no
  external closure to import.
- A newly surfaced
  [Lean file named as a JC2 proof](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AEjonanonymous/Jacobian-Conjecture-2D/main/JacobianConjecture2D.lean)
  ends with the assertion that the constant coefficient of a nonzero
  constant Jacobian is nonzero. It does not state or establish invertibility.
- The [lower-Newton-side preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.09430) is dated
  2016 with a 2017 revision. A newer publication listing is not evidence of
  a newly proved all-degree exclusion. The September sweep's bounded HC4
  and real Lorentzian results also must not be read as complex JC2.

Protect a genuinely independent geometric-construction slot: a nontrivial
etale surface map with source explicitly A2, not merely an affine rational
surface, a boundary graph, or an arbitrarily long formal lift. Require an
explicit family and an affine-plane recognition test before a large search.
The known tangent-sweep geometry motivates this, but I found no checked
plane construction ready to outrank the full-ideal opportunity above.
