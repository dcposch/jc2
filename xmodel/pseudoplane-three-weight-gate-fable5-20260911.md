## B. Polynomial abc lemma, proved inside the report

The identities W=a'c−ac'=c'b−cb' follow by substituting a=c−b, recomputed. At a root of multiplicity e of a, of b, or of c, (t−α)^(e−1) divides W; the roots of a,b,c are distinct, so deg W≥deg(abc)−ν. Also deg W≤deg a+deg b−1, one of a,b being nonconstant, else c is constant. Hence deg c≤ν−1; permuting the roles (c+(−b)=a and so on) gives the other two bounds.
