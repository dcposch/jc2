# Full-plane radial descent without Euler stability

ROOT manual producer, September12,2026,13:57UTC. MANUAL/UNREVIEWED.
This is one discriminator for the proposed inference from a descended
radial one-form and finite unramified normalization to Euler stability.
It is not a polynomial Keller map or a JC2 counterexample.

## 1. Exact full-plane ring and identification

Let R=C[x,y], u=x^2-1, and let sigma(x,y)=(-x,-y). Put

    a=x^2, b=xy, c=y^2, d=u*x, e=u*y,
    S=C[a,b,c]=R^sigma,
    B=C[a,b,c,d,e]=S+uR.

The equality follows by even/odd total-degree decomposition: the odd
polynomials form S*x+S*y and u is even. Equivalently,

    B={r in R : r(1,t)=r(-1,-t) for every t in C}.

For the reverse implication of this description, the odd part
r_odd=(r-r o sigma)/2 vanishes along x=1 and then along x=-1, so u
divides it. Its even part belongs to S. Thus B is the exact pinching ring,
not just a subring that happens to identify the indicated points.

R is finite over S, hence over B: it is generated as S-module by 1,x,y.
It is birational over B because x=d/u and y=e/u in Frac B. Hence R is
the finite normalization of B. The elements x and y are not silently
adjoined to B: x distinguishes (1,t) and (-1,-t), so x is not in B.
The finite graph into A5 given by (a,b,c,d,e) is not an embedding.

Away from u=0, B[1/u]=R[1/u]. At x=1 or -1, the two components a,b
have determinant J(a,b)=2x^2, a unit in the local ring. Their differentials
generate the source cotangent space, so Omega_(R/B) vanishes there too.
The normalization is therefore unramified EVERYWHERE on the whole A2.

The exact conductor I={r in R:rR subset B} equals uR. The easy inclusion
is its definition S+uR. Conversely r,rx in B give equal values at paired
points (1,t),(-1,-t). If the common r-value is v, the rx-values are v
and -v, so v=0. Thus r vanishes on both complete lines, proving u divides
r. This proves the ideal equality, not only a support statement.

## 2. A descended radial one-form, and failed Euler descent

Let alpha=(x dy-y dx)/2 and E=(x partial_x+y partial_y)/2. There is an
explicit KAEHLER one-form with coefficients and differentials entirely
in B whose pullback is alpha:

    beta=(1/2) d_b - e d_d - ((4-3a)/2)*b d_a.

Here d_a,d_b,d_d mean the differentials of the named GENERATORS a,b,d;
they are not new variables or derivatives with respect to a fictitious
independent five-variable coordinate system. Directly,

    db=y dx+x dy, da=2x dx, dd=(3a-1)dx,
    e dd+((4-3a)/2)b da
       =[(a-1)(3a-1)+(4-3a)a]*y dx=y dx.

Thus beta pulls back to alpha, and d beta pulls back to dx wedge dy.
In particular source area lies in the image of Omega_B^2; this is not
the existence of one decomposable polynomial Keller pair in B.

Nevertheless E(u)=x^2=a does not vanish on u=0, so E(I) is not contained
in I. Also E(d)=(3/2)d+x. Since x is not in B, E(d) is not in B and
E(B) is not contained in B. The positive sanity check B=R has descended
alpha, conductor R and E-stability; the obstruction here is the pinching,
not a source puncture or a pole. The source of this control is ALL A2.

## 3. Why this does not supply the special hypersurface Keller graph

There is also a short dualizing-form obstruction to B being an affine
hypersurface in A3. This part uses the stated standard finite duality and
nodal residue facts; it is not a fresh proof of those facts.

The exact pinching description in section1 identifies two disjoint smooth
divisors by (1,t)~(-1,-t), with no higher congruence condition. Its completed
local ring along the glued curve is the fiber product of two smooth surface
rings over their common smooth curve ring, hence is isomorphic to
C[[t,n_+,n_-]]/(n_+ n_-). Thus the surface is locally a simple double
normal crossing along that entire curve. Its dualizing sheaf is invertible
locally. A dualizing two-form pulls back to forms with at most simple poles
on the two branches, whose RESIDUE ONE-FORMS SUM TO ZERO under the gluing.
For n_+ n_-=0 this is the direct pair dn_+/n_+ wedge dt and
-dn_-/n_- wedge dt. The cancellation condition is not a positivity claim.

Suppose B were an affine hypersurface in A3. Its dualizing module would
then be free, generated by a global form eta. Finite normalization duality
identifies Hom_B(R,omega_B) with omega_R in their common rational-form
space; when omega_B=B*eta, this identifies I*eta with R*(dx wedge dy).
Since I=uR and the only units of R are nonzero constants, the rational
pullback of eta would necessarily be kappa*(dx wedge dy)/u, kappa in C*.

At x=1 its residue is (kappa/2)dy. At x=-1 its residue is
-(kappa/2)dy. Pull the latter back by the actual gluing y=-t, while y=t
on x=1: BOTH residues become (kappa/2)dt. Their sum is kappa dt, not
zero. This contradicts the required nodal cancellation. Consequently B
cannot be an affine hypersurface in A3; its dualizing line bundle is not
globally trivial. This is a GLOBAL obstruction, not an embedding-dimension
count inferred from our choice of five generators.

## 4. Scope and the named source gap

This full-plane control refutes the inference

    finite birational unramified normalization from A2
