## New mechanisms and cross-avenue connections

### A. Cube-orbifold branch signature — **NEW exact compiler mechanism**

Suppose a source-derived loaded chart gives rational functions
`v,Y in C(x)` and an exact identity

```text
Y^3 = R(v).
```

Remove cube factors from `R`.  Let `r` be the number of distinct places of
`P1_v` at which `ord(R)` is nonzero modulo three, including infinity.  If the
cyclic cover is connected, Riemann--Hurwitz gives

```text
2g-2 = -6 + 2r,       hence       g=r-2.
```

Thus `r>=3` rules out nonconstant `v`: a nonconstant solution would define a
nonconstant map `P1_x -> C_R`, impossible for `g>0`.  The packet's parity
curve has five squarefree numerator roots, two squarefree denominator roots,
and unramified infinity, so `r=7` and `g=5` in one line.

The practical extension is to compute `r` **stratumwise**.  Discriminants,
pairwise resultants, and subresultant degrees enumerate exactly where roots
collide, numerator and denominator share a root, or exponents become
multiples of three.  Most strata can die from the branch signature without
normalizing a curve or decomposing the whole coefficient ideal; the residual
`r=2` genus-zero strata receive an explicit parametrization and the original
Taylor boundaries.  Every client must derive `v` and `Y` reversibly; weights
alone do not license the cover.

This connects avenues 3, 25, 27, and 28 through avenue 38's exact
subresultant machinery.  It is not the known genus-five calculation itself;
the new object is the finite collision-stratified branch-signature compiler.

source-typed TD6 ideal in the remaining auxiliary coefficient variables.

`(C,U)` family before setting `U=0`**, and saturate by the homogenizing

The projection

```text
pi : Xbar -> A2_(C,U)
```


image `pi(Xbar)` is closed and disjoint from the whole line, so its complement
is a genuine Zariski-open empty neighborhood of that line in the
`(c1,c3)` surface.  If it is nonempty, its components are the precise
infinity escapes that invalidate an affine Nakayama argument; their base

### C. Common architecture: properness before specialization — **NEW connection**

The maximum-12 and TD6 mechanisms are two forms of the same firewall.
Maximum-12 completes a rational coefficient trajectory to a proper cyclic
curve; positive genus blocks a map from `P1`.  TD6 completes auxiliary
coefficient space projectively; proper projection blocks solutions from
escaping to infinity as the transverse modulus specializes.  In both cases,
specializing first destroys the decisive boundary data.  This suggests one
shared exact engine for divisor strata, quotient rings, saturation, and
proper-image certificates rather than separate point samplers.
