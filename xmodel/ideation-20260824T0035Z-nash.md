## 2. Reranked bottlenecks

### Proof side

1. **A global finiteness/properness mechanism.** The highest-value target is a
   direct theorem such as regularity of field traces, or an equivalent
   prohibition on poles along the asymptotic divisor. It would imply
   integrality and bypass `G2-PSC`, book coverage, and a degree ceiling.
2. **A polynomial-origin boundary constraint stronger than determinant
   support.** The exact thick-line gradient cokernel must be combined with
   integrability of its columns, trace/residue reciprocity, or the commuting
   frame; determinant, Hodge, leading power, and local polar excess alone have
   explicit decoys.
3. **A genuinely end-to-end pure architecture.** If the boundary-book route
   remains primary, it needs a corrected Sigray source theorem and a total,
   fail-closed full-configuration landing map. Proving `G2-PSC` alone would
   leave too many independent gaps to rank first.
4. **Absolute/cofinal type or degree control.** Type-relative KJN cannot do
   this without a bounded provenanced type menu; alternatively a direct global
   proof must make enumeration unnecessary.
5. **Uniform exclusion after coverage.** Residue-A, composite single-pole,
   off-axis, and post-jump sectors matter only after the endpoint and coverage
   are honest; isolated cell kills rank below the four items above.

### Disproof side

1. **Global algebraization of a nonautomorphic Keller object.** The decisive
   gap is not another modular point but passage from a compatible infinity
   germ to two global polynomials with constant Jacobian and certified
   nonproperness/collision.
2. **Existence and structure of an infinite characteristic-zero formal
   branch.** D43 has not yet shown a common integral smooth point, formal
   inverse-limit survival, or whether the tower is finite type versus carrying
   persistent free tail parameters.
3. **Algebraic specialization inside the formal family.** If the tower is
   smooth and underdetermined, one must locate a rational/algebraic generating
   series by Padé/Hankel or recurrence constraints; a generic formal solution
   will not be a polynomial counterexample.
4. **Global nonautomorphism certificate.** Even an algebraic germ must be
   glued with source provenance and shown to yield a nonproper Keller map;
   automorphism towers demonstrate that large boundary complexity is not
   enough.
5. **Alternative lifting routes.** An odd-characteristic collision surviving
   all Witt levels remains legitimate but currently has less concrete signal
   than the D-series formal branch.

## 3. Idea cards

### Card A — TRACE-REG: cancel trace poles to force finiteness

**Role:** strongest proof attack; genuinely new mechanism; cross-connects
avenues 7, 16, 25, 28, 29, 31, 33, and 39.

- **Target claim.** Let
  (A=\mathbf C[f,g]\subset B=\mathbf C[x,y]),
  (K=\operatorname{Frac}A\), (L=\operatorname{Frac}B), and
  (d=[L:K]), with (J(f,g)\in\mathbf C^*\). Prove the scoped trace-regularity
  statement
  \[
    \operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(x^m),\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(y^m)\in A
    \quad(1\le m\le d),
  \]
  or the stronger `Tr(B) subset A`.
- **Exact conditional deduction already available.** If the displayed
  regularity holds, Newton identities put every coefficient of the
  multiplication characteristic polynomials of (x) and (y) in (A).
  Hence (x,y) are integral over (A), so (B=A[x,y]) is finite over (A).
  A finite planar Keller map is finite etale; over (mathbf C), connected
  finite etale covers of (mathbf A^2) are trivial, so the map is an
  automorphism. This implication is elementary characteristic-zero algebra;
  **trace regularity itself is only a proposed theorem**.
- **Novelty.** This does not try to bound a local carrier or enumerate a
  degree. It identifies the obstruction to finiteness as the principal parts
  of finitely many symmetric functions of inverse branches. Since
  (dx\wedge dy=df\wedge dg), field trace gives
  \[
    \operatorname{Tr}(h\,dx\wedge dy)=\operatorname{Tr}(h)\,df\wedge dg.
  \]
  Thus the missing assertion is a concrete cancellation of polar parts along
  the asymptotic divisor, approachable through residues, monodromy, and the
  exact polynomial-origin boundary identity.
- **New evidence used.** The pure-boundary identity and thick-line support are
  exact and dual-confirmed; the G5 class-kill and Henon tower show that
  determinant support, leading power, conductor, or polar excess separately
  are insufficient. The trace must use the global sum over inverse branches.
- **Dependencies/evidence tier.** Standard field trace and Newton identities
  are `PROVED`; the residue cancellation is `CONJECTURE/PROPOSED`. It needs no
  GGV packet, Sigray landing, or degree bound. Local tests on a filed formal
  germ are only falsifiers of proposed local proofs, not tests on an actual
  counterexample.
- **Cheapest discriminator/software.** Implement a Newton--Puiseux trace
  summation engine. On a generic target arc approaching one asymptotic
  component, compute principal parts of the first several Newton sums of the
  inverse (x)- and (y)-branches. Gate it on tame automorphisms and on a
  deliberately non-Keller generically finite control, then feed the most
  completely specified residue-A formal data. In parallel, derive the
  coordinate-free residue formula for
  `Tr(h dx wedge dy)` on a normal-crossings compactification.
- **Interpretation of outcomes.** If Keller equations force cancellation not
  shared by the non-Keller control, isolate the first nontrivial identity and
  attempt an all-(m) recurrence/global residue proof. If a formal Keller
  model satisfying every currently used boundary identity has a nonzero trace
  pole, then those identities cannot prove TRACE-REG; identify the missing
  polynomial-origin/global condition rather than abandoning the exact
  criterion. If the trace cannot even be reconstructed from filed local data,
  that demonstrates that existing books discard the global branch pairing
  needed by a properness proof.
- **Cost/time.** One research lane plus one symbolic implementation lane,
  approximately one to two days; no large Groebner computation.
- **Stop condition.** Stop after either an exact noncancelling formal control
  with the same proposed inputs, or proof that the available tree/passport data
  do not determine the trace principal part. Do not scale to many depths or
  degrees without a first separating identity.
- **Expected information gain.** Very high: success gives a direct JC2 route;
  failure states exactly why local boundary data cannot see properness and
  prevents several avenues from rediscovering the same missing global sum.
- **Resurrection trigger.** A constructed asymptotic component, a total
  coupled-monodromy object, a new global residue theorem, or a polynomial-
  origin identity that distinguishes the formal noncancelling control.

## 5. Shared hidden assumptions exposed by the reset

1. **Finite depth is evidence for algebraization.** It is not unless the
   prolongation tower is finite type or an algebraic-specialization condition
   is imposed; positive-dimensional finite jets can persist generically.
2. **The GGV packet is information-sufficient for a decorated Sigray tree.**
   This has not even been tested as an injectivity/compatibility statement,
   independently of proving transport.
3. **A local type-relative ceiling can become global later.** “Later” hides a
   separate bounded-type and provenance theorem, potentially as hard as the
   original compactness problem.
4. **Polynomial origin is captured by the identities already filed.** The
   Henon and G5 controls show that determinant support, leading power, local
   conductor, and polar excess each admit large-complexity decoys; the missing
   condition may be global branch pairing rather than another local scalar.
5. **More exact bounded exclusions materially shorten JC2.** Without an upper
   bound or a direct global theorem, they improve evidence and priority but do
   not shrink an infinite search to a proof.
6. **Independent public actors imply independent mathematical evidence.**
   Shared Belyi/quintic lineages and coordinate systems can make several
   implementations one proof path; provenance must be crosswalked before
   counting replication.
7. **Two primes plus a p-squared correction indicate a characteristic-zero
   component.** They do not without one common integral model and the required
   smoothness/flatness or an exact characteristic-zero construction.
8. **A characteristic-zero formal germ is close to a counterexample.** It
   still lacks convergence/algebraicity, global gluing, polynomiality,
   constant Jacobian at all orders, and nonautomorphism.
9. **A different-model review removes shared-source risk.** It does not when
   producer and reviewer inherit the same mistranscribed source object; source
   reconstruction and alternate representations remain necessary.
