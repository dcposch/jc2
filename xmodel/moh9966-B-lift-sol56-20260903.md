## 3. Why the zero-jet slice is not the full chart

It is harmless to remove `a_0` by an old polynomial translation, and `a_2`
can be absorbed into `z` while centring the double root.  The `a_1` term is
different.  Write `u=a_1`.  For the cubic approximate root,

```text

K|_(x=0)=w^3(w-1)^8,
w=u*x^2+x^3*z.                                         (3.1)
```

The fixed major face has boundary weight 32, so its strict perturbations obey
`3r+4q>=33` at `w=1`.  Parameterizing this divisibility first leaves 21
coefficients.  Expanding (3.1), killing every term below
`x^9`, and fixing the `x^9` coefficient to

```text

```

gives 18 linear equations.  The exact constant-pivot elimination has 18
`Q*` pivots and no residual equation.  Three tail coefficients remain free:

```text

```

Together with `(a,u)` this is a five-dimensional `h` order space.  Every
original row was checked after the resolved triangular substitution, and no
division by `a` or `u` occurred.  In particular, `u` survives symbolically;
these order rows do not license setting `u=0`.  Later `T_2,T_3` or Jacobian
rows could still force it.

This calculation is only `H-ORDER-SPACE`, not the full lift: it does not
invent the unprinted simultaneous Laurent-field gauge, the `4/9` centre, or
the complete canonical definitions of `T_2^psi,T_3^psi`.  It is nevertheless
a decisive scope check: no source row yet identifies the zero-jet slice with
Moh's full coefficient variety, so its nonemptiness or emptiness cannot be
promoted.
