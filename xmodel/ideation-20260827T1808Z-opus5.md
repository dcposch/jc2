## 6. Correctness-preserving software / AWS acceleration

**Name:** `ROWORACLE` — residue-basis row solving with an `O(1)` predicted-rank
checksum.

**Exact inputs.**  The frozen raw slot lattice and window bounds; the face
polynomial `H` (or `K`); the row index `m`; the reduced twist
`4nu = m mod 4`; the multiplicity multiset `{e_i}` and `r = deg rad(H)`.

**What it replaces.**  Today each determinant row is solved by dense exact
elimination over the full window.  `ROWORACLE` instead (i) predicts
`codim = r - 1 + k_m` and the row rank in `O(1)`; (ii) constructs the
particular solution `S_m` by the explicit pole-order-reduction recursion, whose
indicial coefficients are `4(j - nu) h(a)` and are invertible exactly off
resonance; (iii) reduces the row to `r - 1 + k_m` explicit scalar functionals.

**Correctness preservation.**  The prediction never enters a proof.  Only the
back-substituted exact identity `L_nu(S_m) = F_m - (mixed term)` is evidence,
and it is checked over `Q` termwise.  The oracle is used solely to *size*,
*shard*, and *alarm*.

**Parallel decomposition.**  Rows are independent given the prefix, and the R6
survivor strata are 4-parameter families.  Shard by `(stratum point, row)`.
Each shard is milliseconds of exact `Q` arithmetic — my desk harness completed
72 row-instances, including a wasteful three-way pole-cap sweep, in ~15 s on
one core.  A `20^4` grid over the perfect-square stratum times 12 rows is
about `2 x 10^6` shards, well under an hour on the 512-vCPU ceiling; the
symbolic-parameter version is orders of magnitude smaller and should be run
first.

**Certificate / replay format.**  One JSON line per shard:
`{H, m, r, e_multiset, k_m, four_nu, window_w, predicted_codim,
observed_codim, cokernel_functionals, S_m, residual_is_zero,
sha256(inputs), sha256(S_m)}`.  Replay = recompute `L_nu(S_m)` and compare.

**Evidence firewall.**  (i) `predicted_codim != observed_codim` is a **halt**,
not a warning — it means either the truncation boundary `w >= deg H - 1` was
crossed or the row architecture differs from the model.  (ii) The oracle may
not be cited in any promoted statement; only per-row exact identities may.
(iii) Rows 32–34 are flagged `OUT_OF_STABLE_RANGE` and must be solved densely.

**Stop rule.**  Stop at the first halt; otherwise stop when the stratum is
swept or when two consecutive rows add no new codimension beyond the predicted
`r - 1 + k_m` (which by §3.1 means the tower has been exhausted).

## 9. One insight a differently-trained researcher is likely to miss

**Resonance makes the gate *stronger*, not weaker — and the campaign's
control fixture is the worst possible proxy for its own survivors.**

The natural instinct is: a resonant row is one where the homogeneous equation
acquires a solution, i.e. extra freedom, so the row constrains *less*.  That
is backwards.  The Euler characteristic is fixed:

```text
dim H^1 - dim H^0 = r - 1     (Deligne, rank one, regular singular, U affine)
```

so a kernel appearing at a resonant row *adds* to the cokernel.  Concretely,
on branch P (`H = A^2`, `A` squarefree quartic, `r = 4`):

```text
rows 23, 25, 27, 29, 31 :  codim 3      (non-resonant, k = 0)
rows 24, 26, 28, 30      :  codim 4     (resonant,    k = 1)
```

Exact check, two lines: at row `m` the operator is
`L(S) = 4HS' + (m mod 4) H'S` on `S in K[X, 1/rad H]`, and the target is
`K[X]_{<= 38-m}`.  Row 24 (`4nu = 0`) gives `F_2 = 4HS'`, whose image is
`{F_2 : all residues of F_2 dX/(4H) vanish}` — four conditions, and `S = const`
is the kernel.  Row 23 (`4nu = 3`, equivalently `-1`) gives
`F_1 = 4HS' - H'S = 2(2AQ' - 3A'Q)` with `S = Q/A`, injective, three
conditions.  Verified over `Q` on nine `H` and rows 23–30 (72/72).

The second half is sharper and directly operational.  `r` is *maximal* on the
squarefree control fixture `H = X^8 - 1` (`r = 8`, codim 7 per row) and small
on every promoted R6 survivor (`r <= 4` or `5`, codim 3 or 4 per row; `r = 1`
on the perfect-square boundary stratum, codim 1).  So the campaign has been
measuring gate strength on the one fixture where it is roughly *double* its
value anywhere it matters.  A well-trained instinct — "use a generic fixture,
genericity is safe" — is exactly wrong here, because R5/R6 have already proved
the survivors are non-generic.  Every "the tower cuts hard" reading taken from
`R0_ARTIFICIAL_CUSP_CONTROL` needs restating per stratum before it is used to
justify further differential work.
