**Not `NO_VERDICT`.** Every charged source, compiler, manifest, FIRST flag, affine map, and H18 unit record named by H15/H18/H19R1 is present and rehashes. The missing objects are outputs of a bounded reuse of that same machine, not missing inputs.

Variable 29 is pivot position 13. Variable 27 is free.

Affine-map E3 coordinate 12 is nonzero in exactly five records, all parameter-free and q-free. With `α = 62500000/27`:

| pivot | coordinate 12 |
|---|---|
| `x_30` | `α (V^2 + 48 U^3)/(V^2 U^2)` |
| `x_31` | `26 α / V` |
| `x_32` | `-20 α U / V^2` |
| `x_33` | `α /(V U^2)` |
| `x_34` | `-α (V^2 - 4 U^3)/(V^2 U^3)` |

No affine-map coordinate-12 term involves `y_27` or any other free variable. Independently, `x_31` times `(84/13) U` equals the H18 unit `(3500000000/9)(U/V)` as rational functions, so a `Q(U,V)` combination of these five constants *can* produce the unit. That is not a multiplier certificate: it ignores E3 mixing and does not cancel `500000000/(27 U) y_27`.

Consequences, still inside FIRST cancellation:

1. The raw `(29,)` term dies after substitution because `x_29 ↦ 0`. If the omitted addend `f1_times_dg2:i=13:j=1` is a multiple of `x_29` as a full E3 polynomial, **omission changes a cancellation cofactor and not the substituted coordinate-12 endpoint**. If that addend has factors other than `x_29`, the endpoint can move. The AWS client must distinguish these by emitting the addend's full E3 support before substitution.
2. The raw `(27,)` term survives as a free linear form in coordinate 12. H18 has empty parameter monomial, so this term is cancelled only by E3 products of other parent terms with affine images that carry `y_27` in coordinates other than 12 (`triangular_solve=free_27;nonzero=8`).
3. The H18 unit is produced by those mixed products (and possibly the five constant affine coordinate-12 images above), not by rewriting the two raw `1/U` records.

H15 already knows `max(map(len, p13), default=0) <= 2` and every FIRST source is linear. Polarization against the 38-row module is exact and does not need Groebner.

Licensed polynomial identities, valid in the total ring `Q[C,V,U]` before any specialization (AWS client must `assert` both):

```text
F = C*U - V^2 + U^3
H = C - 3*U^2
B3 = 4*C^2*U^2 - 4*C*V^2*U + 24*C*U^4 + V^4 - 20*V^2*U^3 + 20*U^6

U*H - (V^2 - 4*U^3) = F
B3 - V^4 = F * 4*U*(C + 5*U^2)
```

The specializations `B3 = V^4` and `U*H = V^2-4U^3` remain licensed **only after** `F=0`. Do not invert `V` or `V^2-4U^3` in the total ring: neither lies in the radical of `U*H*B3` before `F=0`.
