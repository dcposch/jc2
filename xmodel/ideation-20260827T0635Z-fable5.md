## 7. Adversarial scope audit of my best proposal (card 1, `TRIVIAL-LOCUS`)

Attacking my own strongest submission:

1. **Sparse-only blindness.**  The classifier sees finite-support
   `Q[rho]`-sections.  A chart could be raw-unclosable because of a
   *non-sparse* or genuinely formal (infinitely supported) section the
   classifier never visits.  Therefore "no section found through size 3"
   licenses sweeps but must never be quoted as "certificates exist" or
   "the chart is closable" — it is one-directional evidence only.
2. **Obstruction requires chart compatibility.**  A section obstructs a
   `T-a1` certificate only if it satisfies the chart's ambient
   constraints (`J1=0`, ordering-substitution compatibility) in addition
   to killing the rows.  The classifier must record and check these per
   target; a raw section off `V(J1)` obstructs nothing on this chart.
3. **Instantiation-property risk.**  All-depth decidability rests on the
   canonical-tail structure (every deeper-jet monomial vanishes on the
   support).  V35 needed new endpoint depths at grade 19, which shows the
   generating data grows with grade in *variables*; my card makes the
   per-support instantiation check mandatory and fail-closed, but if that
   check fails for interesting supports, the whole mechanism degrades to
   grade-by-grade prolongation and its advertised "forever" strength is
   lost there.  That degraded outcome must be reported as `GAP`, not
   papered over.
4. **Scheme-theoretic solving is not optional.**  V34's Kummer quintet
   proves rational-point search misses real sections; the classifier must
   decide properness of elimination ideals over `Q`.  This costs more
   than point search and is the step most tempting to shortcut; the
   registration should forbid accepting "no rational point" as "empty".
5. **Marginal-value honesty.**  V37-style sweeps are cheap; the expensive
   objects are Groebner prolongations, and the classifier does *not*
   replace them for grade-limited positive-dimensional survivors (a
   support with no all-depth section can still carry deep finite
   survivors that only prolongation kills).  The card's value claim is
   confined to: eternal-nonmembership preflight, the `a1`-unit decision,
   and unification of the section census — not "replaces support search".
6. **Even total success is local.**  A complete `T` plus a positive Q2
   answer changes routing inside avenue 4/31 for this literal source and
   family only.  It proves nothing about coverage, deck/square, `G2-PSC`,
   the off-family load-timing fan, order two, maximum twelve, or JC2, and
   a negative classification is not evidence that Gate T will close.
