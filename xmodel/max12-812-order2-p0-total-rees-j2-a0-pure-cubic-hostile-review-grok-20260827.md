## 6. Ordered T-a1, separated from `A0-CERT-P`

Nothing in this section is a premise of the T-a0 theorem.

Ordered chart: `J1 ∪ {a0} = 0`, exceptional coordinate `a1` only. 31

on this chart are `Q[rho]` (`qa1` is absent). A typed certificate
`a1^N k^M (1 + rho W)` of weight `5N+4M` therefore has, for each
grade-`g` row, a cofactor homogeneous of complementary weight
`5N+4M-g`; in particular every grade-15 cofactor at target weight 15
lies in `Q[rho]`.

### 6.1 Hand obstruction at `(N,M)=(3,0)` — CONFIRMED

Independent scan of every rho-free monomial of all 31 nonzero ordered
rows:

- The only rho-free monomial dividing `a1^3` is `a1^3` in `Tg15_3`,
  coefficient `-1/16` (pin `3c11307ce1acc33dd2b0efd72b4cab76e7ee690b325e0a19c8d1e886ba57d0f1`,
  87 terms). Fable5's truncated pin `3c11307c…` matches.
- The only rho-free monomial dividing `a1 k rs1^2` is `a1 k rs1^2` in
  `Tg15_3`, coefficient `-5/512`.
- No grade-11 or grade-12 row contains `k`. Grade-10 rows are `0`.

Anchor forcing: the `Tg15_3` cofactor is in `Q[rho]`, so its rho-free
part is a constant, and that constant must be `-16` to produce `a1^3`
on the left. The same constant times `-(5/512) a1 k rs1^2` produces
`(5/32) a1 k rs1^2` at `rho^0` on the right. No other rho-free row
monomial divides `a1 k rs1^2`, and cofactors are polynomials, so that
monomial cannot be cancelled. The left-hand side `a1^3 (1 + rho W)`
has no such term at `rho^0`. Contradiction.

This is a theorem about polynomial identities of shape `(3,0)`, not a
theorem that the ordered chart is nonempty, and not a theorem about
`(N,M)` with `M ≥ 1` or `N ≥ 4`.
