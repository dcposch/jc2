## 2. Attack: formal-series unit, field-valued nonzero, algebraic localizer

These are three different operations on three different rings. The producer writes them as one iff. That sentence is the only claim here that is false as written.

Let `R` be the coefficient ring of the literal total source: a polynomial ring over `Q` in the jet names, including `k,k1,k2c,...`. The emitter's series `k10` lives in `R[[sigma]]`.

1. **Formal-series unit.** An element of `R[[sigma]]` is a unit if and only if its constant term is a unit of `R`. The constant term is the indeterminate `k`. That element is not a unit of `R`. Therefore `k10` is not a unit of the unlocalized formal-source series ring. It becomes a unit in `R_k[[sigma]]`, and only there.

2. **Field-valued nonzero coefficient.** At a geometric point with values in a characteristic-zero field `K`, the specialized series is a unit of `K[[sigma]]` if and only if the image of `k` is nonzero in `K`. The family name `unit-k10` is this open. Over a field, “unit” and “nonzero” coincide for the constant term. They do not coincide in `R`.

3. **Algebraic localizer in a certificate.** The `T-rs` identity inverts the polynomial element `k` in a presented chart ring by restricting to `D(k)`, equivalently adjoining `1-v*k`. The V18 `T-cs` localizer list is `(qrs, rho, 1-u*cs, 1-v*k)`: again the polynomial `k`, not the series `k10`. The factor `35` is a unit of `Q`, so `35*k` and `k` cut the same open. This localization is not inversion of the series, and it is not the statement that `k` is already a unit of the unlocalized source ring `A`.

The licensed filing is only this: take the source ring of the *named* Gate-T family to be already the open `D(k)`. Then (2) and the closed complement of (3) are the same set `V(k)`, and (1) becomes a unit after that localization. The factor `k` remains in every identity of type (3). If instead `A` is unlocalized, `V(k)` is a genuine residual stratum of every certificate that inverts `k`. Fable5 recorded both readings. Pinning the first does not empty `V(k)` in the unlocalized source.

“Scope-intrinsic” is therefore allowed only as: inside the named unit-`k10` family, `D(k)` is the family's own open, so it is not an extra on-family residual to budget beside the six Rees charts. It is not allowed as: `k` is a unit of the unlocalized source, or `V(k)` is empty, or the `D(k)` decoration may be erased from theorems.

## 3. Attack: `V(k)`, `V(k10)`, and the sibling fan

Three different closed sets are being asked to share one name.

- **`V(k)`.** Vanishing of the constant term of the source series. This is exactly the non-unit locus of `k10` in `R[[sigma]]`, and exactly the complement of the algebraic localizer `k`. Under the confirmed naming, `V(k)=V(k0)`.
- **`V(k10)` as specialized/raw constant.** Table §5.4 and the odd-sheet promotion write `k10` / `k0` for that same constant. Then `V(k10)=V(k)`. Listing both as two Gate-T strata is the double count Fable5 flagged.
- **`V(k10)` as vanishing of the series.** This is `V(k,k1,k2c,k10_3,...)`, strictly smaller than `V(k)`. Delayed/positive-order load (`k=0`, some later jet nonzero) lies in `V(k)` and not in this set.

The producer is right that counting `V(k)` under each unit-`k10` Rees chart *and* counting a global `k10=0` family is one complement counted twice. The producer is not entitled to flatten the complement into a single scheme-theoretic sibling. Cusp successor already called it a positive-order/zero-load *fan*. Square-branch firewalled lists often separate `k10=0` from positive-order `k10`. This review does not decide that internal split. It forbids only the on-family double count.
