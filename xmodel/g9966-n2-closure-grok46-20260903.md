## 4. The face ODE

Xu (7.1), from Moh Prop 4.1: `∂(T_s(σ), g(σ))/∂(t,π) = -J·(T_s)_f(σ)\,t^{-2+δ}`.
On the §8 leaders `T_3 = q\,t^α`, `g = p^9 t^β`, `(T_3)_f = p^{22}t^{22(-8+3δ)}`

```text
α = 13(-8+3δ) - 1 + δ = 40δ - 105,     β = 9(-8+3δ) = 9(3δ-8),
```

the Jacobian is `t^{α+β-1}(9α q p^8 p' - β q' p^9)`. The `t`-exponents match
the right-hand side (`α+β-1 = 22(-8+3δ)-2+δ`). Cancelling `p^8` (and absorbing
`J∈k^*` together with the omitted leading coefficients of `T_3,g,(T_3)_f`
into a scalar `λ`) gives the *face ODE* in `k[π]`:

```text
(E)   9α q p' − β q' p = λ p^{14},     p' = dp/dπ,  q' = dq/dπ.
```

**Leading-term identity.** For `deg p=3`, `deg q=n`, the `π^{n+2}` coefficient
of the left-hand side is `p_3 q_n(27α − n β)`. For `n=40`,
`27α-40β = 27(40δ-105)-40(27δ-72) = 45`, independent of `δ`. Thus monic `p,q`
of those degrees force `λ=45`. If `n≠40`, the left-hand side has degree `n+2`
unless `n=27α/β=3ρ` with `ρ := 9α/β = 5(8δ-21)/(3δ-8)`. For every extra order
below, `ρ ∉ ℤ`, so `n=40` is the *only* possible degree of a nonzero polynomial
solution (the right-hand side has degree 42). At `δ=5/2`, `ρ=10∈ℤ` and both
`n=40` and the homogeneous `n=30` (`λ=0`, `q ∝ p^{10}`) occur; that is the
survivor.

Ring for the linear runs: `Q[z]_{\le 40} ⊕ Q·λ`, coefficient field `Q`,
unknowns `(q_0,…,q_{40},λ)`, generator order that of monomials in `z`. Matching
names of `p` and `q` prove nothing; the kernel is computed in this ring.

**Valuation budget (Cor 7.5-shaped).** At a root of `p` of multiplicity `m`
and of `q` of multiplicity `r`, the two left-hand terms of (E) have valuation
`r+m-1` and leading coefficient proportional to `9α m − β r`. The right-hand
side has valuation `14m`. Hence either

- *non-resonant:* `9α m ≠ β r`, so `r+m-1=14m`, i.e. `r=13m+1`; or
- *resonant:* `r = ρ m ∈ ℤ_{\ge 0}`, and higher terms must still match.

Extra roots of `q` (not roots of `p`) must be simple (`s-1=0` against
`val(λ p^{14})=0`). Sum of the `r_i` is `≤ 40`. This is the same peeling Xu
invokes ("step by step deduce `q=p^{13}(π-c)`"), made local at each root.
The global all-non-resonant vector on `[1,1,1]` is `r=(14,14,14)`, sum `42>40`,
already over degree; Xu's `p^{13}(π-c)` is `r=(13,13,13)` plus leftover `1`,
