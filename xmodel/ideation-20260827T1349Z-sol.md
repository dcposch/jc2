## 3. New mechanism and cross-avenue connection

### New campaign mechanism: the square-factorial divisor-parity test

For `R=k[P,Q] subset A=k[x,y]`, translate any condition of the form

```text
a^2 b in R,  b square-free in A  =>  a,b in R
```

into the principal-divisor identity

```text
2 div_A(a) + div_A(b) = F^* div_R(r).
```

The local Keller condition controls ramification multiplicities in the
affine part; it does not automatically prove that the half-divisor and parity
divisor descend as *principal target divisors*.  The proposed observable is
the two-saturation/parity defect of the pullback monoid of principal divisors,
with boundary poles retained.  Compute it on one complete decorated boundary
book, together with the component permutation action.  A nonzero defect gives
an explicit obstruction to the claimed factor-closure step; vanishing gives
a concrete lemma that can be compared against `G2-BD`, not a JC2 theorem by
itself.

It also gives a falsification oracle: evaluate the precise endomorphic
