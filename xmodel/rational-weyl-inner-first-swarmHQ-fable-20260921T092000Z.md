## Item 1. Bracket and localization: CONFIRMED

QP-PQ = (1/R')*d*R - R*(1/R')*d = (1/R')*(R*d+R') - (R/R')*d = 1. This uses
only d*f=f*d+f' and commutativity of C(x); it needs R'!=0, i.e. R nonconstant
in characteristic zero. Imports actually used: (i) the PBW basis x^i d^j of
W; (ii) S is an Ore extension of a field by a derivation, hence left and
right Euclidean, so a right (and left) Ore domain; (iii) W is a Noetherian
domain, so it has an Ore quotient ring D, unique up to W-isomorphism; every
element of S is q^-1*w with q in C[x]\{0}, w in W, so the division subring
of Frac(S) generated by W is Frac(S), hence Frac(S)=D. No other theorem.

## Item 2. Right-fraction order and leading coefficient: CONFIRMED

In S with LEFT coefficients, a_m d^m * b_n d^n = a_m b_n d^(m+n) + terms in
which b_n is differentiated and the d-power drops. So ord(AB)=ord A+ord B
and lc(AB)=lc(A)lc(B) in the field C(x); products of nonzero elements stay
nonzero. Directions: the RIGHT Ore condition gives, for nonzero b,e in S,
nonzero u,v in S with b*u=e*v. Since (b*u)^-1=u^-1*b^-1, a*b^-1=(a*u)(b*u)^-1
and c*e^-1=(c*v)(e*v)^-1=(c*v)(b*u)^-1. Equality of the two fractions gives
a*u=c*v after right multiplication by b*u. Subtracting ord(a)+ord(u)=
ord(c)+ord(v) and ord(b)+ord(u)=ord(e)+ord(v), and dividing the matching
lc identities, gives independence of the representative. With b=1 the
definition agrees with the S-order, and on W with the PBW d-degree.

## Item 3. Multiplicativity, inverses, invariance under every T: CONFIRMED

For nonzero b,c choose nonzero u,v with b*u=c*v (right Ore). Then
u=b^-1*c*v, so b^-1*c=u*v^-1 and (a*b^-1)(c*e^-1)=a*u*v^-1*e^-1=
(a*u)(e*v)^-1. From b*u=c*v: ord u-ord v=ord c-ord b and lc(u)/lc(v)=
lc(c)/lc(b). Substituting gives ord=(ord a-ord b)+(ord c-ord e) and
lc=(lc a/lc b)(lc c/lc e). Hence ord:D*->Z and lc:D*->C(x)* are group
homomorphisms (1=1*1^-1 has ord0, lc1), so ord(T^-1)=-ord T,
lc(T^-1)=lc(T)^-1, and for all nonzero A,T: ord(TAT^-1)=ord A and
lc(TAT^-1)=lc(T)lc(A)lc(T)^-1=lc(A) because C(x)* is abelian. The proof
never uses commutativity of D and never replaces C(x) by D. Equivalent
standard form: a multiplicative map from the nonzero elements of a right
Ore domain into a group extends uniquely to the unit group of its Ore
quotient ring; the producer's direct verification is complete on its own.
