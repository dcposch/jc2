## New mathematical connections

### 1. Right-action transport turns D12 into a structured AS lifting complex

Let `A(s,t)=(s-s^3,t)` and let `B` be either displayed tame source
automorphism.  Without a support cap, right composition by `B` is an exact
isomorphism between the coefficient/lifting problems for `A` and `A∘B`.
The only new mathematics is therefore the fixed total-degree-12 box: after
composition with `B^{-1}`, its 182-dimensional coefficient space becomes a
specific sparse transported support rather than all polynomials of a small
degree in `(s,t)`.

Modulo three the AS differential is the identity, so the first determinant
correction is a divergence equation in transported coordinates.  For the
`(9,12)` seed the first residual is `-s^2`, and the visible solution
`R_2=s^2t` explains the correction `3u^2y`.  This gives both a small
hand-controlled positive lane and a full 182-variable completeness lane.
The latter is mandatory for any emptiness claim.

### 2. Fixed-support all-depth survival is already the counterexample gate

There are 91 monomials of total degree at most 12 in two variables, hence 182
output coefficients.  The determinant has total degree at most 22, hence 276
coefficient rows.  For a fixed residue seed, the solutions modulo `3^n` form
nonempty closed subsets of the compact coefficient box.  Nonemptiness for
every `n`, even without a preselected compatible chain, yields a compatible
`Z_3` point by nested compactness.

Its determinant is exactly one.  Each common residue target ball is covered
bijectively by each of the three distinct source residue balls, so the lift
is noninjective over `Z_3`; base change gives a characteristic-zero Keller
counterexample.  Thus each fixed D12 seed has an honest dichotomy: finite
death at some modulus, or disproof of JC2.  Finite survival alone remains
only evidence.

### 3. A support-pressure filtration may expose the first obstruction

The unconstrained rational normalization

`(s-s^3, t/(1-3s^2))`

has determinant one and expands 3-adically by increasing powers of `s^2`.
For the `(9,12)` transform, the canonical next term `9s^4t` lies just beyond
the simple weight boundary `3a+b<=12`.  Finite-degree corrections can reroute
that pressure through `P_t Q_s` cross terms, which is exactly why survival at
one or several extra digits is possible.  Track the first exported monomial
in the filtered determinant complex and compute its cokernel class after all
interior corrections.  A persistent nonzero class would be an effective
fixed-support escape theorem at degree 12; repeated cancellation may instead
expose a genuine formal branch.

This connects rows 3, 4, 12, 19, and 38 without claiming that any one of
their existing theorems supplies the obstruction.

### 4. The two seeds test missing proof-boundary strata, not selected leaves

The `(9,12)` and `(8,12)` leading faces are unit monomial Kummer cores.  The
Q8 and TD6 pipelines must answer a precise source question: does their
normalization include these monomial-core/trivial-parameter boundary strata
at `p=3`, and if so are all divisions integral there?  If not, these seeds
identify a coverage boundary rather than contradicting a selected-leaf
certificate.  Build the interface map symbolically and fail closed on every
division by `3`, rather than substituting them informally into a generic
chart.
