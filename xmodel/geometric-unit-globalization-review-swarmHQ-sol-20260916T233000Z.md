## Geometry and divisor audit — CONFIRMED

The normalization C_E of A1_t in E is a connected smooth affine curve: its coordinate ring is finite over C[t], is a domain, and is torsion-free over the PID C[t], hence finite flat. P:A2->A1 is smooth because its gradient is nowhere zero, so pi:X=A2 x_A1 C_E -> C_E is smooth. At a closed b of C_E, the residue field is C and the scheme fiber is precisely P=a, where a is the image of b. It is smooth and irreducible by the two stated hypotheses, hence reduced and geometrically integral. The geometric generic fiber is integral as well: if P-t factored after algebraic closure of C(t), its finitely many factor coefficients would descend to a finite cover over a nonempty base open, and on a further open both factors retain positive degree and nonzero leading coefficient. A complex specialization there would factor P-a, contradicting the all-fiber hypothesis. Smoothness already gives geometric reducedness. Thus X is smooth, normal and integral, and E is algebraically closed in the function field of its geometric-generic-integral fiber.

For a unit u of the E-generic fiber, both u and u^-1 have no horizontal divisor on X. Every vertical prime divisor is the **single reduced irreducible** fiber pi^-1(b) for one b. Accordingly div_X(u)=pi^*Delta for a finite-support divisor Delta on C_E. This fails if a special fiber splits, as the P=xy control confirms. It does not require Delta to be principal or X factorial.

The chosen line produces Y=ell x_A1 C_E -> C_E finite flat of rank e: C[ell] is free of rank e over C[t] for a degree-e one-variable polynomial, and base change preserves that rank. The rational function u/pi^*g has zero divisor on the inverse-image open; normality of X makes it and its inverse regular there. It therefore restricts as a **unit** on all of Y over that open, including singular or intersecting points. Multiplication by this unit is an invertible endomorphism of the finite locally free h_*O_Y module, so its determinant norm is a unit on the base. ## Quadratic interface — CONFIRMED at its conditional scope

Any nontrivial quadratic E/C(t) can be written C(t)(z), z²=D(t) with D a nonconstant squarefree polynomial; denominators are removed modulo squares. The smooth affine normalization of A1_t is C[t,z]/(z²-D), including the ramified points. Its base change has coordinate ring C[x,y,z]/(z²-D(P)), free of rank two over C[x,y]. Therefore a global unit w=A+Bz has **polynomial** coefficients A,B in C[x,y], and its determinant norm A²-D(P)B² is a unit of C[x,y], hence a nonzero complex constant. Scaling A,B by a complex square root yields the displayed norm-one equation. This polynomiality follows from the global unit and the actual normalization ring, not from clearing generic denominators.

If w is non-base, the map (P,A,B):A2 -> S={U²-D(T)V²=1} is dominant. Indeed S is an integral surface (D is nonsquare in C(T)). If its image had dimension at most one, P nonconstant would force A and B algebraic over C(P). The geometric integrality just established makes C(P) relatively algebraically closed in C(x,y), so A,B would belong to C(P). Then A+Bz would belong to E, contrary to non-base w. The consequence is expressly conditional on a non-base unit over a **quadratic** extension; a unit requiring higher degree does not automatically yield such an S-map. No logarithmic Kodaira/Pell obstruction is proved or imported.
