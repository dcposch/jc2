## 3. Independent exact audit of the `M`-cokernel lemma

### 3.1 Statement and proof

Set

```text
H=X^8-1,
M: Q[X] -> Q[X],       M(Y)=4H Y' + 6H'Y
                         =4(X^8-1)Y'+48X^7Y.
```

For every `d>=0`, direct differentiation gives

```text
M(X^d)=(4d+48)X^(d+7)-4d X^(d-1),                    (3.1)
```

with the second term zero for `d=0`.  If nonzero `Y` has degree `d`, the
leading term of `M(Y)` has degree `d+7` and coefficient
`(4d+48)lc(Y)`, which is nonzero over `Q`.  Hence `M` is injective and every
nonzero element of `im(M)` has degree at least seven.  Therefore

```text
im(M) intersect Q[X]_{<=6} = 0.                       (3.2)
```

Conversely, if `f` has leading term `cX^n`, `n>=7`, use (3.1) with
`d=n-7`:

```text
M(X^(n-7))=(4n+20)X^n-4(n-7)X^(n-8).                (3.3)
```

Subtracting `c M(X^(n-7))/(4n+20)` strictly lowers the degree.  Induction
reduces every polynomial to degree at most six.  Existence plus (3.2) gives
the unique direct sum

```text
Q[X] = im(M) direct-sum Q[X]_{<=6},                  (3.4)
dim_Q coker(M)=7.                                    (3.5)
```

Equivalently, the canonical remainder satisfies

```text
[X^7]=0,
[X^n]=((n-7)/(n+5))*[X^(n-8)]       for n>=8,        (3.6)
```

and is therefore an exact seven-coordinate vector.  A desk exact-rational
mutation replay checked that the remainder of (3.1) vanishes for
`d=0,...,80`.

For the specific global remainder,

```text
M(X)=52X^8-4,
[X^8]=1/13,
[H]=[X^8-1]=-12/13 != 0.                             (3.7)
```

This is consistent with the reviewed fixture

```text
M(X/48)=1+(13/12)H,                                  (3.8)
```

because (3.8) gives `[H]=-(12/13)[1]` in the cokernel.

**Verdict: PROVED, at the exact algebraic scope (3.4).**  This is a
self-contained polynomial-linear theorem, independently derived here.  It
was not stated in the pre-round repository: the prior artifacts prove the
leading-degree obstruction and the one fixture, but not surjectivity modulo
degrees `<=6` or the seven-dimensional cokernel.  This partial synthesis is
not a canonical promotion or a review of any stronger GGV statement.

### 3.2 What the theorem does and does not buy

It **does** turn the question

```text
given a specific polynomial E22 in Q[X], is E22 in im(M)?
```

into exact vanishing of seven rational coordinates.  Consequently, once a
raw compiler proves that all admissible changes of the endpoint are exactly
polynomial `M(Y)`, every global polynomial `H`-multiple is controlled by a
finite obstruction vector.

It does **not** yet turn the whole raw-provenance gap into seven free numbers:

1. R2 supplies rootwise local Morse coefficients in the etale algebra
   `Q[X]/(H)`.  It does not serialize a unique polynomial `E22` in `Q[X]`
   arising from the literal raw `2S/3S` rows.
2. Higher-`u` cleanup and raw sidecars must be proved to change the endpoint
   by polynomial `M(Y)`.  If rational `Y` with `H`-poles is allowed, (3.4)
   is not the relevant cokernel.  R3 proves a pole reduction only for its
   artificial squarefree replacement edge, not the original `8_28` object.
3. The sixteen Galois-stable zero/nonzero patterns of `V8` are only branch
   types.  They do not enumerate the continuous coefficients or prove any
   type is raw-source realizable.  One interpolant per type is insufficient
   without the raw-image equations.
4. Rootwise endpoint value `1` determines a polynomial only modulo `H`.
   The exact `Hq(X)` term is precisely what the raw compiler must produce;
   the cokernel then tests it but does not predict it.

The sound merged interface is therefore

```text
literal raw jets
  -> provenance-constrained U11,U14,V8 and sidecars in Q[X]/(H)
  -> a globally serialized polynomial E22 in Q[X]
  -> rem_M(E22) in Q^7.                               (3.9)
```

T's unified carrier condition

```text
H'*(6V8*U14+(9/2)U11^2)=1  in Q[X]/(H)               (3.10)
```

is the factorwise endpoint condition inside the middle arrow.  G's theorem
supplies the last arrow.  S's demand for raw-row provenance supplies the
first.  None alone excludes a face.

### 5.3 What is new in the square-free direction?

reduced principal divisor remains reduced; G cites the broader known
square-free-preservation equivalence.  The repo already contains the same
etale multiplicity mechanism on an AS109 divisor and names the upstream
square-factorial/root-closed package.  Therefore “Keller pullback is
square-free” is not a new proof route.

S's principal-divisor parity defect, A/K's reduced-versus-factor-descent
split, and G's bounded `w(f,g)` test are different executions of the genuinely
global question: do factors/components descend?  Merge them as D5.  Do not
assume that reducedness implies irreducibility, connectedness, principal
half-divisors, factorial closure, or properness.

### 5.4 R3 and rational pole removal

the replacement edge squarefree is the wrong degeneration for original
`8_28`.  Both can be true at their stated scopes.  R3 is a valuable exact
negative control; no report supplies the typed theorem that the original
