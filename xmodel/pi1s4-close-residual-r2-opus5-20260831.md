**4.2 A closed form for `M_emb` (CHECKED, not promoted).** Two ingredients. *(i) Recursion.*
Let `r` be the length of the minimal (non-embedded) resolution of `C`. After it, `C_r` is
smooth and `I(C_r, E_r) = m_{r-1}`, so exactly `m_{r-1}` further multiplicity-`1` blow-ups are
needed to make `C` transverse to the exceptional configuration at a smooth point; hence
`M_emb(C) = sum_{j<r} m_j + m_{r-1}`. *(ii) Closed form.* Writing `beta_1 < ... < beta_h` for
the characteristic exponents of `C` with respect to a transverse coordinate,

```text
(4.2)   M_emb(C) = mult(C) + beta_h - 1 .
```

Verified by hand on seven germs spanning `h = 1, 2` and both branches of the top-level
Euclidean step:

| germ `(s^a, ...)` | char. exps | mult. sequence | `M_emb` | `a + beta_h - 1` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `(2,3)` cusp | `3` | `2,1,1` | 4 | 4 |

| `(s^6, s^8+s^9)` | `8,9` | `6,2,2,2,1,1` | 14 | 14 |

(each row cross-checked against `sum m_j(m_j-1)/2 = delta` computed from the semigroup

**4.3 The exact reduction (PROVED-HERE, given (4.1)-(4.2)).** Combining,

```text
(M-INF)  <==>  a + beta_h - 1 <= 3d - 3   <==>   beta_h <= 2d + n - 2 ,
```
using `a = d - n`. So the entire residual content of `(M-INF)` is an **upper bound on the last
characteristic exponent of the place at infinity**. Sanity: on the coprime stratum `h = 1` and
`beta_h = beta_1 = b = d <= 2d+n-2` for `d, n >= 1` — with slack `d+n-2`, matching the
charged report's slack `n+d-1` up to the `M` versus `C'^2` shift. The crude route the charged
report warned about is confirmed dead: `beta_h <= 2 delta_infty + a - 1` (from
`c(Gamma) = 2 delta_infty` and `n_h >= 2`) gives only `M_emb <= 2a + 2 delta_infty - 2`, which
already fails on the coprime stratum for `d >= 5`.

**4.4 What supplies the bound: the semigroup at infinity (PROVED-HERE).** `D_1 = im(gamma)`
with `gamma` birational, so its affine coordinate ring is `C[p,q]` with normalisation `C[t]`,
and `delta_aff = dim_C C[t]/C[p,q]`. Filtering by `deg_t`,

```text
(4.3)   delta_aff = #( N \ S ) ,      S := { deg_t h : h ∈ C[p,q] } ⊇ ⟨ d, n ⟩ ,
```
a numerical semigroup (`gcd = 1` by birationality). With
`delta_infty = (d-1)(d-2)/2 - delta_aff`, any **lower** bound on `#(N\S)` is an upper bound on
`delta_infty` and hence, via `beta_h <= 2 delta_infty + a - 1`, on `beta_h`. This is the
mechanism, and it is sharp enough in the small cases.

*Worked case `(d,n) = (4,2)`.* §1.2 already forced the `(2,5)`-cusp: `a = 2`, `beta_h = 5`,

*Worked case `(d,n) = (6,4)`.* Here `a = 2`, `b = 6 = 3a`, so Lemma 1.3 puts us in the `a | d`
regime with `beta_1 > 6` odd, one characteristic pair, germ `(2,beta_1)`,

would break `(M-INF)` at `beta_1 ∈ {15,17,19}`. But (4.3) bites: `S ⊇ ⟨4,6⟩` needs an odd
