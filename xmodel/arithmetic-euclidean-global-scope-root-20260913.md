noninvertible F, Proposition5 gives image counting O(R^d), d=[K:Q],
only for Q or imaginary quadratic K;
Proposition6's broader statement assumes the Uniform Bound Conjecture and
genus>1. None supplies the required existence or opposing growth bound.
The source's clearing-map construction changes which source sublattice
is counted; it does not turn finitely many points into an infinite set.

Diophantine implication. Arbitrary characteristic-zero coefficients are not

real volume, not the lattice population of a possibly thin, nonconvex
inverse image of the target box. Replacing that missing population bound
