## 3. New mechanism and cross-avenue connection

### Differential-subfield ramification lemma

The genuinely new mechanism in this scan is an elementary terminal filter
for every rational normalized coefficient component.

> **Lemma candidate.**  Let `t in C(x)` be nonconstant.  If
> `dt/dx = R(t)` for some `R in C(t)`, then `C(t)=C(x)`; equivalently `t` is
> a Mobius transformation of `x`.

At a finite source point with ramification index `e>1` and finite target
value, `ord(t')=e-1`, whereas `ord(R(t))` is divisible by `e`, impossible.
At a pole of order `e`, equality similarly gives `-e-1=e*m` for an integer
target order `m`, again forcing `e=1`.  Thus a degree-`d` map can ramify only
at source infinity.  Riemann--Hurwitz demands total ramification `2d-2`, but
one point contributes at most `d-1`; hence `d=1`.

This turns normalization into a complete dichotomy.  Positive-genus
components admit no nonconstant `P1` trajectory.  On a rational component,
if the terminal Keller vector field descends to `t'=R(t)`, every nonconstant
trajectory is degree one and can be decided by a finite Mobius substitution.
The hypothesis that the vector field descends must be checked exactly; a
remaining Kummer scale or nonautonomous load is not allowed to be hidden.

The cross-avenue connection is **Hurwitz/invariant normalization (`18+25`)
to strip/terminal ODE rigidity (`3`)**, with an algebraization falsifier for
`4+19`: if a formal AS survivor algebraizes through a constant rational
coefficient curve and its derivation descends, the same ramification lemma
forces a degree-one parameter.  The mechanism is not differential-Galois
avenue `45`; it is a finite algebraic ramification certificate.

History checksum before the `NEW` label found earlier component/genus and
finite-state proposals, but no prior use of the implication
`t' in C(t) => [C(x):C(t)]=1` or its ramification proof.  Thus the full-fibre
quotient atlas below is a new generalization of existing genus work, while
the differential-subfield filter itself is new to the repository.

### Card A — `MAX12-DIFFERENTIAL-QUOTIENT-ATLAS`

- **Dependencies:** frozen order-three coefficient fibre and Taylor
  boundaries; exact `mu_3` weights; confirmed full-absorption boundary;
  reviewed parity result when available; an exact terminal derivation on the
  coefficient ring.
- **Experiment:** generate a separating invariant ring; stratify only by
  factors already forced by discriminants/Taylor denominators; saturate each
  stratum; compute one-dimensional components, normalization, genus, and the
  induced derivation.  For genus zero, choose `t` and test exactly whether
  `D(t)` lies in `C(t)`.  If yes, apply the lemma and substitute the general
  Mobius function into the unreduced source equations.
- **Cheapest discriminator:** on each component, a modular function-field
  degree/genus computation plus characteristic-zero verification of one
  minimal polynomial and `D(t)` membership.  Do not begin with an ambient
  primary decomposition of the full unreduced coefficient ideal.
- **Outcomes:** all genera positive gives immediate exclusion; rational plus
  autonomous gives a finite degree-one system; rational plus nonautonomous
  identifies the missing scale/character to adjoin; a surviving exact Mobius
  solution is a high-priority near-counterexample and must be reconstructed
  through every original Taylor row.
- **Stop condition:** stop a chosen projection after two modular primes show
  dimension or inseparability inconsistent with a curve atlas; change
  invariant coordinates rather than enlarging an ambient Groebner job.
- **Expected information gain:** very high.  Every outcome classifies a
  mandatory residual component or exposes the precise scale obstruction.

### Card B — `AS-SOURCE-COMPLETE-TRANSITION`

- **Dependencies:** hostile confirmation of the erratum; byte-equal corrected
  integer sources; canonical representatives for every accepted digit; a
  precise fixed-degree cap and state schema.
- **Experiment:** define a candidate state containing exactly the residue and
  divided-carry data claimed to determine the next row.  Before graph search,
  prove state sufficiency by symbolic substitution and adversarially search
  for two histories with the same state but different successors.  Only then
  enumerate the directed graph and its terminal strongly connected
  components.
- **Cheapest discriminator:** the same-state/different-successor collision
  test.  It is cheaper and more honest than computing another depth under an
  unproved Markov assumption.
- **Outcomes:** a collision means refine the state or certify unbounded-state
  behavior; an acyclic graph gives a bounded obstruction; a cycle gives an
  all-depth fixed-cap formal branch, not yet a polynomial counterexample;
  support or carry growth along every cycle is an algebraization obstruction.
- **Stop condition:** stop finite-state language if every source-complete
  refinement grows with depth or block-Hankel rank grows without bound;
  retain the resulting unbounded-state theorem.
- **Expected information gain:** very high on the disproof side because it
  distinguishes extinction, recurrent formal survival, and invalid state
  compression.
