### The face identity and its parameters

The charged face identity (batch-2 report, "Exact face equation and
parametrizations") is, with `X = u_s*delta - v_s`, `b = 9X`,
`a = W*X - 1 + delta`,

```text
9a*q*dp/dz - b*p*dq/dz = 9(v_s-u_s)*p^(W+1),
```

equivalently, after dividing by 9,

```text
a*q*p' - X*p*q' = (v_s-u_s)*p^(W+1),
deg p = u_s,   deg q = W*u_s + 1.
```

Here `p` is the face polynomial of the separation, its root multiplicities are
the split partition, and `z` is the face coordinate; `'` is `d/dz` and is never
overloaded as a characteristic or a prime label.

`W` is given in the charged charts only as a number per row (`31, 23, 23, 13`).
It is reproduced in closed form here as

```text
W = (3n - 2*M2)/d_3 = (297 - 2*M2)/11        (n=99, d_3=11)
S1: (297+44)/11 = 31   S2,S3,S4: (297-44)/11 = 23   S7: (297-154)/11 = 13,
```

which agrees with every charged chart. It also agrees with the row-generic form
`W = (-mu_s - 2)/d_s` used by the existing principal-minor split-order driver
`box/g108minor-20260903/g108_minor_driver.py:226`: from the Moh p.150 semigroup
of these rows `-mu_3 = 299 - 2*M2`, so
`(-mu_3 - 2)/d_3 = (297 - 2*M2)/11` identically. The two are the same number,
derived two ways. The degree bookkeeping `deg q = W*u_s + 1` reproduces the
charged `63, 93, 70, 53` exactly.

**The screen does not depend on `W` anyway.** Write `t = W - k`. Then

```text
k = a/X = W + (delta-1)/X,   so   t = -(delta-1)/X = (delta-1)/(v_s - u_s*delta),
```

which contains no `W`. The low branch `nu = k*lam` needs `k*lam` integral, and
`k*lam = W*lam - t*lam` with `W*lam` an integer, so it is equivalent to
`t*lam` integral --- again no `W`. And in the counting bound
`W*a_H + #H + k*a_L <= W*u + 1` the `W` cancels identically, leaving
`#H <= t*a_L + 1`. Every (L) kill therefore depends only on `(u_s, v_s, delta)`,
and (G) depends only on `delta`. This was checked mechanically by rerunning the
local-exponent test for all 78 `(delta, partition)` pairs of the five open rows
together with S8, at every `W` from 5 to 59: **zero** verdicts changed
(`box/g9966n1b3-20260903/w-independence.json`, `status=PASS`). A wrong `W`
cannot turn a live face into a dead one.

### Two exact necessary conditions

**(G) Galois stability.** Write `delta = P/Q` in lowest terms. The minor roots
are Puiseux series in `x^(1/Q)`; the substitution
`x^(1/Q) -> zeta_Q x^(1/Q)` permutes them and multiplies the coefficient of
`x^delta` by `zeta_Q^P`, a primitive `Q`-th root of unity because
`gcd(P,Q)=1`. Hence the multiset of roots of the face polynomial `p` is stable
under `z -> zeta_Q^P z`. Its nonzero roots therefore lie in orbits of size
exactly `Q` carrying equal multiplicity; only `z=0` may be fixed. A partition
that admits no such arrangement is impossible.

**(L) Local exponents.** Let `r` be a root of `p` of multiplicity `lam` and let
`nu` be the multiplicity of `q` at `r`. Both sides of the face identity have a
well-defined order at `r`. The left side has order `>= lam + nu - 1` with
leading coefficient proportional to `X*nu - a*lam`; the right side has order
exactly `lam*(W+1)`. Therefore

```text
either  nu = k*lam  with k = a/X  (and then k*lam must be a nonneg integer),
or      nu = lam*W + 1            exactly.
```

Summing over the roots against `deg q = W*u_s + 1` gives the counting bound

```text
#{high roots}  <=  t * (sum of the low multiplicities) + 1,      t = W - k.
```

Both conditions are pure necessity: no lower bound is promoted to an equality,
and no chart is declared solvable merely because (G) and (L) pass.

## An ideal-preserving reduction for the dense charts

### Why the charts were undecidable

The rows are dense. For `[5]` the median row is 24 KB and the `h=0` band alone
is 30 MB; the whole chart is 51 MB of generators in 111 variables, and the
all-simple chart is 1.09 GB. Batch 2's four bounded `guided_gb` calls on `[5]`
all ended as clean timeouts, and a monolithic `std` here does not finish either.
Feeding such an input to a Groebner engine is the 385 KB-row failure mode again.

### The reduction

A row whose partial derivative in some variable `v` is a nonzero **constant**
`c` is exactly `c*v + w` with `w` free of `v`. Then

```text
R/(c*v+w)  =  k[all variables except v],       v |-> -w/c
```

is an isomorphism, so eliminating `v` is a triangular ring automorphism
followed by a projection: it is exactly ideal preserving. There is no choice,
no localization, no support truncation, and no lower bound promoted to an
equality. In particular the residual is the unit ideal if and only if the chart
ideal is.

`box/g9966n1b3-20260903/band_reduce.py` runs this natively in Singular. Rows are
streamed in increasing size, which is essentially the `h`-adic band order with
the top band first, and each row is pushed through the accumulated substitution
--- applied as a single Singular `map`, one evaluation pass per row rather than
one pass per pivot --- before a new pivot is sought. Phase 2
(`box/g9966n1b3-20260903/phase2.py`) maps the expensive `h=0`/`h=1` rows through
the phase-1 substitution, which collapses them by one to two orders of
magnitude, then pivots again.

**Soundness of a capped reduction.** Because each elimination is an isomorphism,
the residual `J_S` built from any subset `S` of the chart rows sits inside the
image of the full chart ideal `I`. Hence `J_S = (1)` implies `I = (1)` and kills
the chart, while a nonunit `J_S` decides nothing at all and is typed `OPEN`.
This one-sidedness is stated in `decide_residual.py` and is respected below.

## Controls

`box/g9966n1b3-20260903/controls/controls-summary.json`.

**Genuine tame automorphism (negative control), PASS.** The literal tame family
`(f,g) = (x^2 + 2*mu*y, x)` has `J = -2*mu`, constant, and specializes at
`mu = -1/2` to the honest polynomial automorphism `(x^2-y, x)` with inverse
`(u,v) -> (v, v^2-u)`. Run through the same `guided_gb` path with the same
localizer discipline (`mu*mu_inv-1`) it comes back

```text
accepted=true  unit=false  dimension=0  vdim=1  nf_all_zero=true  returncode=0
```

so the pipeline does not manufacture unit ideals out of a locus that genuinely
contains a Jacobian-constant map.

**Perturbed control, RECORDED not claimed.** `run_perturbed_control` is off on
these inhomogeneous localized charts. `guided_gb`'s bad-hint control needs a
predicted Hilbert length, which an inhomogeneous chart does not supply, so
firing it would be vacuous. No promotion in this lane rests on it. (This is the
same posture as batch 2 and the same failure mode already recorded for positive
dimensional charts.)

**Reduction replay gate, PASS --- and it caught a real defect.**
`box/g9966n1b3-20260903/reduction_gate.py` rebuilds the substitution map from
the *published* `SUBST` lines in a fresh Singular session, applies it to the
original rows the reduction consumed, and checks two things: `RESOLVED`, that no
published image still mentions a pivoted variable, and `REPLAY`, that the
multiset of nonzero images equals the published residual exactly. The first run
of this gate **failed**: the reducer was publishing each pivot's right-hand side
as recorded at pivot time, while it was back-substituting only into its internal
resolved map. The published list was therefore stale --- a single Singular `map`
does not iterate, so replaying it gave 162 images instead of 66. The reducer now
publishes the resolved image `IMG[slot]`, and the gate returns

```text
resolved=1   replay_rows=66   published_residual_rows=66   multisets_equal=true
status=PASS  (exact Q and modulo 32003)
```

Anything derived from the stale map before the fix was discarded and recomputed.

**`W`-independence control, PASS.** `box/g9966n1b3-20260903/w_independence.py`
reruns the local-exponent test for all 78 `(delta, partition)` pairs of the five
open rows and S8 at every `W` from 5 to 59 and finds zero verdicts that move
(`w-independence.json`, `W_dependent_verdicts=0`). The one fitted quantity in

## FALLACY-v2 audit

- **Flag/place/series.** The strict-below split window `1<delta<v_s/u_s` is kept
  separate from equality at the ceiling, which belongs to the descended top-root
  partition and is not a split. Denominator shedding (`den(delta) <= u_s`) is a
  window condition and is never confused with the Galois orbit condition (G),
  which is about the root multiset.
- **Per-ray/exit-set charge.** No exit price is asserted; no
  `charge_basis` line is emitted, matching the charged triage's
  `no_exit_price_assertion`.
- **Carrier/attainment.** This is the fallacy the lane exists to remove: the
  charged `survivors` dictionary was a representative list, and it is replaced
  by an exhaustive screen over all 66 pairs. Conversely the 12 survivors are
  typed as necessary faces only, never as attained joint solutions.
- **Floor/attainment.** (L) gives an exact dichotomy `nu = k*lam` or
  `nu = lam*W+1`, derived by matching orders on both sides, not a floor promoted
  to equality; the counting bound derived from it is used only to kill.
- **`sat()` wrapping.** No `sat()` call is used anywhere in this lane. The
  localization is the explicit Rabinowitsch generator `T*c*omega - 1` carried as
  an ordinary ideal generator in the declared ring.
- **Raw remainder degree.** The affine pivot step is not a normal form and does
  not depend on a term order: it is `R/(c*v+w) = k[rest]` with `c` a nonzero
  constant, so no leader can vanish and the zero case is the row being dropped.
- **Variable/ring map.** Every ring is declared explicitly with its
  characteristic and generator order; the substitution is applied as a named
  Singular `map` from `R` to `R`, and the surviving-variable list is recorded
  with each residual file.
- **Prime label/derivative.** In the face identity `'` is `d/dz` and `z` is
  named; `p` and `q` are the face polynomials, never a characteristic. The three
  screening primes are always written as `32003, 32009, 32027`.
- **Merge-free/M-descent.** Not invoked. The Prop 6.3 descent is applied only to
  the no-split complement and only after the strict-below charts are listed as
  separate duties, exactly as batch 2 established.
- **Target/arrival index.** The descended datum `(n',m',M2',V2',K',u')` is kept
  distinct from the original skeleton `(n,m,M,V,u_s,v_s)` throughout; the
  superficial coincidence `K'=9` between S4 and an unrelated `K=9` chart is not
  used.
- **`POSDIM` discipline.** No positive-dimensional component was produced, so
  no point extraction, `(F,G)` reconstruction or `J=const` test was owed. Had
  one appeared it would have been flagged hard, not reported as a
  counterexample.

## Limitations

1. **No new death.** The tally is unchanged at `3 dead / 5 open`. Nothing in
   this report should be read as progress on the count.
2. **The face screen is a screen.** It certifies that at most 12 split leaves
   can occur. It does not kill any of them, and a surviving leaf is not a
   Keller pair.
3. **(G) is derived, not cited to a printed line.** (`W` is no longer a soft
   input: it matches the row-generic `(-mu_s-2)/d_s` identically, and the screen
   is `W`-independent regardless.) The Galois-orbit condition
   is a standard ramification argument on Puiseux expansions and is written out
   above; the OCR of the charged Moh scan around Propositions 6.1--6.4 is too
   degraded to quote a line number for it honestly, so it is presented as a
   proof rather than as a citation. It is used only to kill, and every chart it
   kills at an integral `t` would otherwise have had a face solution. The
   attribution is exact: of the 54 kills, 19 come from (L) alone, 25 from both,
   and **10 from (G) alone** --- `S2/S3/S7` at `delta=5/3` for `[3,1]`, `[2,2]`,
   `[2,1,1]`, and `S4` at `delta=5/2` for `[2,1]`. So if (G) were rejected the
   duty would be 22 split leaves rather than 12, and nothing else in this report
   would change.
4. **Only S1 was touched computationally.** Six of its seven charts have a
   gated phase-1 reduction, and only `[5]` has the full image of the chart ideal
   in the reduced variables (over `Q` and modulo `32003`). One chart has rows
   but no reduction. S2, S3, S4 and S7 have exact specifications and no emitted
   rows in this lane, so 98 of the 105 no-split charts were never built here.
5. **The 12 split joint charts are unbuilt.** No major-incidence, effective
   `T2/T3` or Jacobian coupling was formed for any of them.
6. **The `(1)-(13)` classification completeness of Moh is assumed throughout**,
   as in every prior lane in this line.
