## 3. Centered expansion, two-form, and the `[s^{-1}]J` formula

```text
G_s = g1 + 2s g2 + 3s^2 g3 + O(s^3),
F_t = p' + s f1' + O(s^2),
G_t = q' + s g1' + O(s^2).
```

