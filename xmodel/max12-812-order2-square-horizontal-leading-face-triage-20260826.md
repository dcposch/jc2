# `(8,12)` order-two square fan: horizontal leading-face triage

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: **HAND INITIAL-DIVISIBILITY TRIAGE, CONDITIONAL ON COMPLETE SOURCE
SUPPORT AND NONZERO-COEFFICIENT REPLAY.  NO FAN OR BRANCH VERDICT.**

## Scope

Work on the unit-load generic-square chart `D(p*k0)` with

```text
a=ord_sigma(A)>=1,  r=ord_sigma(R)>=2,
c=ord_sigma(C)>=3.
```

Use the exact lower hull generated by `AC,C2,R3,RC,A2`, with `RA2` retained
only on `(a,r)=(1,2),c>=5`.  This note records the leading faces which die
without a successor computation and the two root-allocation faces which do
not.

## Initial rational forms

Up to nonzero scalar coefficients, the relevant leading Laurent terms are

```text
AC   =  (3/4)*A*C/L,
C2   =  (3/8)*C^2/L^2,
R3   =  (5/16)*k0*R^3/L,
RC   =  (5/8)*k0*R*C/L,
A2   =  (5/32)*k0*A^2/L,
RA2  = -(3/8)*R*A^2/L^2.                           (1)
```

The exact source client must verify (1), the lower-unitriangular
source/Laurent bridge, and that no globally dominated support term joins a
claimed leading face.

## Every `C2`-incident leading face dies

The companion lower-hull lemma proves that `RA2` is leading only at
`(a,r)=(1,2),c>=5`; there `C2` is strictly later.  Thus a leading face
containing `C2` contains no other `L^-2` term from (1).  After clearing
`L^2`, its numerator reduces modulo `L` to

```text
(3/8)*C^2.
```

Polynomiality would imply `L|C^2`.  On `D(p)`, `L` is a squarefree
quadratic and `C` is a nonzero linear polynomial, which is impossible.
This eliminates `C2` whether it is unique or tied with any combination of
`AC,R3,RC,A2`.

## Two other unique faces die immediately

If `A2` is the unique leading term, polynomiality gives `L|A^2`; if `R3`
is unique, it gives `L|R^3`.  Squarefreeness of the quadratic `L` and
linearity/nonvanishing of `A,R` contradict either divisibility.  The unit
`k0` is used here.

## The two allocation faces

If `AC` is unique, polynomiality gives `L|A*C`.  Over the etale splitting
algebra this forces, up to deck swap,

```text
L=u*v,  A=alpha*u,  C=gamma*v.                     (2)
```

If `RC` is unique, `L|R*C` similarly forces

```text
L=u*v,  R=beta*u,  C=gamma*v                       (3)
```

up to deck swap.  Neither (2) nor (3) is a contradiction; successor grades
are required.  The `d=1`, shifted `d=2`, and shifted `d=3` companion notes
address large portions of (2).

## The `RA2` boundary

At `(a,r)=(1,2),c>=6`, the leading face is exactly `A2,R3,RA2`.  Clearing
`L^2` and reducing modulo `L` first gives

```text
L | R*A^2.
```

Thus `A` and `R` occupy opposite roots.  Orient `A=alpha*u`, `R=beta*v`.
At `u=0`, both `A2=A^2/L` and `RA2=R*A^2/L^2` are regular, while

```text
R3=R^3/L=beta^3*v^2/u
```

has an unmatched simple pole.  Hence every `c>=6` point on this boundary is
impossible already at the leading grade.

At `c=5`, `AC` joins the leading face, but the earlier suggestion that it
may cancel the post-allocation `R3` pole is false.  The exact-source
maximal-pole producer and independent hostile review show that the first
root equations allocate `A` and `R` to opposite roots; at the nominated
`A` root, `AC`, `A2`, and divided `RA2` vanish while `R3` gives the unit
terminal `+/- (5/2)k0*lambda^3*bu^3`.  Consequently the single vector

```text
(a,c,r)=(1,5,2)
```

is empty on `D(p*k0)`.  Authority is the narrow promotion
`max12-812-order2-square-d1-tied-a1c5r2-maxpole2-promotion-20260826.md`;
this correction does not extrapolate to a neighboring face.

## What remains after this triage

This note does not eliminate unique `AC`, unique `RC`, mixed leading faces
without `C2`, or the exceptional `RA2` vector.  In particular faces such as
`AC+A2`, `AC+RC`, `R3+A2`, and `R3+RC` can satisfy nontrivial root equations
and must not be killed by a one-term argument.

The note also does not cover positive-order or ramified loads, `p=0`,
`k0=0`, the exact-square zero section, terminal/Taylor conditions, fan
exhaustiveness, order two, maximum twelve, or JC2.
