### New exact invariant observation

Let `B/A` be a rank-three algebra with trace-zero module `E` and Miranda
binary-cubic data.  Under a basis change `g in GL_2(A)`, the binary-cubic
discriminant is multiplied by the appropriate sixth power of `det(g)` (up to
the harmless convention for the determinant twist).  For
`A=C[u,v]`, `det(g)` is a nonzero constant.  Therefore:

> **EXACT:** the zero divisor of the full discriminant polynomial is
> independent of the chosen global trace-zero basis, even though the degrees
> of the four displayed Miranda coefficients are not.

Thus a quadratic presentation gives an intrinsic discriminant divisor of
total degree at most eight.  AL3 does **not** imply a lower bound on that degree:
high-degree coefficients can cancel, and a low-degree discriminant need not
admit a low-degree basis.  The value of the observation is that it supplies a
basis-safe object on which AL3's fixed-basis frontier and the rational-forest
surface obstruction can meet.

There is a second exact local identity.  For two finite free orders
`O subset O_tilde` in the same separable rank-three total quotient algebra,
the determinant of the trace pairing changes by the square of the lattice
index determinant.  Hence, divisor-wise,

`Disc(O/A) = Disc(O_tilde/A) + 2 Index(O_tilde/O)`.

When `O_tilde` is the normalization and all finiteness/flatness hypotheses
hold, the first term is the normalized-cover different and the second is the
conductor/index contribution.  Identifying its local length with a particular
curve `delta` invariant requires checking the actual order and vertical/basepoint
stratum; that identification is not asserted globally here.

## Software and systems self-improvement

### Immediate systems decision: UPGRADE — `ARTIFACT-FINALIZE/v1`

The premature-hash incident exposes a lifecycle bug: a digest was treated as
stable before the producer had irreversibly finished the file.  The remedy is
a two-phase artifact transaction, not more manual checking.

1. A producer writes only to a uniquely owned `.partial` path under an open
   lease containing lane ID, PID/start token, frozen basis, and intended final
   path.  Canonical consumers reject partial paths.
2. The producer closes the body, writes exactly one `BODY-END`, fsyncs, and
   invokes one finalizer.  The finalizer verifies the lease owner, computes
   body bytes/hash, appends the seal, fsyncs again, and atomically renames to the
   final path.
3. Only after the rename does it create an immutable sidecar manifest with
   full SHA-256, body SHA-256, bytes, frozen basis, lifecycle label, producer
   receipt hash, and Git blob ID once staged.
4. Canonical hash references are generated from the manifest.  Hand-copied
   digests are rejected.
5. A staged-tree precommit checker resolves every newly introduced digest
   against the Git index, rejects a still-open lease, detects any post-manifest
   mutation, rejects ambiguous prefixes, and verifies that “promoted” has the
   required review receipts.

**Acceptance test:** deliberately hash a live dummy artifact, append another
line, and require finalization/precommit to fail; then close/finalize a clean
artifact and require its body hash, full hash, manifest, and staged Git blob to
agree.  Also test two writers targeting one final path; exactly one must win.
