## 3. Attack A — Definition 3.3 and no remerging

### A.1 Contact ultrametric

Definition 3.2 (p. 10) first defines series-contact as
`O(series,series*)=min{j: c_j != c*_j}` on two different series of the
same form, then place-contact as the maximum of that quantity over
corresponding representatives, else `-1`.  It does not define `O(P,P)`.
Reflexivity of Definition 3.3 therefore forces the audit convention
`O(P,P):=infinity`.  Without it, `(P,u)~(P,u)` is not even printed.

For three same-side places, raw series-contact is an ultrametric because it
is a first-difference index.  Deck substitutions
`x^(1/kappa) |-> zeta x^(1/kappa)` multiply the coefficient at exponent
`j/kappa` by a unit and preserve pairwise first-difference, so the max over
corresponding representatives remains ultrametric.  Statement 3.2's coherent
`Omega` (proof absent in the thesis; audit `VERIFIED_WITH_NIT`) realises the
same numbers.  Cross-form: `O=-1` on mixed sides, and the inequality holds
by the case split (same-side pair has `O>=0>-1`; mixed pair has both sides
`-1`).  Thus

```text
O(P,R) >= min(O(P,Q), O(Q,R)).
```

This is Lemma A1 / Sol Lemma 2.1, independently re-derived.  It does not
need Statement 3.2, though `Omega`-language later in Part B does.

```text
I_P(t)=I_Q(t)  <=>  (P,t)~(Q,t)  <=>  t<=O(P,Q).
```
