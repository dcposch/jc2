# Early recon of td-13 entry 13-2b

## Executive summary

1. The cap-free budget-(11) seed menu has ten strict-window rows: three carrier-side alternatives and seven genuine opponents to the displayed (3/10) carrier; the latter have gaps (3[5/16]+2[2/5]+2[3/4]).
2. Retaining the full degree (P), as H8 requires, adds three zero-price (u=2)-prefixed resonance contexts of gap (3/8); a thin ((w,M)) deduplication loses them.
3. Corrected H8 (=P/\mu), (\mu\mid M), leaves both (2/5) rows and the (M=3) resonance explicitly inhabited, while charged continuations repair several apparent valuation kills.
4. No promoted fact replaces L-A on the \(M=2,3\) entry branches; **CONJECTURE 13B-SAFE-SUFFIX** is the minimal freeze-like replacement still needed.
5. **VERDICT: (c) STRUCTURALLY HARDER**: the \(k\mid3\) arithmetic is mechanical after its exact cap port, but 13-2b needs new all-off-axis control and the real sibling-X ties need a simultaneous-death lemma.

## 3. What replaces the L-A carrier?

Nothing promoted does.  L-A proves an (M=1) chain uncharged; both entry
states here have (M>1).  R1.2 freezes (w) only after one has already
restricted to neutral (n=1) letters.  NF-D's (M)-ledger is monotone only
on that neutral cylinder, while charged rows above increase (M) to
(4,5,9).  AM makes (M=1) absorbing only after a drop.  Thus these facts
give conditional horns (the (l=1) carrier, B1, and BR1) but do not force
the global route into one of them.  Budget bounds the number of positive-
price events; it does not remove the zero-price BR3 resonance or the
unbounded co-scaled neutral words.

The minimal freeze-like replacement needed before this menu can support a
tower kill is:

> **CONJECTURE 13B-SAFE-SUFFIX.** In a completed Q+E5/E5F-realized direct 13-2b route containing the carrier (1.2) and an opposing atom of gap greater than \(3/10\), the portions of both incoming chains after those two atoms and before their merge consist only of \(M\)-preserving neutral letters.

This is exactly the conclusion needed to stop the charged H8 repairs in
§1.4.  It is genuinely new geometric/E5F/ODE content: it is false at bare
P0+H8+budget tier, as those repairs demonstrate.  It is also only a freeze,
not a kill: B4, B5, and BR3 are already H8-live under neutral continuations.
A first-death/CAP analysis of those rows is still needed.  Separately, the
withdrawn beyond-core bound leaves completeness of the full positive-price
window to the canonical compiler; the seed calculation does not silently
promote that open obligation into 13B-SAFE-SUFFIX.

## 4. The two side obligations

### Sibling-X ties in 13-3 and 13-4

The leaf arithmetic is mechanical but the global tie is new mathematics.  A
neutral (M=1), degree-(2) carrier has
(x(\nu)=(\nu+1)/(2\nu)=1/2+1/(2\nu)), strictly decreasing in legal odd
(\nu\).  Thus away from ties one selects the smallest (\nu); in 13-3 the
(w=4) resonance has ((\Delta,n,\nu)=(4,2,3)) and gap (7/6), so it is
selected whenever present, while in 13-4
(X_*=x(\min_i\nu_i)).  The ties are real under corrected H8: with
(\nu=5), each degree-(2), (M=1) leaf has
((d_p,d_q,P/\mu,g)=(5,6,10,3/5)), while the (M=2) opponent has
((d_p,d_q,P/\mu,g)=(10,6,20/2,3/10)).  Hence 13-3 has a two-X tie at
(3/5), and 13-4 a three-X tie at (3/5).  No promoted restriction lemma
says that a single global ladder may relabel or sequentialize these equal
deaths through a nested merge.  The needed statement is **CONJECTURE SIM-X**:
equal maximal sibling X vertices in an H8-synchronized ladder form one
simultaneous death block, whose forced step must respect the intersection of
all live caps and is invariant under later sibling/nested attachment.

### The (k\mid3) lattice in 13-2d

This is a mechanical CAP-DEN extension once the exact degree-(3) X-side cap
(k\mid3) is proved.  Type ((3,4)) gives
((k_0,l_0)=(3,4)), (\alpha_1=8/3), (g_{\rm top}=7/3), and
(X_\nu=(\nu+1)/(3\nu)) for odd (\nu\ge3); the opponent maximum is
(1/4<1/3<X_\nu).  On the full register lattice
(\alpha\equiv a/3\pmod{\mathbb Z}), (a\in\{0,1,2\}), the X-death
denominator is that of

\[
                  \frac{(a+1)\nu+1}{3\nu}.              \tag{4.1}
\]

It is (\nu) or (3\nu), and at the only smallest case (\nu=3) it is
(9) in all three residue classes; it never divides (3), and a shrink to
cap (1) also refuses it.  This is nonvacuous: neutral (\nu=11) on both
leaves gives (33/1=99/3=33), with gaps (4/11) and (4/33).  The cap is
load-bearing, however: replacing (k\mid3) by the conservative (k\mid9)
creates the genuine escape

\[
                 \frac83-1+\frac49=\frac{19}{9},
                 \qquad 9\mid9.                         \tag{4.2}
\]

So the lattice sweep is routine exact arithmetic; establishing the precise
(3)-cap from the live degree-(3) pole, and showing attachments only shrink
it, is the required port premise.

## 5. Verdict

**(c) td-13 is structurally harder.**  The (k\mid3) block is td-11-shaped,
but 13-2b already defeats each promoted shortcut: H8 is inhabited, zero-price
resonance can preserve (M=3), positive-price (2/5) intruders are live,
charged continuations repair apparent (p)-adic deaths, and (\Omega)
grows inside infinite co-scaled families.  Independently, 13-3/13-4 require
SIM-X for genuine simultaneous deaths.  Thus the current map needs at least
13B-SAFE-SUFFIX, a charged all-off-axis first-death analysis, and SIM-X (or
one substantially stronger global theorem that honestly implies all three),
before the ordinary CAP-DEN/finite-menu rounds can start.  Nested composition
and the Q+E5/E5F refile remain additional perimeter obligations, not hidden
kills.
