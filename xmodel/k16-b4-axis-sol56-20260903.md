**Verdict: EXACT RESIDUAL; the requested all-\(t\) promotion is not proved.**

1.  The frozen recurrence gives an exact indexed formula for every pure

## 1. Typed setup and coefficient target

Put

\[
 q=2t+1,\qquad e=3t+1,
\]
\[
 H_t(y)=12q^2y^2-12q(t+1)y+(t+1)(3t+2),\qquad
 A_t=\mathbf Q[y]/(H_t),
\]
and \(d=2qy-(t+1)\).  Then

\[
 A_t=\mathbf Q[d]/(3d^2-(t+1)).                     \tag{1.1}
\]

This reduced rank-two algebra splits as
\(\mathbf Q\times\mathbf Q\) when \(t=3s^2-1\), with factors \(d=s\) and
\(d=-s\); unit claims must then be checked on both factors.  See
(`k16-terminal-proof-sol56-20260903.md:45-72`) and cone gate
(`k16-cone-gate-gpt55-20260903.md:43-61`).

Let

\[
 R_t=A_t[b_4,q_{2,0},\ldots,q_{t-1,0},b_3],\qquad
 I=I_{t,+}=(T_{t,1},\ldots,T_{t,2t-1}).
\]

The weights are

\[
 \operatorname{wt}(b_4)=1,\quad
 \operatorname{wt}(q_{j,0})=j,\quad
 \operatorname{wt}(b_3)=t+1,
\]
and \(T_{t,k}\) is homogeneous of weight

\[
 w_k=4t+1-k\quad(1\leq k<2t).                       \tag{1.2}
\]

Define the actual axis map, rather than identifying it with an affine chart,

\[
 \pi:R_t\longrightarrow A_t[b_4],\qquad
 q_{j,0}\mapsto0,\quad b_3\mapsto0,
\]
with kernel \(P=(q_{2,0},\ldots,q_{t-1,0},b_3)\).  Homogeneity gives unique
coefficients \(c_{t,k}\in A_t\) such that

\[
 \pi(T_{t,k})=c_{t,k}b_4^{w_k}.                     \tag{1.3}
\]

For \(k=0\), the corresponding statement is
\(\pi(T_{t,0})=T_{t,0}(0)+a_tb_4^{4t+1}\), treated in Section 6.

The frozen JSON stores \(R=-E_t\), while the question/Sol convention is
\(T_{t,k}=[X^k]E_t\).  All coefficients recorded here use the latter, so they
are the negatives of the frozen expressions.  Negating all rows changes
neither ideals nor norms.

## 4. The \(b_4\)-axis radical statement

Let

\[
 C_t=(c_{t,1},\ldots,c_{t,2t-1})\subseteq A_t.
\]

From (1.3),

\[
 \pi(I)=(c_{t,k}b_4^{w_k}:1\leq k<2t),
\]
so the exact axis-killing condition is \(C_t=A_t\).  A single unit coefficient
is sufficient.  At a split index it is not necessary: two complementary zero
divisors can generate \(A_t\) even though neither is a unit.  Fibrewise,

\[
 V(\pi(I))=V(b_4)\ \cup\ \bigl(V(C_t)\times\mathbf A^1_{b_4}\bigr).  \tag{4.1}
\]

If \(C_t=A_t\), lifting the quotient statement gives precisely

\[
 b_4\in\sqrt{I+P},\qquad
 P=(q_{2,0},\ldots,q_{t-1,0},b_3).                    \tag{4.2}
\]

It does not give \(b_4\in\sqrt I\).  After base change to a geometric fibre
\(A_t\to K\), a weighted counterexample is

\[
 J=(q_{2,0}-b_4^2,q_{3,0},\ldots,q_{t-1,0},b_3).      \tag{4.3}
\]

The axis restriction contains \(-b_4^2\), and all later coordinate-direction
claims hold.  Nevertheless

\[
 R_{t,K}/J_K\simeq K[b_4],
\]
so \(b_4\notin\sqrt J\) and the cone has a nonzero curve with
\(q_{2,0}=b_4^2\).  Thus all proposed coordinate restrictions can hold while
the global conclusion fails.

The genuine statement has the equivalent forms

\[
 b_4\in\sqrt I
 \Longleftrightarrow b_4^N\in I\text{ for some }N
 \Longleftrightarrow I R_t[b_4^{-1}]=R_t[b_4^{-1}]
 \Longleftrightarrow I|_{b_4=1}=(1).                  \tag{4.4}
\]

The last equivalence homogenizes a chart certificate.  This is the whole
chart, with \(q,b_3\) variable, not the point
\(q=b_3=0\).  The charged `TOPT` test is a sufficient stronger version using
only the top rows (`k16-terminal-proof-fable5-20260903.md:276-284,380-402`).

## 5. Cone assembly and order of subchart arguments

The requested order is correct only after (4.4), not (4.2), is proved.  Work
on a geometric fibre \(A_t\to K\), put

\[
 B=(b_4),\qquad Q=(q_{2,0},\ldots,q_{t-1,0}),\qquad
 \mathfrak m=B+Q+(b_3).
\]

Use the elementary absorption identity

\[
 J\subseteq\sqrt L\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\sqrt{L+J}=\sqrt L.       \tag{5.1}
\]

Assume the genuine global \(b_4\in\sqrt I\), then (10.1), then Theorem
B3-AXIS.  Applying (5.1) in exactly that order gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt I
 &=\sqrt{I+B} &&(b_4\in\sqrt I),\\
 &=\sqrt{I+B+Q} &&(q_{j,0}\in\sqrt{I+B}\text{ for every }j),\\
 &=\sqrt{I+B+Q+(b_3)} &&(b_3\in\sqrt{I+B+Q}),\\
 &=\mathfrak m. &&(I\subseteq\mathfrak m)
\end{aligned}                                                        \tag{5.2}
\]

The irrelevant ideal is maximal fibrewise (over split \(A_t\), radical but not
globally maximal).  Equation (5.2) gives (V0), then (8.1); see
`k16-cone-gate-gpt55-20260903.md:63-94`.

The expression suggested in the task,

\[
 \sqrt I\supseteq (b_4)+\sqrt{I+(b_4)},
\]

is false in general.  Monotonicity goes the other way:
\(\sqrt I\subseteq\sqrt{I+(b_4)}\), and the right side of the suggested
expression is simply \(\sqrt{I+(b_4)}\).  Its reverse containment becomes true
only after \(b_4\in\sqrt I\) is already known, when it is the first equality
of (5.2).  Without that global first step, (10.1) plus B3-AXIS proves only
\(\sqrt{I+B}=\mathfrak m\), namely that the \(b_4=0\) slice is the origin.

## 6. Alternative component/\(\tau_t\) branch

Define the homogeneous part without relying on the global sign convention:

\[
 \tau_t=T_{t,0}-T_{t,0}(0),\qquad
 \pi(\tau_t)=a_t b_4^{4t+1}.                         \tag{6.1}
\]

If all \(c_{t,k}\) vanish on a fibre, its whole \(b_4\)-line lies in \(V(I)\).
This is containment, not a component claim; the latter also needs \(P\) minimal
over the fibre ideal.

The exact tau criterion on the restricted axis is

\[
 \pi(\tau_t)\in\sqrt{\pi(I)}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a_t\in\sqrt{C_t}.       \tag{6.2}
\]

Since fixed \(A_t\) is separable Artinian, \(\sqrt{C_t}=C_t\).  Fibrewise,
\(a_t\) must vanish on every factor on which all positive coefficients vanish.
Failure refutes (8.1) on that fibre; success only makes this line harmless.

Once the q-directions and B3-AXIS have killed the \(b_4=0\) slice, the exact
weaker all-chart target for (8.1) is

\[
 \tau_t|_{b_4=1}\in\sqrt{I|_{b_4=1}},                 \tag{6.3}
\]

which may be tested by the Rabinowitsch ideal

\[
 1\in\bigl(I|_{b_4=1},\ 1-z\tau_t|_{b_4=1}\bigr).    \tag{6.4}
\]

Chart unitness proves (V0); (6.3) is the exact tau route to (8.1).  The \(k=0\)
recurrence evaluates \(a_t\), but supplies no fixed all-\(t\) factorization.
No nonunit fibre occurs for \(t=2,\ldots,7\); uniformly, both \(C_t=A_t\) and
the off-axis tau test remain open.

## 8. Verdict and exact residual

Every positive \(c_{t,k}\) is a unit for \(2\leq t\leq7\), including both
\(t=2\) factors.  Uniformly, only (2.1)-(2.2) is proved.

What is **not** proved is

```text
OPEN[B4-AXIS-UNIT-UNIFORM]:
  C_t=(c_{t,1},...,c_{t,2t-1})=A_t for every integer t>=3.
```

A sufficient target is \(\mathcal N_{t,1}\ne0\) for all \(t\ge3\); here it is
represented only by an indexed recurrence, and no fixed polynomial was derived.

Even promotion of that statement would yield only (4.2).  The exact missing
cone input is independently

```text
OPEN[B4-GLOBAL]:
  b4 in sqrt(I_{t,+}) for every integer t>=3,
  equivalently I_{t,+}|_(b4=1)=(1), or b4^N in I_{t,+}.
```

Only `OPEN[B4-GLOBAL]`, followed by (10.1) and B3-AXIS in the order (5.2),
implies \(\sqrt I=\mathfrak m\), \(\dim I=0\), (V0), and then (8.1).  Therefore
the requested “\(b_4\)-axis analogue” does not close delta 17(jjjj), and
claiming that it does would be the precise subchart-to-full-radical fallacy
that the charged gate forbids.
