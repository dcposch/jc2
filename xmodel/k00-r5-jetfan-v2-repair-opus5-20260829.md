## 4. Leading cone, rank fan, cokernel structure

Put `Q_ell :=` transverse-degree-2 part of `R_ell` and

```text
A(q) = 16 q1 - 4 q3 + q5,        B(q) = q0 - 4 q2 + 2 q4.
```

Two exact identities, verified as polynomial identities in six variables:

```text
Q3 + (1/8) Q1 = (3/16384) A B,
Q4            = (3/524288) (B^2 - 64 A^2).
```

*Zero set, both inclusions.*  `Q_ell` vanishes identically on `A=B=0` for all
seven `ell` (checked on the parametrization below), so `V(A,B) ⊆ V(Q)`.
Conversely, over a field the two identities give `AB=0` and `B^2=64A^2`; `AB=0`
forces `A=0` or `B=0`, and either case forces the other to vanish.  Hence

```text
V(Q_1,...,Q_7)_red = V(A,B),
x = ell4(a,b,u,v) = (2b+2u, a, b, 8a+v, b-u, 16a+4v),
```

a 4-plane; the parameters are intrinsically `u = x2 - x4`, `v = x3 - 8 x1`.
This is a reduced field-valued split; no nilpotent scheme claim is made.

*Differential.*  For `x` on the 4-plane the exact factorization
`DQ_i(x)[q] = alpha_i(x) A(q) + beta_i(x) B(q)` holds with

```text
i      alpha_i                beta_i
1      (3/1024) u            -(3/1024) v
2      (3/256)  v             (3/16384) u
3     -(3/8192) u             (3/8192) v
4      0                      0
5     -(3/131072) u           (3/131072) v
6      0                      0
7     -(3/1048576) u          (3/1048576) v
```

Rows `1,3,5,7` are all proportional to `(u,-v)`; row `2` is proportional to
`(64v, u)`; rows `4,6` vanish.  Every nonzero `2x2` minor is a nonzero rational
multiple of

```text
Delta = u^2 + 64 v^2.
```

Hence `rank DQ(x) = 2` iff `Delta != 0`, `= 1` iff `Delta = 0` and
`(u,v) != (0,0)`, `= 0` iff `u = v = 0`.  This exhausts the cases; the fan is a
reduced field-valued split.

*Cokernel.*  Since the image of `DQ(x)` is `{(alpha_i A + beta_i B)_i}` and
`(A,B)` is onto `K^2`, the five functionals

```text
E4,  E6,  E3 + (1/8) E1,  E5 + (1/128) E1,  E7 + (1/1024) E1
```

annihilate the image at every point of the 4-plane, and they span the full
cokernel when `Delta != 0`.  When `Delta = 0` with `u = eps*8i*v`,
`eps in {+1,-1}`, the image drops to a line and there is exactly one further
independent functional,

```text
E2 + (eps i / 2) E1.
```

This sixth functional is the whole of the repair in Section 6; it is the row
the rejected producer did not have.

def qsub(poly,ds,md):
    """substitute q_i -> ds[i] (truncated series); drop degrees > md"""
    out=S(N); cache={}
    for k,v in poly.items():
        if sum(k)>md: continue
        term=S(N); term.c[0]=P.c(gg(v))
        for i,e in enumerate(k):
            if not e: continue
            if (i,e) not in cache: cache[(i,e)]=ds[i]**e
            term=term*cache[(i,e)]
            if term.order() is None: break
        out=out+term
    return out
