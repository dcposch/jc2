## Three detailed idea cards

### Card 1 — `NEW CONNECTION`: a horizontal cubic residue and a finite square fan

**Mechanism.**  Regard the cleared receiver modulo `L` as a section of the
residue algebra `k[z]/(L)`, not as a scalar tied to one coordinate chart.  The
V5 identity says that first-order motion of `p` adds a connection term already
generated by the grade-fourteen equation:

```text
Delta = -ell*Num14 mod L,
Num15moving = -A^3 mod (L,Num14).
```

This suggests a precise contact-raising lemma: throughout the polyhedral
region in which `R,C` have sufficiently high contact relative to the first
nonzero `A`, the first non-`L`-divisible obstruction is always the cube of that
leading `A` coefficient.  If true at arbitrary shifted `A` order, every finite
`ord(A)` is impossible; V5 is the first horizontal-connection check, not the
induction itself.  The nonsquare `U^2` closure is the positive control showing
why the raw nonreduced ideal and its ramified rays must be retained.

**Dependencies.**  The reviewed square half-weight source/Faber bridge and
fixed-`p` A-prolongation; producer V5 moving-`p` identities; the complete raw
square normal ideal, including `p,R,C,A` and load motions.  Do not use the
radical alone.

**Cheapest discriminator.**  Before another large saturation, compile a
symbolic valuation table for the first possible nonpolynomial terms as a
function of `(ord(A),ord(R),ord(C),ord(p-p0))`.  Demand an exact finite cone
cover.  Reuse the cube separator on the stable cone; run the already launched
`c=1,c=2` clients; add only the missing `c=3`, `ord(R)=1`, mixed equality, and
root-allocation faces.  Check chart overlaps from the complete source rows.

**Outcomes.**  A horizontal cube on every high-contact cone gives an induction
forcing `A=0` there.  Units on every low cone reduce the generic `p != 0`
square branch to its tangent/zero section; terminal and both Taylor receivers
must still decide that section before the component is called closed.  A
nonunit cone is promoted immediately to a source component, normalized, and
sent to terminal/Taylor reconstruction on both the proof and counterexample
lanes.  Failure of the cone cover means the fan model, not JC2, must be
redesigned.

**Stop condition / expected information.**  Stop square AWS fan-out when every
normalized source cone is either unit/contact-raised or routed to one explicit
surviving component with overlap proof, and the tangent/zero section has a
named terminal/Taylor successor.  Expected information gain: **very high**;
this is the shortest route to deciding the only remaining order-two
first-normal component.

### Card 2 — `NEW`: TD6 as a rank-one conormal line with a quadratic norm atlas

**Mechanism.**  First separate genuine rank-drop geometry from a bad pivot
trivialization.  The common `FGL` denominator across `q2`--`q10` suggests one
rank-one CURRENT functional represented in nine local coordinates.  Compute it
fraction-free as a cokernel line/Plücker section over `Q[C,V,U]`; denominators
then become transition functions unless they occur in the relevant Fitting
ideal.

The producer identities give a particularly small exceptional atlas.  On
`D(U)`, put `x=V^2/U^3`, `s=F/U^3`, `g=x^2+4s^2`.  Besides the displayed
formula for `L/U^15`, there are exact norm interpretations

```text
g = Norm_{Q(i)/Q}(x+2*i*s),
L/U^15 = Norm(2*g + x*(x+2*s)*y),   y^2=-(x+4).
```

Thus `F=0` is the rational line `s=0`, `G=0` splits over one quadratic
extension, and `L=0` pulls back to one low-degree equation on another
quadratic cover.  Over an algebraic closure the intersections are tiny: on
`D(U)`, `G=L=0` gives only `(x,s)=(0,0),(-4,2i),(-4,-2i)`, while
`F=L=0` gives `x=0,-8`.

**Dependencies.**  Reviewed V78/V81/V82 Stage-A source typing, exact CURRENT
replay from original rows, producer V82QSD/V82QSF identities, both dead-axis
columns, and the q11/q16 symbol-split control.  The norm atlas must not be
treated as a source-fibre theorem before review and raw rebuild.

**Cheapest discriminator.**  Build one universal denominator-cleared
`q2`--`q10` column family and compute two independent pivot orders plus the
presentation-independent maximal-minor/Fitting support.  If `F,G,L` cancel in
the global section, retire them as chart denominators.  If they are genuine,
raw-rebuild CURRENT on `F=0`, the two `G=0` norm charts, and the `L=0` quadratic
cover, with the finite intersections as unit tests.  Reconcile with q11--q14,
q16--q24, q15 gauge, and `d10,d15` before any K2.

**Outcomes.**  If the full affine CURRENT system is inconsistent on the
generic open, work moves only to explicit factor strata.  If it is consistent
with zero homogeneous kernel, it has a unique square-zero correction rather
than “no deformation”; only a nonzero surviving kernel licenses K2.  Units or
inconsistency on every raw factor chart close the fixed A3 section.  A genuine
current survivor is simultaneously a proof debt and a possible boundary-
family seed.  Pivot dependence without Fitting/Bezout glue invalidates only
the presentation.

**Stop condition / expected information.**  Stop isolated q-axis and K2 jobs
once the global CURRENT section and factor cover are exact.  Expected
information gain: **high**; it replaces at least 27 naive factor-by-axis
rebuilds with one module and a small norm tree.
