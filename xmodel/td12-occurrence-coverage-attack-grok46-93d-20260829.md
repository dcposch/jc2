component. Let `γ` be the characteristic sequence of a pole `P_j`,
descending to `(0,y)` (Proposition 9.2). Then:

1. `γ` is a finite chain of vertices. (The Eggers–Wall object attached to

   `a(P)=max_j min(v_j,O(P,P_j))`.)
2. `M` is defined at every vertex of `γ` (Notation 8.1 / T7 pin `M=b` at
   poles, with the listed P4 inputs). Let `V*(γ)` be the earliest vertex
   of `γ` at which `M≠1`, or the root `(0,y)` if none exists. This is
   well-defined because `γ` is finite and totally ordered rootward.
3. If `b_j=1`, then `M=1` at the pole. On a merge-free `M=1` ancestry,
   `M=1` propagates and the first break has the resonant-jump anatomy of
   `MP5`--`MP7`. Combined with the finite `w`-alphabet (DEPTH DS3) this is

   pole-side subtree of `V*` inside `U` is the singleton `{P_j}`. The
   entire continuation of `γ` below the pole is an off-axis configuration.
5. Unused actual microchildren at vertices of `U` are pairwise distinct
   Statement-3.18 orbits. Flags off `U` attach uniquely at `I_P(a(P))`.
   Their first-separation carriers may be inserted once into Corollary 7.1

## 4. No-go: `td=12` and the U1 equality route are unforced

### 4.1 Interface impossibility

Write `Φ_td12` for the statement “the T2-selected Sigray-normalized pair
`F` has `td(F)=12`”, `Φ_U1` for “some fibre of `F` has an actual U1
merge”, and `Φ_B25` / `Φ_S17` for named occurrences of those vertices.

The available universal lower bound is T5: `td(F)≥6`. A `td≥12` floor
exists only as a **route-conditional** source-mass estimate for the
corrected labelled one-P0 U2 family with three arrival multiplicities
equal to three. That estimate is not a theorem about `F`. Moreover the
same labelled family is then representative-dead at equality
(`5+5+1>td-2=10`). A killed route cannot force the selected pair to
realize the equality case of its own floor.

U1, as reviewed, is a labelled arithmetic-progression schema. Its
instantiation on `F` is an extra existential hypothesis. The pattern-tier
witness at type `(2,3)`, `td=12`, `m=3`, `[2,2,2]` shows that *if* such a
merge occurs with those entry data, the formal continuation space is
infinite in `ν_G`. It does not show that `F` has those entry data.

B25 and S17 additionally require type `(2,3)` and a specific dirty child
(resp. sibling) of a U1 trunk. Inside the single advertised U1
progression, legal `ν_G` are `5,7,11,13,17,19,23,25,…`. The dirty step
with `ν=25` landing at `(w,M)=(2/3,3)` is one formal P0 child, not a
forced vertex. The S17 cell is a different route state; the two `ν=17`
siblings share a parent family but are not identified with B25.

No cited GGV result, sheet theorem, or Euler inequality supplies
`td(F)≤12`. Corollary 7.1 is an inequality floor. KJN is type-relative
and becomes an absolute ceiling only after a finite/cofinal type menu,
which is absent (REDUCTION §7.2). Therefore neither (A) a sandwich
`6≤td≤12` forcing equality at 12, nor (B) exclusion of every `td≠12`, is
available.

Hence `JC2 false ⇒ Φ_td12` is **ABSENT**, and so are
`JC2 false ⇒ Φ_U1`, `Φ_B25`, `Φ_S17`. Conditional lemmas of the form
`Φ_td12 ∧ Φ_U1 ⇒ (contradiction ∨ Φ_B25 ∨ Φ_S17)` may still be
meaningful; they are not occurrence theorems, and their hypothesis is not
discharged. That is exactly `TD12-U1-ACTUAL-LANDING`. It is not forced,
and it is not the smallest next lemma (Section 5).

## 6. Cross-connection audit

Promote none of the following. Each lacks a complete arrow from the T2
pair to a usable conclusion.

**Minimal topological degree.**
T5 gives `td≥6`. Well-ordering of `N` yields a td-minimal counterexample
if JC2 is false; that pair need not be the T2 GGV-minimal pair, and
minimality in `td` is not minimality in `gcd(deg P,deg Q)`. No theorem
identifies the two minima. No theorem computes the minimum. Selecting a
td-minimal pair still leaves Escapes A, B, D at `td=6,7,8,9,10,11`.

**Degree divisibility.**
T7 writes `Λ=abαβ/ν` and `td=ΣΛ`. This constrains entries at fixed `td`,
not `td` itself. TDBOUND law (ii) `td | (αβ) Π b_i` is a conjecture,
evidence-downgraded, and even if true would not force `td=12`.

**Finite/cofinal type control.**
At fixed `td`, `β≤td` so only finitely many types occur (T7 arithmetic).
There is no type menu independent of `td`. KJN(`C`) implies
`td≤C αβ` only after such a menu and provenance (`REDUCTION.md` §7.2).
The arrow “finite types ⇒ `td` ceiling ⇒ occurrence” is therefore
missing its first hypothesis.

**GGV minimality.**
T2 selects `F_GGV`. TRANSPORT carries its type. The GGV farm, Laurent
cuts, and Proposition 4.3 (`[P,Q]=x^2`) are not polynomial Sigray inputs
(Critical 3). Residue-A degrees `(168,252)` and the `(72,108)` record are
distinct lex-minimal pairs (TRANSPORT 5.2). GGV minimality does not
prefer `td=12`.

**Boundary-tree Euler accounting.**
Reviewed Corollary 7.1 is an actual-weight **inequality**. Printed (22)
and `(22-cl)` are unproved. First-separation carriers are floors. A floor
`Σλ≤td-2` cannot force `td=12` and cannot name a vertex. MP8's
equality-based no-charge claim remains quarantined.

**Monodromy.**
U1 rigidity `Rad=T^r-A` means the arriving orbit bases are the `r`-th
roots of one number, i.e. a single `(rν)`-orbit. That is coefficient
shape on an already-assumed U1 merge. It is not a monodromy constraint
forcing U1, B25, or a degree. Deck-orbit residues at B `j=17` vanish
identically. No complete arrow.

**TDBOUND law (i) `td≤αβ`.**
Unfalsified, coincidence-risk, not a theorem. If it were proved, type
`(2,3)` would satisfy `td≤6`, hence `td=6` by T5, and the type-`(2,3)`
td12/U1/B25/S17 target would be **impossible**. The live frontier would
be residue-A at equality `td=αβ=6` (Escape A). This is a sharp interface
tension, not a promotion: the current shorthand target is incompatible
with the leading candidate degree ceiling. Type `(3,5)` would still allow
`td=12≤15`; that sector's 14-cell skeleton is list-relative dead
(`BOOK-TD12`) and is not the U1 `[2,2,2]` family.

**`G2-PSC` / `G2-BD`.**
Neither implies the other. `G2-PSC` remains absent and is bypassable by
pure Sigray. `G2-BD` is a post-residue-A delay bound; Escape A is
residue-A itself, so `G2-BD` cannot be invoked to force td12.
