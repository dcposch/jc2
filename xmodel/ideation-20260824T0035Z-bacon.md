## 2. Reranked bottlenecks

### Proof direction

1. **Global source and typed landing/coverage.**  Either prove a pure Sigray
   source theorem, or prove `G2-PSC` with provenance; then define every allowed
   post-jump transition and prove coverage.  The present reduction does not
   permit mixing the two starts.
2. **A Keller-specific degree/type capacity.**  `DIR <=> PC <=> RPMC => KJN`
   gives only a type-relative ceiling.  A proof still needs bounded type
   provenance or a new invariant that charges each pole-tree event against a
   globally finite budget.
3. **The residue-A exit.**  The live sufficient chain is only
   `A-SCALE => UCD-A-min => G2-BD`; neither unrestricted UCD nor the formal
   towers prove or disprove these residue-A statements.
4. **Exact local exclusions and external perimeter.**  Exact rational cCa/R1
   certificates and verified third-party terminal certificates can secure a
   degree frontier, but even complete success here does not close JC2 without
   the reduction/transcription bridge and a cofinal degree argument.

### Disproof direction

1. **D43 common integral model plus a standard-smooth point.**  This is the
   nearest bounded test that can turn modular survival into a characteristic-
   zero coefficient point.
2. **Compatibility and algebraization after a D43 lift.**  A point of the
   truncated D43 system is not a Keller counterexample; it must extend through
   the inverse system and yield an honest polynomial map satisfying every
   reduction hypothesis.
3. **Algebraize a formal tower or find an odd-characteristic lift.**  The
   A-SCALE/DIR formal families and characteristics 3/5 are valuable because
   they attack assumptions orthogonal to the current finite book, but neither
   presently supplies an actual Keller map.

The first operational bottleneck is slightly different from either list:
replay and provenance-crosswalk the newly found SuperMind and Ziwei Guo
artifacts before making any independence-count claim, while preserving the
already replayed Helali/Suzuki support at its explicitly conditional perimeter.

  prime-specific NF presentation and fixed-fiber band-34 bottleneck by one
  proof-producing object that reduces identically at both audit primes.
- **New evidence required:** an independently reviewed integral 34+184-row
  emission; exact reductions matching both canonical modular presentations;
  an all-218 replay modulo `p^2`; a unit Jacobian minor at a full-cell point;
  localized syzygies showing that the remaining rows lie in the ideal generated
  by the pivot rows; and a flatness/standard-smoothness certificate.

  census at two primes, the 184/184 source-to-NF trace at 105337, the source-

### Card B — Boundary torsion passport and blow-up capacity

- **Target claim:** construct a coordinate-invariant boundary passport from the
  homogenized Jacobian derivative matrix that (i) can be computed on both a
  GGV corner and a Sigray pole tree and (ii) charges nontrivial pole-tree events
  against a finite Keller-specific capacity.  A successful first version may
  yield only a type-relative bound; an absolute/cofinal bound additionally
  needs type provenance.
- **Avenue IDs:** 2, 7, 25, 26, 28, 31, 33, 34.
- **Novelty:** let `A` be the homogenized 2-by-2 derivative matrix with
  `det(A)=j Z^N`, pull it to a log resolution, saturate its cokernel, and record
  generic Smith exponents on every boundary component together with residual
  Fitting lengths at component intersections.  The proposed decorated dual
  graph is a **torsion passport**.  The experiment is to discover and then
  prove a blow-up balance law in which each genuine Sigray jump consumes a
  nonnegative residual length.  Because the construction starts from the map,
  its valuation data could also serve as the missing invariant fingerprint for
  a GGV-to-Sigray transport rather than identifying the two coordinate frames
  by assertion.
- **New evidence required:** exact Fitting/Smith profiles for a diverse tame-
  automorphism control suite, the high-kappa Hénon tower, solved GGV charts, and
  the residue-A local model; a coordinate-change and blow-up invariance proof;
  an exact dictionary from Sigray decorations to passport entries; and a
  non-tautological capacity inequality using more than the determinant order
  alone.
- **Dependencies and evidence tiers:** the pure-boundary determinant identity
  and thick-line support are exact.  Nonnegativity, additivity under blow-up,
  recovery of Sigray types, and any capacity bound are conjectural.  The
  Hénon tower is an exact negative control against accidentally reproving
  unrestricted UCD/K2C.  A GGV-to-Sigray conclusion remains conditional until
  provenance and completeness are proved.
- **Cheapest discriminator:** implement local Smith/Fitting-profile extraction
  for small tame maps and the Hénon family, then perform the same calculation
  after explicit source/target coordinate changes and one blow-up.  Stop if
  the profile is coordinate-dependent, can be redistributed arbitrarily, or
  produces only the tautology that the exponents sum to `N`.
- **Interpretation of each outcome:**
  - If a nonnegative invariant survives coordinates and blow-ups and each
    typed jump has positive cost, it becomes a new conditional proof lemma and
    a candidate pure-Sigray capacity.
  - If it is invariant but its budget grows with unbounded type, retain it only
    as a type-relative result and feed the remaining type problem to avenues
    25/26.
  - If it distinguishes residue-A data but cannot reconstruct provenance, use
    it as a landing checksum, not as `G2-PSC`.
  - If the Hénon controls violate the proposed absolute inequality, narrow the
    statement to the residue-A/Keller-minimal setting rather than discarding a
    potentially useful local law.
  - If formal or actual controls realize arbitrary passports with the same
    determinant divisor, stop the capacity mechanism.
- **Cost/time:** two days for a sparse CAS prototype and controls; up to one
  week for a credible local-algebra formulation and adversarial review.
- **Stop condition:** no escalation to a theorem lane unless the invariant is
  coordinate-stable, passes Hénon controls, and gives information strictly
  stronger than `sum Smith exponents = N` on at least one solved chart.
- **Expected information gain:** **high but high-variance**—the cheap pilot can
  kill a seductive but tautological idea, while success would simultaneously
  attack the source/transport and degree-capacity walls.
- **Resurrection trigger:** an end-to-end typed pure-Sigray landing theorem, a
  new exact boundary identity, or an independently bounded type would justify
  revisiting a passport that initially yields only a relative bound.

## 5. Acceleration schedule

These jobs are independent enough to run in parallel without launching a
large fleet computation:

1. **Evidence lane:** sandboxed external replay/crosswalk and cCa2 sparse-
   certificate pilot (Card C).
2. **Disproof lane:** hostile D43 model review followed by the common integral
   emitter and a bounded non-origin smoothness search (Card A).
3. **Proof lane:** a two-day torsion-passport prototype on controls, with a hard
   no-theorem gate if it is coordinate-dependent or tautological (Card B).

Standardize all three lanes on content-addressed inputs, deterministic seeds
where the interface exposes them, machine-readable claim perimeters, exact
small checkers, and fail-closed verdicts.  Cache monomial dictionaries and
Macaulay sparsity patterns across primes; separate producer, verifier, and
hostile-review code paths; estimate matrix size before solver launch.  These
practices increase throughput without confusing repeated execution with
independent mathematical evidence.

## 6. Hidden assumptions and ranking invalidators

### Hidden assumptions to expose

- The GGV-minimal representative selected existentially is assumed, without a
  current theorem, to admit the decorated Sigray normalization required by the
  books.
- A finite or executable local transition list is assumed to be total; missing
  off-axis and post-jump states can invalidate every downstream book census.
- A type-relative KJN ceiling is sometimes mentally treated as absolute even
  though the type parameters have no independent cofinal bound.
- The determinant divisor `j Z^N` may constrain only total torsion, not its
  distribution; Card B could collapse to a tautology.
- The D43 modular component may be vertical, singular, or an artifact of a
  prime-specific parked presentation; two modular points and a unit Jacobian
  minor do not rule these out.
- A smooth characteristic-zero point of a finite tail system is assumed to
  extend through all depths and algebraize; neither implication is currently
  certified.
- A formal pole-mass tower is assumed to reflect a realizable Keller map; the
  existing formal countermodels establish no such realization.
- Multiple modular unit ideals are assumed to indicate Q-unit ideal; without a
  rational cofactor or a proved height bound this remains only heuristic.
- Exact external terminal algebra is assumed to be attached to the same
  reduced polynomial system and to a complete global reduction; transcription
  and provenance are load-bearing.
- Two external artifacts with different prose or repositories are assumed to
  be independent even when their field polynomial, passport, coordinate map,
  or certificate support may share a common lineage.
- A failed local normal-form job is assumed to indicate singularity; it may be
  only bad point choice, term order, or implementation cost.
- A survivor in a bounded or fixed-prime search is assumed to be closer to a
  counterexample, although it may disappear under integrality, higher depth,
  or honest-map constraints.

### Events that would invalidate this ranking

- A reviewed pure-Sigray source plus total typed landing theorem would move the
  capacity/degree wall unequivocally to first place and reduce `G2-PSC` urgency.
- An explicit, provenance-preserving `G2-PSC` theorem would instead validate the
  hybrid route and make landing/coverage the dominant software target.
- A common integral, standard-smooth D43 point would make compatibility and
  algebraization the campaign's immediate top priority.
- A proof that the D43 component is vertical or necessarily singular would
  sharply lower D43 and redirect disproof compute to odd characteristic and
  formal-tower algebraization.
- A rational cofactor for cCa2/cCa6 or a fully replayed external certificate
  would settle that local evidence question but would not remove the global
  source, landing, and cofinal-bound bottlenecks.
- Failure of the Helali/Suzuki replay or of the reduction/transcription bridge
  would move exact `(72,108)` reconstruction to the top evidence priority and
  weaken the current degree-frontier conclusion.
- A boundary passport that survives controls yet yields only an unbounded-type
  budget would lower Card B as a global proof route while preserving it as a
  transport checksum.
- Algebraization of any nonautomorphic formal Keller tower into an honest
  polynomial map would supersede the proof ranking and become a direct
  disproof event.
- A credible public full proof or counterexample with dependency-closed exact
  artifacts would trigger immediate hostile replay before any scheduled lane.
