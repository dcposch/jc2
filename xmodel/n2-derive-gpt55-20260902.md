## 4. Coefficient Derivation

Everything below is normalized by the single nonzero factor `F`.

### r-family

For `6a Z eta^3 r'`,

```text

      = -F (3n alpha + (n-1)delta).
```


```text

```



```text

```

### s^2-family

For




### E1-family

Let `h=4e alpha + epshat`.  The top coefficient of `E` is `b A^2 h`.
The three high-degree `E` subfamilies contribute:



```text

```

```text

      = 2n - 2e - 3

beta:   -2(3n+2e-4)
```


