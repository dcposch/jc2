## 2. Strongest shared hidden assumption

The dangerous common shortcut is **ordinary first-order transversality at
`ord(w)=1` being treated as exhaustive of landing**.  An actual normalized
trajectory can be ramified over `w=0`, have `ord(w)>1`, meet an exceptional
`a`-value where rank drops, or reach the divisor only after some coefficient
goes to infinity.  A generic `Q(a)[epsilon]/epsilon^2` inconsistency kills
only unramified generic arcs.  Conversely, a large Zariski tangent space need
not integrate to an arc.

Therefore no finite-boundary kill is licensed until the calculation either:

- proves the relative normal cone/arc initial ideal empty for every allowed
  weight and raw exceptional factor; or
- embeds the tangent result in an explicit Rees-chart cover whose union is
  certified in the original saturated `Q[w]` source.

The analogous AS/TD6 guardrail is that a cokernel class computed at generic
rank need not decide nilpotent or lower-rank fibres (`A=[x], b=y` remains the
control).  Fitting strata are discovery aids; only original-row identities
or raw rebuilt fibres prove coverage.

The best proof combination is:

1. compute the exact relative normal cone of the finite
   `x5=0,x3=a!=0` family with `w`, `r8`, terminal, and Taylor rows;
2. factor every actual exceptional polynomial in `a` and rebuild those
   fibres raw;
3. in parallel, enumerate a source-derived Rees fan for coefficient infinity
   and `p=0`, with exact saturation/union certificates;
4. apply the reviewed selected-Q8 positive-genus exclusion only after the
   landing union is complete.

The post-cutoff ordinary-tangent control has already returned **compatible**
on the sole finite loaded family, so the cheapest remaining separator is the
weighted/next-order version of step 1: compute the initial systems for
`ord(w)=m` and the first possible orders of `x5` and `r8` (normalizing the
common gcd of weights), with the terminal and both Taylor rows.  If every
weighted initial system is unit or terminal/Taylor-inconsistent, the Q8 route
is genuinely near a leaf closure and deserves the largest share.  A
source-valid weighted survivor immediately redirects that share to its one
component instead of a large projective Groebner run.  This test directly
attacks the shared hidden assumption; repeating ordinary tangent rank would
have zero information gain.

For disproof, the cheapest new separator is p=2 depth 4 at caps 3 and 4.  The
post-cutoff depth-3 witness
`(x-x^2, y+2xy+4x^2y)` is the truncated inverse of `1-2x`; it suggests, but

conjecture; `D=3` survival would instead raise p=2 as the cheapest bounded-
