## 4. Quotient, extraction, deck, and `rho=0`

The engine is the qring `R/(sigma^13)` with `sigma` first in `dp`. The leading term of `sigma^13` is `sigma^13`, so reduction kills exactly the monomials of `sigma`-exponent `>=13`. Multiplication in the qring is the truncation of polynomial multiplication. That is the correct prefix ring.

Coefficient extraction is the owner’s exact-division loop, started at grade 0 because the common `sigma`-order over `Q[rho]` is not known (and is expected to drop: `F6|_{sigma=0}=-4*rho^2`):

```text
G_g = subst(Q_g, sigma, 0),
Rem_{g+1} = Q_g - G_g,
Q_{g+1} = Rem_{g+1} / sigma,
require  sigma * Q_{g+1} = Rem_{g+1}.
```

`sigma` is a zero-divisor in the qring: `Ann(sigma)=(sigma^{12})`. Quotients are therefore unique only modulo `sigma^{12}`. Pollution of that form sits in positive powers of `sigma` after `subst(-,sigma,0)`, so every extracted `G_g` is still the unique coefficient of `sigma^g` in a reduced representative of degree `<13`. Multiply-back failing is a hard `extraction=0`. This is not a hidden torsion certificate; it is prefix arithmetic. No Rees saturation is performed.

Deck: `subst(TPhi, rho, -rho) == TPhi`. The source is a polynomial in `rho^2` (through `p=-2*rho^2` and `p^2` in `r`), in odd characteristics. Checking the unextracted `TPhi` is enough: extraction commutes with the coefficient homomorphism `rho |-> -rho`.

Special fibre: `subst(TPhi, rho, 0) == FPhi` as polynomials in the *full* jet ring, without setting extra total jets to zero. Extra jets `ell4,cs3,az3,…` are therefore required to be absent from the specialized prefix, or the map fails. Coefficientwise, the same comparison is repeated on every `G_g`. Those two checks commute: `rho` and `sigma` are independent, and `rho=0` is a coefficient homomorphism. Redundancy here is desired.

`FPhi` itself is extracted in the same qring. If grades `0..9` of `F` vanish, `FPhi` is divisible by `sigma^{10}` already in the polynomial ring, recovering the owner’s common-order-10 prefix through grade 12.
