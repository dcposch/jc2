## 6. Finite literal census versus finite semilinear family-record endpoint

`REDUCTION.md` Critical 4 is explicit: the endpoint cannot be a finite
set of literal cells. The reviewed td8 equal-join route already contained
infinitely many full formal cells with one reduced successor. The
minimally plausible codomain is a finite exceptional residue plus
finitely many **normalised semilinear family records**, each carrying
parameter progression, affine laws, coefficient character, consumers,
exceptional values, and provenance. U2 (`nu_G = 1`, unbounded `lex`) and
Q+E5 case III are the named leaks. Critical 5 adds that cap-free reduced
`(w, M)` finiteness does not give off-axis completeness. Critical 7 adds
that a run over `td = 6, …, 14` cannot reduce JC2.

Keep these two finiteness claims in different columns.

**Literal census.** A finite list of named terminals, edges, or cells at
a fixed budget. This wave's examples: the 13-edge maximal-budget menu
with exactly two P1-fitting terminals; the td8 affine equal-join family
kill; LL-1's residue at `td = 6, m = 2`; a proposed `td = 6..14` price
sweep. These are finite because the menu is fixed. Killing one family is
a census event. Repricing the 13 edges under DICHOTOMY is a census
repair. Neither is a landing theorem.

**Semilinear family-record endpoint.** A proof that only finitely many
labelled records exist, plus a finite residue. That is the Critical 4
codomain. Pattern-fibre finiteness (Q+E5, PROMOTED for fixed two-pole
case III) is not book finiteness. P0-to-P1 and first-integral `nu >= 2`
finiteness (PROMOTED) close two departure types of one record class, not
the record set. Nested `nu = 1` U2, intervening P0 chains, recursively
variable bases, and case-III existence remain the leaks. A numerical
`kbar`/`nu` cap is a known-false substitute for this lemma and is not
revived.

Opus/Fable “price every vertex in every book with `Λ`” is a literal
census proposal wearing an endpoint costume. Even a perfect census of
`td = 6..14` leaves Critical 7 untouched. Sol's “compile physical exit
partitions and labelled semilinear family records” is the right
*design* of the endpoint and is not a kill gate (Grok-blind: LL-2 is
design, not a kill gate; LL-1 is frozen at `td = 6, m = 2`).

U2R is the unique cheap test of the *endpoint* reading: if the direct
edge is already an infinite formal family, the finite-record hypothesis
is false at that scope, independently of any census. That is why U2R
