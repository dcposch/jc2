## 3. Shape from old-coordinate polynomiality

Let `P(X,Y)=sum_{i+j<=D} p_ij X^iY^j`.  Its literal transform is

```text
Omega(P)=sum p_ij x^-i(a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2+x^3y)^j.                 (3.1)
```

Conversely, on a monomial,

```text
Omega^-1(x^e y^k)
 = X^(k-e) [X^2(Y-a_0)-a_1X-a_2]^k.                            (3.2)
```

Equations (3.1)--(3.2), not a guessed polygon, are the exact polynomiality
test: negative `X` powers and terms of total degree greater than `D` must cancel
after coefficients with the same image are combined.  A single old monomial
can only produce

```text
0<=k<=D,             4k-D <= e <= 2D+k,
```

but membership in this envelope is not sufficient, because inverse-image
coefficients are correlated.

Combining the top and radius-two weights gives the following useful maximal
Laurent envelopes:

```text
F:  max(k-12,4k-66)  <= e <= 132+k, 0<=k<=66;
G:  max(k-18,4k-99)  <= e <= 198+k, 0<=k<=99;
T2: max(k-10,4k-55)  <= e <= 110+k, 0<=k<=55;
T3: max(k-25,4k-145) <= e <= 290+k, 0<=k<=145.
```

counts.

## 5. Branch C: exact exclusion in the declared radius-two ODE system

If `H` has three simple roots, resonance is impossible because `2r=25` has no
integer solution.  Equation (4.1) forces multiplicity 14 at each root, hence
`deg R>=42`, contradicting `deg R=40`.  This kills C under the radius-two
`T_3`-leader premise just typed.

For a coefficient-system check, work on the squarefree stratum.  If
`R=H^jR_j`, then (4.1) becomes

```text
2H R_j' +(2j-25)H'R_j = k H^(14-j).
```

For `j=0,...,12`, reduction modulo `H` shows `H|R_j`: the integers
`2j-25` are nonzero and `H'` is a unit modulo squarefree `H`.  Thus
`R=H^13L`.  Scale `R`, hence `L`, to be monic and absorb that nonzero scalar
into `k`; then `L` is monic linear and the remaining necessary equation is

```text
H'L+2HL'-kH=0.                                                (5.1)
```

Normalize `H=z(z-a)(z-b)`, `L=z+ell`.  The four coefficients of (5.1) are

```text
f1=5-k,
f2=(k-4)(a+b)+3ell,
f3=(3-k)ab-2(a+b)ell,
f4=ab ell.
```

The ring is `Q[a,b,ell,k,T]`; the squarefree/nonzero stratum is selected by
`f5=T*ab(a-b)k-1`.  Singular returns `[1]` in each of `F_32003`, `F_32009`,
`F_32027`, and `Q`, in under 0.014 seconds per run.  Each run passed the empty
wrapper, nonempty wrapper, and unsaturated-nonunit controls.

expanded rational certificate has five summands and maximum multiplier total


## 7. What the exact full system would require

two monic total-degree arrays and parameters `a_0,a_1,a_2`.  Form `F,G` by

```text

```

filtration-preserving h-adic/gauge eliminations that map this system to eleven
variables.  Pp.209--210 omit precisely those eliminations and the resulting
support.  Saturating an invented eleven-monomial slice would not answer the
question.

Under the declared `T_3`-leader premise, the reduced ODE systems are exact
necessary images of the full Jacobian system.  Their logical force is then
asymmetric: emptiness of C lifts to the full fixed-radius-two C coefficient
branch, while nonemptiness of B does not lift backward without solving all
omitted coefficient equations.  Without that premise, both statements are
only about the conditional ODE systems.

There is a second exhaustiveness gap.  P.209 asserts the radius-two description
but does not prove that principal minor roots cannot first split at another
admissible radius.  As an external supplement, Yansong Xu, *Intersection

`EXTERNAL-SUPPLEMENT`, not part of the frozen-source proof.
