## 9. A likely-missed exact derivation

Let `C_a` be a connected smooth generic affine `P`-fibre, let its smooth
completion have genus `g` and `s` ends, and let `q=Q|C_a` have degree `td`.
Because the Keller map is unramified on the affine fibre, the relative
different of `q` is supported at the ends and Riemann--Hurwitz gives

```text
deg Diff(q) = 2g - 2 + 2td.
```

Also `b1(C_a)=2g+s-1`.  If the provisional finite-end identity

```text
sum_(finite ends S) e_S = td + b1(C_a) - 1
```

is correctly typed, then pure substitution yields the exact equivalent
budget

```text
deg Diff(q) = td + sum_(finite ends S) e_S - s.
```

Thus the finite-end identity is not merely another passport checksum: it is
exactly a formula for the total length of the boundary different.  The
missing theorem can be localized.  One need not bound each positive polar
term directly; it is enough to inject every corridor-exit mismatch module
into the corresponding stalk of the relative-differential module without
double counting.  This also explains why the signed adjunction ledger alone
failed: it controlled a global difference of positive and finite-end terms,
whereas the different is a positive torsion-length object.

Exact check: verify `b1=2g+s-1`, the degree and support of `q`, and the two
displayed identities on one known fibre; then compute
`Fitt_0 Omega_(Cbar_a/P^1)` locally at each end and compare it with the
candidate exit lengths.  Any mismatch in support or length falsifies the
proposed bridge immediately.
