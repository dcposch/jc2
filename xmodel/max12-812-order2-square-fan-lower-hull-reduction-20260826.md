# `(8,12)` order-two square fan: exact lower-hull reduction

Date: 2026-08-26

Status: **HAND POLYHEDRAL LEMMA AND COMPILER SENTINEL.  NO SOURCE-ROW,
SQUARE-BRANCH, ORDER-TWO, OR JC2 VERDICT.**

## Setup

Use the weights from the correction-aware square fan, after subtracting the
common absolute grade ten.  For the positive-order load
`k10=sigma^q*k`, they are

```text
AC      = a+c,
C2      = 2c,
RA2     = 2+r+2a,
RAC     = 2+r+a+c,
RC2     = 2+r+2c,
kR3     = q+3r,
kRC     = 1+q+r+c,
kR2A    = 3+q+2r+a,
kA2     = 4+q+2a,
kAC     = 4+q+a+c,
A3      = 5+3a.
```

Here `a,r,c>=0` and `q>=0`; the unit-load horizontal domain further has
`q=0`, `a>=1`, `r>=2`, and `c>=3`.  This note concerns only the affine
lower hull.  It does not assert that cancellation on any retained face is
impossible.

## Global dominance

The three displayed completeness sentinels are strictly above an unloaded
term everywhere:

```text
RAC = AC+(2+r),
RC2 = C2+(2+r),
kAC = AC+(4+q).
```

There is a fourth global non-face term:

```text
kR2A-kR3 = 3+a-r,
kR2A-kA2 = 2r-a-1.                                 (1)
```

If `kR2A<=kR3`, then `r>=a+3`, whence

```text
kR2A-kA2 = 2r-a-1 >= a+5 > 0.
```

If `kR2A<=kA2`, then `a>=2r-1`, whence

```text
kR2A-kR3 = 3+a-r >= r+2 > 0.
```

Thus `kR2A` cannot be at most both `kR3` and `kA2`; one of those two is
strictly lower at every point.  Consequently `kR2A` is never contained in a
lower face, for any `q>=0`.  It must remain in complete source expansions as
a support sentinel, but it must not generate a primitive fan cone.

For a positive-order load the complete candidate lower hull therefore has at
most the seven generators

```text
AC, C2, RA2, A3, kR3, kRC, kA2.                    (2)
```

No claim is made here that all seven actually occur for every chosen fan
domain.

## Unit-load horizontal domain

Now put `q=0`, `a>=1`, `r>=2`, and `c>=3`.  Then

```text
A3-kA2 = a+1 > 0,
RA2-kA2 = r-2 >= 0.                                (3)
```

Hence the ordinary lower envelope is generated by exactly

```text
AC=a+c,  C2=2c,  R3=3r,  RC=1+r+c,  A2=4+2a,       (4)
```

except that `RA2` must be retained on the part of the lower hull where it
ties `A2`.  Equality in (3) requires `r=2`.  For `A2` to be lower than the
five functions in (4), one needs

```text
c>=a+4,
2a+4<=3r,
r+c>=2a+3.
```

With `r=2`, `a>=1`, this forces `a=1`; the first inequality then gives
`c>=5`.  Conversely, at

```text
(a,r)=(1,2),  c>=5,
```

one has

```text
RA2=A2=R3=6
```

and every other function is at least six.  This is the entire `RA2`
lower-hull locus in the unit-load horizontal domain.

The five primary closed cells, useful as independent compiler checks, are:

```text
AC:  a<=c, a+c<=3r, a<=r+1, c<=a+4;
C2:  c<=a, 2c<=3r, c<=r+1;
R3:  3r<=a+c, c>=2r-1, 3r<=4+2a;
RC:  a>=r+1, c>=r+1, c<=2r-1, c<=3+2a-r;
A2:  c>=a+4, 2a+4<=3r, c>=3+2a-r.
```

Equalities describe shared faces; strict inequalities describe the
corresponding unique-minimum interiors.  These formulae are direct pairwise
comparisons with the five functions in (4), not a source obstruction
calculation.

### Parametric unique-`AC` cells

At an integral point where `AC` is uniquely minimal, strict comparison with
`C2` and `A2` gives

```text
1 <= d:=c-a <= 3.
```

Strict comparison with `RC` gives `s:=r-a>=0`, and comparison with `R3`
is

```text
a+3s>d.                                             (5)
```

Relative to the first `AC` weight, the subsequent candidate gaps are

```text
C2:   d,
A2:   4-d,
RC:   1+s,
R3:   a+3s-d,
RA2:  a+s+2-d,
A3:   a+5-d.                                        (6)
```

Thus the apparently unbounded unique-`AC` interior has only the following
parametric successor families:

```text
d=1;
d=2 with s=0, s=1, or s>=2;
d=3 with s=0 or s>=1,
```

plus the small-`a` equality faces already visible in (6).  In particular the
whole `d=1` family is

```text
a>=2, c=a+1, r>=a;
```

there `C2` is exactly one grade after `AC`, `RC` joins only for `s=0`, and
`R3` also joins only at `(a,c,r)=(2,3,2)`.  The companion residue note gives
a conditional double-pole discriminator for this family.  Equations (5)--(6)
are coverage and sharding data only; they do not decide any of the other five
families.

## Required emitter controls

An exact fan emitter should fail closed unless it verifies all of the
following before emitting source clients:

1. none of `RAC`, `RC2`, `kAC`, or `kR2A` labels a primitive lower face;
2. at `q=0` no `A3` face is emitted;
3. at `q=0`, `RA2` occurs only on `(a,r)=(1,2), c>=5` and is tied with
   `A2,R3`;
4. the union of the five closed cells above covers the full registered
   domain `a>=1,r>=2,c>=3`;
5. every equality face is assigned to all incident source terms, and the
   `AC`/`RC` root orientations are preserved rather than saturated away.
6. the unique-`AC` output agrees with the six parametric families in
   (5)--(6), rather than enumerating an integer box.

For `q>0`, equation (2), rather than the five-function unit-load list, is the
licensed starting set.  In particular `A3` can become minimal when the load
is delayed, and `RA2-kA2=r-2-q` changes sign.  The unit-load reductions must
not be imported across that boundary.

## Scope firewall

This lemma reduces an affine weight arrangement.  It does not verify that the
eleven weights exhaust the complete source expansion, compute any initial
source ideal, decide any cancellation face, establish contact raising or fan
coverage, handle the exact-square zero section, or prove any part of the
global landing or complexity-ceiling obligations.
