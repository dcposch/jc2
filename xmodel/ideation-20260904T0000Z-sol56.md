### The exponential surcharge identifies the wrong object

Normalize `Q_0` to be monic and reduce `Q_r` modulo it.  If



most `2^(t-1)` words, or `2^(t-2)` modulo simultaneous root swap.  The same
factor appears in intersection theory.  The direct top-tail degrees are


whereas `deg R_r=2E_r` in weights `(1,...,t-1)`, `1<=r<t`.  If the respective

