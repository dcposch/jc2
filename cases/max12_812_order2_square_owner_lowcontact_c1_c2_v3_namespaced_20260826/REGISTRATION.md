# Registration: namespaced rootwise low-contact endpoint

Date: 2026-08-26

The frozen V2 rootwise compiler failed closed for two purely syntactic
reasons: its Laurent coefficients were emitted as `c1ah*`/`c2ah*` while the
bridge requested `c1h*`/`c2h*`, and its generic analytic identifiers were
reused after a Singular ring change.  This V3 pins V2, changes only those
names, and reruns the unchanged source rows, Laurent bridge, moving-divisor
recurrences, and rootwise ideal-membership sentinels.

Exact Q is the producer and `F_65521` is a control.  The endpoint is only the
generic-square `c=1,c=2` low-contact gate on `D(p*k0)`, not square-branch or
order-two closure.
