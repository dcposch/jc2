## 2. Independently re-derived local algebra

Let `k=QQ(q)`, `K=k((t))`, `L=k((u))`, and `t=u^e` in characteristic zero.
The `K`-basis is `1,u,...,u^(e-1)`.  Reduction of a monomial is

```text
u^n = t^floor(n/e) u^(n mod e),       0 <= n mod e < e.
```

The multiplication matrix of `u` is the companion shift for `U^e-t`; this
presentation, together with a reduced numerator/denominator in `U`, is the
coordinate multiplication datum.  The field trace is independently derived
from that companion basis:

```text
Tr(u^n) = e*t^(n/e) if e divides n, and 0 otherwise.
```

For the monogenic normalization `R=k[[t]] -> S=k[[u]]`, the different is
generated by the derivative of `U^e-t`:

```text
Different(S/R) = (e*u^(e-1));  different exponent = e-1.
```

As an independent consistency calculation, the trace-pairing matrix
`(Tr(u^(i+j)))_(0<=i,j<e)` has one nonzero entry per row: `e` at `(0,0)` and
`e*t` at `(i,e-i)` for `i>0`.  Its determinant is

```text
(-1)^floor((e-1)/2) * e^e * t^(e-1).
```

The checker must obtain the same discriminant from these two derivations.  A
mismatch is `RECEIVER-BUG`.
