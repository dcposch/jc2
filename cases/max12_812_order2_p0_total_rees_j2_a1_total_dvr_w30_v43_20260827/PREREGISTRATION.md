# V43 preregistration: ordered-`a1` weight-30 total-DVR discriminator

Date: 2026-08-27

## Frozen question

Let `J` be the ideal generated by the 70 literal actual-total ordered-`a1`
source rows through grade 19 after imposing

```text
rs=cs=c0=c1=a0=0
```

but retaining `rho`.  Decide only whether there is a polynomial

```text
U(rho^2),  U(0)=1,  with  a1^6*U(rho^2) in J.             (V43)
```

By the ring/map theorem in the pinned design report, (V43) is exactly the
exponent-six instance of an honest closure-first ordered-chart certificate
`a1^6*(1+rho*W)`.  It is not a raw localization statement.

## Required construction

1. Regenerate the general-`rho` rows through grade 19 from the literal V35
   emitter.  Their `rho=0` images must bridge all 70 rows through the pinned
   V37 loader.  Require 65 positive-weight variables, sigma homogeneity, and
   even rho parity.
2. Put `t=rho^2`.  At weight 30 enumerate every complementary-weight
   monomial multiple of every nonzero row, with no cofactor total-degree or
   support cap.  Restrict only to the exact support-incidence component of
   the target `a1^6`.
3. First decide `a1^6` membership after `t=0`.  Exact-Q nonmembership is a
   final negative answer for V43.  Finite-field output is screening/selector
   evidence only.
4. Only after a positive `t=0` gate, compute the syzygies of `[A_30(t)|b]`
   over `Q[t]` (and independently over a requested finite field).  A last
   syzygy coordinate with nonzero constant term is a positive certificate;
   if every last coordinate is divisible by `t`, exponent six is obstructed.
5. Save and replay any selected syzygy and normalize its last coordinate to
   constant one.

## Controls and outcome policy

- Run the pinned V42 replay first.  Its A1 `a1^8` branch identity is the
  real-source `rho=0` positive control; its fixed mutation of `Tg19_7` must
  leave a nonzero residual.
- The univariate engine must pass both toy modules: target in `(target)` is
  positive, while target `(1,0)` against column `(1,-t)` is negative despite
  becoming positive at `t=0`.  The second is the specialization/saturation
  failure mode.
- Exact-Q and finite-prime lanes use independent selector primes `65521` and
  `65519`.  The exact-Q lane is authoritative.  Modular agreement never
  substitutes for an exact rational certificate.
- Each lane has a six-hour wall cap and 192-GiB virtual-memory cap.  Stop and
  report before any exponent `N>6` or weight above 30 is launched.
- A compiler failure, timeout, OOM, missing bridge, empty/corrupt output, or
  disagreement is `NO VERDICT`, never nonmembership.

## Scope

Even a positive result concerns only the frozen grade-through-19 ordered
`T-a1` chart.  It does not prove source/landing coverage, close the terminal
receiver, establish Gate T, order two, maximum twelve, or JC2.
