# Preregistration — squarefree Poisson recurrence through degree 15

Status: frozen before execution. All exact algebra runs on registered AWS
hosts only.

## Scope

Work over characteristic zero with the squarefree binary cubic

```text
K = x*y*(x-y),  P9=K^3,  Q12=K^4.
```

Check, independently of the band compiler, the Poisson-bracket recurrence for
the original denominator-free homogeneous determinant rows through degree 15.
The checker must verify:

1. the homogeneous polynomial centralizer of `K` through degree 18 has
   dimension one in degrees divisible by three (generated by the appropriate
   power of `K`) and dimension zero otherwise;
2. the complete degree-18 row is exactly
   `K^2*[K,3*Q11-4*K*P8]`, and the displayed solution
   `Q11=(4/3)*K*P8` kills that original row;
3. the complete degree-17 row is exactly `[K,W16]` with
   `W16=K^2*(3*Q10-4*K*P7)-(2/3)*P8^2`, and after the reduced-point
   divisibility `P8=K*R5`, the displayed formula for `Q10` kills that row;
4. the complete degree-16 row after `P8=K*R5` is exactly `[K,W15]`, and
   its reduction modulo `K` is exactly
   `(4/9)*R5^2*[K,R5]`, the source of the next rootwise divisibility gate;
5. after `R5=K*a2`, the displayed `Q9` formula kills the complete degree-16
   row, including the degree-nine centralizer parameter `lambda*K^3/3`;
6. the complete degree-15 row is exactly `[K,Z14]`, where

   ```text
   Z14=K^2*(3*Q8-4*K*P5)+F14,
   F14=-(4/3)K^2*a2*P6+(4/9)K*a2^2*P7-(2/3)P7^2
       -(5/81)K^2*a2^4-lambda*K^4*a2;
   ```

7. after the reduced-point consequence `P7=K*b4`, the explicit formula for
   `Q8` kills the original degree-15 row.

The exact identities are polynomial identities. The implications
`K | P8`, `K | R5`, and `K | P7` are separately charged reduced-geometric-point
divisibility lemmas for a squarefree product of three distinct linear forms;
they are not assertions about nilpotents or equality of obstruction ideals.

## Acceptance and refusal

Accept only if both an independent standard-library exact centralizer checker
and a Singular identity checker pass. The runner must refuse Darwin,
non-Linux, non-AWS, wrong hostname, and wrong registered tag.

No endpoint proves a full Keller map, lower determinant bands, integral
lifting of a B9 residue witness, selected-Q8 landing, maximum twelve, a
counterexample, or JC2.
