# Preregistration — normalized `(9,12)` first quadratic gate

Consume the complete `3^145` normalized family modulo `3^10`.  Use all 146
coefficient digits in the fixed `(P<=9,Q<=12)` support and all 276
determinant rows for the transition to `3^11=177147`.

Quotient first by the complete fresh-digit operator, split the predecessor
directions into their mod-three image and exact three-divisible spectators,
and eliminate the spectators coefficientwise from the quadratic Kuranishi
map.  Emit the residual ANF/QF_BV system.  SAT requires literal integer
replay and exact `(9,12)` bounds; zero residual equations must be certified by
an exact coefficient-image containment, not samples.
