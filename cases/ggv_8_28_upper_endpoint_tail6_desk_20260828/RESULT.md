## 1. Literal endpoint carrier

In the complete prefix-nullspace coordinates, the constant endpoint
coordinate is exactly

```text

p32  = G15[X^1],
p110 = F7[X^0].
```

Consequently every field-valued endpoint point has both `p32` and `p110`
nonzero and satisfies `p32=-p110^(-1)`.  This is an actual localization,
not a permission to normalize `p110=1` globally.

## 2. First nonlinear block: row 12

Write

```text
C=X^4-1,  H=C^2,  A=F6(X),  deg A<=10.
```

Row 12 has 28 displayed equations.  Its same-row constant matrix on the 16
weight-12 parameters has rank 12 and four free modes.  The remaining 16
displayed compatibility polynomials have rational row-space rank eight.
That row space is exactly the span of the eight coefficients of

```text
A(X)^2 mod H.
```

Thus a field point must satisfy `H | A^2`.  Because `C` is squarefree, this
forces

```text
A=C*B,  deg B<=6.
```

This is a field-radical reduction.  It is not an equality of the original
nonreduced row-12 schemes.

## 3. Second nonlinear block: row 13

After substituting `A=C*B`, row 13 has 27 displayed equations.  Its
same-row constant matrix on the 13 weight-13 parameters has rank 11 and two
free modes.  The 16 displayed compatibility polynomials have row-space
rank nine.  That rank-nine space is exactly generated by

```text
the eight coefficients of B*(4*C*F7-B) mod H
```

and the scalar

```text

    -(b0*b4+b1*b3+b2*b6+b3*b5)/2
    -(b2^2+b4^2+b6^2)/4.
```

Reducing the first eight equations modulo `C` gives `C | B^2`, hence a
field point has `B=C*V`, `deg V<=2`.  Under this substitution,

```text
E = V0*(V0-4*p110)/4.
```

Therefore the complete field-valued cutoff-six endpoint locus is covered by
exactly two row-13 branches:

```text

B4: V0=4*p110.
```

Since `p110` is an endpoint unit, `V0` is nonzero on B4.
