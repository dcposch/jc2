# V37 preregistration: ordered-a1 rho-zero graded certificate ladder

Date: 2026-08-27

Let `I_19` be the homogeneous ideal generated by every frozen ordered-`a1`,
`rho=0` literal source row through grade 19.  V37 decides the complete
fixed-weight membership questions

```text
W17: a1*k^3
W18: a1^2*k^2
W19: a1^3*k
W20: a1^4
```

by spanning every monomial multiple of every row at the required weight.
There is no cofactor-degree cap.  Variables absent from every row are
spectators: polynomial extension is faithful and evaluation of spectators
at zero proves that omitting them preserves membership of these targets.

The W17 and W18 targets are preregistered isolated monomials and must return
one-coordinate exact duals.  W19 is the first open, complete mixed target:
later rows cannot alter its answer.  W20 is open, but a nonmembership result
is only for rows through 19 because an unfrozen grade-20 row could enter with
a constant cofactor; membership would be final and monotone.

The solver uses a modular echelon only to propose an invertible pivot minor.
It solves that minor over exact Q with python-flint and accepts only an exact
full-product replay.  It emits either a polynomial membership identity or an
exact coefficient functional annihilating every product and taking the
target to one.  Modular nonmembership alone is never accepted.

Any membership empties the corresponding raw rho-zero open locus; because
`k` is a unit in the named family, W17--W19 would empty `D(a1*k)` and W20
would empty `D(a1)`.  A dual proves only raw-row nonmembership.  Neither
outcome supplies the missing saturated-Rees/base-change comparison or Gate T.
