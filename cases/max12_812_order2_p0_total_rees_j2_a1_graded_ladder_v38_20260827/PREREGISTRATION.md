# V38 preregistration B: hardened exact graded membership decisions

Date: 2026-08-27

## Frozen question

Let `I_19` be the homogeneous ideal generated by all 70 named (51 nonzero)
frozen ordered-`a1`, `rho=0` literal source rows through grade 19.  For every
pair

```text
(i,j) with i >= 1, j >= 0, and 5*i + 4*j <= 25,
```

V38 decides over exact `Q` whether `a1^i*k^j` belongs to the fixed-weight
piece of `I_19`.  This includes the primary exponent-four/exponent-five ladder

```text
W17 a1*k^3,   W18 a1^2*k^2, W19 a1^3*k,   W20 a1^4,
W21 a1*k^4,   W22 a1^2*k^3, W23 a1^3*k^2, W24 a1^4*k, W25 a1^5,
```

and all lower/mixed shape controls, including `a1*k` (W9), `a1*k^2`
(W13), `a1^2*k` (W14), and the separate W25 target `a1*k^5`.

The expected all-product and target-component sizes are pinned from two
separately preregistered and independently replay-validated AWS censi:

Every monomial multiple of every row at the target weight is included; there
is no cofactor-degree or total-degree cap.  Variables absent from every row
are faithful polynomial-extension spectators and are omitted.  The exact
bipartite connected component containing the target is sufficient because no
linear combination from a disjoint component can affect the target.

## Exact decision and rank certificate

A registered selector prime is used only to propose independent row indices
and pivot monomials.  The proposed square minor is reconstructed and proved
invertible over exact `Q`.  Every nonbasis component row is solved against the
minor and replayed coefficient-by-coefficient over `Q`; this proves the
reported rank and that the selected rows span the complete component over
`Q`.  The target is then solved against that exact basis.

- Membership is accepted only after an exact full polynomial identity replay.
- Nonmembership is accepted only after an exact rational functional evaluates
  to one on the target and zero on every product in the full fixed-weight
  problem.  Modular nonmembership is never a decision.
- W17 and W18 must pass explicit isolated one-coordinate functional controls.
  Every other census-isolated target is checked in the same way.
- The compiler emits complete selectors, exact ranks, and canonical integer
  fraction pairs.  The validator rejects boolean, string, or floating-point
  substitutions for integers; rejects nonreduced or negative-denominator
  fractions; checks exact result keys and canonical JSON; reconstructs the
  exact rank/span and decision; and binds the compiler stdout to the result
  SHA-256.

Each target is a separate AWS compiler/validator job.  In particular W25 jobs
cannot block harvesting W21--W24.  The two host lanes use distinct requested
selector primes, 65521 and 65519.

## Completeness and permitted interpretation

For targets of weight at most 19, nonmembership is fixed-weight final: later
positive-weight rows cannot alter it.  Every negative result at W20--W25 is
only nonmembership in `I_19`, the ideal generated by rows through grade 19;
unfrozen rows of grades 20 through the target weight may change that negative
answer.  In contrast, every positive membership identity is monotone and
final under adjoining later rows.

If a target with positive `k` exponent were a member, it would empty the raw
`rho=0` locus on `D(a1*k)`; a pure `a1` power would empty the raw locus on
`D(a1)`.  Conversely, nonmembership of W17 (`a1*k^3`) forces nonmembership of
its lower `k`-divisors by ideal multiplication; W18 similarly controls lower
divisors of `a1^2*k^2`, and W19 controls `a1^3`.  The explicit low-weight jobs
are independent typed-enumeration controls for this algebraic implication.

No result here supplies a saturated-Rees/base-change comparison, honest-chart
nonemptiness, Gate T, a counterexample, or a proof of JC2.
