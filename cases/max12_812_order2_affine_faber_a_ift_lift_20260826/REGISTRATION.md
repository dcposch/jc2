# Affine-Faber `A`-face rational `J` point and formal lift

Date: 2026-08-26

This dual-AWS client replays one exact normalized ordinary-Faber point on the
corank-one exceptional face.  It checks exact divisibility by `t^3`, the
three zero odd rows, the nonzero leading `J`, the full lift Jacobian, and the
next even divided jet.  Exact Q is the producer; characteristic 65521 is a
software control only.

The client does not by itself identify the normalized arc with a literal
total-Rees source arc and does not impose terminal `[6,2]`, Taylor, order
two, or JC2 conditions.
