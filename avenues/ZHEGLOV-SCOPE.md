## 3. Top risk steps (3-5)
**R1. Endgame conjugation calculation, S6 Steps 4–7 (lines 3220–3390).** Hand symbolic computation in the bespoke vector-form calculus (Γ_1, Γ_2, σ-shifts, ∫-symbols): computes S̄_{−N−1}, γ_1=−a/(ln+Ndn)+dn/2, γ_2=−d_2/(2(Nd_2+l_2)), then E:tildeQ_N,I giving A=½d(M̃_{I−1}+1)(dn−M̃_{I−1}−1), B, C; then L:tail averaging; final c_{N,I;r(I)} formula. One sign/offset error anywhere destroys the contradiction. This is exactly the part "simplified" in v5 — i.e., rewritten twice (v4, v5). Includes the one-line inequality claim **A≠0** (line 3256, argument compressed: M̃_{I−1}=ord(Q_{I−1})nd−M_{I−1} forces dn−M̃_{I−1}−1≠0).
**R2. N-independence lemma (L:distinguished coefficients, 3168–3210).** The other jaw of the contradiction. Subtle order-bookkeeping on which monomials of X_{N,k−1}^p can contribute at order r(k); separate delicate special case M_{I−1}=d_2=2 (l_2=1) using vanishing (Q_{N,I})_{ord−1}=0. If a monomial slips the inequality E:inequality_for_j, N-independence fails and there is no contradiction.
**R3. S4 preparatory induction (T:DC-pairs + T:subrectangular_sequence, 1517–2357, ~840 lines).** Long induction with repetitive case exhaustions ("this case is impossible" at 2241, 2350); each step invokes L:polynomials with specific parameter substitutions (A, d̃, l̃, z̃) plus divisibility forcing d/l=d_2. Author himself flags: proofs "often similar or even repetitive... described in detail to avoid errors". Distance=p+q−1 exactly (E:distance) is the quantitative output later needed in S6.
**R4. String-equation correspondence for NON-coprime orders (S5, esp. T:one-to-one-correspondence & inverse, 2620–2790).** Extends A. Schwarz's coprime-order bijection; needed precisely because subrectangular DC-pairs have non-coprime orders. Feeds R:distinguished_coefficient (distinguished coefficient =0, via Newton–Puiseux + Wilson, rank-1). Formal-series existence/uniqueness/normalization arguments; the whole transport of the DC-pair to the Puiseux-series side rests here.
**R5. L:Phi_N(P) (2919–3120, ~200 lines).** Two-case analysis of how the Φ_{N,1}-family's head-chopping sequence relates to the original (lengths, ε_i equality, ord Q_{N,i}=(d_2N+l_2)ord(Q_i)/d_2, distance head↔tail = N+1). Both R1 and R2 consume its formulas; "relies heavily" on R3.
Lower risk: S2 imports ([GZ1] "to appear in Izvestiya" = refereed; [A.Z] published Steklov Proc.; [GGV] published J.Algebra 2014 — Prop 6.9 subrectangular reduction, Th 4.1(3)).

- **R4 → (c), partial (a).** Correspondence theorems are conceptual; but cutting/glueing the tail is algorithmic on truncated series — can numerically instantiate: take explicit commuting rank-1 pairs (e.g. KdV/Burchnall–Chaundy pairs), glue tail, check [Q,P]=1 to finite order, and vice versa. Distinguished-coefficient=0 claim testable on explicit rank-1 examples.
- **R5 → (a) spot-check + (c).** Order/GCD formulas testable on synthetic subrectangular pairs *if* candidate DC-pair surrogates exist (none do — vacuous domain; test instead on the automorphism examples where the machinery applies with non-maximal distance, cf. R:spectral_data_for_string).
- **Global obstacle for (a):** all S4/S6 statements quantify over hypothetical DC-pairs (empty set if theorem true). Directly instantiable objects: automorphism pairs (φ(x),φ(∂)), string-equation solutions in D_1, commuting rank-1 pairs. Falsification target must be a lemma whose hypotheses these NON-vacuous objects satisfy: L:polynomials (any polynomials), S5 correspondence (any string solution), L:Q_p tail shape (any normalized P plus Q with [Q,P]=1 — automorphism pairs qualify!).
