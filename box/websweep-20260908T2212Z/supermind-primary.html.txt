Topology · finite covers
Projection potentials and Lyashko–Looijenga rigidity for plane Keller maps
Establishes the exact projection-potential identity, its
inertia-hypergraph interpretation, and exclusion of the
zero-potential wall.  A bounded consequence is
b1 ≥ 9 for a general linear leaf.
Scope. The positive-potential sector remains uncontrolled.

II
Plane curves · singularity theory
Singularity budgets for rational one-place curves and plane Keller maps
Proves N ≤ 2R + 2 for rational
one-place curves and, when the whole reduced nonproper set is
one such curve, the page-density inequality
(d − 3a)R ≤ a + 1.
Scope. No reduction to the one-component hypothesis is asserted.

III
Polynomial pencils · monodromy
Common pencils and Suzuki saturation for affine-disjoint nonproper components
Places every affine-disjoint pair in one primitive pencil,
proves Suzuki saturation, and excludes the exact clean
nodal-cubic support in every degree.
Scope. An arbitrary hypothetical counterexample need not contain such a pair.

IV
Newton polygons · exact algebra
The degree of a possible counterexample is at least 125
Excludes the two residual degree-pair exceptions in the cited
small-degree reduction through exact quintic-field identities,
a Bézout certificate, and a rank-52 specialization witness.
Scope. This is a lower bound, not an arbitrary-degree classification.
