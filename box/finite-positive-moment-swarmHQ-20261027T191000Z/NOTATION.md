# Publication clarification

In the proof's FRAME paragraph, representing measures extend positively
homogeneously from normalized classes: `mu_(tD)=t mu_D` for `t>=0`, including
`mu_0=0`. Thus `mu_(P D)` means a finite positive measure, whose mass need not
be one. In particular, `P theta=a theta` gives `mu_(P theta)=a mu_theta`.
The normalized measures `mu_theta` and `mu_n` are probabilities. This is the
interpretation accepted in the [publication review](publication-review.json);
it does not change the sealed proof, hypotheses, or conclusion.
