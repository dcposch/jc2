## 2. Proper powers: useful reformulation, missing source construction

Fable's reduced-base observation has a correct algebraic component. Under the imported geometric factor bound, a common homogeneous form can be written H=h^e up to scalars, with h a line or a product of two distinct lines with coprime multiplicities. In the two-line case the elementary monomial-bracket calculation gives ker[h,-]=K[h] after the stated field extension. Tops H³,H5 become h^(3e),h^(5e). The centralizer obstruction has been moved to a different exponent problem; it has not been removed from15w by changing a name.

The leap from this reformulation to an AUTOMATIC general canonical-reference/kernel theorem is unsupported. Let A=a_s(R)+F, B=b_s(R)+q_s(R)F+G, where b_s'=a_s' q_s+rho_s in K[s][T], derivatives being in T. The exact product-rule identity is

    [A,B]=[R,T]+[F,G],
    T=a_s'(R)G-rho_s(R)F-(q_s'(R)/2)F².

It is valid with all scalar coefficients retained, but it does not prove ord G>=2j. The omitted term is rho_s(R)F. At3/5 the accepted reference supplies ord rho>=3D>j, so this term starts after2j. With3e/5e the same inequality is not automatic. Fable CardB includes ord G>=2j among its input assertions and then describes it as supplied by K[h]; that is the first missing implication, not an established universal frame.

These are combined-homogeneous references of degrees9d and15d when T has degree d, and b'=a'q+rho holds term by term. ## 3. Sol's closed-threshold lemma: valid-looking abstract improvement, weak current payoff

I independently agree with the proposed finite lemma at its literal polynomial hypotheses. Split T into combined-homogeneous pieces T^(n), n<=N. Since R has degree D, their brackets have distinct combined degrees D+n-2, so ord_s[R,T]>=N implies the same bound for each piece. For a piece's first order ell<n, ell<N and its bracket coefficient is[H,T_ell]=0. The centralizer and homogeneity give T_ell=a H^i, iD=n-ell. Subtract a*s^ell*R^i; this is polynomial, degree n, and preserves the bracket while raising the order. If ell=n the remaining coefficient is a scalar times s^n and already commutes. There are finitely many orders0 through n. Thus[R,T]=0. Zero pieces and N=0 cause no exception. This is a hand proof, not a promotion or an executable verification.

The sharpness control R=H=uv, T=s^(N-1)u for N>=1 really has nonzero bracket -s^(N-1)u and order N-1; no Keller hypothesis is present. It is not disqualified by the balanced-top theorem above. It tests a changed polynomial at the actual threshold, not merely a predicate.
