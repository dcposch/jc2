`ord_s[R,T] >= N  ==>  [R,T]=0`.

This is a genuine threshold mechanism, not a relabeling of the accepted strict lemma. For a homogeneous piece `T^(n)` of degree `n<=N`, distinct `n` give brackets of distinct combined degrees, so the global order bound applies to every piece. If the first coefficient of `T^(n)` is at order `ell<n`, its bracket coefficient is `[H,T_ell]=0`; homogeneity and the exact centralizer make `T_ell=aH^i`, and subtracting `a s^ell R^i` raises the order without changing the bracket. If the first coefficient occurs at `ell=n`, it is a scalar times `s^n` and already commutes. Finite induction exhausts the polynomial. This also shows why equality was harmless but absent from the accepted statement. The bound is sharp in this general form: with `H=R=uv`, whose polynomial centralizer is `K[uv]`, and `T=s^(N-1)u`, the bracket is a nonzero multiple of `s^(N-1)u`; order `N-1` does not suffice.
