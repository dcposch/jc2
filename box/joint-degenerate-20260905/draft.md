## 3. The cone-vertex theorem

Fix one of the three corrected charts `C` (major `h3`/`h2` structure with its
D2/D1 rows, outer D2 preblock and D1 offsets, localisers `c ≠ 0` or `ρ ≠ 0`).
For a truncation `T = (T_p, T_J)` let `R_T` be all rows the engine can express:
`F`/`G` minor pole rows at local powers `≤ T_p` (including the leading targets
when `T_p` reaches them), Jacobian bands at `KJ` t-powers `1..T_J`, outer
offsets. Let `Δ ⊂ C` be the coordinate subspace on which every outer
coordinate vanishes except the four constant terms `b, c', d, e` of
`A2, A3, B1, B2`.

**Theorem.** (i) On `Δ`, `F = h2^3 + b h2 + c'`, `G = h2^2 + d h2 + e`, so
`J(F,G) = (3h2^2 + b)(2h2 + d)·J(h2,h2) ≡ 0`: every Jacobian row of every
positive degree vanishes identically on `Δ`. (ii) On `Δ` every pole row is a
polynomial in the `h3`/`h2` coordinates alone: the G rows at local `≤ L` are
equivalent to `a_p = 0` for `p ≤ ⌈(L+1)/2⌉ − 1` and, at the leading power, to
`a_P = (target)^{1/2}`; the F rows are implied (`K2^3`) except for fixing the
sign of `a_P`. The constants never enter: `b·h2(σ)` first appears at local
`104 > 96` (F, D=108), `d·h2(σ)` at `68 > 64` (G), `c', e` at `108, 72`; at
(99,66) δ=2 at `93 > 81`, `60 > 54`, `99`, `66`; at δ=5/2 (τ) at `195 > 189`,
`129 > 126`, `198`, `132`. (iii) The outer preblock and D1 rows are linear
homogeneous in the outer coordinates and never touch the four constants (their
weights `284, 428, 140, 284` sit at offsets `8, 12, 4, 8` above `W0 = 276, 416,
136, 276` at D=108, and the B1 offset-4 band has `max(0, 279−280) = 0` rows;
at (99,66) offsets `6, 9, 3, 6` above `189, 285, 93, 189`, with the A2/B2
offset-6 and B1 offset-3 bands empty). Verified in code at D=108: the four
constants are free symbols of `outer_state`. (iv) The localisers and the
`h3` incidence rows are `h3`-level conditions, satisfied on `Δ` whenever they
are satisfied at all. Hence

```text
   V(R_T)  ⊇  Δ_T := { (h3,h2) ∈ major chart : h3 incidence rows, a_p rows to depth T_p }
                     × A^4(b,c',d,e) × {other outer = 0}          for every T_J < n+m−2.
```

**Corollaries.** (a) If `Δ_T ≠ ∅`, no unit exists at depth `T`, whatever the
Jacobian bands say. (b) A unit at any `T_J < n+m−2` forces `Δ_T = ∅`, i.e. the
`h3`/`h2` single-polynomial subsystem — which contains no Jacobian row and no
outer coordinate — is already inconsistent; the joint unit is that
inconsistency reappearing after the outer pivots. (c) If the `h2`-only pole
system is consistent to its full depth, the only row of the entire chart that
`Δ` violates is `J_0 = 1`, at `KJ` t-power `n+m−2`. Then

```text
   T_0(D=108 δ=3)   = KJ t-power 178 = stage 178   (pole rows exhausted at stage 92 (F) / 60 (G))
   T_0((99,66) δ=2) = KJ t-power 163 = stage 163   (pole rows exhausted at stage 77 / 50)
   T_0((99,66) δ=5/2)= KJ t-power 163 = stage 163  (pole rows exhausted at stage 181 / 118, τ-units)
```

and "depth `≥ T_0`" means the complete system: there is no truncation
advantage left, because the row that excludes `Δ` is the last one.

**Answer to (2).** Yes, with the precise degenerate object being `Δ_T`, not the
grading vertex: the rows at every finite depth are satisfiable by an
algebraically dependent assignment as long as the `h2`-only system is. The
rows that "break" this are only (α) the degree-0 Jacobian normalisation and
(β) the single-polynomial rows themselves (`h3` incidence, `h2` pole rows,
faces, floors, pins). The localisations `c ≠ 0` and D2-minimality
`Hc_5_3 = 2·jet1·jet2 ≠ 0` do not: they are satisfied on `Δ` (the witnesses
have `c = 1`, `Hc_5_3 = 2`).
