## 2. Group 1 — literal given-representative rectangle argument

**ML applies to the given pair.** ML's normalization (pp.302-305) opens with "Consider now a Jacobian pair f,g" and uses only algebraic independence of f,g (proved from the linear terms), the Lemma on independence, and the Dixmier derivation. No minimality of degree is assumed anywhere in pp.302-305; minimality-flavoured reductions appear only later (p.308, "we can replace g by a polynomial with smaller total degree"), and both new proofs correctly refuse those. Every automorphism used is a source automorphism of C[x,y]: cases 2) and 3) are elementary maps y -> y - c1 x^l or x -> x - c1 y^l, case 4) is linear, and the final orientation step x -> y, y -> -x is linear. CONFIRMED.

**Case exhaustion, checked locally.** A w-homogeneous polynomial tau of weight alpha+beta with alpha,beta>0 has support in {(1,1),(1+beta/alpha,0),(0,1+alpha/beta)}; for alpha != beta at most one pure power is integral, giving cases 1)-3) with l = beta/alpha or alpha/beta >= 1, and for alpha = beta tau is a quadratic form, which is case 4) when it has two independent linear factors and is excluded when it is a square (then J(rho,tau) = 0, contradicting J(rho,tau) = rho). So ML's four cases are exhaustive and l = 0 never occurs for a strictly positive weight.

**Termination, checked locally (ML states it only for cases 2-3).** In case 2 the monomial x^(i+lj) is the unique support point of that bidegree, so deg_x f = i+lj; after y -> y - c1 x^l every monomial has x-degree <= its old (1,l)-weight <= i+lj with equality only for the new leading form c x^i y^j, whose x-degree is i < i+lj; deg_y does not increase. In case 4, deg_x f = deg_y f = deg f = i+j before the step and both drop to at most i+j-1 after it. Hence deg_x f + deg_y f strictly decreases at every step, including the linear step, and the process ends with every positive-weight leading form a monomial. Then w = (1,eps) and (eps,1) show supp f contains (d_x,d_y), and the (1,1) Dixmier equation J(x^a y^b, tau) = x^a y^b with polynomial quadratic tau forces (a-b)B = 1, so d_x != d_y. CONFIRMED.

**Proportional Q rectangle.** With (r,s) the joint corner of Pbar, every strictly positive weight has Pbar_w = alpha x^r y^s. The bracket's top component has weight w(Pbar)+w(Qbar)-alpha-beta >= w(Qbar) > 0 (coordinator's count; Astra's Prop 4.1 route is equivalent), so [x^r y^s, Qbar_w] = 0. Since [x^r y^s, x^i y^j] = (rj - si) x^(r+i-1) y^(s+j-1) with distinct outputs for distinct (i,j), every monomial of Qbar_w lies on the ray of (r,s), and positive homogeneity leaves one monomial. The (1,1) monomial (t,u) is the farthest support point on the ray; any other positive w cannot pick a nearer ray point because (t,u) has larger w-value. Letting w tend to (1,0) and (0,1) gives supp Qbar in [0,t]x[0,u] with (t,u) a nonzero corner. CONFIRMED.

**Inverse-substitution degrees.** With U = psi^-1(x), V = psi^-1(y), P0 = sum p_ij U^i V^j. Because i <= r, j <= s and M,N >= 1, (r,s) is the unique maximiser of Mi+Nj on the support; U_M^r V_N^s is nonzero in the domain C[x,y] whether or not U_M and V_N are dependent; every other term has strictly smaller degree. So deg P0 = Mr+Ns, (P0)_top = alpha U_M^r V_N^s, and likewise deg Q0 = Mt+Nu. With (t,u) = lambda (r,s) this gives deg Q0 / deg P0 = lambda, so the reduced ratio is derived from two rectangles plus the positive functional (M,N), never assumed. CONFIRMED.

**Zero and linear cases.** r = 0 makes Pbar univariate in y; the bracket -p'(y) Qbar_x = const != 0 forces Pbar linear and Qbar = a x + q(y), an automorphism, contradicting the Division Lemma (Chau, Section 3, over C). The swap makes 0 < r < s. M,N >= 1 because U,V are nonconstant. CONFIRMED.

Failed arrow: uniqueness of the maximiser of Mi+Nj (here M=1,N=1 for U = x+y^2, V = y gives the tie 2 = 2, and the two leading terms cancel to x).
