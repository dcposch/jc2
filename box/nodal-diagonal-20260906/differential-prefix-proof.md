# Exact early characteristic differential reconstruction

This is an ideal-level identity over an arbitrary commutative Q-algebra A, including coefficient rings with zero divisors. It does not discard nilpotents and is not merely an equality of radicals.

Use normalized finite series F(t,w)=sum F_r(w)t^r and G(t,w)=sum G_r(w)t^r, with fixed monic P(w), F0=P³ and G0=P². Let

 E=F²−G³, T=3G_t F−2G F_t.

Direct differentiation gives G E_t−3G_t E=−F T. Write C(Q) for the ideal of A generated by all w-coefficients of a polynomial Q(w). For every r>=1, let I_<r=sum_{k<r}C(E_k). Since E0=0, induction proves

 I_<r=sum_{k=0}^{r−2}C(T_k),
 I_<r+C(E_r)=I_<r+C(T_(r−1)).

For the induction step, take the t^(r−1) coefficient of the differential identity and reduce modulo I_<r. Terms involving earlier E coefficients vanish; terms involving earlier T coefficients vanish by the induction hypothesis. The remaining identity is

 r*P²*E_r=−P³*T_(r−1).

Monic P² is a nonzerodivisor in (A/I_<r)[w], regardless of zero divisors in A/I_<r. Therefore r*E_r=−P*T_(r−1). Multiplication by a monic polynomial preserves a coefficient ideal: C(PQ)=C(Q), because the highest w-coefficient of PQ recovers the highest coefficient of Q, and descending subtraction recovers each remaining coefficient with no division except by the leading unit. Since r is invertible over Q, the two coefficient ideals agree after adding I_<r. This establishes the induction.

Now expand

 T_(r−1)=L_r−2r*G0*F_r,
 L_r=sum_{i=1}^r (5i−2r)*G_i*F_(r−i),

with G_i=0 for i>m. All F coefficients in L_r have index below r. Divide L_r monically in w by G0=P²:

 L_r=P²*Q_r+Rem_r, deg_w Rem_r<deg_w P².

The coefficient transform between a polynomial and its monic quotient/remainder is invertible and A-linear. Consequently

 C(T_(r−1))=C(Q_r−2r*F_r)+C(Rem_r).

Thus F_r=Q_r/(2r) is an exact unit-pivot graph elimination, provided EVERY coefficient of Rem_r is retained. It gives an isomorphic coefficient quotient and has inverse the original F_r coordinate. If deg_w G_i<=m−i and deg_w F_(r−i)<=n−r+i, then deg_w L_r<=n+m−r and hence deg_w Q_r<=n−r. The required F degree support is automatic; a higher quotient coefficient must still be emitted if a different input presentation permits larger degrees.

All later characteristic rows, complete leading targets, source rows and every Jacobian row remain required. Extending the homogeneous recurrence past the pG arrival without its inhomogeneous term would change the ideal.

Audit outcome: the proposed early recurrence is exact and valid over arbitrary Q coefficient quotients. The proof depends on monicity, retaining all remainder coefficients, and the stated prefix cutoff; it supplies no unit or properness decision by itself.
