# A linear subset of the actual characteristic upper equations

Let L=eqK, D2=L−W, and retain the complete necessary target family

    T=Q^e−P^q+Σ gamma_uv P^u Q^v,
    P=h^e+Σ alpha_i h^(e−i), Q=h^q+Σ_(i≥2) beta_i h^(q−i).

All blocks have y-degree below K; h is monic of y-degree K. Every target coefficient gamma_uv is a scalar, and the permitted lower target monomials have degree at most L−K. For (e,q)=(3,2), the unique monomial of degree L−K is PQ; for (4,3), it is P²Q. These facts are the already derived Proposition 3.1 family in the main report. Actual source realizations satisfy deg_y T=D2 and have scalar nonzero leader by Proposition 2.2 with the proved ell extension. Write U for its full upper-degree and scalar-leader coefficient equations, with the forced scalar target pivots included/eliminated as in the canonical lambda derivation.

Suppose alpha1 is nonzero, and let d=deg_y alpha1. The term

    −q alpha1 h^(eq−1)

in Q^e−P^q has degree L−K+d. It is the unique highest correction to the cancelled monic h^(eq) terms. Indeed, two alpha1 factors have deficit 2(K−d)>K−d, and every occurrence of alpha_i or beta_i with i≥2 has deficit at least K+1>K−d. Additional correction factors only increase these positive deficits. This comparison takes actual y-degrees; all lower coefficients of h contribute at strictly lower y-degree and cannot change the leader. The case alpha1=0 already satisfies every conclusion below.

If W>K, then D2<L−K. If d≥1, the displayed term has degree strictly greater than L−K, so no lower target monomial can cancel it. This contradicts deg_y T=D2. Thus alpha1 belongs to k[x]. The coefficient of y^(L−K) in T is then exactly

    −q alpha1(x)+gamma_top.

Every other raw correction has smaller y-degree, and the highest lower target monomial is monic of degree L−K. The entire displayed coefficient is an upper row because D2<L−K. Since gamma_top is a scalar, it follows that alpha1 is a scalar. Consequently **A1_b,a=0 for every (b,a)≠(0,0)**. The top target pivot is gamma_top=q A1_0,0. The scalar A1_0,0 remains free.

If 0<W<K, then D2>L−K. The same unique-highest-term argument forces d≤K−W. Thus every alpha1 coefficient with a>K−W is zero. At a=K−W, the coefficient of y^D2 is exactly

    −q [y^(K−W)]alpha1(x).

No lower target monomial reaches D2. Two alpha1 factors now have degree at most L−2W<D2; any alpha_i/beta_i correction with i≥2 has degree at most L−K−1<D2. Scalar attainment therefore additionally forces **A1_b,K−W=0 for every b>0**. The scalar coordinate A1_0,K−W remains free and its nonzero multiple −q is the leader on this locus. In C70 and C127 the G support contains no positive-x coordinate at that deficit, so this latter list is empty.

These constraints are polynomial ideal consequences, not merely radical consequences. Descend through the coefficients at y^(L−1), y^(L−2), … . At deficit r≤K, the new alpha1 coefficient occurs with constant nonzero pivot −q, and every other contribution is in the ideal generated by previously removed higher alpha1 coefficients, except for the scalar gamma_top at r=K. This follows equally from the preceding deficit comparison or direct expansion. Thus each new coefficient is an explicit rational linear combination of its upper row and preceding constraints. At r=K only its positive-x coefficients are removed. For W<K stop at r=W−1 and then apply the positive-x scalar-leader rows at r=W. This triangular induction proves every listed coordinate lies in U after the already specified scalar target pivots. It uses no division by a parameter, no branch choice, and no extra normalization of h or alpha1.

`UX.json` lists all constraints in the original declared variable order and gives each bidegree. Their counts, in increasing six-class size followed by R001–R004, are

    3,10,2,16,17,23; 18,19,19,13.

Adding these coordinate equations to the Jacobian-plus-c-plus-lambda receiver is a necessary attainment refinement. It is not an arbitrary support specialization. Its points still need not satisfy the remaining U equations or realize source data. A UNIT would be sufficient for a source exclusion only with the full exact cofactor certificate and these explicit necessity maps.

The reduced leader files `*.lambda-UX.txt.gz` are obtained from the EXACT canonical h-adic lambda by the coordinate quotient map sending the listed UX variables to zero and retaining all other original coordinates. `ux_constraints.py` verifies the map term by term, preserving formula custody. It records the exact identity

    lambda−lambda_UX=Σ z D_z,

where every z is a listed UX coordinate; assign each removed monomial to its first such factor and divide by z. Hence adjoining UX makes the two leader localizers equivalent:

    (UX,Z lambda−s^(W+1))=(UX,Z lambda_UX−s^(W+1)).

The same applies to any source Jacobian row f replaced by f mod UX, provided the UX coordinate equations are retained (or the equivalent explicit quotient-ring map is declared). The original ring can retain every G parameter and c. No variable is silently made a coefficient-field unit.
