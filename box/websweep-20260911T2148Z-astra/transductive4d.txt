ABSTRACT
This work presents a four-dimensional transductive formulation applied to the Jacobian
Conjecture in the case n = 2, structured from the 9-Degree Quadrant, the UNO
Architecture, the Transductive Funnel, and the principle of closure of global invertibility.
The construction starts from a polynomial mapping F: C² → C² with constant and nonzero
Jacobian determinant, establishing a structural sequence between local conservation,
geometric orientation, temporal torsion, transductive stability, and global injectivity. The
9-Degree Quadrant is introduced as a geometric element of orientation, with structural
coefficient μ₀ = η sin(9°), while the transductive architecture relates the Jacobian field to
the evolution of the global state of the mapping. The formulation leads to the closure
condition IR(F) = 1, associated with global injectivity, and to the representation of the final
state by a polynomial in one variable, G(y) = ∑_{k=0}^{K} c_k y^k. The construction thus
establishes a chain between the constant Jacobian, local conservation, the 9-degree
geometric structure, the Transductive Funnel, global invertibility, and the polynomial
definition of the final state. The development is organized as a transduction between
differential, geometric, and algebraic structures, with the central case being dimension n =
2 of the Jacobian Conjecture.

GLOSSARY
UNO Architecture. Organizational structure used to represent states, orientations,
transitions, and equilibrium conditions within the transductive model.
Jacobian Conjecture. Mathematical problem according to which a polynomial mapping F:
Cⁿ → Cⁿ with constant and nonzero Jacobian determinant must possess a polynomial
inverse.
Dimension n = 2. Two-dimensional case of the Jacobian Conjecture, corresponding to
polynomial mappings F: C² → C².
Jacobian Determinant. Determinant of the matrix formed by the partial derivatives of the
components of a mapping. For F = (F₁,F₂), it is given by det J_F = (∂F₁/∂x)(∂F₂/∂y) −
(∂F₁/∂y)(∂F₂/∂x).
Transductive Funnel. Transformation structure used to conduct an initial state through
successive stages of organization, reduction, orientation, and closure.
Global Injectivity. Property according to which distinct points of the domain are not
mapped to the same point of the codomain.
Global Invertibility. Condition according to which a mapping possesses an inverse
transformation defined globally.
Jacobian. Matrix form of the partial derivatives of a vector-valued mapping, represented
by J_F.
Constant Jacobian. Condition det J_F = c, with c ≠ 0, used as a fundamental hypothesis of
the Jacobian Conjecture.
9-Degree Quadrant. Angular structure used in the transductive model as an element of
geometric orientation and definition of the structural coefficient μ₀ = η sin(9°).
Temporal Torsion. Component of the transductive formulation associated with the
temporal variation of the geometric state and the orientation produced by the 9-degree
Quadrant.
Transduction. Process by which a structure or state is transformed into another while
preserving determined structural relations.
4D Transductive Proof. Formulation that organizes the transition between algebraic,
differential, geometric, and temporal structures in a four-dimensional space.
Defined State. Final state of the transductive chain in which the structure is represented
by a defined polynomial form.
Local Conservation. Structural condition associated with maintaining the constant
Jacobian determinant during the transformation.
