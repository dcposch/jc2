ABSTRACT
The Jacobian Conjecture states that a polynomial transformation
F: C^n → C^n
with constant and nonzero Jacobian determinant should possess a polynomial inverse.
Recent mathematical developments have profoundly modified the problem: the conjecture
was disproved in dimension n ≥ 3, while the case n = 2 remains the central problem.
This work presents a structural solution proposal for the two-dimensional case using
exclusively the theoretical architecture developed in the works of Cláudio Vicente da Silva.
The construction integrates the Equilibrium Model, the Transductive Funnel, the structural
coherence metric, the Latency operator, the Autonomous Transduction Machine, the UNO
Spiral, the transition from the continuous to the defined state, the localization structure, the
1/2 principle, the 142857 cycle, the π structural ruler, the architecture of memory, coverage,
branching, recovery, and audit, as well as the principles developed in the works on the zeta
function and transductive geometry.
The central idea is to replace the direct passage
