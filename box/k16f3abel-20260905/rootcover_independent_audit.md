# Independent audit of coefficient rigidity and direct controls

Read scope: same-lane `controls_notes.md`, `rigidity_notes.md`,
`controls_emit_direct.py`, `rigidity_coincident_allb.py`, the two t=11
coincident-root logs and integer certificates, `controls_t2_certificate.py`,
and the already-read frozen Abel source. No other lane was read.

One correction is required in the binomial semigroup paragraph. For
arbitrary b, the actual support Sigma is only contained in {j,t+1}.
The displayed membership equivalences must therefore refer to the enlarged
semigroup generated by j and t+1, rather than to the actual semigroup
generated by Sigma. For example t=4,j=2,b=0 has Sigma={2}, so 9 is absent
from the actual semigroup despite j dividing t. The final exclusion
theorem remains valid: the actual semigroup is a subset of the enlarged
one, and the proof only needs necessary membership conditions. The listed
exceptions do not assert existence of any solution.

Apart from that presentation correction, the following checks pass:

- The descending high-row pivots and the B pivot are exact scalar units
  on both normalizer factors. All other unresolved W coefficients lie
  below the relevant row, so the recursion is triangular.
- The weights of F3 are consistent term by term. Scalar division preserves
  polynomial dependence on C coefficients and b; the claimed affine
  dependence on b follows from the weights being below 2(t+1).
- The stabilizer argument and the corrected binomial semigroup case split
  are valid with repeated C roots. The cubic b coefficient on monomial C
  is a scalar unit times t-2, and does force b=0 for t>=3.
- The requested t=2,y=1/5 negative target control conflicts with the exact
  displayed F1--F3 equations. The stated row certificate for B eta checks
  directly, and its reduced family has B=eta=0 and arbitrary b. This is
  consistent with the frozen source: the failure is V0/dimension zero,
  while (8.1) holds. The mismatch must be explained, not silently used as
  a negative control for B eta vanishing.
- The all-b normalization in the coincident-root driver is correct:
  alpha=3/(4y^2), V=W/alpha, beta=B/alpha, e=eta/alpha, u=by,
  K=x^2 C-u. A separate exact SymPy substitution verified the full
  normalized equation and returned
  `INDEPENDENT_ALL_B_NORMALIZATION_EXACT_PASS`.
- At t=11 the specified factors are d=-2,y=5/23 and d=2,y=7/23.
  In each, u ranges over the entire scalar field because y is a unit.
  Thus unit gcd in Q[u] proves exclusion over its algebraic closure as
  well. Rational clearing and primitive-content removal multiply rows
  only by nonzero characteristic-zero scalars.
- Both t=11 logs have exact high rows zero and gcd 1 from rows 22 and 21.
  The recorded integer identity is valid: for quadratics
  f=a u^2+b u+c and g=A u^2+B u+C, Delta=aB-Ab,
  epsilon=aC-Ac, and L=a Delta u+b Delta-a epsilon,
  `(Delta^2+A L)f-a L g=a epsilon^2-b epsilon Delta+c Delta^2`.
  Each recorded right side is nonzero. These certificates exclude every
  b on C=(x-1)^10. Weighted scaling handles C=(x-a)^10 for a nonzero,
  and the monomial argument handles a=0.

These are stratum exclusions and exact controls. They do not prove the
general all-t atom or justify promotion of theorem (T). No CAS process
remains from this audit.
