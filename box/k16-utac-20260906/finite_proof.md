# Exact reduced free closing geometry at N=4,5; modular N=6

This note uses only UF and its marked jets, freshly reconstructing all coefficient equations. It imports no ray equations, no frozen certificate, no `d,y,omega` values from a ray, and no precomputed Gröbner basis. The parameter `d` below is defined afresh by the leading coefficient equation of UF at the actual polynomial degree.

## 1. Actual degree and normalization

Work geometrically over an algebraic closure of Q (or of the explicitly stated finite field). Let a point of the free closing variety give polynomials L,P, with b=1, L(0)=-1,L'(0)=0, P0=-1/4,P1=-B,P2=eta,P3=w2. The usual bottom recursion plus the closing equations is equivalent to the full UF polynomial identity, with deg L<=N,deg P<=2N.

If m=deg L>=2 and n=deg P, then n=2m: the leading degree 4m of R has nonzero coefficient (3/16)lc(L)^4; if n<2m it cannot be matched, while if n>2m the nonzero term (2n-3)lc(P)^2 x^(2n) cannot cancel. The characteristic bound is harmless at all finite fields used here, since n<=12 and p=1000003.

Choose alpha with alpha^m*lc(L)=1 and replace x by alpha*x. The induced parameter map is

    l_j -> alpha^j*l_j, B -> alpha*B,
    eta -> alpha^2*eta, w2 -> alpha^3*w2, b -> b.

The Euler derivation commutes with this substitution, so UF is preserved. This covers every nonzero leading coefficient over the algebraic closure. In the normalized actual-degree-m stratum write

    L=-b+l2*x²+...+l_(m-1)*x^(m-1)+x^m.

The free problem of interest has b=1. Retaining b temporarily restores a positive grading and permits a much smaller exact calculation. This is a variable in a new homogeneous polynomial ring, not an extra premise imposed on the free closing problem.

## 2. Independent leading-degree elimination

Write p=[x^(2m)]P. The top UF equation is

    (4m-3)p²+(3/2)p-3/16=0.

Its roots are p=1/[4(2d+1)], with 3d²=m. For m=2,4,5 this is an irreducible quadratic field over Q; both embeddings are covered because the entire symbolic identity is in Q[d]/(3d²-m). At m=3 the two rational roots d=1 and d=-1 are run separately.

For j=2m-1,...,2, row 2m+j has constant pivot

    A_j=2(2m+j-3)p+3/2=(2m+j+6d)/(2(2d+1)).

These pivots never vanish in characteristic zero for m>=2,j>=2: vanishing would imply (2m+j)²=12m, whereas (2m+2)²>12m. The eta-dependent forcing has degree at most m+2<=2m, so it is absent from these rows; the B-dependent forcing has degree at most 2m+1, so the resulting P_j,j>=2 are independent of B as well. The driver reconstructs these coefficients with ring operations and divisions only by the displayed nonzero scalars.

Set eta=P2, P1=-B, P0=-b²/4. Row 2m+1 is affine in B and its pivot is

    -((4m-3)p+3/4)=-3d/2 !=0.

Solve it for B. The remaining ideal K_m is generated by rows x^4,...,x^(2m), exactly 2m-3 equations, in

    Q(d)[l2,...,l_(m-1),b],
    weights (m-2,m-3,...,1,m), order wp(m-2,...,1,m).

Every genuine normalized actual-degree-m solution with b!=0 gives a zero of K_m with b!=0, and conversely every such zero reconstructs a UF solution. The driver independently checks that the B derivative equals its stated pivot and that rows 0..3 and 2m+1..4m vanish identically after reconstruction. This explicitly handles every generator image and avoids inferring a map from matching variable names.

At m=3,d=-1 there is a resonance if one tries to solve the row for P0 from infinity. This calculation does not divide by that pivot: P0 is prescribed, row 2m is retained among the equations. No resonance branch is omitted.

## 3. Exact calculations and geometry

`finite_geometry.py` generates each Singular script from the displayed UF formulas. Compact output and JSON are `finite_m*_Q_*.{out,json}`. All coefficient controls pass.

    actual m    coefficient field         result
    2           Q(d),3d²=2, b=1           std(K_m)=(1)
    3           Q,d=+1, b=1               std(K_m)=(1)
    3           Q,d=-1, b=1               std(K_m)=(l2²)
    4           Q(d),3d²=4, b variable    GB size17, dim0, min e with b^e in K_m:4
    5           Q(d),3d²=5, b variable    GB size60, dim0, min e with b^e in K_m:5

The low strata also admit direct small-polynomial checks (`finite_low_resultant.py`). At m=2 the remaining row is -(6d+7)/40, which is nonzero under 3d²=2. At m=3,d=-1, writing h=l2, rows5 and6 are (3/15625)h⁴(34h³-775) and (3/31250)h³(59h³-1375); h!=0 would force 775*59=1375*34, whereas these products are 45725 and 46750. At m=3,d=+1, h=0 fails the last row. For h!=0 put z=h³; rows5 and6 imply f=1986398181z²-16122815232z-3806398081024=0 and g=48281373z²-392411136z-49433741312=0. Their exact resultant is 7323721655258186443534573944585584640000, nonzero. Thus no actual degree3 solution on this branch is lost in the Gröbner computation.

At m=3,d=-1 the driver also checks exactly, after l2=0, that B=eta=0 and P+L²/4=0. Undoing the x normalization gives the entire cubic family

    L=-1+a*x³, P=-(L²)/4,
    B=eta=l2=0, w2=a/2.

If L is constant, it must be -1. Then G=-Bx and deg R<=3. A polynomial P of degree n>=2 would give the unique highest term (2n-3)lc(P)² x^(2n), since 2n>n+1 and 2n>3. Hence deg P<=1 and the jets force

    L=-1, P=-1/4-B*x, eta=w2=0.

Direct substitution verifies this family for arbitrary B. There is no actual degree1 stratum because L'(0)=0.

Therefore, for N=4 and N=5, exactly and in characteristic zero,

    sqrt(I_close,N)=J_N
      =(u,l2,l4,...,lN,l3-2*w2,B*w2),
    u=4eta+3l2.

Both inclusions are proved: every UF solution is in the two listed families by the exhaustive degree stratification, and every point of either family satisfies UF by direct substitution. The proposed ideal is radical because its quotient is Q[B,w2]/(B*w2). Equality over the algebraic closure descends by faithful scalar extension to Q.

The curve is reducible, with two affine lines meeting transversely at the origin. Its normalization is A1 disjoint union A1, and each component has genus0. Its ordinary projective closure is two projective lines meeting in one point and has arithmetic genus0. A single irreducible-curve genus should not be assigned without this qualification. On the first axis eta=0,w2=0 and B is its parameter; on the second eta=B=0 and w2 is its parameter. Thus B and w2 are each outside the radical, by explicit points, not merely by failure of bounded power searches. u,l2,T2,B*eta vanish on both components. This curve is neither the whole monomial locus nor just the cubic P=-L²/4 family: it also has the constant-L, arbitrary-B component.

These exact radical equalities do NOT prove the frozen modular minimal nilpotence indices19 and27 over Q. They prove existence of some characteristic-zero powers. The actual minimum remains a separate ideal-membership calculation.

## 4. Full modular N=6 control

All actual degrees are covered over the SAME prime p=1000003. Split quadratics are handled by separate prime-field roots, never by pretending a product algebra is a field:

    m2:  d=804413,195590                    both unit at b=1
    m3:  d=1,-1                            unit; (l2²) at b=1
    m4:  3d²-4 irreducible, F_(p²)          GB17, dim0, min b-power4
    m5:  d=140269,859734                    each GB60,dim0,min b-power5
    m6:  3d²-6 irreducible (d²=2),F_(p²)    GB230,dim0,min b-power6

The residue classes m/3 are 333335,1,666670,333336,2 respectively. Euler criteria are +1,+1,-1,+1,-1. The two listed roots in each split case square to m/3; all top-recursion pivots and the B leader are explicitly checked. Thus the same degree argument proves

    sqrt(I_close,6 over F_p)=J_6.

This is a modular result only. The exact m6 attempt passed the coefficient-construction controls but timed out after 600.108 seconds while computing std(K_m); it was terminated and yields no exact radical claim. It is not an unverified extrapolation from m4,m5, nor does a unit ideal modulo p by itself imply a characteristic-zero unit ideal.

## 5. Uniform limit

The two displayed families solve UF for every truncation bound N>=3. What is not obtained from the finite calculations is exclusion of actual degree m>=6 in characteristic zero, or of any unbounded m. The leading-degree reformulation gives an exact, unbounded residual problem: for every m>=4 prove b in sqrt(K_m), or prove the weaker implication eta=0 on V(K_m) at b!=0. A uniform equality V(I_close,N)=V(J_N) would be stronger than U-TAC and is not licensed by the tables.
