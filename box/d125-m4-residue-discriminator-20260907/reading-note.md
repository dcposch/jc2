# Two literal reading clarifications

In report §3, the compact expression `C_gp²=b⁴` means **([gp]C)²=b⁴**, the square of the gp coefficient. It does not denote [gp²]C, which vanishes by even parity. The preceding coefficient list and the exact check both give [gp]C=−b².

The ordinaryness proof for W works over the stated finite coefficient algebra without needing to assert it is a field. The v⁰ coefficient of φ(R) is exactly3u. Multiplication by3u is injective in K[u] for every Q-algebra K, so a negative lowest v coefficient of φ(W) cannot disappear in φ(R)φ(W). This supplies the regular-leading-coefficient premise of the valuation step.

These clarify the closed body and do not change its formulas, hypotheses, or claimed finite-jet scope.
