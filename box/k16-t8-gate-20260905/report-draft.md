## 4. The specialization direction: properness, not flatness alone

presentation of the finitely generated positively graded algebra. Proj

closed subset of a local scheme contains its closed point; the empty special
fibre excludes that point. Hence X itself is empty, so its generic fibre is
empty, and `V(J_K)={0}`. This is the precise direction needed for promotion.

fibre, and has nonempty generic fibre. Thus **affine mod-p emptiness cannot

For b4=0, normalize its first nonzero q_j, j=2,...,7, using a j-th root over
