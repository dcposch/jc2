### 3.1 The previously missing upper-envelope step

Let P have a constant-Jacobian polynomial mate and unique top-total monomial lambda*x^a*y^b with a,b>0. Then deg_x P<=a and deg_y P<=b.

Suppose a support point(i,j) has i>a. Necessarily j<b and (i-a)/(b-j)<1. Let t be the maximum of these finitely many ratios and choose positive primitive integers rho>sigma with sigma/rho=t. The(rho,sigma)-face contains(a,b) and at least one point to its right and below. Every point with i<=a,j>b has strictly smaller weight, since the total-degree leader is unique; all other nonattaining points do too. Thus this face is nonmonomial and its counterclockwise END is(a,b).

The polynomial Euler element E has weight rho+sigma. Since rho>sigma>0, its only possible monomials are xy and, if integral, y^(1+rho/sigma). If the latter is absent, E is a monomial, which is incompatible with this nonmonomial face by the Euler-element monomial property. If present, its END is(0,1+rho/sigma), neither(1,1) nor parallel to(a,b). This contradicts the Euler endpoint condition. No i>a is possible. Interchanging x,y gives the other bound. All weights used here are strictly positive, and E is POLYNOMIAL; no Laurent clause is imported.
