# Smooth omitted boundary curves

**PROVISIONAL / MANUAL / PASS_CONDITIONAL.** ROOT authored the proof; Sol independently reviewed it. JC2 remains unresolved.

For a normal integral affine complex surface X containing a marked U=A², suppose q:X→P¹ restricts to a linear coordinate on U and q⁻¹(A¹)=U. Every reduced omitted prime curve E is smooth. With the retained finite birational normalization A¹→E, this gives E≅A¹. Rational singularities of X are not required.

The [exact author proof](../../xmodel/smooth-omitted-boundary-swarmHQ-root-20261030.md) is preserved with its original pre-review label. Its [independent review](../../xmodel/fiber-normality-review-sol56-20261030T0210Z.md) accepts the argument; this page records the subsequent integration status without changing either sealed body. The conditional status concerns named standard foundations and attachment to the inherited actual-Keller normalization package, not an assumption of the lemma's central conclusion.

A proper normal completion of the coordinate ruling is dominated by blowups of P¹×P¹. Point-blowup O-cohomology vanishing and the low-degree Leray injection give H¹(T_V,O)=0 over an affine base neighborhood. A two-affine cover gives H² vanishing. The full-fibre exact sequence, then its quotient to each reduced component C, gives H¹(C,O)=0; the normalization sequence forces C to be smooth rational.

## Dependencies and limits

The [ruling lemma](ruling-lemma.md) and its [independent review](ruling-review.md) supply q under their marked normal-affine extension hypotheses. The retained [boundary-normalization review](boundary-normalization.md) supplies the finite birational A¹ normalization in the campaign's actual-source package. Its former finite-bijective-only qualification is strengthened by the new proof; its historical bytes remain unchanged.

The proof imports characteristic-zero resolution/common resolution of surfaces, normal-target pushforward of O, Leray, properness, and finite normalization of curves. It includes the point-blowup Čech calculation directly. [Stacks 0C5Q](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C5Q) supports the regular-surface resolution framework. The partial [Stacks 0G5H](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0G5H) excerpt is not used beyond its supplied hypotheses; the direct calculation is the justification here. These foundations have not all been audited from first principles.

Smoothness is asserted for each reduced omitted curve, not for the ambient surface or the whole possibly nonreduced infinity fibre. No immersion of E's target image follows. Normal ramification, image singularities, omitted-sheet degrees and sheet-budget slack remain uncontrolled. This does not prove that E is empty, finite normalization is surjective from U, field degree is one, or the Keller map is invertible. No novelty or JC2 claim is made.
