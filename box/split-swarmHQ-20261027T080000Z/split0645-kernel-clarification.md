# SPLIT0645 — explicit kernel check for the converse count
ROOT clarification after the independent review; this paragraph was not separately reviewed. It spells out the final converse in proposal Step10 without changing the count or hypotheses.
A normalized V0 preimage nu of eta under H^n has finite values on every nonzero element of C[H_1^n,H_2^n]. It cannot have a nonzero polynomial g with nu(g)=+infinity: g is algebraic and nonzero over the target fraction field, so clearing denominators in its minimal polynomial gives a_0(H^n)+a_1(H^n)g+...+a_r(H^n)g^r=0 with nonzero a_0. The constant term has finite value and every other term has infinite value, contradicting the valuation inequality. Thus nu has zero kernel and extends to a field valuation; its unique rescaling with restriction eta is one of the counted prolongations.
This uses only generically finite field degree and the finite-valued target valuation; it does not assume global integrality, properness, or a finite normalization equal to the source.
