## 2. McKay–Wang plus the centralizer: primitive TWO-factor H only

McKay–Wang1991, Theorem5, printedp42, bounds the distinct irreducible factors of the leading form of the GIVEN polynomial by r+s+2 when the leading Jacobian is the monomial x^r y^s. Normalize the nonzero constant scalar by a target scaling and set r=s=0. Over an algebraic closure, H therefore has at most TWO distinct projective linear factors. This is not a claim about the number of places of a normalized generic fibre, and does not use a degree-changing source normalization. The source is the primary author-uploaded article, retained as its complete browser text extraction, not as PDF bytes. [McKay–Wang primary article](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Stuart-Wang/publication/243064554_A_Note_on_the_Jacobian_Condition_and_Two_Points_at_Infinity/links/55b5379008ae9289a08a68ce/A-Note-on-the-Jacobian-Condition-and-Two-Points-at-Infinity.pdf).

The additional centralizer restriction is elementary typing. It survives extension K to E: expand the finitely many coefficients of a polynomial commuting with H in a K-linearly independent coefficient basis; every resulting K-polynomial commutes with H and hence belongs to K[H]. Thus its E-linear combination belongs to E[H]. No algebraic-closure assumption is silently added to the original theorem.

If geometrically `H=a L^D` has one factor, D>=3, then L commutes with H but is not in the scalar polynomial algebra generated by H. This violates the centralizer. If

    H=a L1^r L2^s,  r,s>0, L1 and L2 distinct,

and e=gcd(r,s)>1, then `L1^(r/e)L2^(s/e)` gives the same contradiction. Therefore **exactly two factors and gcd(r,s)=1** are necessary. Conversely in linear coordinates x=L1,y=L2, the monomials commuting with a*x^r*y^s satisfy r*j-s*i=0, and their distinct bracket-output indices do not mix. For coprime r,s these monomials are exactly powers of x^r*y^s. This verifies the centralizer typing without importing a general closed-polynomial theorem. Here r+s=D; the exponents are unequal for D>=3. No restriction r,s<=2 follows.

McKay–Wang is printed over C. For the field-point claims used here, the finitely generated characteristic-zero field containing all coefficients and the finitely many geometric factor coefficients embeds into C. Factor multiplicities, polynomial identities and nonzero scalars are preserved. This is field-point transport only, not a theorem over arbitrary rings or nilpotents. The OCR extraction damages some displayed proof equations; Theorem5 states its argument by analogy with Theorem1, using the earlier1988 lemmas. Those upstream lemmas were not reproved here.
