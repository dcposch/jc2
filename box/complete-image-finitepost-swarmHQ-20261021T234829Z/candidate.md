# Complete-image obstruction for arbitrary rational postcomposition

## Precise statement

Work over C. Let U,S be smooth integral algebraic surfaces and f:U->S a dominant
morphism. Let C0 be an irreducible positive-dimensional curve in U,and let E be
a complete irreducible reduced curve in S. Assume f(C0)=E set-theoretically and
f is etale at every point of C0. It suffices that each point of E have at least
one etale preimage in C0,but use the stronger hypothesis for this version.

There is no rational pair g=(p,q):S-->A2 such that the rational composition
g composed f extends to a REGULAR QUASI-FINITE morphism H:U->A2.
In particular,if U is an open subset of the source A2 containing C0,no such
composition can extend to a polynomial Keller map on the whole source plane.

No field-generation condition C(p,q)=C(S),birationality of g,chosen two-form,
projectivity/rationality of S,or properness of f is assumed. The added etaleness
of f along C0 distinguishes this statement from the older birational-chart result.

## Proof to audit

For y in E choose x in C0 with f(x)=y. Put A=O_(S,y),B=O_(U,x).
Etaleness makes A->B flat;as a local map it is faithfully flat. Dominance
embeds K(S) into K(U). For r equal to p or q,write r=a/b in Frac(A),b!=0.
The pullback r has a regular representative h in B,the corresponding coordinate
of H. Equality in K(U) gives a=bh in B. Faithful flatness contracts ideals:
bB intersect A=bA. Thus a=bc in A for some c,so r=c belongs to A.

For completeness,ideal contraction follows by applying faithful flatness to
A/(b):its natural map into B/(b) is injective. Equivalently,if a nonzero cyclic
submodule generated by a mod b mapped to zero,flatness would embed its tensor
product in B/(b),while faithfulness would make that tensor product nonzero.

This works for both coordinates and every y in E. Therefore their regularity
loci contain E,and g is a morphism on a neighborhood W of E. A regular function
on a complete irreducible reduced curve over C is constant;normality of E is not
needed (one can pull back to its complete normalization). Hence g(E) is one point.
On f^(-1)(W),the regular maps H and g composed f agree on a dense open and thus
everywhere. Consequently H contracts C0,contradicting quasi-finiteness. QED.

## Checks and failure modes

- Flat descent is essential to this proof:for f(s,t)=(s,st),the rational
  function Y/X pulls back to regular t at (0,0),but is not regular at the target
  origin. This f is not flat/etale there;dominance alone is insufficient.
- A1 mapping into its projective completion P1 is not set-surjective. A dense
  image cannot replace f(C0)=E;an omitted point may carry a target pole.
- Without quasi-finiteness,a constant postmap is harmless and no contradiction
  follows. Singularity of a nodal E is harmless only because ambient S is smooth
  and the etale-preimage premise includes the node,not merely its smooth locus.
