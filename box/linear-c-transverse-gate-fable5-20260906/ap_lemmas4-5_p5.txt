Lemma 5. Let k be a field. Polynomials f, g ∈ k[x1 , . . . , xn ] \ k are alge-
braically dependent (over k) if and only if there exists a closed polynomial
h ∈ k[x1 , . . . , xn ] such that f, g ∈ k[h].
Proof. Assume that f, g are algebraically dependent. By the Noether Nor-
malization Lemma, there exists an element r ∈ k[f, g] such that k[r] ⊂ k[f, g]
is an integral extension. By Proposition 1, the integral closure of k[r] in
k[x1 , . . . , xn ] has a form k[h] for some closed polynomial h.
   Conversely, if f, g ∈ k[h] then these polynomials are obviously alge-
braically dependent.                                                        
