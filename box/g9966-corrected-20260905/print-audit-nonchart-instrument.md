# A printed characteristic instrument beyond the finite root chart

This is a derivation of a possible next instrument, not a claim that it was added to the branch engine and not a branch kill. The existing root/remainder chart records necessary consequences of characteristic data; it does not assert that every chart point actually has those characteristic data. In particular the positive-degree Jacobian rows admit J=0.

There are two distinct ways to exclude a pure-power point `F=H^3,G=H^2` with H monic of y-degree33. The direct Keller instrument adds a scalar lambda_J and the equations `J(F,G)=lambda_J`, `Z_J*lambda_J−1=0`, retaining every source coefficient. If all positive-degree Jacobian rows were already imposed, the extra condition is just nonvanishing of the constant coefficient. Setting that coefficient to0 is not part of the necessary Keller chart.

A characteristic instrument is also licensed by the charged Moh PDF. It must use Moh's characteristic T2, not the degree11 auxiliary h3. The exact bridge is:

1. Printed p151 Lemma2.1, freshly inspected in `moh-p151.png` (M:593–613), gives `e=n−1=98` for a Keller pair; the other printed condition is that the first nonconstant eta coefficient has x-degree1.
2. Printed p150, final auxiliary-number display (`moh-p150.png`, M:572–582), gives `q2=M2−M1=143`, `lambda2=99*(−66)+33*143=−1815`, `mu2=lambda2/d2=−55`.
3. Since `M2=77<98=e`, printed p152 Prop2.2(1),(2) (`moh-p152.png`, M:682–690) supplies a specialized characteristic polynomial `T2^psi(G,F)` of exact y-degree55 whose leading coefficient is a nonzero constant. The word monic here means a unit leading coefficient, as the author explicitly says in the proof on p154, M:774–778. It must therefore be represented by a free nonzero scalar, not silently normalized to1.
4. Printed p157 Prop3.1 (`moh-p157.png`, M:934–943) expresses `T2=T1^3+sum c_(j,a) F^j T1^a`, with `a<3` and `99*j+66*a<=198`; equality occurs uniquely at `(j,a)=(2,0)`. The coefficients are in k[x] before specialization psi and constants after psi. `T1^psi=G+constant`: its target G-degree is1 and its composed y-degree66 precludes a positive F-power. Absorbing its constant shift in the free coefficients produces the exhaustive finite family

```
Q(F,G)=G^3−F^2+a*G^2+b*F*G+c*F+d*G+e0.
```

The coefficient−1 on F² is forced by cancellation of the monic y198 terms. Every lower coefficient a,b,c,d,e0 remains free. The weighted monomial list and the target degree55 are generated by `print-audit-characteristic-instrument.py`, with output in its corresponding JSON. No special pure `G^3−F^2` identity is asserted for a general source pair.

An implementation would add these five target coefficients, a scalar lambda_55 and a localizer Z_55 to the full source chart. It must impose every x-coefficient of every y-degree above55 in Q, every positive x-coefficient of `[y^55]Q`, the constant-coefficient equation `[x^0 y^55]Q−lambda_55=0`, and `Z_55*lambda_55−1=0`. The total degree of Q is at most198, so this is finite. No generator in the source chart may be dropped when these rows are added. One can subsequently use Q* elimination or component-aware localization, with the ring map and leader controls recorded.

On the **restricted pure-power control** `F=H^3,G=H^2`, Q becomes

```
b*H^5+a*H^4+c*H^3+d*H^2+e0.
```

Because H is monic of y-degree33, the successive leading y-degrees165,132,99,66 force b,a,c,d to vanish if `deg_y Q<=55`. Then Q=e0 and cannot have a nonzero y55 coefficient. The lambda_55 localizer consequently gives−1. This excludes the displayed pure-power points from the characteristic-degree55 locus. It does not prove that the unrestricted full root chart, with all coefficients retained, is empty. Other J=0 configurations need not be pure powers of a degree33 polynomial, so this characteristic instrument alone is not equivalent to the Keller condition.

The full root-chart survivor should therefore be reported as a necessary-chart survivor, not a Keller pair. Its exact characteristic failure, if it is a pure-power point, can be stated using this printed instrument without mistaking a unit on that restricted pure-power locus for a branch kill.
