# Independent continuation certificate

This audit concerns implication of completed pole rows and exact Jacobian
continuation in the declared source ring. It does not substitute a dimension
for row coverage. The companion executables and JSONs are
`print-audit-pole-membership` and `print-audit-resume-jacobian`.

## Pole rows by coefficient-ideal membership

Let s be the local cover parameter, a the derived h3 floor (9 or21), and
P the derived minor face. After quotienting by **all emitted strict scalar
coefficient equations**, write

    h3=s^a(P+s*h+), C2=s^(2a)(u+s*u+), C3=s^(3a)(v+s*v+).

Every displayed tail is arbitrary. Nothing is discarded in the source
coefficient ring. The h2 leader is H0=P^3+E, with E=uP+v. The emitted h2
target equations are the coefficients of E. For the effective outer blocks,
the floors are6a,9a,3a,6a. Denote their leaders a2,a3,b1,b2. The emitted
outer equality differences are RF=a2*P^3+a3 and RG=b1*P^3+b2. Then

    F0-P^9 = E*(H0^2+H0*P^3+P^6+a2)+RF,
    G0-P^6 = E*(H0+P^3+b1)+RG.

Both identities were independently expanded and checked exactly. If
E(pi)=sum e_i*pi^i and M(pi)=sum m_j*pi^j, every coefficient of E*M is
sum e_i*m_(k-i), hence belongs to the ideal generated by the e_i. This
proves every endpoint coefficient lies in the ideal of the actual emitted
h2/F/G target coefficients. The strict product coefficients vanish by
nonnegative series convolution after the emitted strict rows. This covers
all endpoint coefficients, including fixed highest powers absent from the
historical lower-support tags.

The unshifted outer floors are obtained by subtracting the local cover1
or2 from the effective floors; the extra t in the actual normalized F/G
assembly restores that shift exactly once. The helper asserts all endpoints
against generated source data. Fourteen negative controls per branch test
removed strict or target equations. In particular, deleting one C3 strict
row at local3a-1 gives F coefficient3P^6 at9a-1 and G coefficient2P^3 at6a-1;
retaining all floors while deleting the inner target permits H0=P^3+1 and
produces nonzero endpoint errors. Thus a floor without its coefficient
rows or a floor without its equality target cannot pass this certificate.

This universal proof requires the independent phase-chain replay of every
emitted strict and target equation. Once that replay passes, re-expanding
all large F/G products adds no mathematical condition: membership is already
certified by these identities. The helper does not claim the chain replay
has passed merely because the universal identities have passed.

## Exact w-coordinate bracket and complete slot coverage

For physical P=t^-D K and Q=t^-E L, x=t^-1,y=w/t gives

    t^(D+E-2)[P,Q] = D*K*L_w-t*K_t*L_w-E*K_w*L+t*K_w*L_t.

The w terms from differentiating x cancel. Substituting z=w-1 is an affine
polynomial-ring automorphism, so the same normalized bracket is valid in
either coordinate. A monomial pair at slots(r,q),(s,k) contributes

    ((D-r)*k-(E-s)*q) * K_(r,q)*L_(s,k)

at slot(r+s,q+k-1). The audit compared384 actual physical monomial pairs
with direct x,y differentiation, including degree33,66,99 normalization
pairs. It also checked six independent coordinate-commutation pairs and
the full abstract product-rule factorization for
F=H^3+A*H+B,G=H^2+C*H+D. All checks passed.

The final normalized Jacobian has degree bound99+66-2=163, so every scalar
slot is `(r,k)` with r>=0,k>=0,r+k<=163. There are13530 slots, of which
13529 have positive global degree; the final slot(163,0) is the actual
constant coefficient. The continuation subtracts the same mapped Jc at
that slot, preserving its already declared inverse equation.

The audit read the actual weak raw-row labels across all Jacobian phases.
It verified complete coverage of r0..41 for delta2 (6027 slots) and r0..46

## Native arithmetic is an injected subring, not a smaller chart

The FLINT backend uses `Q[w,V]`, where V is the set of every symbol actually
occurring in the mapped source blocks supplied for the current cutoff. It
embeds into the caller's full declared polynomial ring `Q[free][w]` by
w->w and each native generator v->the identical declared coordinate v.
The generator set is derived from the expressions, ordered explicitly,
and the same index table is used for FLINT encoding and decoding. The
maintained residual ideal is applied after arithmetic; it is not discarded
when the smaller arithmetic context is created.

`print-audit-native-subring.py/.json` checks actual saved phases. In the
observed delta2 t53 phase,95 coefficient generators are included in271
full free coordinates;176 unused coordinates remain represented in the
full ring. At delta52 t64,35 occur among75 free coordinates, leaving40
unused in this arithmetic block. Every coordinate removed between the
phase's free_before and free_after sets is exactly a newly recorded pivot
target. Native arithmetic performs no coordinate removal.

All t powers are nonnegative. Products add powers; the normalized bracket
also contributes at r+s, since its t derivative is accompanied by the
normalizing t. Therefore an input term above the cutoff cannot affect the
requested band, including after z=w-1. A coefficient variable affecting
a lower term through the maintained graph map is collected automatically
because the mapped expression is the input. An unused variable may still
occur in the residual ideal or a later source coefficient and remains in
the caller's full ring.

An exact native zero is the zero polynomial in Q[w,V], hence remains zero
under the full-ring embedding and in every subsequent quotient. The caller
nevertheless enumerates every k=0..163-t and fills a missing coefficient
with exact zero. Saved phase files retain only nonzero rows. Consequently
final row replay must reconstruct the complete coefficient vector and then
compare its entire nonzero list with the stored list. This certifies the
omitted entries as literal zeros; it must not merely verify the rows already
listed. The observed phases have111/100 expected scalar slots and84/74
saved nonzero rows. Those27/26 omitted slots are specifically recorded for
that replay requirement.
